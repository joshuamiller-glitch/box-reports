A. LEVEL CONFLUENCE.
- Levels: prior day high / low / close; overnight high / low; prior value-area high / low and point of control; today's opening-range high / low (5, 15, 30 min); initial-balance high / low; session VWAP and its 1 and 2 sigma bands; weekly and monthly open; round 100s and 50s; naked points of control; the nearest unfilled gap edge. All known causally at the signal second.
- Cluster = levels within D points of each other, D = 5 / 10 / 15 and 0.1 / 0.2 x ATR. Cluster strength = plain count, and a second version counting distinct KINDS (a prior-day level, a profile level, a VWAP level, a round number count once each).
- Measure first, no trading: does price react at clusters more than at single levels and more than at random prices the same distance away (bounce rate, time spent, break-and-run rate), per session.
- Then for every signal: distance to the nearest cluster AHEAD of the trade (between entry and target) and BEHIND it (between entry and stop). Test: skip or half size when a strong cluster sits ahead inside the target distance; shorten the target to just before it; allow full or pressed size when the cluster is behind the entry (protecting the stop) and the path ahead is clear; place the stop just beyond a cluster behind.
- New-book version: fade the first touch of a strong cluster in a chop call; trade the break-and-retest of a strong cluster in a trend call.
