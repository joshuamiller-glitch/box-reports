## Mechanism under test and the structural fact
Adding only when under water raises exposure on exactly the trades that lose. On a martingale price with a symmetric 60/60 bracket a staged add is a zero-mean bet minus cost, and its tail is worse unless sized down. Hence the verdict is decided by risk-capped sizing and tail metrics (maxDD, worst week, worst 20 weeks), not by mean pts. Positive expectation needs the order-book gate to raise P(recover to the target | dip) enough to pay 0.25 pt entry tick + 0.242 pt commission per add-lot + the measured target correction (1.4/1.5/4.2/4.3 pt) when the target is moved nearer.

## Cell grid (F = 1,728 per book-set; 11 evaluation sets = 6 live books + stack total + 4 D6-family books; best-of-N and RC are computed within each set over its 1,728)
sizing (3): initial/stages = 50%/1, 33%/2, 25%/3 (remaining live size split equally; risk-capped sizing below).
X (8): 5, 10, 15, 20, 30 pts and 0.25, 0.5, 0.75 x stop distance (60 pt stop: 15/30/45; WT 45 pt stop: 11.25/22.5/33.75), measured from the CURRENT AVERAGE price (so add 2 is at -X from the average after add 1).
gate (8): none (control); imb5 >= +0.2 with trade; imb5 >= +0.3 with trade; wall behind (liq within 8 ticks behind price >= 2 x trailing median of the same quantity, same ET hour, prior 20 label days; the 10 visible levels end ~2.5 pts from price so this is the NEAR wall proxy for "resting size between price and stop"; the true stop wall is not visible in mbp-10); no pulled size on my side in 10 s (strict: pulled_sup10 == 0; relative: pulled_sup10 <= trailing same-hour p25) as two gates; delta60 > 0 with the trade; absorption (adverse-aggressor volume per (1+adverse ticks) over 10 s >= 2 x trailing same-hour median). Gate is evaluated at the end of the trigger second; failed gate keeps the stage armed up to 60 s after the first touch while close stays beyond the level.
target (3): rebased on the new average (same pts from the average), original target price kept, average + half the original distance.
stop (3): (i) original stop price, adds of the remaining live size (total <= live lots, so dollar loss at the original stop <= single-entry loss by construction); (ii) stop rebased on the average (same pts from the average), total <= live lots; (iii) RISK-CAPPED / risk-matched: original stop price, each stage sized so that dollar loss at the ORIGINAL stop of the whole position equals the single-entry loss when all stages fill (stage budget (1-f0)*stop/S divided by the add's distance to the original stop), total lots capped at 2.0 x live (never more $ risk at the stop than the single entry). Max worst-case $ at the in-force stop vs the single is computed and reported for every stop variant.
Fills: every fill MARKET +1 tick adverse (FEES/MEASURED V0), decided at the end of the trigger second at that second's close; exits: stop 1.01, time-block 0.995, target year correction 1.4/1.5/4.2/4.3 (THP MEASURED exit costs); commission $4.84 RT per contract per fill. Stack-level sweep cost per order (equal_risk.excess) is netted as in the stack evaluation. Baseline = the same rows with the single full-size entry on the SAME cost basis ("M-basis single"); stack deltas are added to the official audit weekly base (46,785/wk) exactly as stk_f did for engine deltas.
Lock engine: parents plus children rows through a copy of sl_lib.run_book (entry test on the parent only, children follow the parent; day give-back arm and week floor flatten the whole position and cancel adds after the lock second); sl_lib is not modified.
Never-under-water: reported per X (share of trades where the no-gate cell makes zero adds) and share of trades never below entry.
