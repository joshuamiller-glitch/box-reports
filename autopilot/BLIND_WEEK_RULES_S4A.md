## 1. Lock rules (scope PerBook) and the P7 / K7 halts
**The rules** (identical to the engine's sl_lib per-book stages; 'always inside the engine', d7_v13x header): R = the book's R in dollars (planned lots x stop x $20; the lock R scales with lots only when the multiplier exceeds 1, never under Q1-A).
(1) ENTRY CAPS (realised only): a new entry is skipped when the book's realised P&L of the label day (positions already closed at the entry second) is >= +5R or <= -2R. (2) DAY GIVE-BACK: e(t) = realised + open mark-to-market of the book over the label day on the 1-second path; the first second with running peak >= 2R and e <= 50% of the peak triggers: every open position of that book is flattened at the next printed second and all later entries of that label day are cancelled, i.e. the book is halted until the label-day roll at 18:00 ET. (3) WEEK FLOOR: same e over the week; e <= -12R flattens and cancels the rest of the week. Order (1) -> (2) -> (3).

So the module can be applied to the replay's trades with confidence.
**Do the rules reproduce 'P7 day-halted by give-back Wed 01:44 -> 18:00' and 'K7 day-halted Thu ~04:35 -> 18:00'?** Structurally yes: a give-back halt lasts to 18:00 ET by construction (the label day rolls at 18:00), and 01:44 Wed is inside the label day that began Tue 18:00 (Asia session), 04:35 Thu is the London session of the label day that began Wed 18:00.
