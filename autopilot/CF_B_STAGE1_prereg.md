# CF-B stage 1 prereg - structure-level confirmations (OTE first; P/D50 + Fibonacci; pivots; prior-day/week/month; round numbers; opening range)
Written 2026-10-30 before any P&L statistic of these cells was computed (only a kernel-equivalence check against the existing resting-limit kernel: net diff 0.0 on 29,800 trades, and a levels causality test: 0 mismatches in 2,244 checks).
Cell list: l2vast/ds3/code/confirm/cf_b_cells.py, 1,870 cells (OTE 420, P/D50+Fib 700, pivots 440, prior anchors 160, round 30, OR 120); sha256 of the name list 2abe33ca2552a5269a4c5dec2f161a35e8b3ea0f465752595d74f7be6ea929c0.
Level generators: cf_levels_struct.py (fractal-5 swings per TF confirmed only at the close of the 2nd following bar; dealing range = [min of last 3 confirmed swing lows, max of last 3 confirmed swing highs]; OTE/P-D at 62/70.5/79/50 from the extreme in the trade's favour; Fibonacci 38/50/62/79 of the last confirmed ALIGNED swing pair (long needs low before high); classic/Camarilla/Woodie pivots, prior-day H/L/C/50% from Globex day, RTH day, week, month = latest completed period; round numbers nearest x100/x50/x25 on the dip side; OR 5/15/30/60 edges+mid usable after the window closes). Swing/dealing range minimum height 4 pt. Validity window: level 1.0 .. (stop distance - 3) pt on the dip side of the signal price, else the trade is UNARMED and takes the floor unchanged. Levels fixed at the arming second (no re-pricing).
Mechanics (all on the 70/30 base: 70% at the signal, the 30% remainder risk-sized at the fill, max 2 lots, target re-based on the average, stop = signal -/+ stop distance, hold dies at original stop / opposite signal / flatten = karm window of the floor): (a) market at first touch of the level (fill next second at close +1 tick); (b) resting limit at the level placed at arming, fill at touch (measured basis), stress label = 1 tick through; (c1) market when adverse excursion >= X AND the level has been touched (X = 3/5/8/10); (c2) once the dip >= X, rest a limit at the level (marketable if already through), fill at touch.
Mechanism and predicted effect: a retracement level is only useful if it is where liquidity is; the null (random level, same distance) says every deeper fill buys a better price but loses fills and sometimes the trend. Prediction: ~0 above the floor per contract once distance is controlled; deeper levels (OTE 79, far pivots) show higher ppc on filled trades and lower fill rate, a distance effect (STRUCTURE). Any edge must beat the random-level placebo at the same distance distribution.
Floor (primary comparison): L2SE pooled split = F 0.7, dip 3 pt, 1-of-8 L2 gate, risk-capped, rebased target (ufe_lib cfgP(.7,3,1)); beside it the ungated plain 3-pt dip split (cfgP(.7,3,0)) and market at signal. Cells have NO L2 gate (so the gated floor includes a small L2 add-information component, +0.06 pt/contract per cycle 4; the ungated floor is the clean structure control).
Bases: N (primary), M, LIVE-CAL (N + stop exits at 2.45), entry cost ce=0. Unit: pts/trade per contract of final position, paired delta vs floor on ARMED trades (and fill rate, kept/skipped). 6 engine books on the lock-engine stack line, all 10 books for per-contract. Windows 2026 (20260101..20260206), L6M, HOLD, PRE, X'; all frames <= 20260206.
Selection: PRE u X' (20230401..20250630) only. Per level family the top-5 cells by SEL paired delta t-stat vs the gated floor (N, min 200 armed trades, fill>=30%) are carried; family-level aggregates (mean over all cells of the family) are the one recent look, plus recent windows for the carried cells. Placebos for carried cells: random level at the same distance distribution (distances permuted across armed trades within book, 20 draws), recent window and SEL. White RC over the family's cells on SEL and on L6M for the carried. Cumulative multiplicity: UF_TRIALS.json append side="confirm" n_cells 1,870 (n_effective estimated from the PCA of cell delta series); cumulative starting count 143,584 + earlier cycles.
Tiers: PASS/CANDIDATE only >= 0 per contract vs the floor in L6M AND HOLD with CI, placebo beaten, RC cumulative p <= 0.05 or forward evidence; else CANDIDATE-RECENT / BEST-AVAILABLE-NEGATIVE / DEAD with failed gates named. Splits/multipliers/re-based targets/fixed distances are STRUCTURE, not L2 edge.
Streams for CF-COMBO: out/confirm/B/streams_touch.npz (per level name int32[n_trades]: first second index k in [kin,karm] where the level is touched, -1 if never; aligned to ls_data.D order; touched-by-k AND = max of touch indices; ORDERED = compare touch indices) + levels.npz (price). Rankings: out/confirm/rankings/stage1B_<book>.parquet from PRE u X' only.
