# CF-ENG stage 1 prereg (2026-10-30) -- FVG / inverse FVG / implied FVG / order block / breaker x 14 timeframes x mechanics a, b, c on the 70/30 base
Written before any scored statistic (only cell-count/fill-rate smoke runs on PRE for engine validation, no selection). Engine: l2vast/ds3/code/confirm/cf_engine.py + cf_events.py (API doc cf_engine_API.md).
Mechanism (one sentence): the held signal waits for a same-direction ICT array to form (a: market when it prints; c1: market when it prints AND the 3-pt dip has happened) or rests the 30% slice at the array's near edge / CE / far edge (b; c2 = only after the dip started); hold dies at original stop/target second, opposite signal, flatten, base exit (karm), session lots 0 via the lock engine.
Predicted effect per family (honest prior): confirmation/limit mechanics are re-based fixed-distance/timing STRUCTURE; arrays at 15 s-5 m are near-random placements relative to a 3-pt dip, so expect delta vs the plain dip floor within +-0.1 pt/contract, negative on market-at-confirmation (chasing after the dip floor's cheaper entry) and mildly positive where the limit sits deeper than 3 pts and fills (b, mid/far). Splits/multipliers/re-based targets are STRUCTURE, not L2 edge.
Cells: 5 families (FVG, iFVG, implied FVG, OB, breaker) x 14 TFs (15s 30s 45s 1m 2m 3m 5m 10m 15m 30m 1h 4h 1d 1w) x {a, b-near, b-mid, b-far, c1, c2-near, c2-mid, c2-far} = 560 cells. All on F=0.7 (70/30 risk-capped, target re-based on average), X=3 pt dip (c), dmin 1 pt / dmax 30 pt level distance, level must lie >= 1 pt inside the original stop. Basis M and N both scored; stress label (+1 tick through) for b and c2; every cell scored for all 9 books (KB PE SM HM MV PB DN ST WT; MA extra; CR unavailable: no 1-s path), session column = session_tag, windows Y26/L6M/HOLD/PRE/X'.
Definitions (exact): cf_events.py docstring (FVG min gap 1.0 pt; iFVG = far edge closed through within 300 bars; implied FVG = body gap with wick overlap; OB = last opposite bar before a displacement bar within 3 bars, body >= 1 ATR14, close beyond OB candle extreme; breaker = OB closed through within 300 bars; maxage = max(1 day, 10 x TF), 30 d weekly). Bars closed only; event knowable at the bar's ek.
Controls per cell (same distance / rate): plain 3-pt dip split (floor, no gate) and L2SE pooled cell, market at signal, random-level placebo (b, c2: 5 draws, distances permuted within book), time-matched random confirmations (a, c1: 5 draws), shuffled-day structures (+7 d, all cells).
Selection: per book, rank the 560 cells on PRE u X' only by paired mean delta vs the plain-dip floor (per planned lot), requiring fill >= 10% of held signals or ranked on stack line; rankings saved out/confirm/rankings/stage1ENG_<book>.parquet BEFORE the single look at L6M / HOLD / 2026 of the top-3 per book (+ pooled-book). Only those top cells are read in the recent windows; full tables are on disk but not used to choose.
Gates / tiers: CANDIDATE-RECENT needs delta >= 0 per contract in L6M and HOLD, PRE u X' delta > 0, beaten placebo (real > 95th pct of its 5 placebo draws, PRE u X' and L6M), CI/MDE shown. PASS needs White RC over the stage's 560 cells per book AND adjusted for cumulative 143,584 + (UF_TRIALS) cells at p <= 0.05, or independent forward evidence -> none possible this stage; otherwise BEST-AVAILABLE-NEGATIVE / DEAD.  All cells appended to DEAD_CELLS.csv (program CFENG1, side entry) and UF_TRIALS.json (side=confirm, n_cells 560, n_effective by PRE weekly delta participation ratio).
Known engine deviation: kernel kills the hold at the original stop/target second (lj_kernel.jrun can fill a dip after the stop second: 13 of 44,370 trades).
Not in this stage: LIVE-CAL/LIVE-P9 bases (BASISL not complete), structure-stop variant, other bases (100% market / pure wait F=0 are supported by the engine, not scored).
