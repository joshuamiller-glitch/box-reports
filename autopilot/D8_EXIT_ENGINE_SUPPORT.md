3. Queue: append 'SET MONTH' lines to /mnt/l2/l2vast/_exiteng/pending.txt (production driver via the dispatchers; box-1 jbjobs pattern is NOT valid for dollars, do not use ex_loop.sh); newest months first; >= 43 months per set, >= 50 per family pooled; both-side + mode-correct sets pool.
