## 3. Port warnings that matter
- The trend_20 bars on RAW records give events 1-2 s EARLIER than the D8 _CANC bars for 284 of 1,768 events (16%). The engine events and every number of this book use the _CANC bars: the NT8 builder must stamp a bar with the time its closing run is known complete (the next print of a different price or side).
- The trend brick keeps WICKS (high/low include the print extremes since the previous brick) and the reversal brick opens at the PRIOR CLOSE (not B back): the opposite of classic Renko. The first bar of a session needs the session first print as the reference.
- Events at or after 08:30 ET are dropped using the bar KNOWN time (a signal at 08:29:59.x would be ordered at 08:30:01 and is dropped); the session-end exit is 08:30 ET.
