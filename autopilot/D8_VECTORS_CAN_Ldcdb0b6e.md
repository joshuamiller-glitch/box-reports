# POINTER: superseded

Use D8_VECTORS_CANC_Ldcdb0b6e.md (causal), D8_VECTORS_CANG_Ldcdb0b6e.md (1 s cap) or D8_VECTORS_CANR_Ldcdb0b6e.md (reference, first-record stamp).
