T-CONFIRM-LAYER ADDENDUM — COMBINATIONS: confirmations together, not only one at a time (user, 2026-10-30)

Applies to T-CONFIRM-LAYER v2 and to T-SIGNAL-DECAY. Every single-confirmation cell still runs; this adds the combinations on top, scored the same way (per contract first, measured fills, recent-first, placebos, lock-engine stack line).

1. What a combination is
A set of confirmations that must ALL be present (AND) — across protocols and across timeframes — before the held signal enters, e.g. "a 15-s FVG forms in favour AND price is at the session POC AND CCI(20) has crossed back above −100", or "5-m order block touched AND VWAP −1σ AND RSI(2) < 10 AND absorption on the bid". Also OR-sets (any k of n), and ORDERED sets (A then B within N seconds, for the time-based items). Confirmations include every item of the catalogue: ICT/SMC arrays, price structure, profile/VWAP levels, moving-average/trend states, every oscillator, candlesticks, the order book/tape, time windows, and the dip itself (3/5/8/10 pts; from the signal and from the running extreme).

2. How to search them (the space is too large to enumerate blindly)
(a) ALL PAIRS of the top-50 single confirmations per book (ranked on PRE+X' only, before any recent look), each pair × the six entry mechanics × the three bases; then ALL TRIPLES among the top-20; then the greedy forward build (add the confirmation that most improves the per-contract number on PRE+X', stop when it stops improving) up to 5 conditions; then k-of-n with k = 2..4 over the top-10. (b) CROSS-TIMEFRAME structure of the same item: FVG on 15 s inside an FVG on 5 m inside a 1-h discount; OB on 1 m at a 15-m OB; POC of the session at the composite POC — nested / stacked arrays as their own combination family. (c) PROTOCOL FAMILIES together: one location item (array / level / band) + one momentum item (oscillator state) + one flow item (book / tape) + one context item (HTF trend / premium-discount / killzone): the "four-legged" confirmation, every combination of the top-5 in each family (625 cells per book per mechanic), and the three-legged subsets. (d) The learned combination: boosting / monotone logit on ALL catalogue items at the signal second and at every held second (interaction terms allowed; depth ≤ 4 so the trees ARE combinations), walk-forward 3-month weekly; the rule-extraction of the fitted trees as explicit AND-sets (so the user can read them: "15-s FVG ∧ POC within 2 ticks ∧ CCI cross"); retained gain of the extracted rules vs the model. (e) Prune everything for FILL RATE: a combination that fires on < 10% of held signals is reported but ranked on the stack line (the skipped signals' P&L counts against it); the per-contract number is shown beside it so a rare, strong combination is not hidden.

3. Gates
Multiplicity handled by the PRE+X'-only selection and ONE recent look per stage; White RC over each stage's cells; the random-level and time-matched placebos run on the combinations too (a combination of random levels at the same joint rate); a combination is CANDIDATE only ≥ 0 per contract in the last 6 months AND HOLD, placebo-beaten, and better than the best of its own members alone (otherwise it is just the member).

4. Report
DS3_CONFIRM_COMBOS.md per stage: the best pairs / triples / four-legged / learned sets per book, written as readable AND-sets with timeframes, per contract first, fill rate, the members' own numbers beside them, the stack line; the plain sentence "requiring A ∧ B ∧ C before the PE signal enters adds X pts per contract on Y% of signals in the last 6 months (MDE Z) vs the best single confirmation W". Anything ≥ 0: an entry rule in THP_POLICY.txt with the full AND-set in D6's vocabulary.
