The mechanism being tested (one sentence)

A live book fires its signal (KB / PE / SM / HM / MV / PB / DN / ST / WT / CR / the D6 session cells); instead of entering, the signal is HELD, and the entry happens only when a second confirmation from another protocol appears — either at market when it appears, or as a resting limit placed inside the confirming structure (an FVG below, an order block, the POC, a VWAP band …) that price has to come down to. The hold dies at the original stop level, on the opposite signal, on session lots 0, at the flatten; no clock timer (timer variants as controls only).

2. Entry mechanics for every confirmation (each a cell family; all on the three bases: 100% market-at-confirmation, 70/30 split with the remainder at the confirmation, pure wait-for-confirmation)

(a) MARKET AT CONFIRMATION: enter at market the second the confirmation prints (bar close for bar-based items; touch for levels). (b) RESTING LIMIT INSIDE THE STRUCTURE, placed at arming: at the FVG top / CE / bottom; at the OB open / mean / low; at the POC / VAH / VAL; at the VWAP band; at the OTE 62 / 70.5 / 79; at the fib level; at the pivot; at the prior-day level; at the round number — the limit rests until filled, the hold dies at the original stop level / opposite signal / session lots 0 / flatten. Fill on the measured limit basis; the +1-tick-through variant as the stress label. (c) DIP + CONFIRMATION: the dip (3 / 5 / 8 / 10 pts from the signal, and from the running extreme) AND the confirmation (the user's "entering on FVGs in favour on dips on all timeframes", "entering on POC on dips") — market when both hold; or the limit at the structure only once the dip has started. (d) CONFIRMATION AS FILTER: enter at market at the signal only if the confirmation is already present at the signal bar (inside / at the structure; oscillator already in the zone); the skipped signals' P&L reported beside the kept signals'. (e) ALIGNMENT / CONFLUENCE: the number of confirmations present (across protocols and timeframes) at the signal and at the dip as a sizer (risk-matched lots) and as a filter (≥ 2 / ≥ 3). (f) STOPS: the original level (signal ∓ 60) as scored; and the structure stop (just beyond the FVG / OB / level, risk-matched lots) as the ICT-style variant; target from the fill / from the average, and the liquidity-target variant (next buy-/sell-side pool). Controls for every family: the plain 3-pt dip (the scored split), market at signal, random-level placebo (same distance distribution), shuffled-day structures, time-matched random confirmations at the same rate. Each cell: per contract (pts/trade, CI, MDE, fill rate, trades kept / skipped and their P&L), stack line on the lock engine, per book / session / window recent-first.
