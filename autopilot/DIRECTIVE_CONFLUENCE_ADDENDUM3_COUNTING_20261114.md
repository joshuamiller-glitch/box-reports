A subset of traders trade purely by counting candles; build every school as causal conditions at the signal second, on 1 / 3 / 5 / 15-min bars and on the 2000-tick, 8-pt range and Heikin-Ashi bars where they are on the box. State for each: published rule (exact) or approximation.

1. BROOKS BAR COUNTING. In an up leg, High 1 = the first bar whose high exceeds the prior bar's high after a pullback bar; High 2, 3, 4 = each later such bar after a further failed attempt; Low 1-4 mirrored for down legs. Conditions: the signal comes on / within N bars of a High 1, High 2, High 3 or High 4 (or Low n) in the trade's direction; the current count against the trade (a Low 2 forming against a long); the count resetting on a new trend extreme; whether the count sits at a moving average or a level (High 2 at the 20 EMA is the textbook version). Follow the convention that a count needs a pullback of at least one bar; report how often two reasonable codings disagree.
2. SECOND-ENTRY RULE. First signal after a pullback skipped, second taken: is our signal a first or a second entry of this leg; bars between the first and second attempt; the failed first entry's extreme taken out or not.
3. LEGS AND PUSHES. Legs in the current pullback (one, two, three); two-legged pullback complete; pushes in the current trend leg (one, two, three = wedge); third push with or without momentum divergence; measured-move completion of leg two equal to leg one; consecutive trend bars in the leg and the largest bar's position in it (a climax bar late in a leg).
4. BAR NUMBER OF THE SESSION. Bar number since the session open on 5-min bars (and since 09:30 ET for the cash session); test every bar number 1..81 as a condition per book, then the windows traders name (the first 6-12 bars, the late-morning bars around 18-20, the midday bars, the last 12); bar number since the session's high and since its low; whether the session's high or low was made in the first N bars. Walk-forward only: a bar number that works in one year and not the next is noise.
5. DEMARK COUNTS in full: setup count 1..9 (buy and sell), perfected or not; countdown 1..13; TD Combo; price flip; the count on the higher timeframe at the same second; "9 or 13 against the trade within the last N bars" as a veto and "9 or 13 with the trade just completed" as a go.
6. STREAKS. Consecutive up / down closes; consecutive same-colour bars and Heikin-Ashi colour runs; consecutive higher highs / lower lows; consecutive new session highs / lows (the 8-to-10 new-records idea); consecutive inside or narrow bars before the signal; three-bar and four-bar sequences (three soldiers / crows, three-line strike, rising / falling three methods) coded exactly.
7. TIME COUNTS FROM A PIVOT. Bars since the last confirmed swing high and low (causal pivots only: a pivot is known only after its right-side bars close); the count equal to or near 5 / 8 / 13 / 21 / 34 / 55 and to fixed counts 9 / 18 / 27 / 36; symmetry (this leg's bar count vs the previous leg's); bars since the last signal of the same book.
8. WAVE COUNTS, mechanical version only: a zig-zag at fixed ATR thresholds labelled as swing 1..5 and A-B-C by rule (higher-high / higher-low sequence, wave 3 not the shortest, wave 4 not overlapping wave 1); conditions: entering in swing 3, in swing 5, in a three-swing correction. Label it an approximation: discretionary wave counting cannot be coded faithfully.
9. HORIZONTAL COUNTS. Width in bars and in volume of the consolidation the signal breaks from; projected target = width x a box factor (point-and-figure style) and whether our fixed target sits inside or beyond it; number of touches of each side of the range before the break (third and fourth touch).
10. COUNTS ON D7's OWN EVENTS. Signals of this book so far this session; consecutive signals in the same direction; flips of the book's engine in the last N bars (many flips = chop); bars since the engine last flipped.

HOW IT IS USED.
- Every count enters the checklist as conditions, and each school is ALSO scored as a self-contained checklist (a pure bar-counting trader's rules applied to our entries): Brooks-only, DeMark-only, second-entry-only, streak-only.
- As new books through the injection hook: High 2 / Low 2 at the 20 EMA with the trend; second entry after a failed first; three-push wedge reversal in a chop call; DeMark 9 and 13 exhaustion fades at a level cluster.
- At the exit: a completed count against an open winner (a 9, a third push, a streak of 8+) as a take or lock trigger; a fresh count with a loser as a reason to hold.
- Redundancy: counting conditions go into the same correlation clustering as everything else.

CONTROLS as in the parent order, plus one specific to counting: for every bar-number and time-count result, the same test on bar numbers shifted by 1, 2 and 3 bars. A real effect fades smoothly with the shift; a mined one vanishes or jumps.
