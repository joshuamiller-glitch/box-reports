DEFINITIONS, all causal.
- THE RANGE (score each definition separately): (a) prior day high / low; (b) prior session high / low and the current session's range once it is 60 minutes old (Asia, London, New York); (c) overnight range; (d) weekly range (prior week high / low); (e) the dealing range on 15 / 60 / 240-min: between the last confirmed major swing high and major swing low (pivot strength 10 / 20 bars each side); (f) the opening range (30 / 60 min).
- EXTERNAL liquidity = the range's own high and low (and equal highs / lows at them). INTERNAL liquidity = confirmed minor swing highs / lows inside the range (pivot strength 2 / 3 / 5), equal highs / lows inside it, and unfilled fair value gaps inside it.
- A SWEEP = price trades beyond the level by at least 1 tick / 2 pts / 0.1 ATR and closes back on the original side within 1 / 2 / 3 / 5 bars (1, 3, 5 and 15-min bars). Record depth of the poke, bars beyond, volume and delta on the poke, time of day.
- Each sweep is tagged internal or external for each range definition; a level that is external to a small range and internal to a larger one carries both tags (e.g. a session high inside the prior day's range).
