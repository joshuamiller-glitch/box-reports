(relay) The catalogue is my design. Research only. It EXTENDS families X1-X11 (orders 03:01Z, 03:03Z, 03:11Z); same subjects (live books' own entries first), same walk-forward at 2 / 4 / 13 weeks, same baseline (fixed bracket + live Trade Health), same "solution" definition, drawdown first. Every item is tried on the LOSS side (when to get out or tighten) and the PROFIT side (when to take or let run). Add your own ideas to the list and say which are yours.

X12. PATH PROFILE OF THE BOOK'S OWN TRADES (from past trades only): cut when adverse excursion exceeds what past winners of this book tolerated (by percentile); take at the favourable-excursion percentile where past trades stopped improving; exit when time in trade passes the time by which past winners had peaked; an EXIT MAP by optimal stopping on the grid (open profit in R x time in trade x volatility state) giving hold / take / cut per cell.
X13. PROBABILITY EXITS: a model fitted on past data of P(target before stop) and expected remaining value given the current state; exit when expected value turns negative, press (trail wider) when it rises. Hazard / survival version for time. (X11 is the general learned policy; X13 is the simple, explainable one.)
X14. ORDER-BOOK MICROSTRUCTURE IN REAL TIME: book imbalance and microprice turning against the position; depth thinning ahead of the stop (liquidity vacuum) or building ahead of the target; spread widening; flow toxicity rising (VPIN-style); price impact per contract rising; cancel-to-add ratio; queue depletion at the best price; iceberg and absorption detection (heavy volume, no progress: take if at the target side, hold if it is defending the stop side); aggressive sweeps with or against the trade.
X15. STOP HUNTS AND ACCEPTANCE: stop only on ACCEPTANCE beyond the stop level (N seconds / one bar close beyond, or volume traded beyond), not on a touch; place the stop beyond the obvious stop cluster (prior swing plus a liquidity offset) instead of at it; take profit INTO a stop-run (price takes out a prior high/low then stalls or absorbs); re-entry rule after a stop-out when the trigger re-fires (tight stop plus re-entry against wide stop).
X16. MOMENTUM AND EXHAUSTION: exit when the move's own momentum rolls over (efficiency ratio, slope t-stat, MACD histogram, RSI or CCI turning from an extreme); delta or CVD divergence at a new extreme; volume climax; three-bar or engulfing reversal on the book's own bars; Heikin-Ashi / Supertrend / PSAR / Donchian N-bar / moving-average flip; Keltner or Bollinger band tag or re-entry; squeeze firing against.
X17. ANCHORS: VWAP anchored at the ENTRY (leave when price loses it); session VWAP re-cross; for fade and mean-reversion books exit at the mean (VWAP, mid-band, z-score zero) and time-stop at the measured half-life; for breakout books exit on return inside the broken range (failed breakout).
X18. VOLATILITY CLOCK: intraday seasonality of volatility (expected range for the next N minutes by time of day) setting stop distance and patience; tighten when realised volatility contracts after entry, widen or take on expansion; flatten or tighten ahead of known high-volatility times (cash open, 10:00, London open, close) and ahead of scheduled releases IF an event calendar is available (the UWREGIME page says FOMC/CPI/NFP dates are not on the box: say what free source exists and the cost of adding it; buy nothing).
X19. OTHER MARKETS AND OPTIONS: ES, ZN/ZB, crude and any stored index future breaking against or confirming; NQ/ES lead-lag divergence; UW net-premium burst or Market Tide turning against the position; dealer gamma regime as an exit STYLE switch (positive gamma: take profits at levels, tight; negative gamma: trail and let run); gamma walls and the flip as targets and as lines not to hold through.
X20. PORTFOLIO-AWARE EXITS (inside a session mix): when another book on the same side stops out, tighten this one; an opposite-side signal from another book as an exit or a tighten; cap on total open risk across books (trim the weakest open trade first); session-level trailing lock on open plus closed profit that trims positions rather than only halting entries.
X21. EXECUTION OF THE EXIT ITSELF: target as a resting limit placed a few ticks in front of round numbers / levels / resting size (queue priority) versus market-on-touch; stop as stop-market versus stop-limit versus marketable limit on trigger; at 4+ lots, staged exit slices over seconds versus all at once; measure fill difference with the engine's book walk and label MEASURED / STRESS.
X22. COMBINATIONS: the best loss-side family with the best profit-side family per book; and a small "checklist" exit (leave when any 2 of k real-time warnings fire), k and the warnings chosen on past data, shown against a same-count random-exit null.
