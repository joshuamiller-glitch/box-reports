# T-FAVORABLE-ZONES — user directive pasted 2026-10-30 (verbatim)

T-FAVORABLE-ZONES — the mirror of NO-TRADE-ZONES: take the signal ONLY when it fires FROM a favourable location (a POI behind it, a discount/premium that favours it, a book that supports it), every protocol, every timeframe, every combination; and the location-quality score (2026-10-30)

Research only. No live, execution-server or chart change. UNTOUCHED never read. MEASURED fills; per contract first; recent-first; session as a column; nothing killed on the pooled number; placebos; lock-engine stack line; prereg per section. Uses the POI catalogue of T-NO-TRADE-ZONES (same structures, same freshness tracking) and the confirmation catalogue; runs beside them and shares their stores.

The question
Where does a signal come FROM when it works? A PE long that fires off yesterday's low, inside a 4-h bullish FVG, in the discount of the weekly range, with a defended bid under it, is a different trade from the same PE long fired mid-range with nothing under it. Nothing has scored "the signal is at a favourable location" as the condition to take it (or to size it up). This program builds the location-quality map and then the rules, with the skipped signals' P&L always beside the kept ones.

A. The location-quality map (descriptive, before any rule; the user wants to SEE it)
For every POI type × timeframe (the full NO-TRADE catalogue: ICT/SMC levels and arrays, Wyckoff range edges / springs, swings / pivots / fibs / round numbers / OR / gaps, profile / VWAP levels and bands, order-book structure, HTF MAs / clouds / bands) — when a live signal fires with the POI BEHIND it (between the entry and the stop, within D = 2 / 5 / 10 / 15 / 20 pts, or the entry inside the zone), per book / session / window recent-first: P(target), pts per contract, MAE profile, vs the same book's signals with no POI behind; the same conditioned on the book at the POI (resting size, refill, absorption, iceberg = "defended"); the same for premium / discount position (the signal in the lower / upper third of the daily / weekly / session dealing range, the OTE zone), for HTF trend agreement (the signal with the 1-h / 4-h / daily Supertrend, EMA stack, Ichimoku, structure BOS), and for "the signal is the second touch / the retest" of the level vs the first. Ranked by the lift in P(target) and in pts per contract: the "most favourable locations in this market" list, with the plain line "a PE long fired within 10 pts above yesterday's low reaches the target X% vs Y% otherwise (last 6 months, n)".

B. The rules (each × base: market / 70-30 / pure; per book; per session)
TAKE ONLY: enter only when ≥ 1 favourable POI of type T on timeframe TF is behind the entry within D (each type / TF / D); only in discount (long) / premium (short) of the TF dealing range (each TF); only in OTE; only with HTF agreement (each HTF tool); only when the book defends the level (resting size ≥ k × median at / just below the POI, a refill, an absorption event there in the last N s); only on the retest (second touch); only when the location is a CONFLUENCE of ≥ 2 / 3 POIs across timeframes (stacked location); only when both a favourable POI is behind AND no trusted reversal POI is ahead within the target distance (the NO-TRADE rule and this one together = "from support, into space").
SIZE BY LOCATION: lots × 1.5 / 2.0 (risk-matched report) on favourable location, × 0.5 / 0 otherwise; the weighted location score (each POI type weighted by its PRE+X' lift from A) as a continuous sizer (lots ∝ score, capped 2×, floored 0) and as a threshold filter.
STOPS AT THE LOCATION: when a POI is behind, the stop just beyond the POI (risk-matched lots: more lots on a tighter structural stop, the ICT way) vs the fixed 60; the hybrid (the tighter of the two); target unchanged / the liquidity target (next pool ahead) / re-based.
THE 30% AT THE LOCATION: on the 70/30 base, the reserve rests as a limit AT the favourable POI behind the entry (the FVG CE, the OB mean, the PD low + 2 ticks, the VWAP band) instead of a plain 3-pt dip (the confirm-layer mechanic, now with the location chosen by its quality rank).
COMBINATIONS (COMBOS method): location ∧ confirmation (e.g. "inside the 4-h FVG ∧ 15-s HA flip ∧ book absorption at the level"), location ∧ HTF agreement ∧ premium/discount ∧ book defence (the four-legged location), k-of-n; the learned version: a walk-forward model of P(target) and E[pts] from all location features at the signal second (with the signal-quality and HTF features), policy skip / half / full / 1.5× / 2×, shuffled-location placebo; the extracted rules in words.
Controls: random levels at the same distance distribution; shuffled-day POIs; the unfiltered book; the skipped signals' P&L beside the kept signals' gain in every row; the per-session fire rate (a filter that keeps 30% of signals is a different book — report trades/week).

C. Robustness requirements (so this order is the strongest version)
Every location definition parameterised and swept (distance D, zone tolerance, freshness, timeframe set); every rule scored on PRE+X' for selection, one recent look; White RC per section; the random-location and shuffled-day placebos on every cell including the combinations; the fill-rate / trades-per-week column everywhere; the stack line with the lock engine (fewer trades change the locks' behaviour); the equal-risk frame labelled structure; per-contract CI and MDE; the drift by quarter of the top locations (which locations worked in 2024 vs 2025-26) and the walk-forward re-selection chain of the top-10 locations (the adaptive version, last re-fit printed); session variants registered, never killed by the pooled number; everything per book AND pooled with the book as a feature.
