# L2-UNTIL-FOUND ADDENDUM — per contract, information edge only (user, 2026-10-30 ~03:10Z) — verbatim-condensed, all items kept
Applies to the standing order and to every cycle from now.
1. THE MEASURE IS PER CONTRACT, size-independent. Primary number everywhere: pts per trade per contract (and $ per trade at 1 lot), with CI and MDE, recent windows first. Stack $/wk at live lots is a secondary line only. Lot scaling, equal-risk factors, lot multipliers, split fractions and re-based targets are STRUCTURE, not edge: a rule whose gain disappears at 1 contract with a fixed 60/60 bracket is not a finding. The 70/30 split is re-stated per contract (labelled a structural result, not an L2 result).
2. THE QUESTION IS INFORMATION: does the book predict the next move at all? Before any rule, each cycle measures the predictive content of the order book directly, per contract, out of sample, recent-first. TARGETS: signed move over the next 10/30/60/300 s and to the next +-5/+-10/+-20 pts (which first), from (a) every signal second, (b) every in-trade second in profit, (c) every in-trade second under water, (d) every second (unconditional). FEATURES: full l2feat library t1-t4, the MBO order-level set where on disk (order lifetimes, cancel bursts, fleeting share, queue position, hidden size), the 10-30 s pre-extreme "lean", the sequence of the last 60 s of book states. MODELS: linear/logit with the information coefficient (rank correlation of prediction and outcome) and AUC per feature and for the model; boosting; hazard model; a sequence model (GRU/TCN on the 60-s window) once; walk-forward 3-month re-fit; shuffled-book placebo and time-shifted placebo (features from 60 s earlier) - the gap to the placebo IS the information. REPORT the information map: IC and AUC by feature family x state (signal/in-profit/under-water) x session x window, recent first; top 20 features at the last re-fit and drift by quarter; plain sentence per state: "the book predicts the next 30-s move with IC x (placebo y) in the last 6 months - enough / not enough to trade on at z pts per contract after costs". Only features/models with out-of-sample IC above the placebo and a per-contract expectation above the measured cost of acting on it become rules. A rule is published only with its per-contract number.
3. WHAT IS NOT A FINDING: fixed-fraction splits, lot multipliers, re-based targets, fixed-distance cuts/trails, "take at X%" rules, and anything whose gain comes from the fill-basis correction. May be reported as structure (per contract), never as L2 edge, never as the reason to stop.
4. STOP CONDITION restated: three per-contract, placebo-beaten, out-of-sample information edges (entry, profit side, loss side) each with a rule that is >= 0 per contract after measured costs in the last 6 months and HOLD. Until then the box cycles; each cycle's information map is appended to DS3_L2_INFO.md and the best per side (per contract) to THP_POLICY.txt with its tier.
