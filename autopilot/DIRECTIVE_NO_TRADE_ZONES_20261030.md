T-NO-TRADE-ZONES — do not take (or size down) a signal that fires INTO a trusted reversal spot: every POI, every protocol, every timeframe, every combination (2026-10-30)

MEASURED fills; per contract first; recent-first; session as a column; nothing killed on the pooled number; placebos; lock-engine stack line; prereg per section. Uses the confirmation catalogue (T-CONFIRM-LAYER + addenda) — the same structures, used the other way round: as places NOT to enter toward.

The question
A KB short that fires 4 pts above yesterday's low, a PE long that fires just under a 4-h bearish FVG, an SM long into the weekly high — the signal is valid but the target sits on the far side of a spot where price is more likely to turn than to continue. Nothing has tested skipping, sizing down, or re-targeting signals by the structure AHEAD of them. Both directions are scored: "ahead" (the trade must travel through the POI to reach the target) and "behind" (the entry is right at a POI that supports it — the opposite, a possible size-UP).

B. The rules (each POI × timeframe × distance × action × base; per book; per session)
DISTANCE: the POI is within D pts ahead of the entry (D = 5 / 10 / 15 / 20 / 30 / 40 / 60) — i.e. between the entry and the target — or within D of the entry in either direction; D also in ATR units and as "the target is beyond the POI".
ACTIONS: skip the signal; size 50% / 67% (risk-matched report); re-target to just in front of the POI (target = POI − 2 ticks) and keep the lots; re-target AND skip if the resulting target < 20 / 30 pts; wait (hold the signal until price trades through the POI, then enter — the confirm-layer mechanic); enter only the 70% and reserve the 30% behind the POI; the inverse rules for a POI BEHIND the entry (size up 150% / keep full; tighten the stop to just beyond the supporting POI, risk-matched).
FRESHNESS / STRENGTH: unmitigated vs mitigated POIs; the number of timeframes agreeing at the level (a confluence POI: PD low = session low = 4-h OB edge); the POI's age; the number of times it has held in the session.
COMBINATIONS (the COMBOS method): any-of-k POIs ahead within D; the weighted POI score (each POI type weighted by its PRE+X' reversal rate, see C) ahead of the entry as a filter / sizer; the four-legged version (structure POI + profile POI + book POI + HTF trend).
The learned version: a walk-forward model of P(target before stop) from the POI features ahead/behind at the signal second (plus the signal-quality and HTF features already built); policy skip / size / re-target; placebo on shuffled POIs.
Controls: random levels at the same distance distribution; shuffled-day POIs; the unfiltered book; the skipped signals' P&L reported beside the kept signals' gain (a filter that skips winners is scored as such).
