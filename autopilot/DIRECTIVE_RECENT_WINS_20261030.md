Rules for the table
It is a VIEW, not a verdict: nothing is promoted by being in it; the tier column carries the gates. The point is that the user can see, in one place, every rule that made money in the most recent regime, how much, and how fragile it is.
Every row names its multiplicity: "1 of N cells in its program" so a row from a 23,000-cell sweep reads differently from a pre-specified rule.
A "what it is" line per row in plain words (one sentence): "exit a losing KB when the 15-s HA flips against, fires on 22% of losers, +0.3 pts per contract L3M".
A summary header: the 10 rows with the largest L3M $ at live lots; the count of rows per program; the sentence "in the last 3 months, the things that improved the stack most were …" and the sentence "the things that look best in L3M but did not exist before mid-2025 are …" (the regime flag, from the drift-by-quarter reads).
Structural rows (splits, lot factors, re-based targets, caps) are included but labelled STRUCTURE in the note; information rows (a feature that predicts) labelled INFO.
Live-fill basis shown where it differs from the measured basis (the stop-cost and target-fill findings apply).
