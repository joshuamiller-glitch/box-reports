# T-RISK-OFF — user directive (2026-10-30), re-sent 2026-10-31 (verbatim)

T-RISK-OFF — stop management as a decision, not a ritual: move the stop (to breakeven, to structure, to MAE+k, to a book level) only when a factor says so; every factor, every combination (2026-10-30)

Research only. No live, execution-server or chart change. UNTOUCHED never read. MEASURED fills (a moved stop fills on the measured stop basis, 2.45 pts worse than nominal on live; the join-fix variant at 0.5 beside it); per contract first; recent-first; session as a column; placebos; lock-engine stack line; prereg per section. Uses the THP/THL path trees, l2feat, the confirmation catalogue (T-CONFIRM-LAYER + addenda), the signal-decay features, the stack state.

Why the retail version died and what this does instead
"BE after +X" and "BE after N min" lost because two-thirds of runners come back through entry before going to target (the MAEMFE order-of-touch table). A stop moved on a CLOCK or a POINT COUNT sits exactly on the retest. This program moves the stop only on EVIDENCE — a reverse confirmation, a book event, a structure break, a stack event — and compares every version to hold-the-original-stop on identical trades, with the target unchanged. The action is never an exit; it is where the stop sits.

A. The stop destinations (each rule chooses one)
breakeven (entry) / entry ± k ticks (k = 2 / 4 / 8, "BE+") / MAE + k pts (k = 5 / 10 / 15; locks in that the worst is behind) / the last structure (the pullback low-high, the signal bar's extreme, the last 1-m / 5-m swing, the FVG/OB edge, the VWAP band, the POC) / a book level (the largest resting level behind price within 10 levels; the absorption price) / a volatility distance (1 / 1.5 / 2 × ATR-1m behind price) / the halfway point to target / "lock X% of open profit" (X = 25 / 50). And the direction: only ever tighter (ratchet) vs allowed to re-widen when the evidence reverses.

B. The triggers (each × each destination × under-water-only / in-profit-only / always × min age 0/1/2/5 min)
REVERSE CONFIRMATION: every mirrored item of the confirmation catalogue (opposing FVG/OB, structure/VWAP lost, MA cross/slope against, oscillator against, HA flip / renko reversal / footprint against, sub-minute movement against) — as the trigger to tighten (not exit).
ORDER BOOK: the book turning against (imbalance flip against, delta turn against, support pulled z ≥ 2, wall behind gone, iceberg against, sweep against, thick book around a winner, microprice against), and the book turning WITH (to re-widen / to leave the stop alone — the "support is holding, do not tighten" gate).
PROGRESS / PATH: MFE ≥ X% of target then a pullback of Y% of the MFE; velocity against; stall (no new MFE for N s while the book is against); the trade's own MAE already deep (MAE ≥ 30 and now recovering: tighten to MAE+k).
SIGNAL DECAY: the book's premise weakened but not invalidated (slope flattening, vote dropping, the level approached) — tighten; invalidated — tighten to the invalidation level (the T-SIGNAL-DECAY cells with "tighten" as the action, re-used here).
STACK: another book on the same side stopped / cut; the stack at −X R; the first book to reach +X R (tighten the others); the opposite-side book entering.
TIME / SESSION: the session boundary approaching (15 / 30 min before 16:55, before a release, before the London/NY open): tighten to structure; the killzone ending.
VOLATILITY / REGIME: ATR regime change mid-trade; a vol spike (range of the last 1 min > k × normal) against; the day type flipping (first-hour range tag).
COMBINATIONS (the COMBOS method): pairs / triples / k-of-n across 1-7 (e.g. "15-s HA flip against ∧ support pulled z ≥ 2 → stop to MAE+10"; "MFE ≥ 50% of target ∧ opposing 5-m FVG forms → lock 50%"), the four-legged version (path + book + confirmation + stack), and the learned version: a walk-forward model of P(stop before target from here) and E[remaining] from all factors, with the policy "stop destination = argmax expected pts per contract after the measured stop cost" (the firm's version), 3-month weekly re-fit, placebos.
STAGED: two-step ratchets (BE+ on the first trigger, MAE+k on the second, structure on the third) and the asymmetric "tighten on evidence, re-widen on counter-evidence".

C. Scoring
Each cell vs hold-the-original-stop on identical entries (and vs the live path TH, the L2 health rules, and the 70/30 base): per contract pts/trade, the split "stops avoided (the trade would have gone to the full stop) / targets forfeited (the moved stop was hit, the trade would have reached the target) / neutral", P(target | tightened but held), fire rate; random-tighten placebo at the same rate and time-since-entry; the shuffled-day version of each trigger; the lock-engine stack line; equal-risk frame reported as structure (a rule that cuts worst-20-weeks allows more lots: report the factor). Recent-first.
