# DIRECTIVE T-CLOSE — the cash-close / MOC study (received 2026-10-27, verbatim)

T-CLOSE — the cash-close / MOC study. The second clock window the live stack does not trade (no entries 15:00–16:54 ET, flat at 16:55), done at the same scale as T-OPEN: every closing idea, every target, every instrument, every bar type, the tape and the book, verdict = MONEY on the stack. Runs after T-OPEN's final (or in parallel with it if T-OPEN's later blocks leave cores idle); all cores at the 90% floor until DS3_CLOSE_FINAL.md exists. Same V2 rules as T-OPEN (preregs ≤ 30 cells, as many as needed; placebo vs best-of-N; 2023-04..2024-01 second holdout; main holdout once per group; equal-risk; executability flag). Research only.

0. Why this window, and the test that decides
The stack is flat 15:00–16:54 and blocks entries there, so a close book adds with zero overlap; its marginal value is its own P&L and the comparison is "stack + close book" vs "stack" on the weekly series, plus the baseline of simply un-blocking the existing books 15:00–16:54 (section 9). Placebos: (a) timing-destroyed; (b) same-rule-random-clock (the identical rule started at a random minute ≥ 60 min from 15:50/16:00 and from any release, matched by session); (c) random-in-regime for direction-filtered cells. PASS requires beating (b).

1. Data, the window and what we do NOT have
NQ 1-s tape and mbp-10 (direct replay) 2023-02+; ES, RTY, YM, CL, GC, SI, ZN, 6E 1-min/tick where on disk. Window 14:30–16:15 (the close), extension 16:00–18:05 (post-close / overnight hold) and to the next 09:30 (hold-to-open).
The MOC imbalance feeds (NYSE 15:50 / 15:55 / 15:58 publications, Nasdaq 15:55 cross imbalance) are NOT on disk. Proxies, each its own cell family and disclosed as proxies: the 15:50:00–15:50:30 price / volume / delta reaction on NQ and ES (the market's response to the publication); the 15:55 and 15:58 update reactions; ES-vs-NQ divergence after 15:50; the day's net direction and VWAP distance (imbalance tends to align with the day's flow); cumulative delta 15:30–15:50. NEEDS.md line: a historical MOC imbalance series (NYSE/Nasdaq) would convert the proxy cells into direct ones — state the vendor options and the expected cost, do not buy.
Reference levels at 14:30: day H/L, open, developing VWAP (from 09:30 and from 18:00), VAH/VAL/POC, IB, the 15:00 price, prior close/settle, ON H/L, the 200-day SMA, round numbers; day type (trend / range / gap-and-go / gap-fill / V-day) classified from the 09:30–14:30 path; 20-day medians of the 15:00–16:00 and 15:50–16:00 ranges.
Calendar columns: opex (monthly / weekly — the pin), roll day, month-end / quarter-end (rebalance flows), MSCI/S&P/Russell rebalance days, half days (13:00 close), FOMC day, day-before-holiday, Friday (weekend hold), mega-cap earnings after the close (NVDA/AAPL/MSFT/AMZN/GOOGL/META/TSLA/AVGO etc. — the 16:00–16:30 reaction), VIX tercile, the compression forecaster.

2. Day type at 14:30 / 15:00 / 15:30 (the interaction axis on every family)
Trend day (close-to-open move ≥ {1, 1.5}× ATR20 share, one-sided delta), range day (inside the IB), V-day / reversal day, gap-fill day, opex pin day (range compression), "late-day trend" (15:00 onward extends the day's high/low), position of price vs VWAP and vs the open at 15:00, ES/RTY/YM agreement, bond close direction at 15:00. Interaction table (type × family × paired delta with CIs) required.

3. Entry families (each its own prereg group)
3.1 MOC-reaction cells: direction of the 15:50:00–15:50:{10, 30, 60}-s move (proxy for the imbalance sign) continued to {15:55, 15:58, 16:00}; same at 15:55 and 15:58 updates; magnitude thresholds ({3, 5, 10} pts in the first 30 s); ES-leads-NQ variants; fade of the 15:50 spike when it exceeds {2, 3}× the 20-day median 1-min range.
3.2 Last-hour continuation: the day's direction (close vs open, vs VWAP, vs 15:00 price, vs the IB) entered at {15:00, 15:15, 15:30, 15:45, 15:50, 15:55}, exit at {15:58, 15:59:30, 16:00, 16:14 (settle)}; trend-day-only variants; VWAP-distance thresholds; "15:00 bond-close pivot" (the 15:00–15:05 move as the trigger).
3.3 Last-hour reversal: stretched days (range ≥ {1.5, 2}× median, price at the day's extreme at 15:00) faded into 16:00; VWAP reversion (price ≥ {1, 2}σ from VWAP at 15:30 → target VWAP by 16:00); the "3:30 reversal" (15:30–15:35 turn); opex-pin reversion (toward the day's VWAP / the nearest 100-strike on opex); closing-range fade (the 15:50–15:58 spike reversed in the last 2 min).
3.4 Closing-range breaks: range of 15:00–15:30 / 15:30–15:50 broken after 15:50 (stop-entry and close-outside variants); the day's H/L broken in the last 30 min (late breakout → hold to 16:00 / overnight).
3.5 End-of-day dip-buy / rip-sell: RSI(2) on 1-min / 5-min / daily at 15:55 (RSI2T is the cross-reference row), IBS of the day at 15:55, close-near-low-of-day buy / close-near-high sell for the overnight hold, 3-day pullback; with and without the 200-day SMA filter; the overnight-drift cells (16:00 → 18:05 / 02:00 / 09:30 next day) as the exit family.
3.6 Post-close 16:00–16:30: the 16:00–16:05 move continued to 16:15 (settle) and 18:00; mega-cap earnings reaction cells (direction of the first 5 min after the 16:00–16:30 print continued to 18:30 and to the next 09:30; mapped execution flagged); the 16:15 settle → 18:00 drift.
3.7 Level interaction into the close: VWAP, day H/L, prior close, VAH/VAL/POC, round numbers, the open — touch-and-hold vs sweep-and-reclaim in 15:00–16:00; confluence cells.
3.8 Cross-asset at the close: NQ vs ES relative strength 15:30–16:00; RTY/YM confirming; ZN's 15:00 close direction; 6E; the bond-equity hand-off. Other instruments' own closes as books: CL 14:30 settle, GC/SI 13:30, ZN 15:00 (and 16:00 settle), 6E 17:00 — same families at their own clocks.
3.9 Footprint into the close (1-min and 15-s): delta share 15:30–15:50, stacked imbalances at the day's extreme, delta divergence at a late new high/low, absorption at VWAP / the day's extreme, unfinished auction at 16:00 (overnight revisit cell), POC migration in the last hour.
3.10 L2 into the close (NQ mbp-10): book imbalance at 15:50:00–15:50:30, depth asymmetry 15:55–16:00, pulled liquidity ahead of the 15:58 update, icebergs at the day's extreme, sweep-and-reclaim of the 15:50 spike level, queue depletion; as triggers and as confirmation columns on 3.1–3.5 (EXEC-ONLY flagged; tick-only proxies flagged NT8-ticks).
3.11 Learned close classifier (V2 guards): at 15:30 / 15:50, features = sections 1–2 + 3.9–3.10, labels = the 15:50–16:00 direction and the 16:00 → 09:30 direction; enter at calibrated P ≥ {0.55, 0.6}; noise features, CPCV, permutation, isotonic in-window, no-tape control; reported, promoted only if it beats the best hand-rule cell paired.
3.12 Controls: the DS2 KB/SM/ST flips restricted to 15:00–16:54 (the un-block baseline), random-direction entry at 15:50 with every bracket in section 4, the mirror of each family, and the K4 overlap row (K4 holds overnight from 16:17 — report the correlation and the combined line, never a change to K4).
