# DIRECTIVE T-OPEN — the opening-drive study (received 2026-10-27, verbatim in the lead's AUTOPILOT.md paste log; this file holds the full text)

T-OPEN — the opening-drive study. One family, done the way a firm would do it: every opening idea, every target, every instrument, every bar type, the tape and the book, and the verdict is MONEY (direct fills, weekly P&L, drawdown, added to the stack), not an entry statistic. Starts when every item already queued (DIRECTIVE_PRIORITY_1027 items 1–6, DIRECTIVE_PBX_CHOP_FULL, T-SESSION-LOCKS, section-A per-session brackets) has its final report; from then on it has ALL cores at the 90% floor until DS3_OPEN_FINAL.md exists. Supersedes the unsent T-EVENT draft. Same V2 rules: mechanism + predicted effect + MDE per cell, preregs ≤ 30 cells (as many preregs as the grid needs — nothing is dropped for being too many; the ledger carries all), placebo vs best-of-N, 2023-04..2024-01 second holdout, main holdout once per prereg group, equal-risk columns, executability flag. Research only; nothing goes live from this file.

0. Why this window, and the test that decides
The live stack is flat 09:00–09:59 ET (the forward-tested block). An opening book therefore adds to the stack with ZERO concurrency conflict and zero overlap with existing trades: its marginal value is its own P&L, and the comparison is simply "stack + open book" vs "stack" on the weekly series. Every cell is scored on that basis, and the live block itself is re-scored as the baseline row (section 9). Three placebos, every cell: (a) timing-destroyed; (b) same-rule-random-clock: the identical rule started at a random minute ≥ 60 min from 09:30 and from any release slot, matched by session (does the OPEN carry the edge, or the rule?); (c) random-in-regime for any cell with a direction filter. PASS requires beating (b); beating (a) alone is labelled "rule beta".

1. Data and the window
NQ 1-s tape and mbp-10 book (direct replay) 2023-02+; ES, RTY, YM, CL, GC, SI, ZN, 6E 1-min (and tick where on disk; RU reports show these series exist). Pre-market 08:00–09:30, open window 09:30–10:30, extension to 12:00 and to 16:00 for the hold cells. The 10:00 release slot sits inside the window: every cell carries a "10:00 release present" column and a variant that flattens 09:58–10:02.
Calendar table (shared, audited): 08:30 release present/type, 10:00 release present/type, FOMC day, opex (monthly/weekly), roll week/day, month-end, half day, day-after-holiday, mega-cap earnings day-after (top-15 NQ weights). These are COLUMNS and conditioning cells, never silent filters.
Reference levels computed before 09:30 each day: overnight H/L/mid (18:00–09:30), pre-market H/L (08:00–09:30), prior-day H/L/C, prior-day VAH/VAL/POC and naked POCs (last 10 sessions), prior-week H/L, developing VWAP (from 18:00 and from 09:30), 20-day median of the 5/15/30/60-min opening range, ATR20 daily, gap vs 16:00 close and vs 16:15 settle, round numbers (00/50), the 200-day SMA, the monthly/weekly opens.

2. Opening type (classified live at 09:35 / 09:40 / 09:45 / 10:00; every entry family is scored overall AND per type)
Dalton types on the 1-min: open-drive (no trade back through the open in the first N min), open-test-drive (one probe through the open then drive), open-rejection-reverse (probe beyond the ON/pre-market extreme, rejected, reverses), open-auction (two-sided inside the ON range). Plus quantitative types: gap class (≥ 0.3 / 0.5 / 1.0 ATR20, up/down, vs close and vs settle), open inside / above / below the ON range and the prior-day range, first-1-min volume vs 20-day median (≥ 1.5× / 2×), first-5-min delta share, ES/RTY/YM agreement at 09:35, pre-market drive (08:30 → 09:30 move ≥ 0.5 ATR), VIX tercile and the compression forecaster. The interaction table (type × family × paired delta with CIs) is a required output.

3. Entry families (all scored; each family has its own prereg group)
3.1 Opening-range breaks: OR = first {30 s, 1, 2, 3, 5, 10, 15, 30, 60} min; break = stop-entry at OR H/L + 1 tick (trade-through, flagged), first 1-min close outside, first 5-min close outside, close outside by ≥ {2, 5} pts, two consecutive closes outside, retest-and-hold of the OR level within {5, 15} min, second break after a failed first break; OR must be ≥ / ≤ {0.5, 1, 1.5}× the 20-day median as conditions; one-and-done vs re-entry on the second break.
3.2 Drive continuation: direction of the 09:30–09:3x move ({1, 2, 3, 5, 10, 15} min) entered at the x-minute mark, at the first {1, 3}-min pullback, or on the first higher-low; "open-drive" cells that enter only when the open has not been traded back through; momentum thresholds (move ≥ {5, 10, 15, 20} pts, ≥ {0.5, 1}× 5-min median range).
3.3 Gap plays: gap-and-go (enter with the gap on the first OR break / drive), gap-fill (fade toward the 16:00 close / 16:15 settle; target the fill, partial fill 50%), gap-and-trap (gap that fills in the first 15 min then reverses back in the gap direction), gap size × gap direction × ON-range position cells.
3.4 Exhaustion / fade: first {2, 5, 10}-min move ≥ {1.5, 2, 3}× the 20-day median for that length → fade to VWAP / to the open / to the OR mid; fade of the ON high/low probe at the open (open-rejection-reverse); fade of the pre-market high/low sweep; "V-open" (first 5-min bar's range ≥ 2× median with a close in the opposite third).
3.5 First pullback: after a drive, the first pullback to {VWAP, OR extreme, EMA20-1m, the 50% of the drive, the first 5-min low} between 09:35 and 10:30; entry on touch / on the arming-bar break (the PB3B machine) / on the first higher-low; one per day.
3.6 Level interaction: open vs ON H/L, prior-day H/L/C, VAH/VAL/POC, naked POC, prior-week H/L, pre-market H/L, round numbers, monthly/weekly open: break-and-hold vs sweep-and-reclaim at each level in the first 60 min; confluence (≥ 2 levels within 5 pts) as its own cell.
3.7 Initial balance and TPO logic: IB = first {30, 60} min; IB extension in the direction of the break (target IB × {0.5, 1, 1.5, 2}); IB range vs the 20-day median as a day-type; A/B period rules (B extends A high/low → continuation; B inside A → range day).
3.8 Pre-market and the 08:30 hand-off: the 08:30 release move's direction carried into 09:30 (continuation vs reversal at the open), pre-market trend (08:00–09:30 slope) with/against the open, pre-market range break at 09:30.
3.9 Cross-asset at the open: NQ vs ES relative strength in the first 5 min (NQ leads / lags), RTY/YM confirming the OR break (2 of 3, 3 of 3), ZN moving against equities, dollar (6E) direction; and the OTHER instruments' own opens as books: CL 09:00 pit open, GC/SI 08:20, ZN 08:20, 6E 03:00/08:30, plus NQ at the 03:00 European open (second-open cells, same families at their own clock).
3.10 Footprint at the open (1-min and 15-s footprint from the tape): stacked imbalances (≥ 3 diagonal ≥ 300%) on the break side of the OR, delta ≥ {60, 70}% one-sided in the first {1, 3, 5} min, delta divergence at the OR high/low (price new high, delta lower), absorption footprint at the ON/pre-market level (high volume, no range), unfinished auction at the OR extreme (revisit cell), POC migration in the first 15 min, cumulative delta making a new extreme with price (confirmation) vs not (fade).
3.11 L2 at the open (mbp-10, NQ only): book imbalance at the touch at 09:30:00–09:30:10 (±3 / ±5 levels), depth asymmetry across the first minute, pulled liquidity on the break side (resting size cancelled ahead of the break), iceberg / refill at the OR extreme (refill count ≥ 3), sweep-and-reclaim of the OR level within N s, queue depletion on the counter side, large-print clusters; each as an entry trigger and as a confirmation column on 3.1–3.5. Executability: EXEC-ONLY (needs L2 at runtime) flagged; the same cells with tick-only proxies (volume, delta) flagged NT8-ticks.
3.12 Learned open classifier (V2 guards): at 09:35 / 09:45, features = sections 1–2 + 3.10–3.11, label = the 09:45–11:00 direction (+30 before −30 pts); enter when calibrated P ≥ {0.55, 0.6}; noise features, CPCV, permutation, isotonic in-window, no-tape control. One block, reported but not promoted unless it beats the best hand-rule cell paired.
3.13 Controls: the DS2 KB/SM/ST flips restricted to 09:30–10:30 (what the stack would have earned in the blocked hour — the baseline row), random-direction entry at 09:31 with every bracket in section 4 (bracket beta), the mirror of each family.

4. Targets, stops and exits (the full grid; every family × every shape is prereg'd in blocks)
Fixed points: target {10, 15, 20, 25, 30, 40, 45, 50, 60, 75, 90, 120} × stop {10, 15, 20, 30, 40, 60, 75}; every ratio reports required vs observed win rate after cost.
Structural targets: OR height × {0.5, 1, 1.5, 2, 3}; IB × {0.5, 1, 1.5, 2}; ON H/L; pre-market H/L; prior-day H/L/C; VAH/VAL/POC; naked POC; prior-week H/L; VWAP and VWAP ±1σ/±2σ; the gap fill (full / 50%); the measured move (pre-market or first-drive height); ATR20 × {0.25, 0.5, 0.75, 1}; the next round number; the 20-day median full-day range from the open.
Structural stops: OR opposite extreme ±{0, 5}; the ON extreme; the open price; the pullback low +5; 1× / 1.5× ATR(14)-1m; VWAP; the 09:30 bar's extreme.
Time exits: 09:45, 10:00, 10:15, 10:30, 11:00, 11:30, 12:00, 13:00, 15:50, 16:00; hold-to-close for drive cells; overnight hold (→ 18:05 / next 09:30) for the trend-day cells.
Trails and locks: Supertrend-1m/5m band, chandelier k {2, 2.5, 3}, swing-structure (last higher-low −5), SAR, breakeven at +{0.5, 1}R then trail {10, 20, 30}, profit-lock +1.5R → +0.5R; scale-out {33, 50}% at {0.5, 0.75, 1}R with the runner to a structural target or trail (two-leg direct replay); re-entry after a stop-out on the second break (on/off).
Negative-R and high-win-rate shapes explicitly: target 15 / stop 45, 20 / 60, 30 / 60 on the drive and gap-fill cells (the win-rate-hump question, with the hurdle table).

5. Bars (level, trigger and footprint bars; built once, audited)
1-s, 5-s, 15-s, 30-s, 1-min, 2-min, 3-min, 5-min, 15-min, 30-min; 100 / 250 / 500 / 1,000 / 2,000-tick; 3 / 5 / 10 / 20-pt range; Renko 5 / 10 / 20 (standard and UniRenko-style); volume 500 / 1,000 / 2,500 / 5,000 contracts; dollar bars $10M / $25M / $50M; footprint (bid/ask volume per price) on 1-min, 15-s, 500-tick and 5-pt range. AS-OF rule (usable when complete), truncation-invariance audit per bar type; executability flagged (NT8-native vs custom BarsType vs tape-only).

6. Order types and execution (the open is the thinnest, fastest book of the day — this is scored, not assumed)
Market at the signal (direct replay at the real second: slices S10x1 and single-clip, cost by clip size at 09:30:xx vs 09:45:xx vs 10:15:xx reported as its own table); stop-entry at the level (+1 tick; MAPPED with the stop-fill caveat AND a direct variant that fills at the first trade through the level on the tape, with the slippage from the next 1-s of prints); resting limit at the level (trade-through, queue model, time-out {5, 15, 30} min); OCO straddle around the OR (both-side stop entries, the other side cancelled; direct replay of both legs); ladder {level, −5, −10}; scale-in on the retest. Latency sensitivity 5 / 50 / 150 ms; cost 1× / 1.5× / 2×. A cell that pays only with stop-entries at level +1 tick is labelled MAPPED-ONLY and gets the direct tape-through variant as its verdict basis.

7. Instruments
NQ primary (direct L2 basis). ES, RTY, YM at 09:30 (mapped; direct where book data exists); MNQ/MES as execution-size variants only. CL at 09:00, GC/SI at 08:20, ZN at 08:20, 6E at 03:00 and 08:30 — each with the same families at its own open; and NQ at 03:00 (European open) as a second-open book. Cross-instrument: the same cell's weekly correlation across instruments (an open edge that exists on all four index futures is a stronger claim than one on NQ alone — report the pooled and per-instrument verdicts), and the portfolio of opens (NQ + ES + RTY + CL + GC opens as one sleeve at equal risk, with their own margin line).

8. Sizing and capacity
Fixed-R $12,000 and $6,000 per trade (lots from the stop), fixed 10, equal-risk to ST10's weekly SD; the 50-cap line is NOT binding (the stack is flat 09:00–09:59) but the margin line and the book depth at 09:30:00–09:31:00 are — report the capacity curve from the real book at the entry second (lots at which the direct cost halves the edge), not from midday depth. Day-of-week, gap-type and VIX-tercile sizing as columns.

9. Scoring — MONEY, on the stack
For every cell, direct basis where NQ, exact lock engine (own day/week rules AND the stack's per-book rules as two rows): weekly P&L series → mean, SD, Sharpe, maxDD, worst week, L8W, trades/wk, win rate vs required, avg win/loss, profit factor, by year, by VIX tercile, by day-of-week, by opening type, by gap class, long/short, 2× cost, holdout (one look), 2023 span, placebo ranks (a)(b)(c), best-of-N line. Then the stack line: "stack + cell" vs "stack" weekly (mean, SD, maxDD, worst week, Sharpe delta, CI of the weekly difference), and vs "stack with the 09:00–10:00 block removed" (the baseline the user already forward-tested: does the open book beat simply un-blocking KB/SM/ST in that hour?). Equal-risk column vs ST10. No cell is DEAD solely for earning less than ST at equal lots; a cell that adds to the stack at the 50-cap is the object of this study.

10. Design
Blocks: (i) levels, calendar, opening-type classifier and bar library built and audited (no cells); (ii) each family (3.1–3.9) on 1-min bars with market entries and a 12-cell bracket core (fixed 30/30, 45/45, 60/60, OR×1/OR, VWAP, ON H/L, time 10:30, time 12:00, hold-to-close, trail ST-1m, chandelier 2.5, scale-out 50% at 1R) — the screen; (iii) the full section-4 target/stop/exit grid on the top 3 cells per family; (iv) opening-type and conditioning interaction tables on the top cells; (v) bars (section 5) × order types (section 6) on the top 5 cells overall; (vi) footprint and L2 families (3.10–3.11) as triggers on the top 5 cells' setups and standalone; (vii) other instruments and second opens (section 7); (viii) the learned classifier (3.12); (ix) the robustness block on the top 10 overall: full parameter surface, day-block bootstrap maxDD, VIX terciles, by year, fill sensitivity, cost sweep, capacity from the open book, mirror, correlation to every live book, the stack-add line with the concurrency replay; (x) the pooled opens sleeve (section 7) with its margin line. Every block's prereg lists the previous block's result it builds on.
