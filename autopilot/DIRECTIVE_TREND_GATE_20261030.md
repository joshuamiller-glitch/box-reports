# T-TREND-GATE — user directive pasted 2026-10-30 (verbatim)

T-TREND-GATE — take the trend books only in TREND conditions: every trend / chop / regime measure, every timeframe, every combination; and the mirror for the mean-reversion books (2026-10-30)

Research only. No live, execution-server or chart change. UNTOUCHED never read. MEASURED fills; per contract first; recent-first; session as a column; nothing killed on the pooled number; placebos; lock-engine stack line; prereg per section. Shares the catalogue stores of T-CONFIRM-LAYER / BARS; runs beside the location programs.

The question
KB, ST, SM, HM, PE, MV, DN, PB7 are trend / pullback-in-trend setups. The only regime gates that exist are KB's EMA-200 side and PB's Supertrend-10m. Nothing has scored "is the market trending right now, on which timeframe, how strongly" as the condition to take (or size) each book — and the EINT1 read that trend strength separates the top and bottom tercile by 3-6 pts in 2025-26 says the answer may be large in THIS market. The reverse question for the mean-reversion cells (MV in part, the range/fade candidates): take them only in chop.

A. The trend-condition catalogue (every item on 1 m / 3 m / 5 m / 15 m / 30 m / 1 h / 4 h / daily, and on the BARS types where defined; each as a continuous value AND as a bucket: deciles, terciles, on/off)
MA geometry: separation between MA pairs (8/21, 9/21, 20/50, 50/200, 33/50/200 ZEN stack; every MA type) in ATR units and in ticks; MA stack order (all aligned / mixed); MA slopes (1 / 3 / 5-bar, in ATR per bar) and slope agreement across timeframes; price distance from the 20 / 50 / 200 in ATR (and "extension" = too far: the exhaustion side); ribbon width and expansion / contraction; HMA colour run length; VWAP slope and price-vs-VWAP distance in σ; Supertrend direction and age (bars since the flip) on each timeframe; Ichimoku (price vs cloud, cloud thickness, tenkan/kijun separation, chikou free); Parabolic SAR run length; Donchian position (price in the upper / lower x% of the 20 / 55 channel) and channel expansion. Trend-strength indicators: ADX (7 / 14 / 20) level and slope, DI spread; Aroon up/down and oscillator; Choppiness Index; Kaufman efficiency ratio (10 / 20 / 50); Hurst exponent (rolling 100 / 200); linear-regression slope and R² (20 / 50 / 100); Vortex; TRIX; MACD histogram sign and run; RSI regime (above / below 50 for N bars); Fisher; Schaff; Vertical Horizontal Filter; the Trend Intensity Index; Ehlers' trend-vs-cycle mode; ATR expansion / contraction (ATR-14 vs its 50-bar median) and Bollinger bandwidth / squeeze state; realised-vol regime (1 / 5 / 30-min) vs the 20-day median; range expansion of the last N bars vs median. Structure-based: HTF BOS / CHoCH direction and count (higher highs / higher lows run on 5 m / 15 m / 1 h); swing-count trend (number of consecutive HH/HL); Wyckoff phase (markup / markdown vs range) on 15 m / 1 h; the day type ex-ante (first-30/60-min range vs median, gap, overnight range, ES-NQ agreement, opening drive) and ex-post classification of the prior day; time-since-the-last-trend-day. Order-flow trend: CVD slope and CVD-price agreement (5 / 15 / 60 min); aggressor delta run (N consecutive bars with delta in one direction); OFI trend; the book's persistent lean (imb10 sign for ≥ 5 / 15 min); volume trend (rising / falling vs median); large-print direction bias over 30 / 60 min; ES / RTY trend agreement (the same measures on ES / RTY, and the three-index agreement count). Cross-timeframe: the count of timeframes agreeing on direction (1 m .. daily) by each tool; "trend alignment score" (weighted by the PRE+X' lift in B).

B. The trend map (descriptive, before any rule)
For every measure × timeframe × bucket, per book / session / window recent-first: P(target), pts per contract, trades per week, MAE profile, vs the book's unconditional; the same for "signal direction agrees with the HTF trend" vs disagrees; the exhaustion read (extension decile: does a KB break 3 ATR above the 20-EMA do worse than one 1 ATR above it?). Ranked by lift; the plain line per book "PE longs with ADX-14 (5 m) > 25 and the 50/200 separated by > 1 ATR reach the target X% vs Y% otherwise (last 6 months)"; the drift by quarter (is the trend premium new?).

C. The rules (each × base: market / 70-30; per book; per session)
GATE: take the book only when measure M on timeframe TF is in bucket B (each M × TF × B from A), only when the signal agrees with the HTF trend (each HTF tool), only when ≥ k of n timeframes agree, only when NOT extended (exhaustion cap: distance from the 20 / 50 ≤ x ATR), only on trend days (ex-ante day type), only when ES / RTY agree; the OFF side: do not take trend books in chop (Choppiness > 61.8, ER < 0.3, ADX < 20, squeeze on).
SIZER: lots × 0 / 0.5 / 1 / 1.5 / 2 by the trend bucket (risk-matched report); the continuous trend-alignment score as the sizer (capped 2×); the exhaustion score as a reducer.
BRACKET BY REGIME: in strong trend the target to 1.5R / 2R or the liquidity target; in weak trend 0.75R; the stop by ATR regime (the T-BRACKET grid conditioned on the trend bucket) — brackets were dead unconditionally; conditioned on trend they are untested.
THE MIRROR for mean-reversion cells (MV, the range / VWAP-fade / Asia-range candidates from the discover programs, the ZEN-type pullbacks treated as fades): take only in chop (the OFF buckets above), size by chop strength; and the "regime switch" book: trend books on in trend, fade books on in chop, scored as one stack.
COMBINATIONS (COMBOS method): MA separation ∧ ADX ∧ HTF agreement; day type ∧ trend strength ∧ ES agreement; the four-legged (MA geometry + strength indicator + structure + order-flow trend); k-of-n; the learned version: walk-forward model of P(target) / E[pts] from all A features at the signal second, policy skip / half / full / 1.5× / 2× and the bracket choice, 3-month weekly re-fit with the 6- and 12-month windows and the ensemble, shuffled-feature placebo, the extracted rules in words, feature importance and its drift by quarter.
Controls: random gates at the same keep rate; shuffled-day regimes; the unfiltered book; the skipped signals' P&L beside the kept signals' gain; trades per week in every row; the stack line with locks (fewer trades change the locks); the equal-risk frame labelled structure.
