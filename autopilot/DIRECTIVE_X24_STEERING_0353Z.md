This is the controller that sits on top of the per-book exit PLAN (03:49Z): the plan's target, runner and trail are not fixed after entry; they are steered while the trade is open by the weight of real-time evidence. Research only; same subjects, reference, fixed-rule and walk-forward rows, drawdown first.

1. EVIDENCE, scored continuously while in a trade, FOR the trade and AGAINST it (each item only if the attribution pass shows it carries information):
 - order book / MBO: size building or pulled ahead of the target and behind the stop; real walls (persistence filtered) appearing, refilling (iceberg) or being eaten; depth thinning in the trade's direction (room to run) or against it; queue depletion; cancel-versus-trade at the wall.
 - order flow: delta and CVD with or against; absorption; sweeps; large prints; speed of tape; toxicity.
 - price action: momentum and efficiency holding or rolling over; volatility expanding or contracting; acceptance beyond or rejection at the next level / liquidity pool.
 - context: other markets confirming or breaking; UW tide / premium bursts and, where intraday history exists, walls and flip moving; range used versus forecast range left; time of session.
2. WHAT THE EVIDENCE MOVES:
 a. TARGET EXTENDS when evidence is strongly FOR (wall ahead eaten or pulled, room opens, flow with): move the target to the next liquidity cluster / level, or drop the target and switch the remainder to a trail. TARGET PULLS IN when evidence turns AGAINST (new real wall ahead, absorption at a level, momentum rolling): take at the nearer level or at market.
 b. RUNNER STYLE SWITCHES: structure trail in orderly trends, volatility (chandelier) trail when range expands, tight tick/brick trail when exhaustion signs appear, line trail on the book's own trigger line otherwise.
 c. TRAIL LOOSENS OR TIGHTENS with the score: wider when evidence is FOR and the book shows protection behind the trade; tighter as evidence turns. Guard rails to test: a loosened trail may never give back more than a stated share of open profit (25 / 50 / 75%) and never falls below breakeven once a partial is banked; loosening allowed only while in profit; the INITIAL risk is never increased beyond the 03:40Z cap.
 d. PARTIAL SIZE ADAPTS: take more at the first target when evidence is weak, less when strong.
3. THREE WAYS TO COMBINE THE EVIDENCE, compared: (i) simple count (how many items FOR minus AGAINST, thresholds stated in advance); (ii) weighted score with weights fitted on past data only, refit 2 / 4 / 13 weeks; (iii) the learned policy X11 given these features with actions extend / hold / tighten / take part / take all. Prefer the simplest that holds.
4. REPORT: against the same plan with a static target and one fixed trail (so the gain from steering is visible), and against the reference; per book and per session mix; how often targets were extended or pulled in and what those trades did afterwards; order changes per trade; MBO versus 10-level versions side by side; random-steering null (same number of extends/tightens at random moments) so the steering, not luck, gets the credit.
5. ORDER OF WORK: evidence items from the attribution pass first; simple count version first on Asia and NY AM live books; then weighted and learned. No idle cores.
