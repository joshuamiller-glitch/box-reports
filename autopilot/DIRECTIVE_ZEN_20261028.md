1. Regime (the side) — each is a separate family axis

R1 price above/below the 5-min EMA200 (long only above, short only below). R2 the 1-min EMA40 above/below the 1-min EMA200 (and EMA50/200, EMA20/200). R3 both R1 and R2. R4 VWAP side (price above/below session VWAP) and R5 VWAP slope (VWAP rising/falling over the last 15 min) and R4+R5. R6 15-min EMA200 side. R7 5-min Supertrend (the T-PBX regime, as the comparator). R8 the full ZEN stack: 5m EMA200 side + 1m EMA40>200 + VWAP same side. Also the distance condition: price within X pts of the 5-min EMA200 (X = 20, 40, none) and time-since-cross (> 15, > 30 min, none).

2. Level (where the pullback is bought/sold)

L1 1-min EMA200 (touch within 2 pts / 5 pts; intrabar low/high reaches it). L2 1-min EMA40 / EMA50. L3 5-min EMA20 / EMA50. L4 session VWAP. L5 VWAP ±1σ (with-trend side). L6 2-min and 3-min EMA200. L7 the prior 1-min swing low/high (structure pullback). L8 a 1-min FVG in the trend direction (price returns into the gap). Each level on 1-min bars and on 2-min, 3-min, 30-s, 15-s, 500-tick, 1000-tick, Renko-4 (what NT8 bar types exist in the library; cite T-PBX B2 for the ones already scored on other levels).

3. Trigger (the "reaction") — this is the ZEN claim; test with and without

T0 touch only (limit at the level, the control). T1 close back above/below the level on the signal bar. T2 rejection bar: wick through the level, close in the top/bottom third, next bar breaks its high/low. T3 engulfing / two-bar reversal at the level. T4 1-min BOS/MSS: after the pullback, a break of the last 1-min pullback swing high/low (with and without a prior lower-high). T5 FVG in favour: a 1-min fair-value gap forms in the trend direction off the level; enter at the gap's edge or on the gap's formation bar close. T6 iFVG: a counter-trend FVG from the pullback is traded through (inverted) in the trend direction. T7 1-min Supertrend / EMA9-21 cross back in the trend direction after the touch. T8 volume/footprint: delta flips positive (or a 1-min volume spike ≥ 2x the 20-bar median) on the bar that holds the level; with the L2 series, absorption at the level (large passive size holds). T9 time at level: held for ≥ 2 / 3 bars without closing through. Combinations of two (T2+T5, T4+T8, etc.) for the top triggers only.

4. Brackets (every shape, since this family is traded with small targets and wide stops)

Structure: stop below the pullback low (plus 2 / 5 pt buffer) with target 20 / the prior 1-min swing high / the day high / the overnight high / 1R / 2R of the structure stop. ATR: stop 1.5x and 2x the 1-min ATR14, target 1x ATR and 20 pt. Partial: 50% at 20 pt, rest to the day high / 2R with stop to entry after the first fill (one cell, as a bracket shape). Trail: stop to the 1-min EMA40 after +20 (one cell). Flat at 14:00 / 15:55 (session rule below). Costs at 1x and 2x.
