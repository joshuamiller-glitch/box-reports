T-ZEN ADDENDUM — the Zen model as written (ZM1-ZM4), added to DIRECTIVE_ZEN (2026-10-28)

Same rules as T-ZEN (research only; screen on PRE, top 30 per family to direct replay and one holdout look; random-in-regime and random-clock placebos on every row; NY windows scored separately; never-empty-handed). This addendum replaces the generic bracket/trigger defaults with the model's own rules and adds its confluence tiers. Where the addendum and the base directive differ, run both and report side by side.

Z1. The model's brackets (replace the 30-60 pt stops as the PRIMARY shapes)

Stop below/above the level by 10 / 15 / 20 pt (the model's rule: 10-15, 20 max); also "invalidation" stops: a 1-min body close through the level / through the stack / past the 50% of the FVG / past the far edge of the OB. Targets: nearest 1-min swing high/low, equal highs/lows (two highs within 2 pt), the nearest 4-hour FVG edge, VWAP / 1-min EMA200 (for reversals), fixed 20 pt, and 1R / 2R of the stop; fill 1-2 pt before the level (the model's rule), also exactly at it. Print the cost-adjusted break-even win rate for every shape next to the realised win rate.

Z2. Management shapes (as bracket cells, not as exit rules on existing books)

(a) 50% and 75% off at the first level, stop to entry immediately after, runner to the next level (VWAP / 1-min EMA200 / 4H FVG) or flat at window end; (b) stop to entry once +10 / +15 pt regardless of scale; (c) 5-min candle trail after the first scale (stop at the last closed 5-min low, ratchet only, exit on a 5-min close below the prior 5-min low); (d) the plain full-size bracket as the control. Report each against (d) paired on identical entries.

Z3. The confluence tiers (the model's central claim: win rate rises ZM1 -> ZM4)

Level and reaction as in T-ZEN, with the tier as a label on every trade:

ZM1: price pulls back to the 1-min EMA200 OR session VWAP, hold candle (wick into the zone, body close back on the trend side), 5-min EMA200 side agrees.
ZM2: the 1-min EMA200 and VWAP within 3 / 5 / 8 pt of each other at the touch (the "stack").
ZM3: ZM1 or ZM2 plus a 1-min FVG (bodies define validity; first or second touch only) or an order block (bullish OB = a down candle whose next candle closes above its open and breaks its high; bearish mirror) at the level (within 5 pt).
ZM4: EMA200 + VWAP stack + FVG + OB all at the level. Report win rate, pts/trade, trades/wk, and the random-in-regime effect per tier and per NY window; the plain line: does win rate rise with tier, and is any tier above its break-even by more than its MDE. Also the layered entry for ZM4 (thirds at zone top / mid / lower third) as one cell.

Z4. Red-flag filters (the model's "when not to trade"), applied to the top cells

Opposing OB or FVG within 10 / 20 pt beyond the entry; scheduled release within 15 min; 20 EMA curling against the trade (1-min EMA20 slope over 5 bars); range day = 5-min EMA200 crossed >= 2 / 3 times in the first 45 min (skip the day, or half size); 4-hour FVG in the opposite direction just above/below; opex afternoon off; the session before FOMC half size; the session after a 300+ pt trend day half size. Each as its own cell against the unfiltered base.

Z5. Extension targets and SMT (advanced chapter)

Standard-deviation projections from the originating swing (-1.5 / -2.25 / -4 of the swing range) as targets for the runner and as reversal-entry zones (T-ZEN section 6 reversals, with the -2.25 as the band); SMT divergence (ES makes a new low/high while NQ does not, on 1-min and 5-min, within 15 min) as a confirmation filter on ZM1-ZM3 entries and as a standalone reversal trigger at the 4H FVG.

Z6. Day typing

The model reads the day by 30-45 min after the open (trend / range by the 5-min EMA200 behaviour and wick dominance). Score the model's own day-type rule as an ex-ante label at 10:15 and report every tier by day type; and the trend-day runner management (Z2c) only on trend-labelled days.
