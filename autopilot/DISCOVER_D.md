## Multiple comparisons and caveats
346 books; the p-values above are family-wise over each family (best-of-N), not across families (a Bonferroni over the four families would put D4 at 0.07 and D3 above 0.2). The per-book Bonferroni of gate_v2 (n_tested = 346) cannot be met by any book with 60 placebo draws. The 2026 concentration of D3/D4 is the main risk: three books built independently (an opening-range breakout fade, a VWAP fade, an overnight-extreme fade) agree because they are the same regime, so they count as one observation of one regime, not three confirmations.
