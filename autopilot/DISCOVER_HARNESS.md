# DISCOVER_HARNESS - standalone-book evaluation harness for T-DISCOVER (ZL-2, 2026-11-03)
Code: l2vast/ds3/code/zones3/discover/book_harness.py (class BookHarness), stack_cache.py (one-time live-stack cache), test_book_harness.py (self-tests). Caches/results: /mnt/l2/l2vast/ds3_zones3_new/discover_cache/ (stack_q1a_ts2.npz = Q1-A ts2 stack core + 6x6x935 cube + vol tercile by day; test_book_harness.pkl). Research only; nothing acts on the live box.

## Input
A trade list DataFrame `trades`: `sec` (entry epoch second UTC), `side` (+1 / -1); optional `lots` (default 1), `stop_pts`, `tgt_pts` (NaN = no target), or `stop_atr`, `tgt_atr` (multiples of ATR(14) of the last closed 5-min bar; gap bars use their own high-low), `tstop_s` (time stop seconds), `E` (entry price override), `session` (else derived from the ET clock), `tag`. Library-test overrides: `kin_override`, `kend_override` (path indices). Build `sec` / `side` from your signal; the harness is the bracket simulator.

## Simulation (the library's market-entry bracket, ls_sim.scan_from)
Entry at the last 1-s path price at or before the entry second (no entry slippage beyond the fee, as in the library); stop and target scanned on the 1-s high / low from the signal second (target from the next second); flat at 16:55 ET of the label day (entries after 16:55 are skipped); no overlap with itself (an entry while its own position is open is skipped, as the engine does). Costs, basis LIVE (measured): stop exits 2.45 pt worse than nominal, market / flat / time exits 0.80 pt, targets at nominal, fee 0.242 pt per lot round trip ($4.84 per contract, FEES.md); $20 per point per lot. Basis 'M' (stop 1.01, time 0.995, target tc per trade) reproduces the engine library and is used only by the self-test. Label day = ET date of (t + 6 h), weekend rolled to Monday (same as the engine); sessions by the ET clock: asia 18:00-02:00, london 02:00-08:30, pre 08:30-09:30, nyam 09:30-12:00, nypm 12:00-15:00, close 15:00-16:55.

## Output (`bh.evaluate(trades, basis='LIVE', placebos=0)`)
Per window L1M, L2M, L3M, L6M, HOLD, MID, PRE, X' (weekly-cumulative of the daily $ series at the stated lots): n weeks, mean $/wk, maxDD, worst-20 (n >= 20 weeks), ulcer, CVaR5, Calmar, MDE (2.8 SE of the weekly mean), trades and hit rate; pooled and per session; the same with the carrying weeks dropped (2026 W29-W39 for the recent windows and HOLD, 2024 W34-51 for PRE, 2023 W21-41 for X') and with the book's own worst drawdown episode dropped; gate columns; marginal value to the live stack; correlations; three placebos on request.
- GATE (T-SIXTY definition, implemented literally): NOW-ONLY = L3M and L6M mean > 0; TOLERANT = NOW-ONLY and PRE and X' means >= -0.5 x their MDE and the book's PRE worst-20 not worse than the live stack's own PRE worst-20 and the L6M -> PRE drop < 100% of the L6M gain (PRE mean > 0); STRICT = L3M, L6M, HOLD, MID, PRE, X' all > 0. ADDITION = TOLERANT and the stack Calmar improves with the carrying week dropped (marginal table).
- MARGINAL VALUE: `bh.marginal(daily)` adds the book's daily $ (at its stated lots) to the Q1-A ts2 live stack (6 books, composite daily series) and reports mean, maxDD, worst-20, Calmar before / after per window, with and without the carrying weeks.
- CORRELATIONS (F2): `bh.correlations(daily)` = Pearson correlation of the book's daily $ with each live book (all sessions and each book x session) overall and by vol tercile (vol_tag_entry_day), and with the stack.
- PLACEBOS (`bh.placebos(trades, n=100, kinds=('shift5','side','days'))`, ranks = share of draws BELOW the real book on L6M / PRE / HOLD mean and Calmar): shift5 = every entry delayed by a random 5-120 min (multiples of 5) inside its session and label day, side kept (forward only: an earlier shift would use the signal's side before the signal existed); side = random side at the real times; days = each entry moved to the same clock time of a random other label day, side kept.
- WALK-FORWARD (`bh.walk_forward({name: daily series})`): for each month 2024-01..2026-10 choose the series with the best Calmar (mean > 0) of the trailing 12 months of label days minus the last 2 days; returns the assembled out-of-sample daily series and the choices.

## Self-tests (test_book_harness.py)
1. The live KB entry list (8,480 library rows) with the library's E, stop, target, flat index and basis M reproduces the engine library trade by trade (max |diff| 0.000 pt on every row) and as a daily series (max |diff| $0.00; total -$163,739 both). 2. KB entries as a fake new book at 60/60 on the LIVE basis: 8,353 trades, mean negative in L1M..HOLD, TOLERANT / STRICT / NOW-ONLY all False; placebo ranks shift5 / side / days on L6M mean 0.55 / 0.55 / 0.25. 3. A null book (5,273 random entries, random side, 60/60): all windows within noise (L6M -$1.6k/wk with MDE $4.3k; gates False), placebo ranks between 0.1 and 0.9, correlations with live books |r| < 0.12, marginal value to the stack negative (L6M mean -1.6k/wk, maxDD +26k).

## Timings (one process)
Load 2 s; simulate 8,500 trades 0.3-0.6 s; evaluate (sim + 8 windows x pooled / sessions / episode drops + marginal + correlations) 0.3 s; 100 placebo draws of one kind on a 5,000-trade book about 40 s (about 2 min for the three kinds); walk-forward over 4 candidate series 1.6 s; ATR conversion 1 s (first call builds the bars: 5 s); stack cache (one-time engine run) about 30 s once warm.

## Known limits
Entry at the signal-second price with no entry slippage beyond the fee (library convention; the live PE book uses a 45-s confirmation); one position at a time per book (no 70/30 split, no TH-L2 cut, no ER sizing: these are live-book modifiers, a new book is scored as a plain bracket book unless you add them); the stack series is the Q1-A ts2 composite at live lots, so "stated lots" for the candidate are on the same $ scale; 1-s bars only (tick-level fills need the book data); ATR bars need a print per minute (gaps are bridged); MDE is the weekly-mean MDE, not a trade-level test.

## UPDATE 2026-11-03 (CAL finding: the T-SIXTY gate passes placebo books at the same rate as real ones) - gate_v1 kept, gate_v2a / gate_v2 / ADDITION added
**Tolerant definition as implemented (gate_v1 = T-SIXTY, unchanged):** NOW-ONLY = L3M and L6M weekly mean > 0 at the stated lots; TOLERANT = NOW-ONLY and PRE and X' weekly means >= -0.5 x their MDE (2.8 SE) and the book's PRE worst-20 weeks not worse than the live stack's own PRE worst-20 and the L6M -> PRE drop less than 100% of the L6M gain (i.e. PRE mean > 0); STRICT = L3M, L6M, HOLD, MID, PRE, X' all > 0. (Source: AUTOPILOT.md T-SIXTY addendum and DIRECTIVE_RECENT_REDO: older windows >= -0.5 MDE, PRE worst-20 not worse than the stack's.)
**gate_v2a** = TOLERANT and minimum sample: trades L3M >= 10, L6M >= 20, PRE >= 60, X' >= 40 and active weeks (weeks with >= 1 trade) L3M >= 6, L6M >= 12, PRE >= 30, X' >= 20 (numbers set to reject the 4-9-trade sparse books CAL used).
**gate_v2** (needs `placebos=100`, `n_tested=N` = the number of books the caller tested in the family): gate_v2a and a Bonferroni-adjusted placebo p <= 0.10, where p_book = the weakest placebo p over the three kinds (shift5 / side / days) of {HOLD mean, stack Calmar gain in HOLD with the carrying weeks dropped}, p = (1 + #draws >= real) / (n + 1), Bonferroni p = min(1, N x p_book). Optional max-statistic p (`family_null=` array of the family's null-world best HOLD Calmar) and `BookHarness.family_adjust(evals)` (Benjamini-Hochberg q) for lists of books.
**ADDITION** = gate_v2 and stack Calmar gain > 0 in HOLD and in L6M with the carrying weeks dropped (2026 W29-W39) and an equal-risk stack mean gain > 0: scale the book by k in 0.02..5 (log grid) and take the largest k whose stack maxDD (HOLD, carrying week dropped) does not exceed the stack's own; the stack mean gain at that k must be positive (`out['equal_risk']`).
**Evidence for the change:** 150 random sparse books (150-500 random entries, random side, 60/60): gate_v1 TOLERANT passes 15, gate_v2a 13 (minimum sample alone barely helps for books of that size); the placebo-calibrated p is what discriminates. A real-looking Asia book (ZB-led, 276 trades) passes v1 and v2a and fails v2 (p_book 0.34, driven by the stack-gain placebo).
**Scale reconciliation (CAL surface):** the stack comparison series is the Q1-A ts2 composite of the six live books at live lots: $2,520,149 over 2023-04..2026-10 (cube sums to the same). CAL's $11.65M was a doubled-lots column (reconciled by CAL in DISCOVER_C_VALIDATION.md: surface rows sum to $2,526,421, within 0.25% of the stack core; the 0.25% is the row-level scaling approximation vs the engine path).

## Multi-instrument (T-INSTR, P2) and Shadow-register helpers (P1) - added 2026-11-04 (ZL-2)
Code: l2vast/ds3/code/zones3/discover/multi_harness.py (class MultiHarness extends BookHarness), shadow_register.py, test_multi.py. Same outputs, gates (gate_v1 / v2a / v2 / ADDITION), placebos and walk-forward as above; every book is valued in $ and added to the NQ Q1-A ts2 stack.
**Input.** `trades` with `instrument` (NQ ES RTY YM ZN ZB 6E 6J GC CL NKD BTC MBT; any key of INSTR), `sec` = the 1-MINUTE BAR-CLOSE boundary B at which the signal is evaluated (bar opened B-60, closed B), `side`, `stop_pts` / `tgt_pts` in points of that instrument (or `stop_ticks` / `tgt_ticks`; tgt NaN = none), size as `lots` (fractional allowed) or `risk_usd` (lots = risk_usd / (stop_pts x $/pt): the equal-risk sizing used by T-SBS/CRT), optional `tstop_s`, `session`, `tag`. A frame without `instrument` is NQ. `bh = MultiHarness(); ev = bh.evaluate(trades, label=..., placebos=100, n_tested='auto')`.
**Live fill model (user, 2026-11-04), implemented.** Signal at the bar close B; first fill at B + 1 s; a multi-lot order is sliced one lot every 2 s (average fill price over the slices that fill before the position exits); stop and target are resting orders anchored on the FIRST fill price and fill at their level (NQ: stop 2.45 pt worse than nominal, target nominal, market / flat exit 0.80 pt, fee 0.242 pt per lot; slices not yet filled when the stop or target is hit are not filled; `lots_filled` and `usd` reflect that). NQ uses the 1-s path (each slice priced at the path close of its second). `fill_model='library'` reproduces the old book_harness entry (test: 278 ZB-Asia trades, +8.9 pt library vs +9.76 pt live fill model at 2 lots, 276 trades).
**Other instruments (1-minute bars only: no sub-minute path on disk except NQ).** Adjusted continuous 1-min bars (te2_lib.load_inst, ts = bar open, 2022-06..2026-10-16); first fill = open of the bar that starts at B + the instrument's market slippage m ticks; slices: average = first fill + `slice_drift_ticks` x (n-1)/2 (default 0: unmeasurable without tick data, stated); stop tested FIRST inside a minute, a gapped stop fills at the bar open, then s ticks adverse; targets need a 1-tick trade-through and fill at their level; time / flat exits at the open of the exit bar with m ticks adverse; flat 16:55 ET; data gap > 30 min closes at the last close. Costs = te2_lib.INSTR (pv, tick, fee RT $, m, s ticks): ES 50 / .25 / 4.84 / 1 / 2, RTY 50 / .10, YM 5 / 1.0, ZN 1000 / 1/64, ZB 1000 / 1/32, 6E 125,000 / .00005 / 5.30 (FEES.md addendum), 6J, GC 100 / .1, CL 1000 / .01, NKD 5 / 5, MBT 0.1 / 5 (micro fee 1.78), BTC 5 / 5 (added, proxy fee 4.84). Fees other than ES / NQ / MES / 6E are PROXIES (FEES.md flags). Self-test: random-entry null books on ES, RTY, YM, ZN, 6E, GC, CL lose about the round-trip cost (hit 46-49%).
**Marginal value to the NQ stack.** The instrument book's weekly $ series is added to the Q1-A ts2 stack core (the same cached stack): before / after table, `bh.diversification(d)` = DIVERSIFICATION AT EQUAL MEAN (the candidate at k x its stated size, the stack scaled by 1 - share so the window mean is unchanged; share 10 / 20 / 30% of the mean from the candidate; windows HOLD / L6M / PRE with the carrying weeks dropped; valid only for k <= 10), and correlations of the book's daily $ with each live book by session and by vol tercile (vol_tag_entry_day), as for NQ books.
**Equal risk for stacking at a second account (`bh.equal_risk_second_account(d)`).** The instrument book runs on a separate account, so two definitions are reported: (a) stack_nodeepen = the largest scale k in 0.02..5 of the book such that the COMBINED NQ-stack + k x book maxDD (HOLD, carrying weeks dropped) does not exceed the NQ stack's own, with the combined mean gain at that k (the account-level question 'does it add mean without adding drawdown'); (b) standalone_match = the scale k2 at which the book's standalone maxDD over the window equals the NQ stack's own maxDD (the same risk budget on the second account) and the book's mean at k2 compared with the stack's mean. `risk_usd` sizing makes all instruments comparable at a stated $ risk per trade.
**Placebos keep the instrument's own calendar.** shift5 (delay 5-120 min inside its session and label day) and side (random side at the real times) are as before; `days` moves each entry to the same clock time of a random other label day drawn from the days on which THAT instrument has bars and with the same weekday (so crypto keeps its weekend days, holidays stay out, CME products keep Sunday evening).
**Bonferroni across the INSTR_* ledgers.** Each slice owner calls `MH.ntested_register(slice_name, owner, count, note)` (appends a row to autopilot/INSTR_NTESTED.md, append-only) whenever its ledger grows; `n_tested='auto'` in evaluate uses `ntested_total()` = the sum over slices of the LATEST row of each slice (minimum 1). gate_v2 (Bonferroni p <= 0.10 on the placebo p of the HOLD mean and of the stack Calmar gain) therefore tightens as the family grows; with 100 placebo draws the smallest p is 0.0099, so a family of more than about 10 tested books cannot pass the Bonferroni test: owners should report the max-statistic p from a shuffled-leader / random-side null world of the whole slice (`family_null=` argument) for slices with many cells.
**Shadow-register helpers for P1 (`shadow_register.py`, for CAL).** Append-only CSVs per book: `<book>_signals.csv` (sig_id, ts_signal_utc, book, session, side, signal_price, stop_pts, tgt_pts, tstop_s, lots (0.5x of research lots), definition_version, regime_flags, logged_by) written at the signal with `log_signal(dir, book, sec, side, price, stop, tgt, tstop_s, lots, session, version, flags)`, and `<book>_fills.csv` (sig_id, ts_exit_utc, exit_price, exit_type, net_pts, usd, note) written at the exit with `log_fill(...)`. `reconcile(dir, book)` replays every logged signal through the harness (LIVE basis, live fill model) and returns logged vs expected net points per signal (self-test: difference 0.000); `weekly_table(dir, book)` gives the Monday table (signals, $, hit rate, mean pts per ISO week); `regime_read(bh, {book: research trade list}, asof_label_day)` gives the monthly causal read: trailing 20 / 40 label-day P&L of each book's research trades, the NQ first-hour range share (median of the last 20 RTH days) and the 30-minute opening-range break-continuation rate (last 40 days).
**Timings.** Load instant; simulate 3,000 trades on 1-min bars 0.03-0.2 s; NQ with the live fill model 0.1-0.5 s per few hundred trades; evaluate with 20 placebo draws x 3 kinds on 5,000 entries 7 s (100 draws about 35 s).
**Cost basis switch.** `MultiHarness(cost_basis="te2"|"z1"|"z1_stress")`: te2 = INSTR ticks (default); z1 = Z1 INSTR_SCALING.md BASE (NQ 0.80 pt market and 2.45 pt stop slip scaled by the ATR ratio f_i, floored at 1 and 2 ticks); z1_stress = 2x. Fees per instrument as above.
**Known limits.** Non-NQ books are valued on 1-minute bars (intrabar stop / target order unknown: stop first, conservative); no tick-level slice drift; instrument fee proxies; the NQ stack is the only base; 1-min bar history for non-NQ starts 2022-06 and ends 2026-10-16 (YM, ZN, GC, CL, 6E; ES / RTY have extensions to 10-30 not used here); 6J has a very small tick value ($6.25) and a price near 0.0067, so use `risk_usd` sizing.
**Per-book flat time (added 2026-11-04 for O7).** A `flat_et` column ('HH:MM' ET, e.g. '16:00') in the trade list replaces the 16:55 flat for that trade (converted to a time stop from the first fill; works for NQ and for the other instruments). Sizing with unequal stop and target: `risk_usd` always means dollars to the STOP, lots = risk_usd / (stop_pts x $/pt) (NQ 40-pt stop at $1,200 = 1.5 lots).

## T-FADE tools (added 2026-11-04, ZL-2) - `fade_tools.py`, class `FadeHarness(MultiHarness)`; test `test_fade.py`
Use `fh = FadeHarness(); ev = fh.evaluate(trades, cond=flags, placebos=100, n_tested=N)`. Everything below is OFF by default, so a plain trade list behaves exactly as in MultiHarness. Live fill model (signal at the 1-min close, first fill B+1 s, 1 lot per 2 s, resting stop / target) applies to every book.
**(1) Trade shapes.** Extra trade-list columns: `stop_px`, `tgt_px`, `half_px` = ABSOLUTE price levels (stop beyond an anchor / extreme + buffer, target at VWAP / session mid / opposite IB edge, half-exit level) in the instrument's price frame (NQ: back-adjusted 1-s path frame); the harness derives `stop_pts = side*(E1 - stop_px)` etc. from the FIRST-FILL price E1 (NQ path close at B+1 s; other instruments the open of the bar starting at B), and drops rows whose level is already through. Or give the distances directly (`stop_pts`, `tgt_pts`, `half_pts`). SCALE-OUT HALF: when `half_pts` / `half_px` is set, half of the lots exits at that level (resting limit, nominal) and the other half runs to the target / stop / time exit; both legs share the stop (no break-even move after the half: not implemented, stated); the merged trade's exit is the later leg's exit (used by the no-overlap rule). TIME STOPS: `tstop_s` (seconds from the bar close) and `flat_et` ('HH:MM' ET) are honoured for NQ and other instruments (checked with BASE-4's O7 list: a 09:41 signal with tstop_s to 16:00 exits at 16:00). RE-ENTRY and ANCHOR BOOKKEEPING: give `anchor_id`; `simulate(trades, one_per_anchor=True, reentry='never'|'once')` keeps the first trade per (instrument, anchor_id, session, label day); with reentry='once' a second trade of the same key is kept only if the first exited by its stop and the second signal comes after that exit. Flat 16:55 ET and no overlap with itself stay as before. Sizing: `risk_usd` = dollars to the STOP (lots = risk_usd / (stop_pts x $/pt)), so equal-$-risk to an NQ lot is risk_usd = 1,200 (60-pt stop) or 800 (40-pt stop).
**(2) Regime-conditioned score.** `evaluate(trades, cond=flags)` where `flags` is a bool array / Series aligned to the trade rows, or a callable `cond(trades) -> flags` (CAL's `fade_cond.py`: `C = FC.Cond(); C.flags(trades)` for the causal switches R_F20 / R_F40 / R_OHS / R_HIT / R_AND / R_OR / R_F40ANY, vol tercile, B02, dow, event days). Output `ev['cond']` has, side by side: `pooled`, `ON`, `OFF`, `ON 2023-25 (same switch)` and `ON 2026`, each with n trades, the window table (L1M..X'), gate_v1 and the stack marginal at 0.5x and 1x. CAL's `FC.conditioned_report(bh, trades, name)` can still be called on the same list for its fuller report.
**(3) Stack marginal value at 0.5x and 1x.** `ev['marginal_scales']` (and `fh.marginal_scales(daily, scales=(0.5, 1.0))`): for every window L1M, L2M, L3M, L6M, HOLD, MID, PRE, X', with and without the carrying weeks dropped, the Q1-A ts2 stack's mean, maxDD, worst-20 and Calmar before and after adding the book at that multiple of its stated lots; `fh.diversification(daily)` (equal-mean diversification) and `fh.equal_risk_second_account(daily)` are unchanged.
**(4) Family max-statistic null (replaces Bonferroni at 100 placebo draws).** `R, famp, M = fh.family_maxstat({name: trades, ...}, stat='HOLD_calmar'|'L6M_mean'|'HOLD_mean'|'L6M_calmar'|'stackgain_HOLD'|callable(daily), worlds=100, kinds=('side','days'), nproc=3, seed=0)`: in each null world every book of the family gets the same kind of placebo (random side at the real times / each entry moved to a random other day of its own instrument's calendar), the statistic is recomputed for every book, and the family null is the max over books (Westfall-Young max-T). Returns a table per book (stat, raw placebo p per kind and the worse of them `p_raw`, max-T adjusted p `p_maxT`, BH q-value `q_BH` across the family), the family p of the real maximum per kind, and the matrix of null statistics. Cost: one simulation per book per world per kind (0.03-0.3 s each): a 300-book family with 100 worlds x 2 kinds is about 60,000 simulations, about an hour on 3 processes; use `worlds=30` for screens. n_tested across slices: `fade_ntested_register(slice_name, owner, count, note)` appends a row to autopilot/FADE_NTESTED.md (slice names FADE_*; the latest row per slice is summed by `fade_ntested_total()`); `FT.bh_q(p)` gives BH q-values for any p list.
**(5) Rows writer.** `FT.FADE_HEADER` and `FT.fade_row(book, session, anchor, trigger, shape, ev, label)` produce the identical ranked-table row for DS3_FADE.md: `| book | session | anchor | trigger | shape | L1M | L3M | L6M | HOLD | MID | PRE | X' | regime-conditioned | placebo | stack gain | label |` with window $/wk at the stated lots, the ON-state numbers beside the SAME-switch 2023-25 numbers and trade counts, the weakest placebo p (and max-T p if present), and the stack Calmar gain with the carrying weeks dropped at 0.5x and 1x (HOLD and L6M).
**Known limits.** No break-even move after the half exit; re-entry only after a stop and only once; anchors and triggers are the caller's (the harness is the simulator and scorer); a trade-list time stop is measured from the bar close. Self-test: random NQ NY-AM entries with stop 30 / half 20 / target 60 / T60: scale-out, anchor bookkeeping (923 first-per-day trades vs 1,430 with one re-entry vs 2,079 unrestricted), regime flags (ON / OFF / 2023-25 / 2026 split) and a 5-book family null all run (family null 6.9 s for 5 books x 10 worlds x 2 kinds).

### T-FADE tools, additions 2026-11-04 (ZL-2)
**be_half (break-even after the half exit).** Trade-list column `be_half` = 1.0 on a row with `half_pts` / `half_px`: once leg A has exited at its half target (path index kA), the stop of the remaining leg B moves to the FIRST-FILL price E1, active from the second AFTER kA; the stop exit keeps the 2.45 pt slip and the fee; target and time stop unchanged (NQ 1-s path only; other instruments keep the shared stop). Leg B is re-walked from kA+1 with E1 as its entry reference: exact for legs of at most 1 lot (total lots at most 2); for larger legs the slice-price drift of leg B is ignored. TIE-OUT vs BASE-4's FADE engine on the harness's own 1-s arrays (NY-AM VWAP +/-1.5 sigma touch-reject, stop 0.5 ATR beyond extreme, target 2R, half at VWAP, 1 lot, 600 trades in both): net per trade identical on 600/600 rows with and without BE (max abs diff 0.0); BE changes the exit type on 84 of 600 trades (268 net values change), mean net -2.882 to -2.825 pt. KNOWN DIFFERENCE found in the tie-out: at 2 or more lots the harness two-leg code fills both half legs at the first fill (no 2-s slice drift) while the engine slices the full lot count 2 s apart; on 600 trades at 2 lots this makes the harness +0.20 pt per trade more favourable (8.64 vs 8.44 scaled pts). Use 1 lot (or the single-leg form) for exact comparability; two-leg books at 2+ lots are slightly flattering in the harness.
**Shifted-line anchor placebo.** `fh.anchor_shift_placebo(build, n=100, lo=10, hi=80, seed, stats=(...))`: `build(shift_pts)` must return the whole book rebuilt on anchor lines moved by shift_pts (shift 0.0 = the real book). Shifts are uniform 10-80 pt with a random sign. Output: per statistic (L6M_mean, HOLD_mean, PRE_mean, HOLD_calmar, stackgain_HOLD) the real value, placebo mean / p95 / max, rank of the real value and p = (1+#placebo>=real)/(n+1), plus the draws. The shift is a price difference, so it is frame-independent, but a book anchored on an absolute-price level (round numbers) must build its REAL lines in the raw contract frame (multiples of 100 are not round numbers in the back-adjusted path frame on about 90% of days). `fade_row(..., line_placebo=T)` appends the shifted-line p values to the placebo cell. Cost: n full book simulations (toy 1 lot book 0.1 s per draw).
