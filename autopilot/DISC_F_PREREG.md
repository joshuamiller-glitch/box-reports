# DISC_F_PREREG - family F: portfolio discovery (ZL-2, 2026-11-03/04, written before any F statistic; ledger DISC_F)
Base: the live stack = Q1-A on run_v13t_ts2 (six live books, live lots, per-book locks), daily P&L cube book x session x 935 label days (discover_cache/stack_q1a_ts2.npz); weekly = ISO week sums; windows L1M..X' and carrying weeks as in DISCOVER_HARNESS.md (2026 W29-W39, PRE 2024 W34-51, X' 2023 W21-41). Candidate sleeves, all at 2 lots through the book harness on the LIVE cost basis (stop 2.45, market exit 0.80, fee 0.242): C1 RTH 09:30 30-min-range fade 60/60 T240 (CAL window_books.orb_book, side flipped, rebuilt); C2 NY-AM VWAP fade d10 60/60 T120 (famD_D4_trades.pkl key D4|nyam|d10|fade|T120); C3 overnight-extreme fade at the open ON_RTH x0 w60 60/60 T60 (famD_D3_trades.pkl key D3|ON_RTH|x0|w60|60|T60); C4 ZB-led Asia (ZB 5-min excess > 2.0 sigma, 60/60; labelled WEAK); C5 = the bundle C1+C2+C3 (DISCOVER_REGIME: one regime counted once). 
F1 ALLOCATION. Sleeves: book x session of the cube (KB asia/london/nyam/nypm, PE asia/london/nyam, SM asia/london, HM asia/nyam, MV london/nyam, PB asia = 14 live sleeves) and the per-BOOK aggregation (6); universes: live only, live + C1..C4 as extra sleeves. Weights for month M use only label days before the first day of M minus 2 (purge): schemes EQ (all weights 1 = the live fixed lots for the live universe), CAL3 / CAL6 / CAL12 (weight proportional to max(0, trailing Calmar over 3 / 6 / 12 months), floor 0, cap 3, normalised so the weights sum to the number of sleeves = the stack's lot scale; all non-positive -> equal), MEAN6 (proportional to max(0, trailing 6-month mean)), IDD6 (inverse trailing 6-month maxDD). OOS months 2024-04..2026-10 (so that 12-month trailing exists for every scheme). Reported per scheme: mean $/wk, maxDD, worst-20, Calmar over the OOS span and for L6M / HOLD / MID / PRE (PRE months from 2024-04) windows, with the carrying weeks dropped where applicable, vs EQ and vs the live fixed lots, at the stack's lot scale and at EQUAL RISK (the scheme's weights scaled by k in 0.3..3 so that its OOS maxDD equals the live fixed stack's OOS maxDD; the mean at that k). Null: 200 draws of random monthly Dirichlet weights (same sum, same cap) walk-forward in the same months; rank of each scheme's OOS Calmar and mean. If no scheme beats EQ with rank >= 0.95 on both Calmar and equal-risk mean, F1 is NULL and closed with one sentence.
F2 CORRELATION AND DIVERSIFICATION. (a) daily and weekly Pearson correlation matrices of the six books (sum over sessions) overall, by session (books active in Asia / London / NY-AM / NY-PM), by vol tercile (vol_tag_entry_day) and by window (early X'+PRE vs recent HOLD / L6M); (b) PCA of the 14 sleeves (correlation matrix): first-eigenvalue share overall and per window, the loading by book; (c) stacked days: per label day the maximum number of books with a same-side position open at any entry second (from engine trade records); days with >= 3 books same side ('stacked'): count, mean daily P&L, worst day, share of the stack's weekly maxDD episodes' loss that falls on stacked days; (d) diversification value of C1..C5: portfolio P(lambda) = (1 - lambda) x stack + lambda x c x candidate with c = stack mean / candidate mean in the window (equal mean; defined only where the candidate mean > 0), lambda = 0.1 / 0.2 / 0.3; report the stack's maxDD and worst-20 before / after in HOLD, L6M and PRE with the carrying weeks dropped; ranked by the maxDD drop at lambda = 0.2 in HOLD (carrying dropped) then L6M. ADDITION labels only with gate_v2 (placebos 100, Bonferroni n_tested = the number of books in the candidate's family: C1 60, C2 and C3 346, C4 1,036) and the equal-risk gain > 0; diversifier without gate_v2 is labelled 'diversifier (not ADDITION)'. Multiplicity: F1 has 6 schemes x 2 granularities x 2 universes = 24 comparisons; F2 has 5 candidates. Post-hoc, Shadow only, nothing is a PASS.
