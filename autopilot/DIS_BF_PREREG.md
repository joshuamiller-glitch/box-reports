# DIS-BF prereg (S4b), 2026-11-04, written before any DIS-BF statistic

Question: where do stop and TARGET fills of the live signal streams deviate from the engine's assumptions (stop at level + 2.45 pt, target at level, market exits 0.80), by time of day, volatility, session class and book? Measurement, Shadow only, no PASS claims, no purchases.
**Cites and extends** Z3's autopilot/DS3_TAIL_STRESS.md section 4 and code l2vast/ds3/code/tail_stress/slip_tape.py (12,955 live-signal stop exits 2023-26: slip_next mean 0.15 pt, median -0.25, p95 5.0, p99 16.5, max 102.3; 515 tail-day stop exits mean 1.05, p95 10.2, p99 48.2). I do NOT redo the pooled figures; I reuse its exact object (the six books' live signal streams from dis_a/dis_lib sources(), V2-style 60/60 bracket, lots 1, BookHarness.simulate on the 1-s path, stop exits etype 0, E = path close at the signal second, slip_next = stop level minus the path close one second after the touch (adverse positive), slip_within = stop level minus the extreme of the touch second) and cut it new ways. My first step is a reproduction check: the pooled stop-exit count and mean must match Z3's (12,955; 0.15 pt +-0.01) or I stop and report.

Cuts (all on label days 2023-04-03..2026-10-30):
- time of day of the exit second (ET), 8 buckets: 18:00-02:00, 02:00-08:30, 08:30-09:30, 09:30-10:30, 10:30-12:00, 12:00-14:00, 14:00-16:00, 16:00-17:00;
- volatility: terciles of the day's 1-s realised range (day high-low from the minute table, label-day terciles over the sample) and of the ATR14 5-min at the signal;
- session class: asia, london, pre, nyam, nypm, close (session_of at the exit second), plus the irregular classes of DIS-AU (EARLY, DAY_AFTER, SUNDAY) as extra rows;
- book: KB, PE, SM, HM, MV, PB.
TARGET fills (new, Z3 did not measure): for the target exits (etype of the target in simulate) measure (i) margin = how far the touch second's extreme went through the target level (long: path high of the touch second minus the level; short mirrored), in ticks (0.25 pt); (ii) touch-only share = target exits whose margin is <= 0 (traded exactly at the level, no print through) and the share with margin < 1 tick; (iii) reversal share = touched-only exits where the path close one second later is back on the entry side of the level by >= 1 tick; the engine fills these at the level, a resting limit may not fill (queue); (iv) for these 'touch-only' targets the P&L if they were NOT filled but the trade ran to its next exit (re-simulate those trades with the target removed: stop 60 / time exit), reported as a $ bound per book and for the stack at live lots (ER Q1-A, session lots) alongside the engine total.
Stat: group means with 95% day-block bootstrap CIs (1000 draws); omnibus test of equal group means per factor by day-block permutation (2000); Spearman of slip with the realised-range tercile; MDE = 2.8 SE; BH over the 24 registered tests: stop slip_next and slip_within, target margin and touch-only share, each by 4 factors (time of day, vol tercile, session class, book) = 16 omnibus + 8 tail-share (share > 2.45 pt for stops, share of touch-only for targets by vol tercile and by time-of-day bucket) = 24. n_tested 24. Labels: groups with < 30 exits are 'thin, not tested'. 'Tail' means > 2.45 pt adverse (the engine's stop cost).
Outputs: ledger DIS_BF (one row per factor level and order type), rows in FADE_ROWS_DISBF.md, n_tested line DISBF_NTESTED.md, report section in DS3_DISCOVER2.md (append only), code l2vast/ds3/code/disc/bf_*.py, data /mnt/l2/l2vast/ds3_dr/bf_*.pkl. If the engine rows do not expose target exits through simulate (etype map unknown), I state it and measure targets from the first-touch of the level on the path instead; the choice is logged before any statistic.
