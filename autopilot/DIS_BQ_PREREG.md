## Protocol (DIS-W)
1. 2. Calibration BEFORE the OOS: for each cell the 1-min pipeline on events 2023-04-03..2026-10 (ext feed) vs the FadeHarness 1-s simulation of the research trades in the same period: weekly $ correlation and mean pt per trade. Rule: weekly correlation < 0.90 (or < 20 trades in either) = 'approximation too coarse': NO VERDICT for that cell.
3. OOS 2013-01..2023-04-02: n, mean pt per lot-trade (stop-first / target-first), week-cluster t, years positive among years with >= 15 trades, weekly $ at the cell's lots, maxDD.
4. Four causal placebos, 100 worlds each: P1 random side; P2 entry delayed 5-120 min inside the session (04:00-09:25 for pre-market entries, 09:30-12:00 for NY-AM); P3 random day of the same weekday; P4 shift5 = trade moved by +-1..5 label days at the same ET time. p = share of worlds with a mean at least the real one; family max-T over the 157 cells per placebo kind; BH q over the cells on the largest of the four raw p.
## Verdicts (fixed now)
- SURVIVES (replicates pre-2023): calibration corr >= 0.90 AND OOS mean pt per trade > 0 AND q_BH <= 0.10.
- WEAK: OOS mean > 0 and raw p <= 0.10 on all four placebos but q_BH > 0.10.
- NEGATIVE / NULL: OOS mean <= 0, or mean > 0 with a placebo p > 0.10.
- NO VERDICT: calibration corr < 0.90 or too few trades.
