# DIS_C prereg (S4b, 2026-11-04 ~19:25Z): weekend / Sunday-open books. Written BEFORE any outcome statistic.
**Weekend table (one row per weekend 2022-01..2026-10):** NQ P0 = raw close of the last 1-min bar before Friday 17:00 ET; NQ P1 = open of the first 1-min bar at/after Sunday 18:00 ET (must exist within 5 min); gap = P1 - P0 (points, bps of P0). MBT and BTC (CME crypto futures, closed Fri 17:00 - Sun 18:00 ET, so the weekend move is the Friday-close to Sunday-open JUMP, visible at the same 18:00 open as NQ): C0 = raw close of the last bar before Friday 17:00, C1 = open of the first bar at/after Sunday 18:00, rb = C1 / C0 - 1; z = rb / SD of the previous 26 weekends' rb (causal). Weekend event flag: a CAL calendar event (DISCOVER_CALENDAR.csv, event types NFP CPI PPI PCE GDP RETAIL_SALES FOMC_DECISION ISM_MFG ISM_SVC) on the Monday label date or the preceding Friday after 16:00 (information known before the open).
**(a) Predictor tests (n_tested 21):** 16 rank-correlation tests: instrument {MBT, BTC} x predictor {rb, |rb|} x target {rb: NQ gap bps, r30, r60, r120 ; |rb|: |gap|, |r30|, |r60|, |r120|} where r_k = NQ close of the bar ending 18:00+k min / P1 - 1 (k = 30, 60, 120); 4 gap-fill / gap-and-go comparisons: among weekends with |NQ gap| >= 5 bps, the share whose price trades back to P0 within 120 min (FILL) and the share whose close at 20:00 is beyond P1 in the gap direction (GO), split by whether sign(rb) agrees with sign(NQ gap) (Fisher exact), per instrument (2 x 2 = 4); 1 event-flag comparison (FILL rate with vs without a weekend event). MDE reported (2.8 / sqrt(n) for correlations, two-proportion MDE for rates). Two samples: all weekends and 2023-04 onward (the harness era).
**(b) Sunday 18:00-20:00 ET session family on NQ (n_tested 180):** one trade per weekend per cell, ZL-2 FadeHarness (NQ 1-s path, live fill model, stop slip 2.45, exit 0.80, fee 0.242 pt, resting stop / target, lots = min(2,400 / (stop x 20), 6)). Entry at the close of the 1-min bar ending t_e in {18:05, 18:15, 18:30}; direction WITH or AGAINST the move from P1 to that close (zero-move weekends skipped); stop 0.5 or 1.0 x Wilder ATR14(5-min) beyond the entry (WITH) or beyond the Sunday extreme so far + that buffer (AGAINST); target: WITH {1R, 2R}, AGAINST {1R, 2R, P0 = gap fill if P0 is on the profit side and >= 0.5 R away}; time stop {60, 120} min (never later than 20:00); flat 16:55. 60 cells; plus 120 'state-gated' versions of the same cells (trade only if sign(rb_MBT) agrees (G+) or disagrees (G-) with the trade direction): 180 cells. Standard: gate_v3 windows L1M..X' (existing HOLD, HOLD_dropH reported separately), placebos x100 (random side / random time within the Sunday window / shuffled weekends = each entry moved to another weekend), family max-stat >= 100 worlds on HOLD mean and L6M Calmar, CAL FC.Cond regime flags, stack gain at 0.5x and 1x with the corrected carrying weeks. With about 190 weekends the weekly-mean MDE is large: results are labelled by n and MDE and no cell is called a lead without the family null.
**(c) Stack Asia cell, Monday / Tuesday label days (n_tested 28):** the live stack's Asia-session daily P&L (Q1-A ts2 cube, sessions asia) on Monday and Tuesday label days, total and per book (7 series), against rb_MBT and |rb_MBT| of the PRECEDING weekend (known at 18:00 Sunday): 7 x 2 predictors x 2 days = 28 Spearman tests plus conditional means by rb tercile; CAL's Mon-Tue 19:00 continuation (FRAGILE) is re-read through the same conditioner only descriptively.
