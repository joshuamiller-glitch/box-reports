## Books (signals at 1-min bar closes; label day from 18:00 ET; off = minutes since 18:00)
- A_GAPGO / A_GAPFILL: gap = Globex 18:00 open minus the previous label day's last close (after the 17:00-18:00 break), weekday opens only; signal at the 18:00 bar close (t01) or the 18:04 close (t05); gap >= g ATR, g in {0.25, 0.5, 1.0}; GO = with the gap (trend), FILL = against it with the prior close as the vwap-type target (fade).
- A_OR: Asia opening range 18:00-18:30; entries 18:30-20:00: first close beyond the range (+x ATR, x in {0, 0.25}, trend) or failed break (wick >= 0.1 ATR then close back within 10 bars, fade).
- L_OR: London opening range 02:00-02:30; entries 02:30-04:00: break (x in {0, 0.25}, trend) / failed break (fade, W 10).
- L_AR: Asia range (18:00-02:00) broken during 02:00-04:00: break (x in {0, 0.25}, trend) / failed break (W in {10, 20}, fade).
- L_STR k: a 02:00-03:00 close >= k ATR (k in {1.0, 1.5}) from the Asia VWAP: fade toward it.
- L_FRA: 03:00 ET Frankfurt-open 5-minute move (close of the 03:04 bar minus close of the 02:59 bar) >= m ATR (m in {0.5, 1.0}): continuation (trend) or reversal (fade), signal at the 03:05 close.
