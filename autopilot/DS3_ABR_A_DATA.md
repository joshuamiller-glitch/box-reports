## READY and load recipe
Root: l2vast/ds3/out/abr/data -> /mnt/l2/l2vast/ds3_abr/data
* `entry/book=<B>/year=<Y>/m=<YYYYMM>.parquet`: one row per entry, 155 columns, float32/int, zstd. `pyarrow.dataset.dataset(root+'/entry', format='parquet', partitioning='hive').to_table(columns=[...])`; hive adds `book` and `year` (entry-month year; the data column for label-day year is `trade_year`). Key `tid` joins `l2vast/ds3/out/thp/trade_index.parquet` (47,131 entries, same ids as THP).
* `outcome_min/book=<B>/year=<Y>/m=<YYYYMM>.parquet`: tid, m (minute 1..M after the entry second, M up to 1375, to 16:55 ET of the label day), cmfe, cmae (running favourable / adverse excursion in pts at the END of minute m, from 1-s highs/lows, entry second included), up (open P&L at the minute end). 30.4 M rows approx.
* `outcome_index/m=<YYYYMM>.parquet`: tid, k_in, k_end (row range of the global 1-s arrays /mnt/l2/l2vast/ds3_g2 SS,SH,SL,SC covering entry..16:55), s_in, s_end, sess_end_ts (end of entry session), M, fin_up/fin_cmfe/fin_cmae. Exact first passage: scan SH/SL rows [k_in,k_end) (stop before target within a row, library rule).
* `score/`: G.npy, M10.npy, MRP.npy (N x 990 float32 pts per lot: gross at touch; measured, 10-lot slip; measured, slip at risk-parity lots), TYP.npy (0 stop 1 target 2 time), TEX.npy (exit minute), score_keys.parquet (rows = tid order; book, window, session, year, week_key, LIVE = stored base-bracket outcome `live_pts`), brackets.parquet (bracket id = (stop*11+target)*5+cap, names), geometry.npz (S, T, slip10, slip_rp, lots_rp, fallback flags). Section B documentation: DS3_ABR_B_ORACLE.md.
* Walk-forward: a bracket's outcome is known at its own exit time (TEX); the base trade is closed at `closed_ts`. Columns `lab_*`, outcome_*, score/* use the FUTURE: never features.
* Dictionary: l2vast/ds3/out/abr/feature_dictionary.csv (161 rows: name, family, tag NT8 / XINST (ES, RTY 1-min bars) / BOOK (needs mbp-10), definition, known_at). 105 NT8, 7 XINST, 19 BOOK feature columns (the 7 intens/flow ones tagged NT8 are computable from tick data with bid/ask classification).

## Content (section A of the directive)
1-min ATR14 and ratio to the 20-day median (atr14, atr_ratio), ATR(5m); 5/30-min realised range (exact from 1-s: range_5m, range_30m; bar based rng5/rng30); overnight range vs median (on_ratio: today from 09:30 ET else previous day; on_ratio_sofar intraday); first-hour range (fh_range, NaN before 10:30); time of day / session / minutes since open and since session start; VIX tercile (expanding, prior days only); ES-NQ 20-min correlation (esnq_corr20m) plus prior-day corr; own-signal strength PROXIES computed from bars (1-min and 3-min Keltner distance, Supertrend 1/5/10 direction, age and distance, HMA9-HMA21 spread, 30-bar pullback depth) - the engines' own internals are not on disk, so these are generic and identical for all books; distance to structure (sd_* = side*(level-E): session H/L, RTH H/L, OR edges, 5-min swing, prior-day ETH and RTH H/L, VWAP +-1/2 sd bands ETH and RTH, nearest ahead/behind); order book at entry (spread, imbalance 1/5/10, best sizes, 4/8-tick liquidity, 10-level depth, 1/10/30-lot sweep walk, aggressor delta 10/60 s, intensity vs same-ET-hour 20-day median, big prints, OFI, microprice); stack (other books open same/opp, unrealised pts, books' day P&L and counts, own-book day P&L); ex-ante day type (prev_daytype, vol_tag, calendar flags wd/monthend/opex/fomc/nfp/cpi/event).
Size at the stop/target price: not available. Stops are >= 20 pts away and mbp-10 shows about 2.5 pts, so the field would be NaN for all entries; the depth/sweep-walk columns are the stand-ins.

## Leakage audit (truncation / garbage-future), run 2026-10-29
Method as THP: every feature recomputed with everything at/after the decision instant (entry second s0) replaced by garbage: 1-s rows >= s0, L2 bins after s0, 1-min bars after the last closed bar, ES/RTY and the new aux arrays, other books' later exits; all non-label columns compared (rtol 1e-5).
* Entry-row test: 132 entries (12 per book, random), 19,272 feature comparisons (1,056 NaN-vs-NaN): **0 mismatches**.
* Aux-series test (new for ABR; THP's audit only garbled at index level): the whole bar-aligned chain (ATR, EMA, Supertrend, session stats, OR, swings, ATR5, Keltner3, ranges, ES corr, ST age, HMA, prior-day levels, overnight range; 38 series) recomputed with bars after a random cut replaced by garbage, 4 cuts (bars 250753, 384589, 463604, 988481): **0 mismatches** at bars <= cut.
* The first audit launch (40 per book) stalled with empty log after 50 min and was terminated by me (DECISIONS.md line); the rerun above is the audit of record.
Soft spots: day-level keys (prior-day ranges, VIX terciles, 20-day medians) use prior days only by construction; ES/RTY are additive back-adjusted; library prices back-adjusted (distances exact, absolute levels offset by a per-contract constant); stack uses library rows of all 11 books (not the lock engine). THP's stk_real_day_pts counted exits one second early (s_out <= ts); here s_out < s0.
