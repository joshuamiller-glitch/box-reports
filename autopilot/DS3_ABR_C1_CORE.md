Long windows (6m, 12m, YTD) are mildly better than 1w/2w/ew5, as in THPC3; recency adds noise. RP-basis means are negative for 1w, 2w, ew5 in all four families.
* Step: weekly > monthly for kNN/tables, monthly > weekly for GBM; INTRADAY (09:30, 12:00) re-fits are no better than daily (GBM 0.01 vs -0.22; kNN 0.09 vs 0.05; tables -0.15 vs -0.12; fixed -0.38 vs -0.40 CL, all inside noise).
* Constant-lots vs constant-$-risk: the $-risk policies pick tight stops at larger lots when the model likes them; mean RP cell delta is negative in every family (GBM -$53, kNN -$57, tables -$5, fixed -$20 per trade) and only 26/68, 31/160, 74/160, 44/102 cells are positive.
* Risk-adjusted GBM (lambda 0.5/1.0): RA05 12m monthly CL +0.84 (PRE +1.40, HOLD +0.11); no better than EV.
* Placebos: PL1 (shuffled features) real-vs-placebo means: GBM CL real +0.44 vs PL1 +0.60 (18 matched cells), kNN +0.08 vs +0.23, tables +0.13 vs +0.19; PL2 (random assignment of the same bracket multiset) 95th percentiles +1.4..1.7 pts for the table/kNN/fixed headline cells and $111 for the candidate.

## Deviations from the prereg (disclosed)
* Regime tables use 4 session groups (asia+london, pre, nyam, nypm+close), not 5-6; table cells shrink to the session mean then the global window mean (m=40).
* Fixed re-selection over all 990 (a990) was run for windows x steps {1w,1m} and for 3m/12m daily only (not intraday) for compute; the 40-bracket and per-book variants cover every window x step.
* kNN session mismatch penalty (4.0) and book mismatch (18.0 squared distance) are as coded; per-book kNN restricts neighbours to the book.
* The distilled-table teacher set was extended (EV teachers) after the first teacher gave a degenerate table; all variants reported. The file choice was made on PRE chain numbers only.
* Chain statistics are shown for all spans after the logged looks; headline cells were frozen in headline.json before. Looks taken: 4 (C1G, C1K, C1T, C1B), logged at one timestamp because the chain was computed in one pass; no cell was added or changed after the PREHOLD line (RA/PL1/loss cells were all in the prereg).
* CR is included in pts statistics and excluded from $/wk at live lots.
* M basis charges the year target correction at every target distance (ABRA caveat); G-basis check (G7) is positive for the candidate.
* Multiplicity: 980 real cells (490 per basis), 676 placebo cells, 16 GBM-loss and 9+ PL1 GBM fits; RC covers all real cells. Machine load was 40-57 on 32 cores throughout; no process killed; <= 12 workers.
