## 7. Reading notes, limits, and the three leads worth a look

1. **The composite's L6M window is one event.** Its L6M maxDD (22.9k) is the week of 2026-01-19; a rule "cuts" it only by hitting or missing that week. That is why no composite-frame rule can be RISK-KEEP in the full sense and why section 3 lists the rules that cut the PRE (241k) and X' (120k) maxDD by >= 20% each. The as-is frame (L6M maxDD 88k, also one week) behaves the same way: 155 zone rules and 402 FZ rules 'cut' it, almost all with PRE/X' deeper.
2. **Volume is the whole effect of the FZ-1 take-only rules.** They keep 7-9% of the trades; their maxDD cut (77-96%) beats the same-volume placebo in PRE and X' but they keep about 0% of the L6M mean (-21k of 21.7k): 11 as-is rows are RISK-KEEP by the letter and DEGENERATE in substance (section 1b). Equal-risk mean for the whole FZ family is negative; the FZ-1 verdict (location carries no information beyond the dip distance) stands.
3. **Zones.** The live 8-line d=20 skip is in the composite already; the 720 variants (other lines, D, fresh/touched/any, halve instead of skip) add nothing the placebo cannot reproduce: one PRE+X' gate-pass in 720 (chance 6), 16 L6M cuts of >= 20% (chance 14), 0 with PRE/X' agreeing. The 4h-bar-mid (CRT M 4h) skip is the strongest L6M-only row (L6M -46%, placebo rank 0.03) and the worst in PRE (deepens 12-20%) and X'.
4. **Meta-labels (FS1/FS2).** 0 of 2,280 stack-changing cfgs cut the PRE and X' maxDD by 20%; the walk-forward models have no output in X' (warm-up), so the X' column is structurally blank; 16 cfgs cut the L6M maxDD >= 20% (all PRE-deeper, 'beats' only the L6M placebo).
5. **CF-COMBO.** 357 live-book cells re-run; 1 PRE+X' gate-pass (PE FVG 2m AND vah, c2_mid F0.7; L6M 16% deeper), 7 L6M-only cuts. The F0.0 cells that cut L6M skip 38-73% of the book's signals; the PRE/X' placebo ranks are 0.8-0.9.
6. **Structure sleeves are the one family with PRE and X' agreement.** NQ IBS (internal bar strength < 0.2, hold one day) at 0.5 x the composite's weekly SD (about 4.2 NQ contracts) cuts the composite's maxDD 36% in PRE and 35% in X' with L6M mean +6.2k/wk (MDE 9.8k) and the sleeve placebo ranks 0.03 (PRE) and 0.07 (X'); COMB_c3_R (the C3 combination) is the same picture. It is a mean-adding diversifier (equal-risk mean +4.1k/wk L6M, +2.6k ALL), the L6M window is unchanged (the sleeve neither helps nor hurts the 2026-01-19 week), it has no D6/D7 slot (overnight NQ hold) and it is a Shadow/paper lead only, not a RISK-KEEP: the placebo bar (0.05) is missed in X' and L6M, and the STRUCT48 PASS tiers it came from were removed by the window cap.
7. **TREND-GATE rows are stored values** (older f73 own frame), 48 rows, no re-run and no placebo; 19 cut the L6M maxDD >= 20%, none agrees in PRE and X'.
8. Not scored (coverage gap, section 0): CF-ENG / CF-A / CF-B / CF-SUBMIN / CONFIRM-BARS / confirm2 / BARS-ENG / bars3 / bars_cand(2) (no stored stream, each has its own entry engine; about 7,000 recent-edge ledger rows); STRUCT48 NM-B (PB variants, A1/A3); ENSM; zone exit actions (rt/wt/b73/fix: A1); RL-1.
9. z50 on one-lot books is a no-op (integer half-up, minimum 1 lot); A6 (PB) rows have no profile-line features (PRF lines never fire there).
