## What was built
* sd_state.py: per-book signal-engine state series rebuilt from the engines' OWN recursions (ST: 1-min ST 120/2.0 + EMA200; HM: HMA20/55 of close - day VWAP; KB: 3-min Keltner n14 m1.0; SM: 10-min ST n7 m3.0; PE: 5-min ST regime + EMA20 arm), all at 1-min bar close, higher timeframes only after the bar closes. Validation: it reproduces the entry signals of the library trade list for ST 100%, HM 99.9%, KB 100%, SM 100%, PE 100%.
* Books covered: ST HM KB SM PE (5,390 / 4,156 / 5,500 / 1,708 / 5,361 trades). NOT covered (state series not built in this pass): WT, MV, DN, CR, PB7/A6, MA, iFVG, O6. Section A is therefore incomplete for those.
* 71 rules x {market exit, half at market, stop tightened to the trigger bar's extreme} x min age {1,2,5} min x {any, under water, in profit} = 1,917 single cells; generic rules for every book (VWAP cross against, ST-1m flip against, close beyond the signal bar's extreme on 1/3/5 m, BOS/CHoCH against on 1/5/15 m, opposite signal of any other live book) plus the book-specific rules of the directive. Combinations: AND of pairs and triples and k-of-n (k=2..4) over each book's top-10 rules, 168 flags x 27 = 4,536 cells/book, run twice (literal member list and members with fire >= 5%) = 45,360 cells.
* Fills: trigger known at the bar close; exit at the close of the first path row after the close second, adverse 0.995 pt (measured market cost). Bases: M, N (no target correction), LIVE-CAL (stop 2.45, market M, target 0), LIVE-P9; the hold arm is charged the same basis. Fees identical on both arms (not netted). 0-minute age == 1-minute for bar-close rules. "Exit at the bar close" == "at once" for the same reason. Per contract = mean over ALL of the book's trades in the window (untouched trades count 0), day x entry-session clusters, MDE = 2.8 SE.

Plain sentences. When KB's 3m close comes back inside the channel, exiting at market costs 3.5 pts per contract over the last 6 months on 84% of trades (MDE 4.2); the trades it exits would have reached the target 45% of the time vs 50% for all KB trades. Exiting on an ST flip costs 3.6 pts on 79% of ST trades (MDE 3.9); touched trades reach the target 43% vs 52%. Same sign in every one of X', PRE, HOLD, L6M for KB, ST, HM, PE; SM is ~0 (touches only 19% of trades). The premise carries little information: P(target | invalidated but held) is only 3-14 pp below the unconditional rate, so the exit sells the 35-47% that would have won to save the losers, and the 1-pt market cost is paid on 65-85% of trades.
