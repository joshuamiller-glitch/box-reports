## NEEDS 1 - NYSE TICK ($TICK), ADD ($ADD), VOLD ($VOLD)
- Source: record forward from the NT8 data feed that is already running (the live NT8 instance on the user's feed) by adding the internals symbols as data series and an indicator/AddOn that writes 1-min (better: 1-s) OHLC of `$TICK`, `$ADD`, `$VOLD` (names depend on the feed provider; for Kinetick/CQG/Rithmic-style feeds `^TICK`, `^ADD`, `^VOLD`; confirm by Instrument Manager lookup) to CSV. Requires the provider's NYSE internals entitlement (often a small monthly exchange-data add-on or included). Cost/effort: effort about half a day (symbols + writer script + a nightly rsync to the box); money: $0 to roughly $10-25/month depending on feed and entitlement (not quoted; user must check). Backfill: most retail vendors offer only 1-2 years of 1-min internals history (TradeStation / Sierra Chart / CQG deeper; the cost unknown), so a usable sample means recording forward for 12+ months or buying a history file; do not assume either without the user's OK.
- First study it enables: TICK extremes (|TICK| > +/-1000 at 1-min and the 5-min smoothed TICK) as an NQ reversal/continuation signal, scored first as a gate on the stack's loss hours and range-day weeks (audit md), then as a stand-alone fade/continue book flat by the close. MDE note: needs >= 2-3 years for a few-hundred-event sample; a 12-month forward record gives roughly 30-60 extreme-TICK days, descriptive only.

## NEEDS 2 - intraday VIX (1-min)
- Source: CBOE publishes the VIX index every 15 s during the cash and (since 2020s) the extended session; 1-min history is sold by CBOE DataShop (End-of-Day / Intraday index data; price quoted on request, order of hundreds to low thousands of USD for multi-year VIX 1-min) and by commercial vendors (Polygon, Refinitiv, FirstRate and similar). Recording forward alternatives: NT8 `^VIX` from the same feed (free in most feeds, delayed on some), or `VX` futures from Databento's `XCBF.PITCH`/CFE dataset (check availability and quote with `metadata.get_cost`, the only call permitted without OK).
- Cost/effort: forward recording as for NEEDS 1 (same writer, half a day, near zero money); history purchase = user decision, nothing bought here.
- First study it enables: VIX intraday spike (e.g. +1 point / +5% in 15-30 min, or intraday VIX above its prior-day high) as an ON/OFF or size gate for the stack's entries, scored on the audit's loss hours / range days and against DS3_DIV_VOLGATE results (D3DVG used the daily VIX terciles only); and VIX-term-structure inversion (VIX9D > VIX) intraday as a regime flag. The daily-VIX version of the gate is already on disk and is owned by D3DVG; intraday adds timing only.

The stack's losses concentrate in range / narrow-first-hour days (about -$4.2M over 440 range days, 61 of the 100 days in the 20 worst weeks). TICK (mean-reverting internals in chop) is the more plausible discriminator of range days than intraday VIX, so NEEDS 1 is the higher-value ask; NEEDS 2 mainly tests timing gains over the daily VIX that D3DVG already scores.
