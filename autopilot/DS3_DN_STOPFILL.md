## 1. Data, coverage (what is real L2 and what is mapped)
- Raw NQ mbp-10 on disk: monthly files 2023-02 .. 2026-10-11 plus daily files 2026-10-12 .. 2026-10-23 (ext). The directive's "ends 2026-10-09" is stale: all 4,797 triggered orders (to 2026-10-23) have raw L2. **Excluded: 9 orders that never trigger on the tape model (kept at library points in every re-score); 100 orders flagged (58 with no trigger record at the instant, 42 roll-week orders with an ambiguous trigger record) are in the re-score with the best available level and in a sensitivity row of the tables (mean 1.44, unchanged) but outside the primary tables (n = 4,706).** Series end 2026-10-16 (windows as the d4a study).
- Method: for every trigger instant, a windowed extraction (30 s before, 2 s after) of the front contract (NQZ6 id used after the instr_map end 2026-10-12); the stop-market arrives at trigger + 5 ms (primary), walks the displayed 10 levels of the state after the last record <= arrival (exec_engine_l2.walk_snapshots; identical to market_sweep, checked on 120 orders, max difference 0.0). Level in raw space = library stop price minus the contract back-adjust offset, resolved per order from the trigger trade record (16 contract offsets; roll-week exceptions flagged). Beyond-depth quantity: 0% at 10 lots, 0.7% at 14.
- Not a full run_program replay: entries are priced on the book; the 60/60 bracket outcomes of the MB model come from the D3LS tape model with the measured slip applied (target limits exact, stop exits slip like the entry). Own-order impact not modelled.

## 5. DN order churn (note only)
The library places a buy stop and a sell stop at every completed 15-min bar (162,621 candidate orders in 3.7 years = 173/day, one cancel/replace per order, at most 2 x 96 per day, nothing between bar boundaries): bursts of about 4 messages at each :00/:15/:30/:45, about 350 order messages per day. That is far below any exchange messaging-efficiency or per-second throttle I know of, but I did not read the CME or broker (NT/Rithmic) limits in this task, so the statement is unverified: confirm that Rithmic does not charge routing on cancelled orders ($0.25/side in FEES.md is applied to filled contracts only) and that no per-day order-count cap applies. Real implications are execution-side: a stop working across a bar boundary is cancelled and re-placed, so a fill that happens in the cancel window is a race the library does not model.

## 6. Deviations and disclosures
1. Raw L2 coverage is to 2026-10-23 (not 10-09); nothing is mapped-only for coverage reasons.
2. Entry fills come from windowed book snapshots + the engine's walk (not a full exec_engine_l2.run_program replay); MB exits are tape-model with the measured slip (the exit book walk at 10 lots is not replayed). The earlier D3LS stop model uses the tape; the real-book 1-lot walk differs from it by +0.8 pt.
3. MB (spec-faithful) turned out harsher than MA (the prereg called MA the conservative gate); both reported, same label. Disclosed in DECISIONS.md.
4. BUG found after the first G1/G2 looks and fixed: 42 roll-week orders (offset ambiguous) were assigned a neighbour-contract offset, giving +-200 to 294 pt slippage values that entered the re-score; corrected (median candidate offset), all stages re-run at the same prereg. Holdout looks were therefore logged twice per group (4 entries in addbook_looks.jsonl, tracks D3DN_STOPFILL_G1 / _G2); v1 outputs kept as *.v1_unresolved_offset_bug. Effect: MA 10 lots WF 13,582 -> 13,748; no verdict changed.
5. Mapped P9 correction (+0.5 + year x exit type) already contains market-class execution cost; adding the walk slip on top is a mild double count in the conservative direction (not removable here).
6. The slippage tables pool all triggered orders including the HOLD window (no cell or parameter selected on them); the re-score cells carry the HOLD columns only after the logged looks.
7. Workers: dn_snap.py (named so it is outside the *_direct.py governor/memguard patterns; <= 8 workers, <= 2.6 GB each, one corepool slot each, memory gate 32 GB checked before each launch; MemAvailable >= 64 GB at every launch; no kills, no retries; all 56 files completed). They are not counted in the governor's 12-worker replay cap.
8. The forward-register rule in DS3_SPEC_TR3_DON15_S ("average fill slippage < +1.0 pt/trade over 4 weeks") is already exceeded by the measured +1.4 (all) / +1.7 (2025-26): the threshold needs recalibrating if DN goes forward (a structural question for MORNING.md, not applied here).
