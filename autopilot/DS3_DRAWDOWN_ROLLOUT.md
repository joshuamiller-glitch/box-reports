## Section D - method notes, deviations and caveats

- **Frames and ties.** Composite = `comp_kw()` of DD-1 (six pieces, parity stop); as-is = `ASK` (market entries, join 2.45, per-book locks at planned-lot R). A skip row is OR-ed into the zone skip (composite) / the zone override (as-is); a size row multiplies the ER multiplier (composite: rounded half-up, enters the running max) or the lots (as-is, no rounding). INST-B / C / F composite+rule daily series equal the programs' stored `stageB.pkl` / `C_stack.pkl` / `F_stack.pkl` (max abs diff 0.0); INST-D equals `res_grid.npz` to float32; INST-A/A2 masks are rebuilt from `a_states` / `b_look1_masks` and reproduce the stored stack lines (A2 K6 NY-PM/close skip L6M +1.22k, p_single K6 NY +1.41k, xm.led_YM_60 +7.27k, ROLLPRE +1.57k, INST-B +1.35k).
- **RE-6 rows.** The programs' bank6 series were produced on the earlier stop-1.28 composite (bridge row 1 of DS3_ONE_CONVENTION). They are re-simulated here on the final parity convention with the NTE-2 masks (R1 = D|691, R2 = D|714); the L6M mean deltas (+2.9k, +2.1k composite) differ from the bank rows (L6M +3.6k / +2.6k, L3M +5.1k / +5.0k) accordingly. Neither is a RISK-KEEP: size-ups raise risk (composite L6M maxDD -0.2% / -16%, as-is -9% / -11%).
- **NTE-2 AND2.** The moved-stop rule is applied to the engine rows as `ro_stack.stack` does (trigger second + destination level from `ro_engine`), with the one-convention parity stop cost per row. The oneconv `d` (61,401 rows) is a superset of the risk-off path-cache universe (44,385 cached trades; the 17,031 later A6=PB7 rows have no 10-s path cache), so (a) the rule never fires on PB7 rows (A6 unaffected) and (b) the cached library PB (now book `PBL`) is the old A6 for the trigger statistics only. With this mapping the program's per-contract gains re-tie exactly for all six cells (PRE / X' / L6M / HOLD: AND2 ALL +0.124 / +0.150 / +0.078 / +0.049; touched 1,025 / 408 / 355 / 428). Placebo = RO-2 P1 random-tighten (same count per book x session, taus drawn from the rule's own, same destination), 200 draws, both frames.
- **Stored-series rows.** MN-1 (nine carried cells) and the INST-A stand-alone A2 are ADDITIVE: the programs' own 5-contract daily $ (3-s decision delay, N basis, `look_trades.pkl` / `sa_all.pkl`) added to each frame's daily series; no lock acts on them (as the programs did). RE-5 EQR 207 / 714 and META cfg373 are scored in their own frames (Table 3). RE-5 EQR rows are equal-risk scalers in the programs' own frames: their c* (1.016, 1.203) is the programs' own; the equal-risk line here uses the ALL-window worst-20.
- **Equal-risk precision.** c is found by secant on the ALL worst-20 with integer lot rounding, so achieved matching is within about 2% (3% for one or two rows); the mean change is read at the achieved run (columns `er_gap` in `results.csv` for the engine rows).
- **L6M reading.** The composite's L6M maxDD is 22.9k and is one week (2025-10-20; the L3M one, 21.4k, is the week of 2026-01-19); a 20% cut there is 4.6k, and for the as-is stack the L6M and L3M maxDD (88k) is one week (2026-01-19). The L-window labels are therefore fragile by construction; PRE (75 wk) and X' (44 wk) carry the evidence, which is why 'PRE and X' agree' is a separate column. No lead agrees in both.
- **Worst-20 and the L windows.** L3M / L6M / HOLD have < 20 losing weeks for the stacks, so worst-20 there is the sum of all losing weeks plus the smallest wins; it is not shown in the tables.
- **Placebo scope.** Skip placebos draw from the live rows of the rule's own books and sessions (stricter than DD-1's all-row draw because the rule only ever acts there); the share q is calibrated on the realised number of taken trades removed (3 seeds, <= 3% error). Size placebos permute the actual multipliers, so a size-up rule's placebo has the same volume added at random times. Rules that remove no volume (adds, stored deltas) have placebo n/a and are not volume-penalised.
- **Workers.** Up to 3 engine processes, OMP/MKL/OPENBLAS/NUMBA = 1, peak RSS about 7-10 GB each (< 30 GB total); short extra processes (mask extraction, tie tests, the AND2 universe check) overlapped the workers briefly.
- **What this does not do.** No combined week lock, no shrink (these are carried-row prices at the programs' own size, not shrunk sizes), no new selection: every row was fixed by the source programs' tiers before any risk number was printed. Not scored: the 10 INST-A2 part (a) target-chooser cells and the 2 INST-B F3a cells (exit/own-engine overrides) and the 10 rows listed under 'no stack unit'.
