## 6. Method and caveats

* Streams: stream_W / 2.35 = live-lot weekly $ delta on the as-is stack (SIXTY-1's convention); stack-level programs (EINT1, ZONES-2, ADDUW, META, UF-ENTRY, SPLIT, L2SE) are lock-engine numbers, the trade-sum programs are not. Weekly buckets: a week is in a window if any day is (L3M = last 13 weeks), so window numbers differ from the day-exact ledger.
* Size 0 (family shrinkage <= 0): the rule has no position at shrunk size, so the Calmar equals the stack's; they are counted, not ranked.
* Calmar = weekly mean / maxDD in the same window, in the stack's own units (0.041 for the stack means 4.1% of the maximum drawdown is earned per week). mean/abs(worst-20) is shown only where the worst-20 is negative.
* Equal-risk mean change is shown where both worst-20 are negative (ALL, PRE, X'); otherwise blank.
* The portfolio is the additive sum of individually computed deltas (no joint re-run); correlated rules overlap (rho shown), the one-per-cluster variant takes the best-Calmar rule of each SIXTY-1 weekly-rho cluster.
* In-sample: the lists are selected on persistence (L3M/L6M positive), so ALL contains the selection window; PRE and X' are the confirmation read for direction only. DD-1 owns the other items of DS3_DRAWDOWN.md and SX-1 the p70 policy and bins: not duplicated.
