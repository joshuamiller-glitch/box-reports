# DS3_ERA_2023: what changed in 2023? Era comparison of the six books' raw-entry edge vs observable market-state variables (DIS-Z)

**Caution carried from DS3_STACK_2013 / DS3_SIGNALS_2013: the 1-minute approximation of the live stack failed validation (weekly correlation 0.66-0.69 vs the 0.80 bar). Everything here is about the books' RAW ENTRIES (V2 percent bracket, 1-lot, 1-min fills, no Trade-Health/locks/zones), NOT the live stack. Descriptive; no causal claim; no PASS; nothing live.**

## Plain sentence
None of the 14 market-state variables I could build from the 1-minute series (realized vol, vol of vol, ATR in points and percent, volume, return autocorrelation at 1/5/15 minutes, 5-minute efficiency ratio, opening-range break rate, first-hour range share, Asia variance share, RTH volume share) explains the raw-entry edge across years or quarters (year-level |rank correlation| at most 0.38, quarter-level at most 0.17, all bootstrap CIs include zero, family-wise p 0.68 or higher; the pre-registered ER5 + AC5 model has R2 0.04 and predicts the 2023-26 edge no better than the 2013-22 average), the two proposed variables (ER of 5-minute closes and 5-minute autocorrelation) barely move across any year, and the edge itself is not a clean "2023 switch" (2023 -0.4, 2024 +1.9, 2025 +1.4, 2026 -2.1 points per trade), so there is no observable variable to track for a regime switch on the live stack from this evidence; n is small (14 years, 55 quarters, 43 post-2023 months) and nothing here shows the absence of a cause, only that these 14 variables do not carry it.

## 1. The edge by year is not a 2023 step
Stack edge = mean net points per trade at 1 lot, all trades pooled, V2 percent bracket. By quarter, the 2024Q2..2025Q4 stretch is positive (2024Q2 +2.2, Q3 +2.5, Q4 +3.3, 2025Q1 +0.5, Q2 +1.4, Q3 +1.8, Q4 +1.7); 2023 quarters are -2.5, -0.1, +0.9; 2026 quarters are -3.6, -0.6, -2.2. Pre-2023 years with a positive pooled edge: 2016 (+0.36), 2017 (+1.15), 2018 (+0.02). So "2023-26 positive" is mostly 2024-2025 (eight quarters); 2026 is back to a 2013-22-like negative level while the live stack made most of its money in 2026 (it is not carried by these entries).

## 4. Pre-registered model: edge ~ ER5 + AC5 (quarter level, standardized)
* All 55 quarters: intercept -0.71; ER5 coefficient -0.23 (95% CI -0.75, +0.29); AC5 -0.34 (-1.01, +0.33); R2 0.041; leave-one-year-out R2 -0.113 (negative: worse than the mean).
* Fit on 2013-2022 only (40 quarters), predicted onto 2023Q1..2026Q3 (15 quarters): actual post mean +0.34, model-predicted -1.28, pre-era-mean forecast -1.10; RMSE 2.47 (model) vs 2.39 (pre-mean); correlation of prediction with actual 0.22. The model does not explain the shift. (2023Q1 is in the post group of this model, so "post" here is 2023Q1-2026Q3, 3 months earlier than the 2023-04 cut elsewhere.)
Reading: if the signals were pure continuation entries, higher ER5 or positive autocorrelation should raise the edge; the data show coefficients of the opposite sign with intervals spanning zero, and both variables are nearly constant across all years (their quarter-to-quarter variation is noise-like: AR1 0.08 and -0.09).

## 6. Post-2023 split by state (months; 43 post-2023 months, 123 pre months)
Variable pre-registered: the largest year-level |rho| = vol of vol (+), direction taken from the 2013-22 quarters (+1); "like 2013-22" = month on the side of the 2013-22 monthly median where the 2013-22 edge was lower. Edge = trades-weighted mean points per trade, 95% block-bootstrap (mean block 3 months) CI:
* vol of vol: like 2013-22 (24 months) -0.05 (-1.35, +1.36); not like (19 months) +0.78 (-0.81, +2.40). In the 2013-22 months the same split gives -1.68 (low vol-of-vol side, 61 months) vs -0.84 (high side, 62 months): both negative.
* ER5 (sign -1): like 2013-22 (23 months) +0.16 (-0.63, +0.94); not like (20) +0.65 (-2.22, +3.21).
* AC5 (sign -1): like (23) -0.27 (-1.51, +1.04); not like (20) +1.17 (-0.37, +2.59).
The point estimates are lower in the months that look like 2013-22 (-0.27 to +0.16 vs +0.65 to +1.17), which is the direction a state story predicts, but the intervals overlap almost completely (19-24 months each) and the same variable does not produce a positive edge on its favourable side in the pre-era (-0.84). It is not evidence of a state dependence.

0DTE share of option volume rises from 0.44 to 0.75 and option volume from 35k to 111k contracts/day; rank correlation with the stack edge: 0DTE share -0.07, option volume -0.04, NDXP share +0.04. The trend in 0DTE share spans the positive (2024-25) and negative (2023, 2026) edge quarters alike, so within 2023-26 it does not track the edge; no pre-2023 comparison exists.

## What this does and does not say
* Does: 14 observable 1-minute state variables, including both the ones proposed, do not account for why the raw-entry edge was negative in 2013-22 and mostly positive in 2024-25; the edge is not stable even inside 2023-26 (2026 is negative); ER5 and AC5 are nearly constant over 14 years, so they cannot be switch variables.
* Does not: rule out causes that are not in these variables (execution/spread and tick-size economics, which this fill model holds constant; order-book or participant structure; the specific 2024-25 trend months; the interaction of the six books' entries with exits that this object omits); say anything about the live stack, whose 2026 result is not reproduced by these entries.
* Limits: 14 years, 55 quarters, 43 months, dependent neighbours; the V2 bracket scales stops with price, which changes the meaning of "points" across years; additive back-adjusted prices; correlations of 14 variables ordered into 6 independent groups; the model and split were pre-registered but the year table was inspected before the model results were read.
