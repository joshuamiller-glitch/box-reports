## Plain sentences
1. The stack is rarely stacked: |net lots| after an entry reached 10 / 14 / 18 on 9.2% / 1.5% / 0.09% of the 21,397 entries (20 entries at 18, the engine maximum), in-market time at >= 10 / 14 / 18 lots is 13.9% / 2.3% / 0.19% (88,635 seconds = 24.6 hours in 3.5 years at 18), and a day reaches a peak of >= 10 / 14 / 18 lots on 655 / 195 / 19 of 927 days; it is an Asia / London phenomenon (entries >= 10 lots: Asia 15.4%, London 11.8%, NY-AM 1.2%, NY-PM 0.4%) built by the same five books (HM + KB + PB + PE + SM at 19 of the 20 entries at 18 lots; with MV at the 20th).
2. A net-lot cap buys a smaller worst-case exposure and drawdown at a price paid every week: cap 8 costs 21.6% of total P&L (mean -$2.9k/wk) for peak net 18 -> 11 lots, L6M maxDD $104.8k -> $74.3k, HOLD $122.0k -> $101.9k, PRE $88.2k -> $76.1k and the worst day only -$38.1k -> -$34.6k (the worst week -$62.6k -> -$56.1k); cap 10 costs 13.4%, cap 12 6.7%, cap 14 2.9% (cap 14 changes the worst day and week by nothing).
3. The SYNTHETIC tail loss falls in proportion to the peak net lots (30-minute worst move, 1,449 pts at 25,000: $522k at 18 lots -> $319k / $377k / $435k / $464k at cap 8 / 10 / 12 / 14) but remains 5x to 8x the observed worst week; each $1k of synthetic tail loss avoided costs about $14 (cap 8), $12 (cap 10), $10 (cap 12) and $7 (cap 14) of weekly mean P&L.
4. A day-loss stop (halt new entries after -$X realised intraday) shortens the worst DAY ($38.1k -> $27.8k at X = 15k, $28.4k at 20k, $34.5k at 30k) but not the worst WEEK ($62.6k -> $54.1k / $69.1k / $62.6k), and it can make the maxDD worse (HOLD $122.0k -> $142.0k at 15k, $128.8k at 20k): removing the entries that follow a bad stretch also removes the recoveries; the effect on total P&L is path-noise (-9.0% at 15k, +2.3% at 20k, -0.6% at 30k, not monotone).
5. Rolling-origin check: the net-lot caps are the only rules whose out-of-sample effect has a constant sign (a smaller maxDD in 2024 and 2026 for caps 8 / 10 / 12, mixed in 2025, always at -3% to -26% of the mean); the day-loss stops chosen from prior years were wrong out of sample (stop15k chosen in 2024: next-year mean -9.9% and maxDD +$12.6k; stop20k chosen in 2026: maxDD +$6.7k and worst week worse by $6.5k).

## 5. MISSING HOOKS (listed as ordered)
(m1) a native stack-level intraday loss stop in the engine (halt of new entries after -$X realised or marked, with lock interaction): the wrapper above skips entries after the first exit second at which the previous pass's label-day realised P&L reached -X, which is path-dependent and noisy (non-monotone total P&L in X); (m2) the cap hook trims but does not halve (the half variant is the scale cell); (m3) the cap counts the 70% leg until the add fills, so the hard cap overshoots on a full-size measure (cap 8 -> peak 11, cap 10 -> 13, cap 12 -> 15, cap 14 -> 16): a cap on the FULL 70+30 size, or on the lots plus the resting add, would give the stated peak; (m4) a marked-to-market (open-loss) day stop.

## 6. Reading for the user (risk appetite, not a recommendation)
The table prices three different things: a net-lot cap lowers peak exposure and the synthetic tail loss (cap 8: $319k vs $522k) at 3% to 22% of the mean and by itself barely changes the observed worst day or week (the worst day and week are not set by the largest net: only cap 8 changes the worst week materially, by $6.5k (cap 10 $0.5k, cap 12 $1.2k)); a day-loss stop lowers the observed worst day by about $10k at 15-20k but leaves the worst week and the synthetic tail where they are and can deepen the HOLD drawdown; and neither bounds a gap through the stops, which is what the SYNTHETIC rows (5x to 15x the HOLD episode) are about. If the concern is the gap scenario, only the lot size at the stressed moment matters (loss scales with peak net lots), so a cap of 10-12 net lots (counting full size) gives a 17-28% smaller synthetic loss for 7-13% of the mean; if the concern is the observed bad day, the observed worst days are not stacked-exposure days.
Caveats: single-path engine runs (no phase averaging; Q1-A has no lock-phase dependence except via multipliers > 1, so phase noise is small for the cap cells and the stop cells are path-chaotic); the net measure counts full lots from the entry; the carrying weeks are corrected but cell numbers are in-sample; the cap and stop cells are not tested against placebos (not a hypothesis test).
