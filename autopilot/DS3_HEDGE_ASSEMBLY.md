### AUDIT VERDICT: the 7.3 is real arithmetic and not evidence. Reject it as a "portfolio lead"; it is a selection ceiling.

**Reproduced.** S8 RTY x3 + B1 BTC_1 x6 + Q1CCO NQ_6 x3 + RU11 GC x4 (stored series / 8 x contracts): clean 2023-08-01..2025-07-31 weekly Sharpe 7.26 linear, 7.31 with the daemon's day-level account approximation (105 weeks, annualised x sqrt(52)); both tie to the REPORT_ASSEMBLY row (7.26 / 7.31). No arithmetic, annualisation or look-ahead error. The problem is what the number is.

1. **It is the best of ~9 million searched subsets on the window it is scored on, and it is below what the search produces by chance.** The daemon's own timing-destroyed null (N1) gives a best-of-search clean Sharpe of mean 7.92 (sd 0.66, p95 9.07); the real 7.26 is rank 0.20 (z = -1.0). The edge-destroyed null (N2: random sign flips, no edge at all) gives mean 6.76, p95 7.25. So a book library with zero edge produces a "lead" of 6.8 and the real one is at that null's 95th percentile and under the timing null's median. Four sleeves with pairwise correlation -0.05..-0.24 diversify mechanically: 4 books at Sharpe 2.1-4.4 combine to 7.3 with no further information.
2. **Outside the selection window it is not 7.** Same series, weekly Sharpe by window (block-bootstrap 95% CI, mean block 4 wk): selection window 7.31 [5.83, 9.25] (n=105); post-selection 2025-08-01..cap 2.86 [0.92, 4.88] (n=28); every other week of the library, 2013-01..2023-07 and post-selection, **0.89 [0.34, 1.42] (n=581)**. Three of the four sleeves (S8 RTY, B1 BTC, Q1CCO NQ) are ~0 outside the window (0.10 / 0.02 / -0.08 over 535 pre-2023 weeks); the three-sleeve portfolio without GC is 5.12 clean, 1.05 post [-1.57, 3.24], 0.20 [-0.37, 0.74] non-clean. **Restated honestly: about 0.9 weekly-Sharpe-annualised on out-of-selection history (CI 0.3-1.4) and 2.9 on the 28 post-selection weeks (CI 0.9-4.9), i.e. 12-40% of the headline.** Persistence ratio post/clean: portfolio 0.39, ex-GC 0.20.
3. **All the out-of-selection support is one sleeve, RU11 GC** (clean 4.36, post 3.97, pre-2023 1.00, non-clean 1.17 [0.62, 1.72]). It carries 44% of the clean mean and removing it takes the post-selection Sharpe from 2.86 to 1.05. RU11 GC is itself `FOUND-PENDING-REPLICATION` with a limit-at-50%-retrace entry replayed on 1-minute bars (fill-bound risk, the related FVG-retrace GC lead was downgraded in RV1: STRICT clean bound 1.20), 2013-22 gross 1.75 / fixed-cost 0.93, ~6,358 trades in the pair window, and no GC row in FEES.md.
4. **The PRE and X' windows are not independent of the selection.** The clean window covers 100% of PRE and 61% of X' days (70% of the clean window is PRE). Any "PRE and X' agree" test on these sleeves reads the selection window twice; only L3M/L6M/HOLD (post 2025-08) are outside it, and that is 28 weeks.
5. **Sizing is calibrated on the same window.** S8 and Q1 weekly SD are both exactly 15,990 and GC 21,320 on the clean span: `volmatch` series (SD set equal to a live 8-lot book on the clean window), B1 `R`-basis. Dollars are therefore NQ-equivalent risk units, not native contracts; the lead's own mean is +24.5k/wk, as large as the whole composite's 22.3k/wk, which no 16-contract-equivalent sleeve set should do.
6. **Trade counts.** S8 RTY fires 23 times in the 105 clean weeks (82.6% winners, top 3 trades = 36% of net): its 2.47 Sharpe is 23 observations. B1 BTC 389 active days, Q1CCO 107 active days (69 of 105 weeks), GC 517 active days (~3,500 trades). Trade-level counts are not stored in the library (daily P&L only).
7. **Alignment.** Sunday-labelled P&L is 18% of the clean portfolio P&L; moving Sat/Sun label days to the next ISO week (the stack's Monday-label convention) takes the clean Sharpe 7.31 -> 6.69. BTC weekend share is 0. Costs: the stored model is 1 tick/side + commission (the RU11 report's own 2x-slippage run: standalone 4.52 -> 3.96); an extra $10-30 per active day per contract-equivalent moves the clean/post Sharpe 7.31/2.86 -> 7.24/2.82, so costs are not the problem; fill realism (GC/S8 limit and open-price entries) is, and it is untested here. Roll handling and continuous-contract construction are not recorded in the library index (no field), so roll is UNVERIFIED for all four.
8. **Not independent portfolios.** The top-10 rows share the same four sleeves with different contract counts (plateau checks vary the count, not the idea); 'PORTFOLIO-LEAD' appears on 8 of 10 rows but it is one lead.

**Verdict.** The ASSEMBLY lead FAILS the validity gates as a portfolio (search-adjusted: below its own null; persistence 0.39; pre-selection history 0.77 [0.15, 1.36] for the four-sleeve mix; 23-trade sleeve; selection window overlaps PRE/X'). * **Dollar-risk conversion (stated).** Library convention: one stored unit /8 = one NQ-contract-equivalent at a 60-pt bracket = $1,200 stop risk (R = $9,600 per 8; `R`-basis for B1, weekly-SD `volmatch` for S8, Q1 and GC). Native tick values: NQ $20/pt (tick 0.25 = $5); RTY $50/pt (tick 0.10 = $5; M2K $5/pt); CME BTC (5 BTC) $5 per $1 of price (tick $5 = $25; MBT 0.1 BTC = $0.50 per $1); GC $100/pt (tick 0.10 = $10). A $1,200 stop equals 24.0 RTY pts, 240 BTC price-dollars, 12.0 GC pts per native contract. The native contract count needs each sleeve's actual stop distance, which the library does not store for volmatch sleeves, so the table prices contract-equivalents in NQ risk dollars (the 0.25 / 0.5 / 1.0 multiples), and a native contract count cannot be stated without the user's stop-distance choice.
* Selection: the sleeves were picked on 2023-08..2025-07 by clean Sharpe from a 245-book pool (top-150 + leads), then 3.0M subsets; survivorship among sleeves is therefore total (what is left is the winners). No PASS claims. Appended to DEAD_CELLS as PRICED, program HEDGE-ASM.
