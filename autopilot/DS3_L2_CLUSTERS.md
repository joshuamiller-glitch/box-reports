## 6. Deviations, caveats, files
* **Deviations/pins (all labelled):** (i) Key 2 of the ranking is computed over ALL possible +-1-step neighbours, so a neighbour that was not engine-run counts as not confirmed (the prereg text said 'neighbours must be engine-run'; with the L2SP sample at 2,691 of 23,772 cells this makes L2SP robustness mostly 0-33%; the hybrid column uses the first-order screen where the engine did not run). (ii) Power horizons extended to 416 weeks after the first run (ADDENDUM 1).
