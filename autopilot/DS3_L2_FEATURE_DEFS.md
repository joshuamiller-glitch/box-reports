# DS3 L2 feature definitions (L2P, 2026-10-29) - what D6 must compute to match the fitted features
Source: NQ mbp-10 book, 1-s tables (tables/NQ/1s). Decision time T = END of second s. Book state = last snapshot with ts <= T (ob_live = age <= 3600 s). Sizes are contracts summed per price level (NOT order counts). "side" = +1 long / -1 short. "sided" = multiplied by side (+ = supports the trade).
Own side ("behind", toward the stop) = bids for a long, asks for a short. "ahead" (toward the target) = asks for a long, bids for a short.

## Core five (the THP_POLICY features)
* imb5   = side * (B5 - A5) / (B5 + A5), B5/A5 = summed resting size on bid/ask levels 1..5 (level 1 = best). Long: + = bid-heavy. Identical to table column imb5 x side (verified max abs diff 0.0 over 9,806 rows).
* imb10  = same over levels 1..10.
* wall_tgt  = resting contracts ON THE AHEAD side at the trade's target price (long: ask size at the target price; short: bid size at the target price).
* wall_stop = resting contracts ON THE BEHIND side at the trade's stop price (long: bid size at the stop price; short: ask size at the stop price).
  Price -> level: tick offset k = round((target - best_ask)/0.25) for a long target (k = round((best_bid - target)/0.25) for a short target); stop: k = round((best_bid - stop)/0.25) long, round((stop - best_ask)/0.25) short. Level index = k+1 (k=0 is the best price). The 10 visible levels are contiguous ticks 99% of the time (span = 9 ticks).
  TIE RULES: (a) 0 <= k <= 9 and the ladder on that side is contiguous (best-to-level-10 span == 9 ticks): wall = size of level k+1 exactly. (b) k > 9 or k < 0 (price not within the 10 visible levels, or already at/through the touch): wall = 0 ("not resting within the 10 levels"), and wall_*_vis = 0. (c) 0 <= k <= 9 but the ladder has a gap (span > 9): price->level is ambiguous, wall = NaN (D6 with the live book can resolve it; research treats NaN as missing, ~1% of seconds). Stop/target = the BASE bracket levels (CR dynamic-exit trades: NaN). A tightened stop would use the tightened level in D6.
  CONSEQUENCE (important): brackets are 33-60 pts (130-240 ticks) away while 10 levels cover ~2.25-3 pts. wall_tgt/wall_stop are visible (wall_*_vis = 1) on ~0.1% of in-profit seconds and 0% (stop) of loss-side seconds early in the trade; in practice they are 0 almost always and only carry information in the last ~2.5 pts before the target/stop. They CANNOT be the main carriers of a policy; use ahead_L1..L10 / behind_L1..L10 (the wall profile at the touch) and cum_ahead/behind5/10, maxlv_* instead. This is a data-horizon limit of mbp-10, not a build issue.
* delta60 = side * (aggressor buy contracts - aggressor sell contracts) over (T-60 s, T]. NT8: classify each print by last >= ask buy / <= bid sell. Identical to table delta60_s.

## Wall profile (every tick between price and the book edge)
ahead_L1..L10 = size at level k (k-1 ticks from the touch) on the ahead side; behind_L1..L10 same on the behind side. contig_ahead/behind = 1 if the ladder is contiguous (level k then = touch +/- (k-1) ticks). cum_ahead5/10, cum_behind5/10 = summed sizes. cum_to_tgt / cum_to_stop = sum of levels from touch up to the target / stop level, NaN unless the level is in the window. tgt_ticks, stop_ticks = ticks from the touch to target/stop. maxlv_ahead/behind = largest single level, maxlv_*_k its tick distance from the touch, maxlv_*_rel = divided by the median level size of the 20 levels. rnd50/100_ahead/behind = pts from raw mid to the next multiple of 50/100 ahead/behind (round-number levels).
Imbalance family: imb1/2/3/5/10 (levels 2,3 = same formula), d_imb5_w and d_imb10_w = imb(T) - imb(T-w) for w=1,5,10,30,60 (sided), imb5_at_ext = imb5 (sided) at the time of the last new MFE (profit) / MAE (loss). depth10 = total size both sides levels 1-10; depth_rel = depth10 / median over the prior 20 label days of the mean depth10 in the same UTC hour (min 3 days). cum_*10_rel = side cum size x2 / same base. spread_t (ticks), micro_off_t = side*(microprice - mid)/0.25, bslope_s/aslope_s = OLS slope of cumulative size vs ticks (behind/ahead), gap_ahead/behind = span-9 ticks of missing levels.
## Flow family (all sided: + = with the trade)
delta5/10/30/60/120; delta_acc = delta10/10 - (delta60-delta10)/50 (contracts/s, recent pace minus earlier pace); ofi1_w, ofi5_w (w=10,30,60) = Cont-Kukanov-Stoikov order-flow imbalance at level 1 / over 5 levels summed over 1-s bins x side; vol10, vol60, intens10 (prints/s); big10/25/50_net30 and _tot30 = prints >= 10/25/50 contracts, net aggressor volume x side and total over 30 s (print COUNTS are not in the 1-s table; volume used); t_since_big_against/with = seconds since the last >=25-contract print against/with the trade (cap 7200); sweep_with/against10,30 = trade-driven touch clears consuming >= 2 levels...(table swp_n_*: sweeps by buy/sell aggressors; with = aggressor in my direction) and sweepvol_*30.
## Queue / refresh (10-s windows)
cons_ahead10 / cons_behind10 = contracts traded AT the best on the ahead / behind side per second (depl_*_trd); refill_ratio_* = size added at the best / (consumed + 1); can_* = size cancelled at the best; iceberg_*10 = consumed / displayed best size (>=10 flagged iceberg_flag_*; the 2x rule of the directive triggers on ~63% of seconds on 1-s aggregation, so 10x is the flag, numeric ratio kept); nclr_ahead/behind10 = trade-driven touch clears; refill_n10, refillfail10, refill_t10_ms from the sweep-refill events (not side specific). pulled_sup/opp{5,10,30} and added_sup/opp{5,10,30}: cancelled/added size over levels 1-10 on the supporting (long: bid) / opposing side. NOTE: iceberg/refresh AT THE TARGET or STOP price is not observable in the 1-s table (target outside the 10 levels); only at the best.
## Other
es_imb5/10, es_ofi5_30, es_delta30 (ES book, sided with the NQ trade; ES 1-s tables start 2023-08, NaN 2023-04..07). vpin50/20 (mean |buy-sell|/250 over the last 50/20 NQ volume buckets) and svpin50/20 (signed x side) in the vpin_ side files. ob_live, ob_age_s.
## Files
/mnt/l2/l2vast/ds3_l2feat/{dp1m,dp10s,ldp1m,ldp10s}/book=B/m=YYYYMM.parquet (symlink out/l2feat), keyed (tid, ts), join to the THP data on (tid, ts). Per-second (dp1s) NOT materialised (400 M rows); the builder l2vast/ds3/code/l2feat/l2f_build.py regenerates any grid.

## Tranches 2-4 and entry-side (added)
t2_ (dp1m): book_entropy, size_hhi, convex_ahead/behind (levels 1-3 vs 8-10 size), gapk10/gapk25_* (first level index >= 10/25 contracts, 11 = none), depth_press5/10 = side*log((B+1)/(A+1)), exit_slip10/30 (average slippage in ticks to fill 10/30 contracts walking the BEHIND side), spread_max10, eobi10/30/60 = side*(net bid event adds - net ask)/(abs sum) with net = adds - cancels (event counts), cancel_sup/opp{10,30,60} (per second), otr60 (book events per trade, 60 s), int_ratio_trd/book (5-s rate / 60-s rate), vwap5/60_mid (ticks, sided), imb5_minus_ema10, concord = sign(imb5)+sign(delta30)+sign(ofi5_30) (sided), cvd_entry/cvd_sess (+ _norm, cvd_div_entry), kyle60 (mid change ticks per 100 contracts net 60-s flow, NaN if |net| < 20), nq_mid_chg10_bps, es_/rty_ micro_off, mid_chg10_bps, lead10 (that market's sided 10-s bps move minus NQ's), rty_imb5/10/ofi5_30/delta30, esnq_imb5_div, esnq_delta_agree, esnq_cvd_agree30.
t3_ (dp1m, tape): last5_sign/sizeW, big_max60(+_side), cnt/net 25/50/100 over 30 s, eff_spread20 (2*|px-pre-mid| ticks), real_spread20 (5-print horizon), footprint of the 1-min bar ending at T: fp_delta/_norm/_range/_delta_rng/_div/_poc_dist/_stack_with|against(_dist) [diagonal 3x imbalance, 3 consecutive levels, min 4 contracts], fp_unfin_ahead|behind (both aggressor sides traded at the bar extreme), fp_prev_*, fp5_* (trailing 5 bars), session profile since 18:00 ET: sess_poc_dist, sess_poc_mig30, sess_vah/val_dist (VA = symmetric 70% interval around the POC), sess_inside_va, vap_entry_since (volume at the entry price since entry), vp_max_ahead40/behind40 (+_dist in pts).
t4_ : z_<f> = (f - median)/MAD of the same 30-min ET bucket from PRIOR label months only; d60_<f> = f - f(previous 1-min row of the trade).
gates/ and features_at(): see l2f_gates.py / l2f_at.py docstrings.
