## 6. LOSS SIDE EXHAUSTED ON mbp-10 + 1-s AGGREGATES; remaining untested structure needs raw MBO
Six cycles (rare-state book triggers, tape / footprint recovery, scale-out, causal-regime walk-forward, time-in-trade stall exits, learned state-trajectory model) on the 10-s mbp-10 book with 1-s aggregates, all scored per contract on N, with PRE+X' selection, one recent look each and placebos: no selective rule is >= 0 per contract on N in L6M and HOLD with its placebos beaten. The best seen-data structure (causal high-vol path trigger on WT) was not reproduced out of sample; the learned model has in-sample structure in PRE that disappears in the last 12 months (worse than shuffled inputs). Remaining untested structure needs raw MBO (order-level) data that mbp-10 aggregates cannot reconstruct:
* **Order lifetime at best** (time from add to cancel / fill for orders resting at the best bid / ask; fleeting share <= 100 ms, <= 1 s): mbp-10 shows only size changes, not whether the same order persists.
* **Cancel bursts** (count and size of cancels in 100-ms / 1-s windows at levels 1-3 per side, sequence of cancel then trade): the 1-s aggregates smear bursts and cannot separate cancel from trade-driven size drops.
* **Queue position at the stop and at the target** (size resting AHEAD of the stop / target price level and its time series, i.e. the full depth beyond 10 levels around a 33-60-pt stop; needs the whole book rebuilt from MBO because mbp-10 sees only ~2.5 pts): the question 'does the level that will be hit still have support' is unobservable today (wall_stop / wall_tgt are finite in 0.05% of seconds).
* **Level age / order age** (time since a price level last refilled; age of the orders making up the best level) and **hidden-size reloads** (iceberg detection from order-ID level execute-and-reappear patterns rather than size ratio).
Dates a pull would need (NQ, GLBX.MDP3, schema mbo, all 24 h because trades span sessions): L6M 2025-08-01..2026-02-06 (recent evaluation), PRE 2024-02-01..2025-07-31 (selection) and X' 2023-04-01..2024-01-31 (second selection window).
