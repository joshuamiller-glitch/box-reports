# DS3_LP_AGAINST_CLEANROOM_REPRO - clean-room reimplementation of ZL-2's LP_AGAINST_25_4h_D40 (ZL-1, V13Y lock None, 2026-11-02)
Rule (from MORNING_FLAGS.md flag #2 and DS3_ZONES_3_L2_TAPE_LP_AGAINST.md only; ZL-2's code was not opened): skip when a single print >= 25 contracts, aggressor OPPOSITE to the trade, printed in the last 4 h, price between entry and target within min(40, td), and no trade at the print's price since. Flagged rows are over the 88,490 engine rows in the d_rows.npz order; saved dollars = minus the V13Y lock-None net of the TAKEN flagged rows ($k). ZL-2 sent its mask path and choices (no code) after my implementation was written. Code: l2vast/ds3/code/zones3/zl1/zlp_clean.py (+ _clean2/_clean3 variants), masks out/zl_new/lp_clean_masks*.npz, ZL-2 mask /mnt/l2/l2vast/ds3_zl/features_tape/masks_lp_var.npz key T25|4h|against|D40.

## Result
My primary reading flags 1,937 rows (464 taken); ZL-2's mask has 2,614 (677 taken); Jaccard 0.69, 74 rows only mine, 751 only ZL-2. The sign agrees in L6M, HOLD and PRE (saved $k mine 44 / 53 / 56 vs ZL-2 77 / 91 / 73; ALL 83 vs 138) and X' is -11 vs -4. One ambiguity explains almost the whole difference (below); with it set the way ZL-2 set it the clean-room mask reproduces ZL-2's at Jaccard 0.93 (2,448 rows; 9 only mine, 175 only ZL-2) and saved dollars 128k vs 138k.

## Ambiguities and what each one changes
1. **Aggressor-side convention at the tape level (changes the SIGN of the result).** The tape side flag is 65 'A' / 66 'B' / 78 'N'. Checked empirically on a month of prints: every 'A' print occurs at a price at or below the pre-trade best bid and every 'B' print at or above the pre-trade best ask (pre_bb_t / pre_ba_t), i.e. 'A' = SELL aggressor, 'B' = BUY aggressor (Databento 'side' names the resting side, not the aggressor). With the characters swapped the same rule flags 2,185 rows and LOSES: -62k ALL (-14k L6M, -14k HOLD, -57k PRE): the effect is specific to the opposite aggressor, and the convention must be stated in the live definition. ZL-2 and I agree (B = buy).
2. **'No trade at p since the print': does the FIRST path second after the print count? (changes 30% of the rows and $45k).** Natural reading (every second after the print's own second counts): 1,937 rows, saved 83k. ZL-2's implementation starts the touch scan one path index after the print becomes usable (print second s, usable at s+1, touch scan from ek+1), so trades in the second right after the print are ignored: 2,448-2,514 rows, saved 116-128k, Jaccard 0.91-0.93 with ZL-2. In the sample of rows only ZL-2 flags, the candidate prints were re-touched at p in the very next second (contains@1): i.e. ZL-2's rule keeps prints whose price is revisited at once. Decide the live meaning explicitly. D7 live would naturally apply 'no trade at p since' to every trade after the print (natural reading, the smaller effect).
3. **Touch test: price range contains p vs price reached/through p.** At 1-s resolution 'range contains p' (SL <= p <= SH) lets a gap through p without a trade at p stay fresh; 'reached or through' kills it: 2,008 vs 1,937 rows (+71 rows, saved 116k vs 83k). With the first-second convention (2): 2,514 vs 2,452 rows. Small but not zero; a tick-level tape test (an actual print at p) would differ again.
4. **The print's own second must be excluded from the test.** Including it kills (almost) every print (4 rows): obvious, but it is why the scan has to start after the print second.
5. **Window inclusivity.** Lower and upper end exclusive vs inclusive: 1,871 vs 1,937 rows (-3%), saved 95k vs 83k: immaterial. ZL-2: print usable from s+1, lower end inclusive, upper exclusive on SS[kin].
6. **Equal-price prints / print aggregation.** Both implementations use single tape rows with size >= 25 (inclusive), no aggregation across rows, seconds or equal prices; order within a second does not matter. No difference found.
7. **Tape source / de-dup and price frame.** I used the de-duplicated tape copies plus the 2026-10 weekly parts (97,476 prints >= 25 with side A/B, 2023-04..2026-10) and put each print in the path frame with a per-(label day, instrument) offset taken from the tape-vs-path match (99.75% of prints fall inside their second's [SL, SH]); ZL-2 uses dropin_redo copies with a per-4-hour-block offset and has no prints on 16 roll days. Residual 175 rows only in ZL-2 and 9-75 only mine (after item 2) are not pinned; roll days and the frame offset are the candidates.

## Verdict
The written definition is reproducible to Jaccard 0.93 once the item-2 convention is fixed, and the result keeps its sign; but 'no trade at p since' is not one rule: the natural reading gives a rule that flags a third fewer rows and saves a third less (ALL 83k vs 138k; L6M 44 vs 77; HOLD 53 vs 91; PRE 56 vs 73; X' -11 vs -4). If this lead is carried to a Shadow log it must carry (a) the aggressor convention A = sell / B = buy and (b) an explicit statement that the scan includes the second right after the print (natural reading) or excludes it (ZL-2 convention), and log both flags.
