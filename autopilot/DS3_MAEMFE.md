# DS3_MAEMFE - MAE / MFE descriptive profile of the THP dataset (MAEMFE, 2026-10-29)
Descriptive only: no scoring, no policy. UNTOUCHED (>2026-02-06) never read. Data: 47,131 THP trades (13 have no path rows and are dropped: n=47,118), 1-s path trees dp1s + ldp1s (profit rows + loss rows = every second the trade is open), columns tid, ts, mae, mfe only.

## Definitions
* MAE = max(0, side*(E - running adverse extreme)), MFE = max(0, side*(running favourable extreme - E)), in NQ points from the entry fill E (library basis), using the 1-s HIGH/LOW of each second (not the close). Measured over the whole life of the trade up to and INCLUDING the exit second (the last row of the trade; row ts = exit second - the exit second's own high/low are in the running extreme, so a target exit has MFE >= target and a stop exit MAE >= stop; within the exit second the order of touch is unknowable). The entry second is included.
* "MAE >= X" is first-touch on the 1-s grid; "MAE > 0" means any adverse excursion at all (>= 1 tick, 0.25). Time to MAE/MFE = minutes from entry to the end of the FIRST second at which the final (maximum) MAE/MFE was reached (resolution 1 s).
* Order of touch: "-X before +Y" = first second with mae>=X strictly earlier than first second with mfe>=Y. Same-second touches are reported separately (n_same_sec) and are in neither order.
* Winner / loser = sign of the live bracket exit P&L on the MEASURED fill basis = side*(exit_px - E) - fill_cost_pts (exit cost per trade_index; the 0.242 pt fee is NOT netted). Winner = pnl>0. Time exits (type 2) are classified by their pnl, so winners > target-hits.
* P(target first | MAE>=X) = share of trades with MAE>=X whose bracket exit was the TARGET (exit_type 1) among trades that touched -X. Because the bracket exits at stop or target (or a time exit, type 2), "target first" is the bracket's outcome; for MAE>=stop the trade is a stop by construction. The CSV also gives P(winner | MAE>=X) (time exits included). Wilson 95% CIs.
* Windows: PRE 2024-02..2025-06, HOLD 2025-07-01..2026-02-06, X (X-prime) 2023-04..2024-01 - the data DO cover X, shown as third column set. 
* Cells with n<30 are flagged with `*` (for the heat table: flagged when the smaller of the two orders has <30 trades). Tie rule above.
