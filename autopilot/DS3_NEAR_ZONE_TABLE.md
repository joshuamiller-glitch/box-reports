**Definitions.** m = the trade has reached m% of its target on the 1-s mid (a trade that fills its target counts as having reached 100%: the mid alone peaks ~1 pt short of a limit fill). After the first second at m: FILLED = the target filled; r50/r25/r0 = (not filled) the mid later retraced to 50% / 25% / breakeven of the target; CL<0 = not filled and closed with negative net P&L (the stop, cut and time exits). GB (give-back event) = reached m, did not fill, closed below 50% of the target; RT (round trip) = reached m, did not fill, closed at or below breakeven. P(f) = share of trades at m for which the target fills BEFORE the mid touches f% of the target (trades that end any other way before either count against, so P is conservative). EV(floor f) = P x target + (1-P) x f x target, per trade in the trades own lots, fees 4.84/lot, compared with HOLD = what the trades actually paid under the live rules. dEV = EV(floor) - HOLD in total dollars over the window (positive = a hard floor at f beats holding); dEVs = same with a 1.2 pt market-exit slip on every floor touch (REDO-R9 central, the floor as a market order) and no slip on target fills. $F / $NF = actual dollars of the trades that filled / did not. P7 12/7/7 = the same P7 trades with dollars scaled per lot to the table 12 (Asia) / 7 (London) / 7 (NY-AM) by the table ratio (12/7, 7/4, 7/4 on the trades actual lots, cap-reduced lots scale in proportion): probabilities identical to P7 7/4/4, dollars scale.

A floor f is only meaningful when f < m (shown as - otherwise). **Caveats (first-order).** Floor touch is read off the 1-s mid, exit assumed at floor level (dEV) or floor level minus 1.2 pt (dEVs); the trades path is the one produced under live rules (loss cuts, stack cuts already applied, so trades cut before m never appear); no re-entry or follow-on trades are re-simulated; the live-week window is 1 week and 33 P7 trades at most (read as colour, not as proof); live-week paths end at the live exit (live profit rules ThL2Trail/PolicyTake already took some trades out before the target, these count as non-fills).
