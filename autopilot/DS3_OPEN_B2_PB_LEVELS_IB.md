## 4. The pullback trap (3.5) - answer
The first pullback after a drive carries a positive stack-add in many brackets (145 of 180 cells have d Sharpe > 0; 58 have CI > 0), but this is the drive direction, not the pullback: effect vs random-direction entries on the same days/times (d) is +14..+21 pts and vs timing-destroyed (a) +13..+26 pts for the top cells. Against random timing inside the drive regime the answer depends on how that placebo is matched (survivor cells, WFPRE, basis T, n=26 cells with a (c)): random bar anywhere 09:35-10:29 on the rule's own days (c2) +7.2 pts mean, on all drive days (c1) +7.7 pts, but **clock-matched random entries on drive days (c3: same time of day as the real events) only +2.8 pts mean (median +2.0), and only 3 of 26 have a CI above 0 (PB_ORX_T_F60 +10.2 [1.9,19.2], PB_M50_B_F45 +9.6 [1.8,16.5], PB_ORX_H_F30 +7.8 [3.0,12.6]) among 180 cells and after selection on the same window**. Reading: most of the apparent pullback edge is "enter the drive early in 09:35-10:00 in the drive direction", the same trap as DS3_PBX_FINAL; the pullback timing adds a small, unproven increment. c1 and c3 were run after the group looks as supplementary placebos (WFPRE columns only; not part of the registered gates).
Entry type: B (arming-bar break) and H (higher low) give the highest Sharpe deltas; T (touch) the most trades. Level: VWAP and OR5 retest dominate; the first-5-min-low retrace (L5) has only 57..208 events. Opening type (top cell, post-lock trades, 09:35 cut): mean $ per trade: OTD +4.4k [1.7k,7.1k] n=140, ORR +4.2k n=56, OA +1.7k n=62, OD -0.6k n=29 (10:00 cut: OTD/ORR +8.3k/+8.4k, OA -1.2k); 2023: -0.9k, 2024..2026 +4.0..+5.4k (unstable); ISM-rule 10:00 release days +9.9k vs +2.4k others.
