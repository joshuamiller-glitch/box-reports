## Caveats
1. D and TW answer different questions: TW says whether a trade on the bar cell earns more than a trade on the 1-min cell; D says whether the cell's total daily P&L beats the control's, so it also moves with the trade count (a cell with half the control's trades gets D ~ half of what the control's remaining trades add). Both are reported; 'KEEPS' needs both. Verdict flags: LOSES = neither HOLD CI excludes 0.
2. Several rows have controls with n 10-60 in HOLD (pre-open SM/KB/PE/MV cells, iFVG range/24, KB range-8, renko-10); the block bootstrap CI is unreliable there and the control-collapse note of the original reports applies (control abs mean -14 .. -38).
3. The original wave-1 G3 gate was sign-only (HOLD paired > 0), so the demotion rule above (G3u: both HOLD unconditional differences > 0) is the same gate re-run on the unconditional measure, not a new threshold; I did not use CI exclusion as a gate because the originals never did. Multiplicity gates (G6, RC/RW) are untouched and still fail for every cell.
4. Not covered (no stored cell series or not tiered LEAD/CR): the DEAD recent rows of BAR-ENG G1/G2/G4 (PE UniRenko London, SM 5-min NY-PM, WT 5-min pre, WT HA15 NY-AM, DN 2500-vol pre) which were already DEAD; the 103-cell G3 grid beyond its top 8 qv cells (the 8 are those that went through the cell-level layer); ICT CRT rows (no selected cell); wave-2 TR/GRID reps (all DEAD).
