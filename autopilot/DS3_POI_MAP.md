## 8. What the map is built on, and what is missing
The POI store (689 streams x 44,370 signals; code/poi/README.md, importable by FZ-1) holds, for every signal second, per stream: nearest fresh / mitigated POI AHEAD (between entry and target) and BEHIND (between entry and stop), age, count. Classes: FVG / iFVG / implied FVG / OB / breaker (+CE) on 14 timeframes 15 s..1 w; profile POC / VAH / VAL / HVN / LVN / naked POC and VWAP + 1-3 sigma bands on session / day / week / month / swing anchors; prior day / RTH day / week / month H L C O 50%; floor and Camarilla pivots; previous-session, overnight and ICT killzone highs and lows; developing day / overnight / RTH / session extremes; day / midnight / 08:30 / 09:30 / weekly / monthly opens; NDOG, NWOG, RTH gap; opening-range edges and 1x / 2x extensions; fractal 3/5/7 swings on 14 timeframes; equal highs / lows (tight, relative); CRT 1 h / 4 h; Fibonacci 38-79 retracements and 127-200 extensions, OTE zone, dealing-range high / low / 50, premium-discount; BOS / CHoCH; HTF EMA / SMA 50-200, Bollinger, Keltner, Donchian, Supertrend, Ichimoku; rule-based Wyckoff range edges / spring / upthrust / SOS-SOW bars; round numbers; order-book walls / density / ABS / ICE / sweep flags at the signal second.
**NAMED GAPS (not built, nothing fabricated):** Wyckoff automatic rally / reaction extremes, climaxes, last point of support / supply and phase labels; trend lines / channel edges; measured-move and 1-2-3 / three-push levels; single prints, poor highs / lows, volume-climax bars and composite profiles; absorption PRICE of the last absorption event, last sweep LEVEL and session book-defended levels; touch counts ('times it held in the session'); per-event release calendar for the time POIs.
