## Scoring method (what runs when the raw table exists)
- Outcome per raw trade (W-PM schema, /mnt/l2/l2vast/_rebuild/s0/<BOOK>/trades_raw.parquet, key <book>_<sig_ts> = sig_id; r_net = net_fee over lots x 20 x stop risk with stop 60 (O7 40, X3 X4 75), mfe_r, mae_r, t_mfe, t_mae, 1-s paths in U.npy): effect on mean R, on MAE and on MFE.
- Per book x session cell: lift (yes minus no), weekly-cluster zw (calendar-week yes-minus-no differences, t over weeks >= 8), the cell nonsense bar = 99th percentile of |zw| of the 200 Block 18 nonsense conditions in the same cell and outcome, same sign in every year (>= 30 per side) and in L6M. Pass rate of real vs nonsense at each stage beside the counts.
- Persistent-draw controls (bucket 300) wherever a skip or size is engine-tested later; walk-forward (trailing 26 weeks, next 2 weeks) for every fitted choice; planted-edge check before any result is read.
- Until the raw table exists: preview scoring on JL2 trades (runs_rf/TL3_EV_JN05W2JL2) for R only; W-CF4 already runs its own preview on signal-universe outcomes (R 338 tests beat the bar of 15,850, 127 also pass years + L6M; MFE 402/145; MAE 648/314: a preview on hypothetical 60/60 outcomes, not a result).
