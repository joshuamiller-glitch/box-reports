# DS3_REBUILD_S2_DESIGN - Stage 2 per-book score design (W-AL1, design only, nothing run)

VERDICT (data ends 2026-10-30; DESIGN ONLY): Stage 2 can reuse the T-ALPHA walk-forward machinery (code/th_alpha: wf.py, score.py, assemble.py) with the unit changed from a 5-minute grid row to a D7 signal; nothing is fitted until Stage 0 raw trades and the Stage 1 survivor lists exist.

## Unit, target, inputs
- One model per book (K7 M7 U7 P7 H7 A7 O7), sessions pooled with session as an input (cells with fewer than 150 trades in a training window are not fitted separately). Row = one raw signal (taken or skipped, Stage 0 raw outcome), key sig_id.
- Target: raw R = net P&L / (stop x lots x $20) of the Stage 0 raw trade (fixed 60 stop, book target), clipped at +-3 R; secondary targets MAE and MFE (points) for the exit stage. Until Stage 0 lands the placeholder is the hyp1m outcome in signals_all.
- Inputs: Stage 1 survivors of all ten families, trade-side signed, graded values (not yes/no) where a graded series exists (the `v_*`, `raw_*`, `fca_*` columns), plus the 0/1 conditions only when no graded version exists; missing = 0 after median fill with a missing flag; at most 60 inputs per book (cluster representatives at |rank corr| < 0.85 chosen on the TRAINING window only).
## Walk-forward (the base of the T-ALPHA code)
- Refit every 2 weeks; train on the trailing 26 weeks of signals ending 3 days before the test block (purge > the longest trade hold of the book; K7/P7 hold up to 14 h, so embargo = max hold, not 3 days: use the book's p95 hold); predict the next 2 weeks. A frozen-26-week variant is run beside it (T-RECENTS finding). No tuning on the test span: ridge alpha fixed at 0.5 n (as in T-ALPHA, shrinks a 0.02 effect to about 60%); GBM check = LightGBM 150 trees, 8 leaves, min 400 per leaf (scaled down to min 40 for signal-sized samples), lambda 50.
- Score = ridge prediction (headline), GBM prediction (check), average (reference). Metrics per book x session x window (L3M, L6M, HOLD, GAP, PRE, X', all): rank IC of score vs raw R with signal-day clustered SE, and the sizing result below.
## Sizing and controls
- Size by score quintile of the trailing 26 weeks distribution (causal cut points): x0.5 / x1 / x1 / x1.5 / x2, capped at the book live cap; mean lots normalised to 1 so the comparison is at equal mean exposure; no signal skipped (size, never skip: path-effect rule). Exact engine only for the finalists; first-order = sum over raw trades of (m - 1) x raw P&L.
- Controls, same procedure each: (1) NONSENSE conditions: the same inputs shifted in time by 11/23/37/60/90 days (same persistence, wrong time) and, separately, 200 AR(1) series with matched persistence (T-ALPHA gen_nonsense); (2) SHUFFLED outcomes: raw R permuted within book x month (persistent: whole 5-minute buckets of one book/side exchange outcomes), the full pipeline rerun 50 times: the real IC and the first-order gain must exceed the 99th percentile of the shuffled-outcome distribution; (3) random size ladder of the same lot distribution, bucket=300 persistent draw; (4) PLANTED edge: add a known +0.02 IC signal to the raw R target and show the whole book pipeline recovers about 40-70% of it (T-ALPHA: ridge recovers 45-70% at 5-15 min); a book whose sample is too small to detect a 0.02 planted edge is reported as not testable, not as zero.
- Power note: 26-week training windows give about 300-1,500 signals per book; a +0.02 IC is below the noise floor there (se about 0.03-0.06). Expect only conditions with |IC| > 0.05 to survive; report the share of books where the planted edge was recoverable before reading any result.
## Reporting
- Table per book: IC (t), first-order gain at equal mean lots, drawdown and worst-week change first, nonsense 99th percentile and shuffled-outcome percentile beside each; the T-ALPHA finding applies: path effects of any re-sizing are +-$55-75k per 6 months on the stack, so the stack-level exact engine verdict needs the same placebo spread before any size rule is a lead.
- Frames to extend for the all-ten-family input table: CF4 5-min frame (final to 2026-11-11), CF5 grid (final to 2026-11-13 after GRIDFIXDONE), CF6 counting grid (to 2026-10), T-ALPHA forecasts (core to 2026-11-13, full set to 2026-10-16): queued after the Stage 1 / Stage 6 deliverables.
