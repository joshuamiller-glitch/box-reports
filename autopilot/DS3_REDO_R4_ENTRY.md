## 0. What the live entry really is (code, not description)
DS7.cs v1.4 L2Arm/L2Pump: L2SplitPct (70) of the session lots go at market in 1-lot slices every 2 s; the remainder is NOT a resting limit: it is a MARKET add fired when price is >= L2GateMinDipPts (3) against the signal price AND >= L2GateRequired (1) of 8 book confirmations hold (checked at most once per second), 1 stage, no time limit (L2GateMaxMinutes 0), cancelled on the stop level / time block / session-lots 0 / any exit. Stop at the original level, target re-based from the average. The engine (replica sim.py) implements exactly this. So "resting offset 1-6 pts" = min dip 1-6 pts (DIP family). A TRUE resting limit is a different order type, scored as the LIM family under a stated fill model: order live 0.5 s after the arm at arm_px -+ offset; fills at the limit when a print trades strictly through it, or when aggressor-against volume at the level reaches the lots (queue 0, optimistic) or lots + one median book level (queue 1); no L2 test, cancelled on the same rules. LIM rows are HYPOTHETICAL (optimistic fills, no live fill evidence) and are never a recommendation.
P7 PB3B 45-s confirm: P7 signals are held until a three-bar pattern prints on 45-s bars (sessions nyam, nypm only), then the split entry runs; engine = ITER14 replica 45-s bars built from the owned tape; other timeframes rebuild the series from the same prints.
Engine: exact replica (S1b tick bar close, TH-L2-S state cut COMB, Q1-A sizer), hooks code/th_sz_entry/entry_hooks.py (class swap, instance config; interface INTERFACE.md); EN_REG (the live setting) is regression-checked: all trade columns identical to S1b-COMB on 202610 and 202305 (all columns, 823 and 260 trades). Fills: market orders walk the book 1.0 s after the decision (LAT_FILL, user-measured); per-variant fill statistics in section 4.

## 5d. Verdict (plain)
No entry-mechanics change is recommended; the live setting (70/30 split, 3-pt dip, 1 of 8 confirmations, 1 stage, no time limit, 1 lot / 2 s, P7 three-bar 45 s nyam+nypm) stays. Reasons: (1) engine path-dependence is large: tiny real changes (NULL4-6) move L6M maxDD by 4-8% and mean by up to 1.2k $/wk, so single cells inside that band are not effects; (2) the pure gate (split 0), dip 2, slice 1 lot / 4 s, confirm in every session, engulf 60 s are clearly WORSE than live (-20% to -34% maxDD, negative mean); (3) the only L6M cell clearing the floor is P7 engulf @45 s (+13.5% maxDD, +1.6k $/wk, HOLD +30.6%, PRE +6.2% / +1.1k $/wk, bi-weekly WF beats live in about two thirds of refit periods) but its neighbours engulf @30 s (-0.8%) and @90 s (+0.2%) and @60 s (-20%) do not support it, and with 22 L6M cells the best-of-family maximum of pure noise is of the same size; keep it as a Shadow candidate for the P7 confirm only; (4) split 50/50, slice all-at-once and dip 4 give higher mean on every window (+0.5k to +4k $/wk) at about the same maxDD on L6M: LEAD-level, same caveat; (5) the TRUE resting limit family was not run (hypothetical order type, optimistic fills in a one-month test: +26k in 202610 at queue 0, which is a fill-model artefact, not evidence).
