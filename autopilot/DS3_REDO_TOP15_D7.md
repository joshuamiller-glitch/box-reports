**How CUTWAIT is built on the D7 v1.1 engine (read before using Table 4).** The ledger rows are scored by the CUTWAIT study engine (per-book lock engine, fresh-signal series, measured fills). Here the same rule is injected into the D7 v1.1 engine as follows. **Cut**: first 1-s close with unrealised <= -X pts (X = 25 for HM, 30 for SM; the "x25 / x30" in the ledger names is the level label, i.e. the cut distance in points, not a pulled-liquidity multiple), market exit at that second's path price minus 1 tick (0.25 pt), injected as an exit through the same hook as TH-L2 (per-lot difference to the original exit, scaled by the filled lots; the cut is measured from the library signal price E and is NOT re-evaluated from the actual fill price of a 70/30 or PE 45-s row, unlike the L2SL cut). It replaces nothing else: the TH-L2 loss cut (-30 / 5 s / 30x) stays ON in the base, so on SM the -30 CUTWAIT cut is the TH-L2 distance without the order-book condition and fires first on many rows; on HM the -25 cut fires before the TH-L2 -30. **Blackout**: after a cut of a row the engine actually TOOK, library rows of the same book entering within N = 30 min of the cut are removed before the lock engine; 'same' = additionally, for the rest of that label day, only same-side rows can re-enter until the next taken trade; 'any' = side-agnostic. The taken set depends on the blackout, so it is iterated to a fixed point (<= 4 passes; every cell converged). **What is missing**: the CUTWAIT study also adds FRESH re-entries (signals the baseline skipped as busy that become tradable once the cut frees the slot). Those are not library trades, so they cannot enter the D7 engine: the page shows cut + blackout without the added re-entries (in the study's own decomposition for HM the added re-entries are about as large as the blackout effect, with the opposite sign in HOLD). **Placebo** for the blackout: the cut stays, the blackout skip set is replaced by a random same-count set of the book's live rows (200-draw target, n shown); the cut itself has no mask placebo (as for the TH-L2 rules).

## Exact D7 definitions

### Exact D7 definitions of the five features (all as-of the LAST COMPLETED 1-min bar before the entry minute; E = signal entry price; side = +1 long / -1 short)

Common: `lc` = last 1-min library bar with minute <= (entry second // 60) - 1 (the bar that closed at or before the start of the entry minute; the forming bar is never used). AUX bars = library back-adjusted 1-min bars (o, c from the library series, h/l = max/min of the 1-s path inside the minute). ATR14 = Wilder ATR14 on AUX 1-min bars (seed = SMA of the first 14 true ranges, then (prev*13 + TR)/14, TR = max(H-L, |H-prevC|, |L-prevC|), gaps included). Label day rolls at 18:00 ET (ETH day 18:00-17:00 ET). Series are continuous since 2023-02, never reset at a session boundary.

| feature | exact formula | bars / source | ATR / normaliser | as-of rule | sign |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| `sig_kelt1` | side * (E - EMA20[lc]) / ATR14[lc]; no band multiplier (D7's "EMA14 +/- 1.0 x ATR10" is NOT this) | EMA20 of AUX 1-min closes, alpha 2/21, seeded with the first close of the whole series | Wilder ATR14, 1-min | lc as above | side-signed |
| `z_vwap_eth` | side * (E - VWAP_ETH[lc]) / SD_ETH[lc]; NaN if SD <= 0 (no volume yet) | VWAP_ETH = cumsum(v*tp)/cumsum(v) from the label-day start (18:00 ET) to lc inclusive, tp = (H+L+C)/3 of the AUX bar, v = NQ 1-min TRADED volume from the L2 1-min table (not bar volume; bars with no L2 volume = 0); SD_ETH = sqrt(cumsum(v*tp^2)/cumsum(v) - VWAP^2) (volume-weighted, population) | divided by the VWAP SD, NOT by ATR | lc | side-signed |
| `htf_vwap_atr` | side * (E - VWAP_ETH[lc]) / ATR14[lc] (same VWAP as z_vwap_eth) | as above | 1-min Wilder ATR14, never the daily ATR | lc | side-signed |
| `htf_on_pos` | side * ((E - ON_lo) / max(ON_hi - ON_lo, 0.25) - 0.5) | ON range = running high / low over `time_1m` (tick-built 1-min bars, points = ticks*0.25) of the label day whose ET end-minute is <= 09:30 or > 18:00, i.e. the overnight 18:00-09:30 ET block, up to and including the last closed time_1m bar `bi` (t_sig <= entry second); after 09:30 the range is frozen at its 09:30 value; NaN before the first ON bar; centred (-0.5..+0.5) | none | bi = last time_1m bar with t_sig <= entry second | side-signed |
| `htf_pd_range_atr` | (PDH - PDL) / ATRd[prior day] | PDH / PDL = previous ETH label day (18:00-17:00 ET) high / low from `time_1m` (previous existing label day: Monday uses Friday) ; ATRd = rolling mean over 14 label days of (high - low), min 5 days, value of the PRIOR completed label day | daily ATR (range mean), NOT 1-min ATR | prior day only | UNSIGNED |

### Tercile rule (the live rule as EINT1 / EF-2 study it)
Expanding per-book rank: rank = (number of the book's earlier signals with a STRICTLY smaller value) / (number of earlier signals), computed only once the book has >= 100 earlier non-NaN values (the first 100 signals of a book are unruled); sides and sessions pooled per book; the universe is every library trade of the book (all standalone signals, taken and not taken by the lock engine), ordered by (entry_ts, tid), the value is inserted after ranking; NaN value = no rank = signal kept. skipBot3 removes the signal when rank < 1/3, BEFORE the lock engine (d7lib `extra_skip`). Masks reproduce D2's rule matrix (`eint1_redo/Wl.npy`) with 0 mismatches on all 12 rows. Frozen-threshold check: skip when the feature < q33 of `eint1_thresholds_frozen.csv` (per-book trailing 12 months to 2026-02-06, one constant for all history). The "cut-vs-expanding bucket difference" table counts the signals that fall in a different bucket (skipped vs kept) under the two rules and re-runs the engine with the frozen cut.

## Notes, limits and what was not done
* **Selection history.** Every row was selected on windows that include the recent ones (ledger ranking by new L3M); REDO-3 shows trailing positivity does not beat noise. Nothing here is a PASS; a RISK-KEEP flag, if any, would be a consistency statement on looked data. L1M / L2M are views (n = 5 / 9 weeks).
* **Frames.** v1.1 base = `d7_v11.run_v11` (TH-L2 loss ON / profit OFF, zones HALF on the 7 non-OTE lines at D20 with OTE-62 B73 D30, X1-X5 and the three signal-state rules in SHADOW, NY-AM 1 lot on K6/H6/M6, Native stop, 70/30, PE 45-s NY, ER(20), Asia cut 50%, 12R combined week lock). As-is = REDO-2 row a (market entries, join stop, no D6 rules, per-book locks, live lots; the v1.1 NY-AM lot change is NOT in the as-is frame). A rule is a skip applied BEFORE the lock engine (d7lib `extra_skip`), exactly as EF-2.
* **Equal-risk mean.** Whole lot table scaled (lscale; integer half-up min-1 lots move with it) until the rule's ALL-window (2023-04-03..2026-10-30) worst-20 equals the base's; secant, <= 7 runs, tolerance 3%; achieved |gap| <= 3.0% across rows; the mean change is rule@c minus base@1. Run for lock 12R and lock off in the v1.1 frame only.
* **Placebo.** Random skip of the same number of the book's live rows (rows with planned lots > 0 in the v1.1 lot table), same book, 200-draw target (n shown per table), v1.1 frame with the 12R lock. Rank = share of draws whose maxDD cut >= the rule's cut (0.00 = the rule beats every draw); p95 = the 95th percentile of the placebo cuts. skipBot3 removes ~1/3 of a book's signals, so the mean effect is largely a volume effect that the placebo prices. Lock-off and as-is frames: no placebo.
* **RISK-KEEP** (EF-2 definition on the new windows): L6M maxDD cut >= 20% AND L6M mean change inside MDE (not significantly negative) AND the cut beats the placebo p95 AND HOLD, PRE and X' maxDD each cut >= 20%. Shown for L6M and for F9.
* **Scored vs not scored.** Not scored on the engine: EINT1 ST sig_kelt1, WT htf_vwap_atr / z_vwap_eth / htf_on_pos / htf_pd_range_atr (ST and WT are not D7 v1.1 books; the lot table has no ST / WT) and CUTWAIT WT x30 (same reason); HM / KB / PE / SM rows ranked below the 12 EINT1 rows scored here (e.g. SM htf_pd_range_atr, L3M -1k) were not scored. SM has no EINT1 row in the top 12.
* **Tercile sensitivity.** The expanding rank is the rule the EINT1 study scored; the frozen thresholds (trailing 12 months to 2026-02-06, one constant for the whole history) are what a fixed-number chart would use. Table 6 gives the signals that change bucket and the frozen-threshold engine run (v1.1, 12R). Under the frozen cut, skipped shares in the new windows stay 29-37%, so the two rules differ mostly at the margin; the exception is KB htf_pd_range_atr, where the frozen cut changes 88 F9 signals (the prior-day range level moved).
* **Tie-outs.** v1.1 reverted -> v1.0d cubes at 0.0000 $/day (4 checks, 935 days; MORNING.md); EINT1 masks == D2's rule matrix (0 mismatches, 12 rows); the five features recomputed independently on 30 signals (definitions section); CUTWAIT cut kernel: not tied to the CUTWAIT study engine (different engine; the page says so), only its rule definition is shared.
* **Not done / pending.** Placebo draws still running at generation time are counted in the n shown; rerun `code/top15/report15.py` + `final_md.py` to refresh. D7 status of every rule: the owner's call.
