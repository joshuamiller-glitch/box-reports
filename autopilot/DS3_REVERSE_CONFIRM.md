## Verdict (read this first)
**Mirroring the entry confirmations as exits does not work as a rule that fires often.** The literal mirror (a new opposing FVG / iFVG / OB / breaker / BRK structure, a Heikin-Ashi flip, an EMA 8/21 cross, CCI / RSI reversal, a renko reversal, a delta-flip, price losing a VWAP band or profile level) fires on 25-90% of trades and loses **-1 to -7 pts per contract per trade in the last 6 months on every book** (0 of 10 books >= 0 for 7 of the 8 headline triggers; MDE 1-8 pts), the same answer as SD-A. What the mirror does carry is information: the trades it fires on reach the target 27-46% of the time against 50-60% for all trades, but the exit sells the 27-46% that would have won and pays 1 pt of cost on most trades, so the saved losses are almost exactly cancelled by the forfeited winners (e.g. FVG-5m against, S7030 base, KB, L6M: +212k saved vs -261k forfeited $ at one contract).
**Nothing is PASS.** 119,620 cell specs (x10 books) were scored across five groups; every program-level studentized reality check is non-significant after the cumulative adjustment (min cumulative-adjusted p 0.08), 0 of 5 groups pass.
What survives the V2 gates are **27 distinct CANDIDATE triggers (44 cell rows incl. neighbours: RA 14, RA2 7, RB1 4, RB2 2, RB3 17 rows) - all of them low-fire (1-12% of trades) "support lost" / "against" events used to TIGHTEN the stop (stop moved to the midpoint between the trigger price and the original stop) or to exit only while IN PROFIT / UNDER WATER, worth +0.1 to +1.1 pt per contract, mostly at or inside their MDE.** The single cell with real statistical support is `TOP|S7030|MA|OR5|tight|age1|uw` (RB3): when ANY of five level-lost events (MA book; lvn1_sw15, OB_1h, lvn1_sw1h, lvn2_day, FVG_1h) occurs while the trade is under water, tighten the stop to the midpoint: **+0.50 pt per contract in L6M [+0.26, +0.79] (MDE 0.38) on 4.4% of MA trades (21 triggers in L6M, 18 of them hit the tightened stop; none of the 18 would have reached the target: P(target | fired) = 0.00 in L6M, HOLD, PRE and X', median 10.5 min from trigger to the stop-out), HOLD +0.47, PRE +0.52, X' +1.03, positive in 12/12 quarters and 5/5 purged folds, 3-s latency +0.51, placebo rank p=0.000 (200 random-exit draws, both shuffled-day controls below), Romano-Wolf p 0.001 / 0.002 / 0.004 (PRE+X', L6M, HOLD) inside its 900-cell family, stack correlation -0.06.** It is not PASS: it sits in a family whose members were picked from the PRE+X' top-50 of RA (10,512 cells/book), the cumulative-adjusted RC p is 0.50, the recent-window DSR with N_cum trials is 0.11 (0.62 with 10.5k trials), MA is not a stack book (policy: MA6 pooled DEAD) and the effect rests on 18 tightened-stop hits in L6M (132 over all windows). Depth control: at the trigger the trade is already a median 42 pts adverse (stop 60), but a price-keyed rule 'tighten to the midpoint once adverse >= A' gives -0.6 to -2.2 pt per contract in L6M for A = 20-54 (fire 22-54%) and only reaches ~0 at A = 58 (fire 21%), so adversity depth alone does not reproduce it. It is a lead for forward evidence, not a rule.
