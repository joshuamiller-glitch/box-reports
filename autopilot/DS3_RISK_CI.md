## Method, deviations, caveats

Prereg `l2vast/ds3/prereg/RB1_RISKCI_prereg.md` sha256 f357846b5f290e9efcedf61cd1afbfa40e75e053a941e45f23651ed34e77d49d (logged in DECISIONS.md before any statistic). Code `l2vast/ds3/code/riskci/` (rc_lib.py, run_rc.py, mk_md.py), results `l2vast/ds3/out/riskci/` (a_window_bootstrap.csv, b_regime_mix.csv, c_horizon.csv, d_chance_riskkeep.csv). Series are the stored daily outputs of ONE_CONVENTION (`oneconv/oc_res.pkl`) and ON_SET (`onset/res_*.pkl`) engines (tie-out: unlocked as-is L6M 21.7/maxDD 88.0, composite 41.1/22.9, composite 12R PRE 15.1/232.6 reproduce the stored tables). Masks capped 20260206; no post-cap file opened; append_cells n/a. Locks are the combined K6-R week lock with m0 1.0; ER sizer feeds the running max.

**Deviation from prereg.** The prereg said the reported chance rate is the max over N1..N3. N1 turned out not to be an identical-risk null (mean-shifting leaves the composite's lower variance), so the text quotes N2 and N3 as the chance range and shows N1 as a separate column; the prereg max is the N1 column in the cases where it is largest. The first-draft intent (calibrate against a composite with identical expected risk) is N2/N3. Also: the regime pools are disjoint by week start (X' 44 + PRE 74 + HOLD 31 = 149), so the pool sizes differ by 1 week from the RK-1 window counts.

Caveats. (i) 149 weeks of one market process: bootstrap blocks of 2-6 weeks capture short serial dependence but not regime persistence beyond that, and cannot create a week worse than the worst one observed (the composite's -120k week 2024-09-30 is in PRE). So all CIs are lower bounds on tail risk. (ii) Regime-mix paths assume the future weeks look like the stated era; the mix is a scenario, not a forecast. (iii) The 12R/30R lock multiple is unknown; locks are priced, not chosen. (iv) maxDD of a 26-week path starts at 0 (no prior peak). (v) Post-hoc; nothing here is a PASS or a rule.
