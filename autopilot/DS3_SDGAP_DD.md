## Method notes, limits (stated, not hidden)

* **Unit.** Trade i of book b: lots x 20 x pts. Book lots: MV 2 in London and NY-AM (0 elsewhere), PB7 1 in Asia, other books at nominal lots (WT 7, DN 2, PB 1, MA 2, CR 2, O6 1, IFVG 1) - WT, DN, MA, CR, O6, iFVG are NOT in the Sunday composite, so only the percentage changes of their own line are meaningful. Baseline = hold-to-bracket N basis (no target-fill correction, as the SD-GAP headline); delta = D_N per trade x 20 x lots. The stack line overlays the delta on COMP|off of DD-1 (the ER sizer, locks and integer rounding are not re-run).
* **The size effect is the finding.** For the exit actions mkt / tight / half the delta is negatively correlated with the trade's own P&L whatever the trigger: the permuted-delta placebo A therefore ranks the rules high (0.69-1.00 for the representatives), the size-cut placebo B (proportional cut of the same absolute volume on random trades) does not (0.01-0.65 own-line). Placebo B was added after the first look as the standing order's own form ("random size cuts of the same volume"); the registered placebo A is shown beside it, both are conservative-to-lenient in opposite directions, 'beats placebo' requires B.
* **Not done:** a lock-engine re-run of M6 / A6 with the exit rule inside (the overlay is first-order; for 1-2 lots the answer cannot change the 20% bar), WT is in the lock engine only as a research book (DS3_DECAY_GAP stack line stands), capacity and latency (stored in DS3_DECAY_GAP quant items).
* **Multiplicity.** 85,212 stored cells re-scored (0 new trials added to UF_TRIALS; G1-SDGAP-DD entry n_repeat 85,212). DEAD_CELLS: the 30 representatives appended (program G1SDGAPDD, tier DEAD).
