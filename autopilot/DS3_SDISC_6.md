## Definitions and caveats
- Regime = Supertrend(ATR 10, mult 3.0) on 5-min / 10-min bars as of the last completed bar before the entry bar. Carrier regime = the net open side count at the previous minute of the ss1 trade tables (ASIA: PE+DN+KB, NY-AM: MV). T1 uses the cell's raw trigger events (before busy) in the 30 minutes before each carrier entry. 'Alone' = the leg's whole trade set in the same window; random-subset rank = share of 500 random same-size subsets of the leg's trades with a lower mean.
- Trend book alone: KB analytic MEASURED 60/60 run in the session (ASIA HOLD +1.70, NY-AM HOLD -0.83) and the carrier composite on the STORED basis (shown as 'alone' in the T1 rows: ASIA +4.76 PRE, NY-AM +4.95 PRE pts/trade).
- Carrier books are not replayable (Sim books DN/MV, lock-kept stack rows for PE/KB): their $ and pts are stored-basis; the stored basis overstates short stops but these books use 60-pt brackets, where the mapped credit was calibrated.
- The item-5 HOLD look already showed the legs alone; item 6 is the first look at the combinations.
- Survivors: the two MEASURED-leg cells with PRE CI > 0 and n >= 100 (ASIA L1 C1, ASIA L2 C1) are appended to out/sd56/survivors_for_A.parquet (cell prefix sd56/6/).
