## 0. Verdict (plain)
- **The book adds nothing to a session cell beyond its own MDE. In no session does any book trigger or filter beat its cell, and in no session does any bar type beat the best of the 1-min / 15-s / 30-s time-bar version, by more than the MDE** (PRE: 0 of 611 (a) items with n>=100 and 0 of 3,653 (b) items with n>=100 have paired difference > MDE; HOLD: 0 of 19 and 0 of 39 PREHOLD items). ASIA and LONDON included: ASIA book-trigger entries (100 triggers x 5 cells) have PRE effect t at most 0.8, LONDON at most 1.0 (a trigger entry is a random entry in session, with the book requirement).
- 20 of the 1,000 (a) items pass the PRE gate, all of them FILTERS on the NOPULL/REF2/IMBCH/DF booleans of the already-alive BA cells (ASIA 9, NY-AM 11); NOPULL keeps 84-93% of the entries, so those items are the cell. Paired difference in HOLD vs the unfiltered cell: -4.3..+7.4 pts, MDE 8-25; none exceeds its MDE.
- 8 filter items and 4+3 bar-type items are tier-alive on the HOLD gates (list in section 3): they are listed as survivors and sent to A, but the "effect" is the cell's own edge (ASIA SM_sst, EM; NY-AM HM, KB), not information from the book or the bar type. SESSION-PASS labels come from the best-of-F sign-flip (ASIA a p=0.04, NY-AM a p=0.02, ASIA b p=0.045); PREOPEN / LONDON / NY-PM not cleared.
- No book trigger or filter and no bar type is alive in LONDON or NY-PM; CLOSE has no cell in the ledger (nothing to select).
- Depth is 10 levels = 2.25 pt: wall-distance targets stay untestable. I used sub-minute windows (15 s and 60 s) because the tape (per trade, with pre-trade best bid/ask and queue) and the 1-s book carry full 24-h coverage; book state is read at the entry second (t - 1 s for the book), not on the closed 60-s bar.

- **TB4B_tick_26_tick_250_ST3_SW62_T2**: T-PULLBACK family B, 250-tick bars; regime = Supertrend(10,3.0) on 3-min bars; pullback level = 62% retrace of the last-60-minute high/low; touch = bar low <= level (long) / high >= level (short), armed 600 s; trigger T2 = first non-touch bar closing beyond the high (low) of the touch bar; market on the bar-close tick; 60/60 bracket; flat 16:55; "26" is the index in a random sample, not a parameter. Code code/pb4/ (pb4_sig.py, pb4_cells.py). DEAD-IN-HOLD (stored HOLD -1,060/trade; MEASURED -9.0 pts).
- **TB4W_03_time_15s_ST3_E20_1_T2**: widening neighbour of the T-PB PASS cell TB4A_time_14_time_30s_ST5_E20_1_T2: 15-s bars, regime Supertrend(10,3) on 3-min bars, pullback level = EMA(20) of 1-min closes, trigger T2 (as above), 60/60, flat 16:55, mode 0.
