## 1. What the live sizer does (user answer from the DS7 code)
At the signal, take the last closed 5-minute bar (bars ending at or before the signal second). ER(20) = |close now - close 20 bars ago| / sum of the 20 bar-to-bar moves (efficiency ratio, 0 = pure chop, 1 = straight line). If ER >= 0.30 lots x1.5, otherwise lots x0.5; lots = max(1, floor(base x mult + 0.5)) with the base after session lots and the zone half; all six books, all sessions; day locks scale only when the multiplier is above 1. Reproduced by the engine exactly (0.0000 $ difference) and by S4b's independent recomputation on every row.

## 3. Is the ER feature sound? (Q4, Q5)
No look-ahead: 100 of 100 audited signals reproduce the closed-bar ER exactly (1 of 100 would match an ER that included the forming bar, 0 of 100 one that was a bar older), and on all 88,490 signals the bar used ends at or before the signal second. Boundary fragility: 7.6% of taken signals lie within +-0.02 of 0.30; +-0.02 of uniform noise would flip 2.0% of all signals (26% of those near the line); 13% change side if the forming bar were included and 15% if the ER were one bar older. The cliff at 0.30 is not where the money is: boundary trades (ER 0.27-0.33, 12% of signals) earn +$151 per trade just below and +$247 just above at equal lots; a smooth ladder (x0.5 to x1.5 linearly between 0.25 and 0.35) changes nothing material (L6M maxDD +4.1%, PRE -6.0%, mean +1.1k / -1.0k per week; at equal risk +0.8k / -0.3k).
Stale bars and gaps: bars are built only from minutes with prints, so a 20-bar window can span a gap. 773 taken signals (4.4%, all Asia: KB 402, HM 169, PE 172, PB 30) use a last closed bar older than 60 minutes (session open after a break or weekend); they average -$139 per trade (-$108k at the ladder's lots; at lots x1: L6M -$83k on 124 signals, PRE +$0.7k on 259, X' -$31k on 172, spread over 185 weeks and all four years). 2,667 taken signals (15%) have a 30-minute-plus gap inside the window and are the better trades (+$265 vs +$123 per trade). Fixing the 773 at x1.0 or x0.5 (describe only): L6M maxDD -11% / -18% but 0% after W29-W39 is dropped, PRE +4% / +0.4% worse, mean -1.2k / -0.4k per week in L6M (MDE 5.6k / 6.0k). Neutralising all gap windows (x1.0 or x0.5) or using only current-session bars (min 10) gave no flagged cell (Q5).
ER at the add: the 30% add fills a median 39 s (p90 12.6 min) after the signal; 46,261 signals have a fill. Re-evaluating ER at the add second (cancel if ER < 0.30 then, halve if ER fell by more than 0.1) cancels 61% of adds, cuts L6M maxDD 31.8% (8.5% with W29-W39 dropped) but PRE gets 5.1% worse, mean -0.5k / -2.1k per week; it keeps more dollars than random cancellations (rank 0.98 L6M, 0.87 PRE) and cuts less drawdown (rank 0.16 / 0.07). Not flagged.

## 4. What the x1.5 side earns and loses, by episode (BASE-4 anatomy, trade level; "at x1" = the same trades at lots without the ladder)
2026-07-20..09-11 (the 2026 drawdown, maxDD $170k): x1.5 trades (37% of trades) lost $141k and carried 63% of the gross loss; x0.5 trades broke even ($1k). At x1 the x1.5 trades would have lost $91k, so the 1.5 multiplier added -$50k; the ladder-off engine run over the same days lost $74k against $140k with the ladder. Roughly half of the loss is size, half is high-ER trades that lose even at x1 (selection that lots cannot fix).
2025-02-03..02-28 (the PRE drawdown, $161k): x1.5 trades (46% of trades) lost $201k, 77% of the gross loss; x0.5 trades earned +$37k. At x1 the x1.5 trades would have lost $128k (multiplier adds -$73k); ladder off -$62k vs -$165k with the ladder.
2024-08-01..2025-01-31 (a good stretch): +$429k with the ladder (ladder off +$435k; same trades at x1 +$392k); the x1.5 trades earned +$319k (at x1 +$189k, so the multiplier added +$131k) and the x0.5 trades +$110k (at x1 +$204k: the halving cost $94k).
The worst 30 trades of every episode are all PE at x1.5 (11 lots against 7 at x1), all with the split add filled and 27-30 of 30 ended by the TH-L2 loss cut.

## 5. What every test concluded (frame: T-SIZER base; Q1 also shows V13T_LIVE single rounding beside)

By the pre-registered rule the x1.5 side is "not supported" as a risk-adjusted add, but it does not lose money; A's drawdown benefit is mostly "less size" (random size-downs cut as much), A flagged under the user's rule.
Q2 (52 cells that keep the x1.5 but limit it: 3-tier ladders, lot caps, session/book limits, ER windows 14/20/30 on 3m/5m, PE-only changes, add held at x1 size): no cell keeps the mean at or above the ladder in both L6M and PRE (0 of 52). Only "x1.5 only for PE", "PE x1.5 only in asia", "PE x1.5 only in asia+nyam" have a small positive equal-risk gain; 3-tier with x1.5 only above ER 0.60 cuts L6M maxDD 55% and PRE 35% for -5.7k/-4.2k per week but is not better than scaling lots down.
Q3 (chance, placebos, walk-forward, stability): in 100 shuffled-day worlds the best x1.5-keeping cell reaches the real result 68% of the time, so those flags are size effects; the size-down-only family reaches its result in 7%. Holding the add at x1 size cuts more drawdown than random add changes but costs more mean (mean rank 0.09 / 0.02). Walk-forward on the cell choice picks mostly size-down cells (PRE maxDD -33% for -5.9k/wk out of sample). K_PE is the best of the x1.5-keeping cells at equal risk but beats the ladder on mean in only 6 of 17 PRE months.
