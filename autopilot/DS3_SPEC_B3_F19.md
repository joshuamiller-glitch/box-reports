# DS3_SPEC_B3_F19 - overnight dip-buy union basket NQ + ES + RTY (D3CH 2026-10-27). Research output for the Sim leader; NOT a live change. Tier: CANDIDATE (fails G4 only: dMaxDD sign in WF), recommended-for-Sim for measurability (3.4 trades/wk).
## 1. Rule (NT8, three 1-minute charts NQ, ES, RTY, ET)
Per instrument, each trading day take the OPEN of the 15:55 ET 1-min bar ("daily series" o_k, k = day index). Indicators on that series only:
* SMA200 = mean of the 200 previous daily opens o_{k-200..k-1} (excludes today).
* RSI(2), halving smoothing: avgUp/avgDown updated (prev + new)/2, first average = mean of the first two changes; RSI = 100 - 100/(1+avgUp/avgDown), 100 if avgDown = 0; the value for day k uses o_k.
* IBS = (o_k - L) / (H - L), H/L = high/low of the 1-min bars 09:30..15:54 of that ET date.
* 3-lower = o_k < o_{k-1} < o_{k-2} < o_{k-3}.
Signal (long only): o_k > SMA200 AND (RSI(2) < 20 OR 3-lower OR IBS < 0.20). Entry: market buy at the close of the 15:55 bar (about 15:56:00). Exit: market sell at the close of the 15:55 bar of the next trading day that has one (Friday -> Monday). No stop, no target. Skip the trade if the entry-to-exit span crosses a contract roll or is >= 5 days. One position per instrument (the hold is one day); the three instruments can fire the same day.
