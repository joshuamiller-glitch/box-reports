## 1. Rule (NT8, OnBarClose; ET wall clock; continuous back-adjusted NQ)
Cell parameters (sf_cells.py `crt()` defaults line 160): htf = 60, etf = -30, etype = 'close', inside = 0, buf = 0, stop = structural, tmode = 'SO' (scale-out), s = 1 tick, L = 3 (swing length, not used by etype close).

**1.1 Range candles (the H1 range).** H1 candles are CLOCK-ALIGNED in ET (sf_fam.candles: slot = floor(((ET minute of day of the 1-min bar open) - anchor) mod 1440 / 60), anchor 0, so :00/:00 of every hour); a new candle starts when the slot changes or the previous 1-min bar is >= 30 minutes older (data gap). Candle high/low = max high / min low of the 1-min bars with that candle id; nmin = bars present. **Range-candle validity ('ok'):** the candle must have nmin >= max(3, 0.2 x 60) = 12 one-minute bars AND the next candle must start no more than 90 minutes after its last bar. The range for the current candle is the immediately preceding candle in the data sequence (id - 1) and only if it is ok; PH = its high, PL = its low. There is no 50% level except as a target. 'Gap rule' = only this data-gap handling (30-min break starts a new candle; > 90 min after a candle voids it as a range); there is no price-gap (FVG) rule in the CRT cells.

**1.2 Sweep / confirmation bars.** Sweep bars are 30-second bars (see 1.1b). A bar belongs to the current candle by the candle id of its LAST 1-min bar.
**1.1b 30-second bars.** **30-second bars (library, l2vast/ds3/code/sf/sf_lib.py `_sec_bars`/`bars(tf)`, lines ~48-75).** Built from the 1-second tape (one record per second that had a trade; Databento NQ trades, back-adjusted continuous front contract): bucket = epoch_second // 30 (clock-aligned, identical in UTC and ET); open = the PREVIOUS bar's close (the very first bar uses the first record's close), NOT the first print of the bucket; high/low = max/min of the 1-s highs/lows; close = last 1-s close; a bucket with no trade record does not exist (no padding, no flat bars); completion time tcomp = (bucket+1)*30 s and the bar is used only from the next 1-s record (as-of, no look-ahead). **Reproduction in NT8: NOT established.** The repo docs only declare 'NT8-native Second bars (BarsPeriodType.Second, Value 15 or 30), OnBarClose' (prereg/TP4_TB4A_time.md flag; DIRECTIVE_DS3.md: 'NT8 supports second and tick bars natively') and state that the identity test against a real NT8 export is PENDING (prereg TP4_*: 'implemented by D3BAR from documentation; identity test against a real NT8 export is pending (D3X)'). No repo document shows that the Rithmic feed builds 30-s bars equal to these. Known structural differences to test on the user side: (a) library open = previous close, NT8 open = first tick; (b) NT8 closes a time bar on the first tick of the next bucket (or a timer) whereas the library knows it at the bucket end; (c) tick content (Databento trade records vs Rithmic Last ticks, repo FVG_NT8_SPEC section 14 marks this [UNVERIFIED]). Because the gap test below uses highs/lows only, (a) matters only through the as-of order timing, but (b) and (c) can shift gap/inversion bars by one bar. **Identity check required on the user side before any use: export 3 months of NT8 30-s OHLC (ET) and compare bar count, H/L per bucket against the 1-s-tape bars; accept only if >= 99% of buckets match in H and L.**

**1.3 Sweep with close back inside (k_crt, per side, one setup per side per candle).** Evaluated on every completed sweep bar of the current candle while the side has not yet triggered and the range candle is ok. LONG (sweep of PL): low < PL - 0.25 (more than 1 tick below) AND PL < close < PH (close strictly back inside the range) AND high <= PH + 0.25. SHORT (sweep of PH): high > PH + 0.25 AND PL < close < PH AND low >= PL - 0.25. The first bar that satisfies all of it is the setup; earlier bars that wick beyond the extreme but close outside or on it do not disarm the side. A bar with low < PL - 0.25 AND high > PH + 0.25 that is not a valid sweep marks that side as used for the rest of the candle (no setup). A long and a short setup can occur in the same candle. **'_ib' variant: OFF (inside=0).** No condition on the earlier bars of the current candle: the first bar that satisfies the sweep test is the setup, even if the candle had already traded outside the previous range on earlier bars (the earlier bar then must have failed the sweep test, e.g. by closing outside or taking both extremes).

**1.4 Entry (etype 'close').** MARKET order at the close of the sweep bar: assumed price E = the sweep bar's close, filled by the library at E at the first 1-s record after the bar completes (kend+1); NT8 will fill at the market after the bar close event (slippage = the live-latency question). Signals are ignored when the minute after the bar falls in the blocked minutes (the library gate: entry minute allowed unless the previous minute opens in 15:00-17:59 ET); the lock layer additionally refuses 18:00-18:05 and 15:00-16:54 entries (irrelevant for LONDON). One trade at a time per book: a setup whose entry minute is not after the open trade's exit minute is dropped, not queued.

**1.5 Stop and targets (ticks = 0.25 pt; all prices rounded to the 0.25 grid with round-half-even on price x 4).** Stop = the sweep bar's own extreme S (long: its low; short: its high), buffer 0 (no extra tick), stop-market; fills at the stop or at the previous 1-s close if that is worse. mid = 0.5 x (PL + PH). If the 50% level is more than 4.0 pt beyond E (long: mid > E + 4.0): SCALE-OUT: TP1 = mid (50% of the position), TP2 = PH (long) / PL (short) (other 50%); otherwise a single target at PH / PL on the whole position. Both targets are limit orders placed at entry (OCO with the stop; the stop covers the full size until TP1 fills; TP2 tested only from the second after TP1). **After TP1 the stop is NOT moved** (stays at S for the remaining half). Risk cap (sf_lib.apply_risk_cap): the setup is dropped unless 4.0 <= |E - stop| <= 60.0 pt and the first target is at least 4.0 pt beyond E. With one contract the 50/50 split is impossible: the library prices a fractional 0.5/0.5 position; a 1-lot account must choose (open item (c)).

**1.6 Flat rule.** Everything open is closed at market at the close of the 1-min bar opening 16:54 ET (16:55 stamp; early-close days the last bar); a LONDON trade almost never reaches it (median hold minutes).

**1.7 Session window (ET).** LONDON: entries 02:00-08:30 ET by entry time (sweep bar complete in that window; ledger entry stamp = end stamp of the entry minute). No entries outside it; exits run to bracket.
