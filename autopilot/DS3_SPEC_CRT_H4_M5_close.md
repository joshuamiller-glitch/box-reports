## 1. Rule (NT8, OnBarClose on a 5-minute series built from 1-minute bars; time = ET wall clock)
All inputs are 1-minute NQ bars of the continuous front contract (library: volume-roll series; the replay used the back-adjusted Databento series, section 2). "Bar open time" below means the open of the 1-minute bar; NT8 stamps bars with their END time (+1 minute).

**1.1 Five-minute bars (entry timeframe).** Bucket = floor(epoch minute / 5), i.e. clock-aligned :00/:05/... (identical in UTC and ET). A bucket is built from the 1-minute bars PRESENT in it (no padding of missing minutes). OHLC from those bars. The bar is complete when its last present 1-minute bar has closed; all tests below use only that completed bar (OnBarClose).

**1.2 H4 candles (the range timeframe), clock-aligned in America/New_York.** For every 1-minute bar: slot = ((ET minute-of-day of the bar open) - 60) mod 1440, integer-divided by 240. Slots therefore start 01:00, 05:00, 09:00, 13:00, 17:00, 21:00 ET (the directive's 01/05/09/13/17/21). A new H4 candle starts at a bar when (a) its slot differs from the previous bar's slot, or (b) the previous bar opened 30 or more minutes earlier (data gap). Candle high/low = max high / min low of all 1-minute bars with that candle id; nmin = bars present. The computation uses the ET wall clock of each bar, so it is DST-safe by construction and no special DST code is needed: NQ is closed at the Sunday 02:00 ET change (and the 2026-11-01 change), the slot arithmetic only needs the wall-clock minute. Session structure that follows: the 17:00-21:00 candle contains only 18:00-20:59 (3 h of bars; the 17:00-17:59 break has no bars), the 13:00-17:00 candle holds 13:00-16:59, the Sunday evening 17:00 candle starts at the 18:00 open, and a candle can be longer than 4 h only through the 25-hour fall-back day (not present in market hours).
- "Previous completed HTF candle" = the candle with id (current id - 1) in the sequence of candles that exist in the data (not "the clock slot 4 hours earlier"). It qualifies as a range (ok) only if it has at least 48 one-minute bars (0.2 x 240) AND the next candle's first bar opens at most 90 minutes after its last bar. So: a candle cut short by an early close (fewer than 48 bars) is never a range; the Friday 13:00 candle is not a range for the Sunday-open candle (gap of two days), nor is any candle followed by a holiday gap above 90 minutes; the daily 17:00-18:00 break (61 minutes) is allowed. The current candle may be partial (e.g. the first candle after Sunday open or after a holiday) and still trades if the range candle qualifies.
- Range: PH = previous candle high, PL = previous candle low. There is no use of the 50% level other than the target (1.5).

**1.3 Sweep (per side, per candle).** Evaluated on every completed 5-minute bar whose candle id (taken from its LAST 1-minute bar) is the current candle, while that side has not yet swept in this candle and the range is ok. Long setup (sweep of PL): Low < PL - 0.25 AND PL < Close < PH (close back strictly inside the range) AND High <= PH + 0.25. Short setup (sweep of PH): High > PH + 0.25 AND PL < Close < PH AND Low >= PL - 0.25. So the wick must exceed the extreme by more than one tick (on the tick grid: at least 2 ticks), the same 5-minute bar must close back inside, and the bar may not also take the opposite extreme by more than one tick. The sweep bar may be the first 5-minute bar of the candle. Earlier bars of the candle that wicked beyond the extreme but CLOSED beyond or on it do not disarm the side (no failed-close invalidation); the first bar that satisfies all three conditions is the sweep. A bar with Low < PL - 0.25 AND High > PH + 0.25 (both extremes) that is not itself a valid sweep marks that side as used for the rest of the candle (no setup). One setup per side per candle; a long and a short setup can occur in the same candle. The inside-bar variant (rh <= PH and rl >= PL before the sweep) is NOT part of this cell (inside = 0).

**1.4 Entry.** Market order at the close of the sweep bar, price assumed = the 5-minute bar's Close E (the library fills at E on the 1-second path from the first second after the bar; the +0.5 pt P9 friction in section 6 is what stands for the real market-order slippage). Signals are dropped when the sweep bar's LAST 1-minute bar opens 15:00-17:59 ET. One trade at a time: a setup whose entry minute (the minute after the sweep bar's last minute) is not later than the exit minute of the open trade is DROPPED, not queued. The lock engine additionally refuses entries whose entry-bar stamp (open + 1 min) is 15:00-16:54 or 18:00-18:04 ET (in practice sweep bars completing at 15:00..16:54; the raw list contains them, 3,617 raw vs 3,516 after locks/windows through 2026-10-23).

**1.5 Stop and targets (prices rounded to the tick grid with numpy round-half-to-even on price x 4, i.e. a .125 midpoint goes to the even tick count).** S = Low (long) / High (short) of the sweep bar itself (not the lowest since the candle began, not a later wick); stop = S exactly, buffer 0, no extra tick. mid = 0.5 x (PL + PH). Long: if mid > E + 4.0 (unrounded) the order is a SCALE-OUT: TP1 = mid (50% of the position), TP2 = PH (the other 50%); otherwise it is a single target PH on the whole position (this happens when the close is already within 4 points of the midpoint or beyond it). Short mirrored (TP1 = mid, TP2 = PL if mid < E - 4). Risk filter (D3SF): the setup is skipped (and does not block anything) unless 4.0 <= |E - stop| <= 60.0 and the first target is at least 4.0 beyond E. Median stop 12.5 pt (quartiles 7.5 / 22, 10-90% 5.25 / 34); median first target distance 36 pt. Order types: stop = stop-market child, TP1 and TP2 = limit orders placed at entry together with the stop (OCO group; the stop covers the full size until TP1 fills). Fill rules (library, 1-second path): stop fills when the 1-second low (high for shorts) touches the stop price; at a gap through the stop the fill is the previous second's close when that is beyond the stop; targets fill on touch at their price; within one second the stop is tested first; TP2 is tested only from the second after TP1 filled.
- **What the stop does after TP1: NOTHING. The stop stays at the original sweep-extreme price for the remaining 50% (no breakeven, no trail; mode 1 in sf_lib.run_setups; the stop price is re-used, only its quantity falls to half).** In NT8: after TP1 fills, change the stop quantity to the remaining half at the same price. Sensitivity (stop to the entry price after TP1, same entries): library list 3,633 raw trades (16 more because trades end earlier), mapped WF +7,225 $/wk (vs 7,371), HOLD 7,800 (8,166), span 2023 +1,077 (-995); conservative WF -2,087 (-1,627), HOLD -5,093 (-4,219), span +763 (-1,293). The variant changes the mapped WF by -2% and the conclusion not at all. Independent replay of the BE variant: 0.55% entry / 1.69% full / 1.83% tolerant mismatch (x_crt_identity_be.json).
- Exit type recorded for a scale-out is the type of the LAST leg (TP1 then stop = ST_Stop with a mixed P&L); the mapping uses that type.

**1.6 Flat time.** Everything open is closed at market at the close of the 1-minute bar opening 16:54 ET (16:55 stamp; early-close days: the last bar of the session), applying only to trades alive before 18:00; a trade opened 18:00-23:59 or 00:00-15:00 runs to its stop, target or the next 16:55. Median hold 16 min, 75% < 57 min, 83 of 3,617 trades (2.3%) reach the flat.

**1.7 Sessions and mix.** All hours with a qualifying 5-minute close: by entry-bar hour (locked trades) 09:xx ET 17%, 01-02 ET 13%, 05 ET 8%, 21 ET 9%, rest spread; exits: 81% stop, 16% final target, 2% flat. Long 48% / short 52% of 3,617; exit counts stop 2,942 / target 592 / time 83. Raw 18.9 trades per week, 18.3 after locks in WF.

**1.8 Lots and locks (as scored).** FIXED 10 lots for every trade regardless of the stop width (prereg: "locks run at 10 lots whatever the stop width"); scale-out quantities 5 + 5. The lock engine is the exact per-book engine at 10 lots with R fixed at 10 x 60 x $20 = $12,000 (te_core.spec_for(10)): day -2R = -$24,000, +5R = +$60,000 (entry-time test on realised net of trades closed before the entry), give-back arm +2R with 50% kept, week floor -12R = -$144,000. These are NOT scaled by the cell's actual risk (R = lots x stop x $20 would be $0.8k-$12k, mean $3.3k per trade): with a median stop of 12.5 pt the locks almost never bind (3,617 -> 3,516 trades, mostly the entry windows). Risk-based lots (12,000/(stop x 20), the PB3 convention) were NOT scored for this cell; at such lots the 4-pt stops would need 75 lots, far above the 50-lot cap, so the cell as scored is the only version with a defined P&L. The equal-risk line of the track (21.2 lots to match ST10's weekly SD) is a weekly-SD scaling, not a per-trade rule.

**1.9 Warm-up.** Only one completed H4 candle and the 1-minute history for the 5-minute buckets are needed; there is no recursive indicator. Recommended NT8 setting: load at least 5 trading days of 1-minute history (so a range candle exists); the measured sensitivity is zero (section 2).

## 3. Data, roll, holidays
Continuous front NQ; H4 highs/lows and the sweep test run on the continuous series, so the roll treatment matters (the 28-in-40 roll-day residual above). Holidays/half days: no bars -> a candle with fewer than 48 bars is not a range; a gap above 90 minutes after a candle voids it as a range. NT8 note: use ONE continuous series (merge back-adjusted) for the 1-minute data and build the 5-minute and H4 aggregates in code from it; do NOT use NT8's own 240-minute bar type, whose bars are anchored to the session start (18:00) and would produce 18/22/02/06/10/14 boundaries, not 01/05/09/13/17/21.

## 4. NT8 implementation notes (UNTESTED in NT8)
- Aggregate 5-minute bars from the 1-minute bars inside one OnBarUpdate (complete when the 1-minute bar ending on :00/:05 closes) and compute the H4 candle id from the ET minute-of-day as in 1.2.
- Evaluate both sides at every 5-minute close; entry = market order on that bar's close (SubmitOrder at OnBarClose), then stop-market at S and limit TP1/TP2 once filled (or an ATM strategy with 2 targets and the same stop); on TP1 fill reduce the stop quantity and leave the price.
- Reject the setup in code if |E - S| < 4 or > 60 or the first target is under 4 points away; reject if the sweep bar's last minute opens 15:00-17:59; ignore setups while a trade is open.
- Flat at 16:54 bar close.

## 7. What could kill it
- Pricing basis (the decisive risk): +9.0k of the 7.4k WF mean is the stop-exit credit calibrated on 60-pt stops; without it WF is -1.6k, HOLD -4.2k. The cell needs a direct or tick-level validation of stop exits on 5-25 pt stops before any weight.
- Entry fill: the cell assumes a fill at the 5-minute close; a market order at the bar close fills at the next trade (often 1-2 ticks away) and the +0.5 pt friction is the only stand-in. At a 12.5-pt median stop each extra point of slippage costs about 8% of the stop; 18 trades/wk x $200 = $3.6k/wk per point.
- Selection: one of 207 D3SF cells; the neighbours CRT_H4_M15_close is DEAD (placebo 0.90), H1/M30/M15 and the CSD/limit entries are DEAD; the offset placebo is only 0.90 in WF; '+11% per protocol' claim not supported (win rate by protocol count 0.354/0.333/0.309/0.328).
- Hit rate 27%: the cell relies on the 50-pt tail (592 final targets and 83 flats carry all the profit); one bad year can remove it; the 2023 span is negative on both bases.
- Rule details that must be reproduced exactly: clock-aligned 01/05/09/13/17/21 candles (not NT8's 18:00-anchored H4), range-candle qualification (>= 48 bars, gap <= 90 min), sweep = first bar with wick > 1 tick AND close strictly inside AND no opposite extreme, stop = sweep-bar extreme (not buffered), mid > E + 4 test for the scale-out, risk cap 4..60, stop NOT moved after TP1, signals dropped while a trade is open.
- Roll days (28 of the 40 entry mismatches): the live chart's adjustment differs again.
- Concurrency/locks: locks not re-derived; fixed-R locks do not protect a 60-pt-stop trade at 10 lots beyond the usual.

## 9. Recommendation
Do NOT add to the stack on this evidence. On the plain mapped basis the cell looks excellent (WF +7.4k/wk at SD 27k, add-to-stack Sharpe +0.55), but that basis credits +4 pts to every stop exit on stops that are 4-34 pts wide, and the same series without that credit loses money in WF and HOLD (-1.6k, -4.2k) and is DEAD. The recommendation rests on the conservative basis. What would change it: a tick-level or live stop-exit test on short stops that measures the real stop-exit correction (break-even needs 18% of the measured +4 pts in WF, 34% in HOLD), plus a market-fill test at the 5-minute close. As a forward item it can stay on the research register (already in paper_all) as a failure monitor only.
