## 1. Rule (NT8; library code l2vast/ds3/code/ic/ic_cells.py line 28-29 (`fv(...,'ifvg',tf=-30,entry='b')`), ic_fam.py `fvg_rows`/`assemble`/`cap`, ic_kern.py `k_gaps`/`k_inv`/`k_zone_retest`/`k_singular`, ic_lib.py `levels`/`nearest_levels`/`run_setups2`)
Cell parameters (ic_cells.py BASE line 15 + overrides): tf = -30 (30-s bars), size = 8 ticks (2.00 pt), sing = 10, entry = 'b', stop = 'far5', tgt = 'fb', dlook = 120 (delivery flag only; not used by this cell's trade rule), kind = iFVG.

**1.1 Bars.** **30-second bars (library, l2vast/ds3/code/sf/sf_lib.py `_sec_bars`/`bars(tf)`, lines ~48-75).** Built from the 1-second tape (one record per second that had a trade; Databento NQ trades, back-adjusted continuous front contract): bucket = epoch_second // 30 (clock-aligned, identical in UTC and ET); open = the PREVIOUS bar's close (the very first bar uses the first record's close), NOT the first print of the bucket; high/low = max/min of the 1-s highs/lows; close = last 1-s close; a bucket with no trade record does not exist (no padding, no flat bars); completion time tcomp = (bucket+1)*30 s and the bar is used only from the next 1-s record (as-of, no look-ahead). **Reproduction in NT8: NOT established.** The repo docs only declare 'NT8-native Second bars (BarsPeriodType.Second, Value 15 or 30), OnBarClose' (prereg/TP4_TB4A_time.md flag; DIRECTIVE_DS3.md: 'NT8 supports second and tick bars natively') and state that the identity test against a real NT8 export is PENDING (prereg TP4_*: 'implemented by D3BAR from documentation; identity test against a real NT8 export is pending (D3X)'). No repo document shows that the Rithmic feed builds 30-s bars equal to these. Known structural differences to test on the user side: (a) library open = previous close, NT8 open = first tick; (b) NT8 closes a time bar on the first tick of the next bucket (or a timer) whereas the library knows it at the bucket end; (c) tick content (Databento trade records vs Rithmic Last ticks, repo FVG_NT8_SPEC section 14 marks this [UNVERIFIED]). Because the gap test below uses highs/lows only, (a) matters only through the as-of order timing, but (b) and (c) can shift gap/inversion bars by one bar. **Identity check required on the user side before any use: export 3 months of NT8 30-s OHLC (ET) and compare bar count, H/L per bucket against the 1-s-tape bars; accept only if >= 99% of buckets match in H and L.**

**1.2 FVG definition (ic_kern.k_gaps).** Gap on HIGH/LOW (wicks), not bodies, and the middle bar does not enter the test. Bullish gap completed at bar j (j >= 2): low[j] - high[j-2] >= 8 ticks (2.00 pt; compared with a 1e-9 tolerance), zone = [high[j-2], low[j]]. Bearish gap at bar j: low[j-2] - high[j] >= 8 ticks, zone = [high[j], low[j-2]]. The gap is known when bar j completes. Minimum gap 8 ticks; no maximum, no ATR scaling.

**1.3 Inverse FVG (iFVG) definition (ic_kern.k_inv; ic_fam.zones/zta).** Long trade (kernel coordinates; shorts are the same kernel on the mirrored series o'=-o, h'=-l, l'=-h, c'=-c): take every BEARISH gap [glo, ghi] (zone = [high[j], low[j-2]]). It is INVERTED at the first bar t > j, with tcomp[t] <= tcomp[j] + invw, whose CLOSE is above ghi (close above the top of the bearish gap; wicks do not count, the close must be beyond the far edge). invw = max(3600, 8 x bar length) = 3600 s. If no such close occurs inside invw the gap is discarded. nin = number of bars strictly between j and t with close >= glo (not used by the trade rule). The inverted bearish gap is now a SUPPORT zone [glo, ghi] for a LONG; a short uses a bullish gap that closes inverted below its low (mirror). Both directions are traded; direction = the inversion direction, no trend filter.

**1.4 Singular filter (sing = 10; ic_kern.k_singular, ic_fam.union_gaps).** Window = 10 bars x 30 s = 300 s. The gap is kept only if NO other gap of the same bar set (bullish or bearish, >= 8 ticks, any orientation) completed strictly earlier than this gap within the preceding 300 s overlaps its price range [glo, ghi] (overlap = other.lo <= ghi and other.hi >= glo). Gaps are compared at their completion times (tcomp of the gap bar j); the check is not delayed to the inversion.

**1.5 Entry order (entry 'b', ic_kern.k_zone_retest mode 1).** A RESTING BUY LIMIT (sell limit for shorts) at the gap MIDPOINT 0.5 x (glo + ghi), placed when the inversion bar t completes (live from the next 1-s record, kend[t]+1). One order per inverted gap. It is CANCELLED when price trades below glo (long; 1-s low < glo, i.e. any trade through the far edge before a fill) and it EXPIRES retw = max(3600, 4 x bar length) = 3600 s after the inversion bar completed. 'Retest' = the first trade through the limit: fill when the 1-s low is strictly below the limit price (long; high strictly above for shorts) - a trade-through, a touch at the price is not enough in the library; fill price = the limit price. Fills only in minutes whose previous minute is not in 15:00-17:59 ET. Order prices and stop/target are rounded to the 0.25 grid.

**1.6 Stop (stop 'far5').** Long: far edge minus 5.0 pt = glo - 5.00 (short: ghi + 5.00). Stop distance d = |limit - stop| = half the gap + 5 pt, so the minimum is 6.0 pt (gap 2.00 pt) and the median is 6.5 (30-s ASIA) / 6.75 (NY-AM 15-s) pt (bracket quantiles 5/25/50/75/95 % in section 4). Stop-market; fills at the stop price, or at the previous 1-s close when that is worse (gap through the stop).

**1.7 Target (tgt 'fb'; ic_fam.assemble, ic_lib.nearest_levels).** 'Nearest liquidity' = the NEAREST ALIVE liquidity level beyond the limit price in the trade direction by at least max(d, 2.0) pt (i.e. at least 1R), at the decision minute md = the last completed 1-min bar at the inversion bar's completion time. Level classes (ic_lib.ALL_LIQ = EQ4 | I5 | ASIA | LON | NY | PD | TL): high-type levels for longs, low-type for shorts. (a) EQ4: two 5-min fractal swings (L = 3, strict inequality both sides, confirmed 3 bars later), same type, within 4 ticks, the later one within the previous 12 swings of the same type, with no 1-min bar between them exceeding the level; level = the higher of two highs / lower of two lows; formed at the later swing's confirmation. (b) I5: every 5-min L = 5 fractal swing high/low, from its confirmation. (c) ASIA / LON / NY: high and low of the 18:00-02:00, 02:00-09:30 and 09:30-17:00 ET sessions (labels from the ET minute of day), formed at the session's last 1-min bar. (d) PD: previous label-day (18:00 to 17:00) high and low from the end of that period. (e) TL: trendline through three consecutive confirmed 5-min L = 3 swing lows (highs) at least 15 min apart, third within 4 ticks of the line through the first two; value extrapolated per 1-min bar. A level is alive until a 1-min bar trades through it (high > level for highs, low < level for lows; trendlines with a 1-tick tolerance); search limits: 6,000 levels back, age <= 4 days (5,760 min), distance <= 250 pt. The nearest alive level is the single target (limit order, full size, mode 0, no scale-out). If NO level qualifies: fallback target = entry + 2 x d (2R). Order-block levels are not used. Equal highs/lows/sessions are used as defined above even when they are only a few points beyond 1R.

**1.8 Risk cap (ic_fam.cap).** After tick rounding the setup is dropped unless 2.0 <= d <= 60.0 pt and the first target is >= 2.0 pt beyond entry.

**1.9 Position logic (ic_lib.run_setups2).** One position at a time. Every setup is an independent working order; a setup is rejected if a trade is open when it is placed or when it fills. Exits on the 1-s path: stop first inside a second; target = limit filled when the 1-s high reaches it (long), tested only from the second AFTER the fill second; everything open is closed at market at the close of the 1-min bar that opens at the first blocked minute (16:55 ET flat; early-close days at the last bar). Median hold is short (minutes); trades entered in a session may run past the session window to stop/target/16:55.

**1.10 Session window and the library lock layer.** The stored rows additionally pass the DS3 lock engine: entries stamped 18:00-18:05 and 15:00-16:54 ET are refused (first stored ASIA entry is 18:05 or later), signals in 15:00-17:59 blocked, and the book-level day/week locks at R = 10 x 60 x $20 = $12,000 (-2R/+5R/arm 2R keep 50%/week floor -12R). With a 6-8 pt stop these dollar locks almost never bind, so the session container should apply its OWN locks scaled to $1,000 R (not specified here; D6 decision).

**1.11 Session window(s) (ET, by ENTRY/fill time) and flat rule.** ASIA: entries 18:05-02:00 ET (fill stamp 18:00-02:00; the lock layer refuses 18:00-18:05); PREOPEN: entries 08:30-09:30 ET (two separate session entries of the same cell). No entries outside the window(s); a position opened inside the window runs to its stop/target or the 16:55 ET flat (the library has no session-end flat; D6 to decide whether to add one - not tested). Pending limits placed inside the window but filling after it close are ignored by the filter in the ledger (the filter is on the fill stamp); live, cancel pending limits at the window end to match.

**1.12 Order types summary (NT8).** Entry: BUY/SELL LIMIT at the midpoint (limit orders; fill = trade-through; NT8 fills a limit when price trades AT it, so live fills will be earlier/more frequent than the library - queue priority not modelled). On fill: STOP-MARKET at far edge -/+ 5.00 pt and LIMIT target (OCO). Time flat: market at 16:55 ET.

## 7. What could kill it
- **Mapped-fill dependence (the main risk).** iFVG stop-out rows (stored mapped basis) lose, beyond the stop distance, +0.50 pt (2023), -0.79 (2024), -3.88 (2025), -4.08 (2026) in ASIA and +0.43 / -0.67 / -3.86 / -3.94 in PREOPEN, i.e. from 2025 a 6.5-pt stop-out is booked at about 2.6 pt. The mapped stop credit was measured on 60-pt brackets (stop-join gains) and is applied unchanged to 6-7 pt stops; at live latency with a stop-market on a 6-pt stop the credit is unlikely to exist. D3SA's MEASURED basis decides; until then treat the stored $ as an upper bound.
- **Limit fills.** The library fills on a strict trade-through and never models queue position; midpoint limits at a tight gap fill mostly when price is moving through, i.e. adverse selection. A touch-fill in NT8 will add trades the library did not have.
- **2023 span negative** (-232 $/trade; 2023 mean -234). The cell earns from 2024: a regime-dependent edge or a bar-construction artefact; no gate was applied to 2023 by rule, but the sign flip is the contrary-year evidence.
- **Bar-type identity.** The gap, inversion and singular tests run on 30-s bars whose open is the previous close and which skip empty seconds; NT8 30-s bars differ (1.1). A one-bar shift in a 2-pt gap test changes which gaps exist.
- **Slippage break-even** is only 1.74 pt/trade (PRE) / 1.94 pt/trade (HOLD); a 1-tick stop-market slip plus a 1-tick limit-target shortfall is 0.5 pt.
- **Multiplicity:** best of 3,098 cells x 6 sessions (F = 18,588; F_eff = 7,230); best-of-F label BOF_Feff:clear (ASIA). Cluster 267, 94 clusters alive.
- Liquidity levels depend on a long state (EQ4 pairs, trendlines, sessions): warm-up of at least 5 trading days of 1-min/5-min history is needed; not measured.
