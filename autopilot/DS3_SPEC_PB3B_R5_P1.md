## 1. Rule (NT8, OnBarClose on a 1-minute NQ series)
All inputs are back-adjusted NQ 1-minute bars (MergeBackAdjusted). Time is ET; NT8 stamps bars with their END time, the code below says "bar open time" where the library tests the start. "Complete" aggregate: a 5-minute bucket whose last 1-minute bar has closed. Buckets are clock-aligned on :00/:05/:10 ... ET (identical to the 18:00 ET session anchor).

**1.1 Regime (direction d), 5-minute Supertrend, evaluated at every 1-minute bar from the latest COMPLETE 5-minute bar:**
- Series: all 5-minute bars of the continuous series, running across the 17:00-18:00 break and weekends without reset. Median price hl2 = (H+L)/2.
- ATR (length 10, recursion with first-bar seed, NOT Wilder SMA seed): ATR[0] = H[0]-L[0]; ATR[i] = (ATR[i-1]*9 + TR[i])/10, TR = max(H-L, |H-C[i-1]|, |L-C[i-1]|).
- Bands: ub = hl2 + 3.0*ATR, lb = hl2 - 3.0*ATR; fub[0] = hl2[0]+3*ATR[0], flb[0] = hl2[0]-3*ATR[0]; for i>0: fub[i] = ub if (ub < fub[i-1] or Close[i-1] > fub[i-1]) else fub[i-1]; flb[i] = lb if (lb > flb[i-1] or Close[i-1] < flb[i-1]) else flb[i-1].
- Direction (flip rule uses the CURRENT bar's band, as tested; this is not the textbook test and not an NT8 library indicator): d[0] = +1; for i>0: if d == +1 and Close[i] < flb[i] then d = -1; if d == -1 and Close[i] > fub[i] then d = +1.
- As-of rule on the 1-minute axis: the 5-minute bar covering minutes :00-:04 (bar-open times) is usable from the 1-minute bar whose open time is :04 (stamped :05), i.e. on the same 1-minute bar that completes it; if that last 1-minute bar is missing it is usable from the next 1-minute bar. Before the first complete 5-minute bar d = 0 (no trade). The same 1-minute bar index therefore sees the regime INCLUDING the 5-minute bar that just closed.
- d = +1 permits longs only; d = -1 permits shorts only.

**1.2 EMA20 (1-minute closes):** alpha = 2/21; EMA[0] = Close[0] (first bar of the series), EMA[i] = alpha*Close[i] + (1-alpha)*EMA[i-1] (this is the NT8 EMA(20) with its first-bar seed). Computed on every 1-minute bar of the continuous series (the value of bar i includes Close[i]). Shorts use the same EMA of the true prices.

**1.3 Touch (arming), evaluated at each completed 1-minute bar i while NOT armed and d != 0:** Long (d == +1): Low[i] <= EMA20[i] AND Close[i-1] > EMA20[i-1] (edge condition: the previous close was above the previous EMA). Short (d == -1): High[i] >= EMA20[i] AND Close[i-1] < EMA20[i-1]. A touch arms the setup on bar i: side = d, arm time = open time of bar i, reference = High[i] (long) / Low[i] (short) (the arming bar's own extreme, fixed). The pullback extreme (lowest low since arming; used only for stop variants not in this cell) is tracked but not used by the scored bracket.

**1.4 Armed state, trigger and re-arm (per 1-minute bar j, in this order):**
1. If the previous 1-minute bar was more than 90 minutes earlier (data gap) -> disarm.
2. If armed and (open time of j minus arm time > 30 minutes, or d[j] != armed side) -> disarm, and processing of bar j continues as "not armed" (so bar j can arm a new setup if it satisfies 1.3 with the new d).
3. If armed and still valid (j after the arming bar, within 30 minutes, same regime side): trigger if High[j] > reference (long) / Low[j] < reference (short). On trigger: signal bar = j, disarm, and bar j does NOT evaluate arming (the next bar may). If not triggered, touches while armed are ignored (no extension, no second touch, the 30-minute window is not refreshed, the reference is not moved).
4. Arming window = the arming bar and the following 30 minutes of bar open times; the trigger can fire on arm+1 .. arm+30 minutes.
5. One entry per arming; after a trigger or an expiry the next touch arms again. The setup runs independently of whether a trade is open (signals while in a trade are dropped by the busy rule in 1.6, not queued).

**1.5 Invalidation:** regime change (including to 0) disarms at the first bar where d differs; expiry after 30 minutes; data gap > 90 minutes. There is no price-based invalidation (a close below the EMA, a new low or a break of the regime band does not disarm by itself).

**1.6 Entry:** market order at the OPEN of the 1-minute bar after the signal bar (signal bar j, entry bar j+1, which must exist and be exactly 1 minute later). Signal bars whose OPEN time is 15:00-17:59 ET are ignored (the signal-time block; the setup itself keeps running through these hours); there is no block for signals during the overnight session. One open trade at a time in the book: a signal whose entry bar is <= the exit bar of the open trade is dropped (not queued). The lock engine additionally blocks entries stamped 15:00-16:54 and 18:00-18:04 ET (entry-bar end time; in the raw list 195 of 8,339 entries are removed by these windows). 10 lots as 10 x 1-lot market slices 2 s apart in the direct pricing (S10x1_g2), the same convention as the other DS3 books.

**1.7 Bracket (DS2 bracket, fixed 60/60; read from the code: stopm '60', tgtm '1R'):** stop-market 60 pt and limit target 60 pt (target/stop ratio 1.0) OCO from the entry (bar-open) price; stop exits worked as touch-join for 3 s then market (XJ3, P9 mechanism, unvalidated live). Flat at 16:55 ET (market at the close of the 1-minute bar ending 16:55, i.e. the bar opening 16:54; early-close days at the final bar). The 16:55 flat applies only to trades alive before 18:00: a trade entered 18:00-23:59 or 00:00-15:00 runs until the target, the stop or the next 16:55. Replay convention if both levels fall in one 1-minute bar: the stop is taken (the library resolves it on the 1-second tape; this is the main source of the residual in section 2).

**1.8 Sessions / mix:** all hours; by entry-bar end hour the 8,339 library entries are 6.1% 00-02 ET, 21.3% 03-08, 10.1% 09, 39.6% 10-14, 0.2% 15-17 and 22.7% 18-23 ET. Long/short 49.6% / 50.4%. Exits 49.1% target, 46.7% stop, 4.3% flat (16:55); median hold 50 min. Raw trades 43.2 per week; after the lock engine 27.3 per week in WF (4,898 trades in the series).

**1.9 Lots / dollars (the lots rule as scored):** risk-based lots = clip(round(12,000 / (stop_pts * 20)), 1, 20) (pb3_core.lots_of, mode 'R', used by run_T in pb3_dfinal). The stop is constant 60 pt for this cell, so lots = clip(round(12,000/1,200),1,20) = **10 lots for every trade**; the cell as scored is therefore identical to fixed 10 lots, R = 10 x 60 x $20 = $12,000 (engine size = lots/14). Locks (exact 1-s mark-to-market engine, firmlib.Ctx.run with te_core.spec_for(10)): per-book day -2R = -$24,000 and +5R = +$60,000 (entry-time test on the day's realised net of trades closed before the entry; a new entry is skipped once breached), give-back arm +2R = $24,000 with 50% kept (1-s clock), week floor -12R = -$144,000, combined day cap off. At the 14/14 scale (R = $16,800): -$33,600 / +$84,000 / arm $33,600 / week floor -$201,600. The lock engine cuts the raw 43.2 trades/wk to 27.3 in WF (19.1 in the span window).

**1.10 Warm-up:** the library indicators run from the start of its price file (first events 2023-02-08, two days before the first counted trade 2023-02-10); the replay from 2022-06-01. The rule needs a first complete 5-minute bar and 21 bars of EMA history; the Supertrend ratchet bands have a memory of days, but the measured sensitivity is zero (section 2). Recommended NT8 setting: load at least 30 days of 1-minute history; do NOT start the chart on the day.

## 3. Data, roll, holidays
Continuous front NQ, back-adjusted; the 5-minute Supertrend and the EMA20 run on adjusted prices over the whole history, so adjusted history matters (the roll-day differences in section 2 are this effect). Flat 16:55-18:00 ET. Holidays and half days: no bars -> no trades; early closes flatten on the final bar. A data gap over 90 minutes disarms the setup.

## 4. NT8 implementation notes (UNTESTED in NT8)
- Build the 5-minute bars inside one OnBarUpdate of the 1-minute series (bucket complete when the 1-minute bar ending on :00/:05/... closes). Do NOT rely on an added 5-minute data series: the callback order of the 1-minute and 5-minute series at the same timestamp decides whether the aggregate is already complete on the signal bar; the rule here is "complete bucket only, usable on the bar that completes it".
- The Supertrend flip uses the CURRENT bar's band and the ATR is the first-bar-seeded recursion; an NT8 library Supertrend/ATR is a different system.
- The trigger reference is the ARMING bar's high/low (fixed). Do not "improve" it to the pullback-low bar.
- The armed state must keep running through 15:00-17:59 ET and across the 17:00-18:00 break (a 61-minute gap does not disarm, but the 30-minute expiry makes it moot); only the signal at an open time 15:00-17:59 is discarded.
- Start the strategy with 30+ days of history so the band ratchets are in steady state.

## 7. What could kill it
- Selection: one of 141 MTF pullback cells scored (best-of-N); the cell is the only distinct direct candidate that beats ST10 at equal risk; verdict CANDIDATE, failing the paired-vs-ST and maxDD-not-worse criteria; placebo rank 0.95 (timing-destroyed placebo) while the P9 random-time trigger at the same take-rate matches the real cell (rank 0.40, section 9a2): the evidence that the pullback timing matters is absent.
- Pricing: the P&L depends on the S10x1 slicing and the XJ3 stop-join (XJ3 = +0.41 pts/trade = 14% of the WF headline); the all-market arm earns 7.9k (-48%); mapped 11.1k; neither slicing nor XJ3 is validated on the live copier (live join rate 8/20 vs replay 0.78 in the DS2 live file). Every 1 pt/trade of extra slippage costs about $5.5k/wk of the 15.2k.
- Side asymmetry: shorts carry 74% of the WF P&L; in the 2023-04..2024-01 span the short side lost; a regime where the 5-min Supertrend shorts stop working would remove most of it.
- Lock dependence: the locks remove 41% of the raw trades (43 -> 27/wk); the P&L shown is the locked one; the identity test does not re-derive the locks.
- Rule details that must be reproduced exactly: the first-bar-seeded ATR, the current-bar Supertrend flip, the edge condition on the previous close vs previous EMA, the arming-bar reference (not the pullback-low bar), the 30-minute window without refresh, the one-bar as-of rule for the 5-minute bar, the signal-time block 15:00-17:59 ET.
- NT8 multi-series callback order (section 4).
- Roll days: signals on roll days differ by a bar in about 1.3% of the cases between two adjusted-price constructions; the live chart's adjustment will differ again.
- Capacity: above about 12-14 lots the sweep cost and risk rise faster than the mean; the 50-lot cap with DS2 allows at most 23 lots.

### Recommendation (one paragraph)
PB3B_R5_P1 remains a CANDIDATE and the P9 block weakens the case rather than strengthening it. What the evidence supports: a positive 5-min-Supertrend-direction bracket book with 27 trades/wk, +15.2k $/wk WF at 10 lots on the direct basis (HOLD +18.7k, span +6.0k), positive under both mirror and cost stress (mirrors lose; 2x cost still +10.9k), positive in each VIX tercile, a maxDD (361k) that is a typical draw of its own bootstrap (planning figure 520k at 10 lots), and at equal risk to ST10 a +2.9k $/wk higher mean with a positive DS3 add-one Sharpe in the holdout (+0.45 with KB+SM+EM, +0.48 with KB+SM; WF-PRE negative with KB+SM). What it does not support: (1) that the pullback entry is the source of the edge: a random minute inside the same regime direction at the same take-rate does as well (rank 0.40), and paired vs ST10 is -0.3 pts/trade [-2.4, 1.7] (MDE 3.0), so the entry rule is not distinguishable from ST's flip entry or from random timing; (2) that the P&L is robust to execution: 14% of the headline is the XJ3 stop-join, the all-market arm halves it, and S10x1 slicing and XJ3 are unvalidated live; (3) any size above about 12-14 lots: Sharpe and the DS3 marginal fall as lots rise and the 50-lot cap with DS2 allows at most 23; (4) a statistically proven edge: V2 gates are not softened, the verdict stays CANDIDATE (not PASS) because the paired-vs-ST criterion and the maxDD-not-worse criterion fail. The case for including it, if any, is dollar-for-dollar at equal risk (+2.9k $/wk, not significant) and its DS3 marginal Sharpe, both of which come from the shared regime with ST10 rather than from the pullback. Forward evidence cannot settle it quickly: at 50k weekly SD a mean of 15k needs about 85 weeks for 80% power versus zero.
