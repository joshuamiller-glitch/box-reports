## 1. Rule (exact)
- Instrument/bars: NQ front month, 1-minute chart, ET clock, full cash sessions only (skip half days / cash holidays / days with a missing RTH bar run).
- Series (current day, RTH bars from 09:30; NOT the NT8 session VWAP): tp = (H+L+C)/3 per 1-min bar; VWAP_t = sum(tp*Vol)/sum(Vol) from the 09:30 bar through bar t; dev_t = Close_t - VWAP_t; sigma_t = POPULATION std of dev_s over s = 09:30 .. t (expanding). (Bar-type check: sigma sampled on 3m / 5m / 15m / volume bars gives the same events on 94-100% of days and the same verdicts.)
- Evaluate ONCE per day on the close of the 15:29 bar (= 15:30:00): z = dev/sigma. Trigger iff |z| >= 1.0 (the surface plateau is z 0.75-1.5, see 4). Side = toward VWAP: LONG if z <= -1.0, SHORT if z >= +1.0.
- ENTRY (recommended): a LIMIT order at the touch at 15:30:00 - buy at the best bid (long) / sell at the best ask (short) - live for 300 s (cancel at 15:35:00 if unfilled); 10 lots; no re-entry; no chase. The harness fills the limit only on a STRICT trade-through (a print 1 tick through the limit), which is conservative versus a real queue fill. (Alternative: market order at 15:30:00 ONLY if a fill at the 15:30 bar open can be verified, see 3.)
- After the fill: OCO bracket target +30 pts / stop -30 pts from the FILL price; if neither, MARKET exit at 16:00:00 (submit at the close of the 15:59 bar). Exit-time sensitivity is nil (15:59:59 / 16:00:00 / 16:00:01 / 16:14:00 / 16:14:30 all within +-$150/wk).
## 2. Window, size, concurrency, locks
- Window: one decision at 15:30:00, flat by 16:00:00. K4 holds overnight from 16:17 (no overlap; weekly correlation to the K4 base series -0.09).
- Lots: fixed 10 (R12k = 20 lots roughly doubles the stack-add but the capacity limit is about 20 lots at 15:30 on the M edge and 10 on the quote-based T edge; worst week -$49k at 20 lots). Max loss per trade 30 pts x $20 x 10 = $6,000 + slippage and fees.
- **Concurrency (new):** the live books are NOT flat at 15:30: positions entered before 15:00 can be open to 16:55. Replaying the live books' open lots at each RVW1 entry: mean 16.5 lots open, p95 50, max 67 (unweighted book lots); the 50-contract cap would be exceeded by the added 10 lots on 11% of entries. Skipping those (cap 50): 551 of 621 trades kept, WF +$2,036/wk (M) [d Sharpe +0.11] and +$1,773 (T); HOLD +$465 (M) / +$400 (T). Cap 60: +$2,090 / +$1,658. So the cap costs about $100-200/wk.
- Own locks: none needed beyond the bracket (one trade a day); scored under the stack's per-book thresholds, which never bind on one trade a day.
