## 0. One-paragraph plain-English description
Every day the first 3 minutes of the NQ cash session (09:30-09:32 ET) are looked at. If the market moved decisively in one direction over those 3 minutes (the net open-to-close move is at least 20 points), the strategy waits for the first higher low (after an up-drive) or lower high (after a down-drive) that follows the first counter-trend bar (any time from 09:33 up to the 10:29 bar) and when that bar (or block) closes it buys (after an up-drive) or sells (after a down-drive) at market, in the direction of the original drive. It then places a protective stop 60 points (240 ticks) away and a profit target 60 points (240 ticks) away; whichever is hit first ends the trade, and if neither is hit by 16:00 ET it exits at market. At most one trade a day, no re-entry.

## 1. Platform setup
- Instrument: NQ front month (continuous contract; the same expiry for the whole day, on roll day use the new front month for the entire day). Tick 0.25 (= $5.00), point value $20. The study price space is the library back-adjusted series with a per-day offset on 15 roll days (code/open2/o2_core.off2); NT8 trades the unadjusted contract, so all levels below are computed from the day's own contract bars.
- Chart: 1 Minute bars, Calculate = On bar close, session template containing the full 09:30-16:00 ET RTH (ETH is fine, only RTH bars are used; the 09:30 bar must be the first RTH bar, not the ETH 09:29 bar). Strategy/chart time zone US Eastern (America/New_York, DST aware). All times ET.
- **Bar time convention**: NT8 stamps a bar with its CLOSE time; the study labels a bar by its OPEN time. "The 09:30 bar" = 09:30:00-09:30:59 = the bar with `Time[0] = 09:31:00`. This file uses OPEN time with the NT8 stamp in brackets where it matters. NT8 stamp = open time + 1 minute.
- Strategy settings: `EntriesPerDirection = 1`, `EntryHandling = AllEntries`, `IsExitOnSessionCloseStrategy = false`, `TimeInForce = Gtc`, `StopTargetHandling = PerEntryExecution`, `OrderFillResolution = Standard`.

## 2. State kept per ET calendar date
- `tradedToday` flag (reset at the first bar of each ET date).
- No median buffer is needed (condition `mv20` is an absolute 20.00-point threshold). Qualifying day (code f_common.rth_days): the 1-min bars 09:30..09:59 all exist with a Close; the study sample starts 2023-02-10.

## 3. Drive / signal
Evaluated once, on the close of the drive bar (the drive bar number 3, open time 09:32, NT8 stamp 09:33:00):
1. Require the 3 bars 09:30..09:32 to exist (no gap).
2. `move = Close(09:32 bar) - Open(09:30 bar)`; `M = |move|`. If `move == 0`: no trade today.
3. `side = +1` (long) if `move > 0`, `-1` (short) if `move < 0` (the trade goes WITH the drive).
4. Condition: `M >= 20.00` points (an absolute threshold; no median is used).  Nothing else filters (no gap, no news, no VIX, no day-of-week, no open-drive "has not traded back" test).
5. Day is armed. (Code: code/open2/f32_drive.py day_event; variant builder make_variant(3, etype, cond).)

## 4. Entry (hl)
**hl = first higher low (long) / lower high (short) after the first counter-trend bar.** Scan completed 1-min bars with OPEN time 09:33 up to and including 10:29. Step 1: the first bar whose close is against the drive (long: `Close < Open`; short: `Close > Open`) is the reference bar B1 (it is NOT an entry). Step 2: after B1, the first later bar whose Low is ABOVE B1's Low (long) / whose High is BELOW B1's High (short) is the signal bar (it may itself be an up or down bar; only the Low/High comparison counts). On that signal bar's close send ONE market order in the drive direction. If step 1 or step 2 does not happen by the 10:29 bar there is no trade. (Code: f32_drive.py etype 'hl'.)
- The latest possible signal bar is the 10:29 bar (NT stamp 10:30:00), so the latest entry is about 10:30:00. A signal at or after the 10:30:00 stamp is never accepted.
- One entry per day: after the order is sent (or the scan ends without one) `tradedToday = true`; no re-entry after a stop or a target, no second signal (code: events are one per day, reentry = 'none').
- **Scored window W1 (09:30-09:45):** the study scored this cell only on entries whose entry instant (signal-bar close + 0-1 s) falls before 09:45:00 ET (sb1_surv.py, session W1_0930-0945). Effect of the rule in PRE: it removes 29 of 166 NY-AM entries (166 -> 137) (latest scored PRE entry 09:44). Implement the generator window (to 10:29) plus the explicit rule "no new entry at or after 09:45:00" to reproduce the scored cell-session exactly; without the rule the cell is the NY-AM version (rows in section 8).
- Study entry timing: the replay (V0) sent a market order at the stored entry instant (signal-bar close, +0 or +1 s depending on the cell family: this cell family fills at the stored second shown in the identity table below) with 1 tick adverse entry slippage.

## 5. Exits (set when the entry fills, from the actual average FILL price `F`)
- Stop: `F - 60` points for a long (`F + 60` short) = 240 ticks, stop-market, full size. Target: `F + 60` points for a long (`F - 60` short) = 240 ticks, a limit order, full size. Stop and target are OCO; no trailing, no break-even, no scaling. If stop and target are both reachable inside the same second the study counts the STOP first.
- Time exit: if neither has filled, cancel both and exit at market at 16:00:00 ET (close of the 15:59 bar; study `tl = 16:00`). On early-close days the position is closed at the last bar. The live stack's 16:55 hard flatten never binds.
- Study costs on the MEASURED basis (V0): market entry 1 tick adverse, stop-market walks the displayed book (measured stop slippage in PRE for this cell: mean 0.84 pt per stopped trade), target limit filled at touch plus the measured P9 Target correction by year (2023 -1.44, 2024 -1.53, 2025 -4.21, 2026 -4.32 pt, added at scoring on target exits), commission $4.84 round trip per contract (FEES.md, NQ proxy = 0.242 pt).

## 6. Sizing and locks
- Risk unit: $1,000 per trade at the stop. Exact lots = 1000 / (60 x 20) = **0.833 NQ**. The study's lot class (the contract count actually replayed) is **1 NQ** (class rule: lots >= 7.5 -> 10, >= 3.5 -> 5, >= 1.5 -> 2, else 1; code sb1_surv.py), i.e. $ risk per trade 1,200 on 1 contract. Micro conversion (NOT replayed, arithmetic only): 10 MNQ = 1 NQ, round-trip fee $1.78 per MNQ (FEES.md); the stop-fill model was measured on NQ.
- Per-book locks used in the O6 scoring (x_locks, closed-trade): R = $1,000; no new entry if the book's day realised P&L <= -2R or >= +5R; day give-back arm at 2R keeping 50 percent; stop the book for the rest of the ISO week at -12R. With one trade a day per cell the day tests are moot for this cell alone.
