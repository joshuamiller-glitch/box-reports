- Instrument/bars: NQ front contract, 1-minute bars (NT8 native), ET clock. Window: signal 09:34 close; entries 09:35-10:29; flat by target/stop, else 16:00 market (harness exit; the live 16:55 flatten applies). One trade per day.
- Drive: D = sign(close of the 09:34 bar - open of the 09:30 bar); M5 = |that move|. Median5 = median over the previous 20 RTH sessions of (high - low of the 09:30-09:34 bars) (as-of the prior close). Trade only if M5 >= 0.5 x Median5 (about 45% of days). No filter on opening type, gap or release days (they are columns, not filters).
- Entry (pb1): after the 09:34 bar, the first 1-minute bar whose close is AGAINST the drive (longs: close < open; shorts: close > open); enter at market at the open of the next bar (on the live stack: market order at the bar close + 1 s; harness basis T fills at max(ask, first print) for buys).
