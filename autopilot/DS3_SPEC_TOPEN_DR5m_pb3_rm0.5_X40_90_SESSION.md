## 0. One-paragraph plain-English description
Every day the first 5 minutes of the NQ cash session (09:30-09:34 ET) are looked at. If the market moved decisively in one direction over those 5 minutes (the net open-to-close move is at least half of the typical 5-minute opening range of the previous 20 days), the strategy waits for the first 3-minute block (bars 09:35-37, 09:38-40, ...) that closes against that move (any time from 09:35 up to the 10:29 bar) and when that bar (or block) closes it buys (after an up-drive) or sells (after a down-drive) at market, in the direction of the original drive. It then places a protective stop 40 points (160 ticks) away and a profit target 90 points (360 ticks) away; whichever is hit first ends the trade, and if neither is hit by 16:00 ET it exits at market. At most one trade a day, no re-entry.

## 1. Platform setup
- Instrument: NQ front month (continuous contract; the same expiry for the whole day, on roll day use the new front month for the entire day). Tick 0.25 (= $5.00), point value $20. The study price space is the library back-adjusted series with a per-day offset on 15 roll days (code/open2/o2_core.off2); NT8 trades the unadjusted contract, so all levels below are computed from the day's own contract bars.
- Chart: 1 Minute bars, Calculate = On bar close, session template containing the full 09:30-16:00 ET RTH (ETH is fine, only RTH bars are used; the 09:30 bar must be the first RTH bar, not the ETH 09:29 bar). Strategy/chart time zone US Eastern (America/New_York, DST aware). All times ET.
- **Bar time convention**: NT8 stamps a bar with its CLOSE time; the study labels a bar by its OPEN time. "The 09:30 bar" = 09:30:00-09:30:59 = the bar with `Time[0] = 09:31:00`. This file uses OPEN time with the NT8 stamp in brackets where it matters. NT8 stamp = open time + 1 minute.
- Strategy settings: `EntriesPerDirection = 1`, `EntryHandling = AllEntries`, `IsExitOnSessionCloseStrategy = false`, `TimeInForce = Gtc`, `StopTargetHandling = PerEntryExecution`, `OrderFillResolution = Standard`.

## 2. State kept per ET calendar date
- `tradedToday` flag (reset at the first bar of each ET date).
- `rng5[date]` = (max High of the five 1-min bars 09:30..09:34) - (min Low of the same five bars), stored once the 09:34 bar has closed, only if the day qualifies (see "qualifying day"). Keep a buffer of the last 20 stored values of EARLIER dates (sliding window); `Median5` = median of the buffer for the previous qualifying sessions, today excluded; valid only with >= 10 stored values (with 10-19 values use what exists; code: f_common.or_median(5, 570) = shift(1).rolling(20, min_periods=10).median() over the study's qualifying-day list). Warm the buffer from history (>= 20 trading days) before the first live day.
- NOTE (exact, from the code): the 20-day median is the median of the FIVE-minute opening range for every drive length D; for D = 5 it is still the 5-minute range, not a 5-minute range.
- Qualifying day (code f_common.rth_days): a cash-session day for which the 1-min bars 09:30..09:59 all exist with a Close (a missing close in the first 30 minutes disqualifies the day for trading AND for the median buffer) and the study sample starts 2023-02-10.

## 3. Drive / signal
Evaluated once, on the close of the drive bar (the drive bar number 5, open time 09:34, NT8 stamp 09:35:00):
1. Require the 5 bars 09:30..09:34 to exist (no gap).
2. `move = Close(09:34 bar) - Open(09:30 bar)`; `M = |move|`. If `move == 0`: no trade today.
3. `side = +1` (long) if `move > 0`, `-1` (short) if `move < 0` (the trade goes WITH the drive).
4. Condition: `M >= 0.5 * Median5` where `M = |move|` and Median5 is defined in section 2 (passes on roughly 45 percent of days for D = 5; the share for other D is not tabulated in this task). If Median5 is invalid (fewer than 10 stored values): no trade. Nothing else filters (no gap, no news, no VIX, no day-of-week, no open-drive "has not traded back" test).
5. Day is armed. (Code: code/open2/f32_drive.py day_event; variant builder make_variant(5, etype, cond).)

## 4. Entry (pb3)
**pb3 = first 3-minute block that closes against the drive.** Blocks are non-overlapping groups of three consecutive 1-min bars, the first block starting at the bar with open time 09:35 (right after the last drive bar) and each next block starting 3 minutes later (block starts 09:35, 09:38, ...), a block must END (third bar) no later than the 10:29 bar. At the close of the THIRD bar of a block, the block is a pullback block if its last Close is against the drive relative to the block's FIRST bar Open: long drive -> `Close(3rd bar) < Open(1st bar)`; short -> `Close(3rd bar) > Open(1st bar)`. The first such block is the signal; on the close of its third bar send ONE market order in the drive direction. If no block qualifies by the 10:29 bar there is no trade. (Code: f32_drive.py etype 'pb3': `j=jd+1; while j+3<=ie: if c[j+2]<o[j] ... sig=j+2; j+=3`.)
- The latest possible signal bar is the 10:29 bar (NT stamp 10:30:00), so the latest entry is about 10:30:00. A signal at or after the 10:30:00 stamp is never accepted.
- One entry per day: after the order is sent (or the scan ends without one) `tradedToday = true`; no re-entry after a stop or a target, no second signal (code: events are one per day, reentry = 'none').
- **Scored window NY-AM (entries 09:30-12:00):** the generator itself ends at 10:29, so the scored window is the full generator window (latest PRE entry 10:05).
- Study entry timing: the replay (V0) sent a market order at the stored entry instant (signal-bar close, +0 or +1 s depending on the cell family: this cell family fills at the stored second shown in the identity table below) with 1 tick adverse entry slippage.

## 5. Exits (set when the entry fills, from the actual average FILL price `F`)
- Stop: `F - 40` points for a long (`F + 40` short) = 160 ticks, stop-market, full size. Target: `F + 90` points for a long (`F - 90` short) = 360 ticks, a limit order, full size. Stop and target are OCO; no trailing, no break-even, no scaling. If stop and target are both reachable inside the same second the study counts the STOP first.
- Time exit: if neither has filled, cancel both and exit at market at 16:00:00 ET (close of the 15:59 bar; study `tl = 16:00`). On early-close days the position is closed at the last bar. The live stack's 16:55 hard flatten never binds.
- Study costs on the MEASURED basis (V0): market entry 1 tick adverse, stop-market walks the displayed book (measured stop slippage in PRE for this cell: mean 0.60 pt per stopped trade), target limit filled at touch plus the measured P9 Target correction by year (2023 -1.44, 2024 -1.53, 2025 -4.21, 2026 -4.32 pt, added at scoring on target exits), commission $4.84 round trip per contract (FEES.md, NQ proxy = 0.242 pt).

## 6. Sizing and locks
- Risk unit: $1,000 per trade at the stop. Exact lots = 1000 / (40 x 20) = **1.250 NQ**. The study's lot class (the contract count actually replayed) is **1 NQ** (class rule: lots >= 7.5 -> 10, >= 3.5 -> 5, >= 1.5 -> 2, else 1; code sb1_surv.py), i.e. $ risk per trade 800 on 1 contract. Micro conversion (NOT replayed, arithmetic only): 10 MNQ = 1 NQ, round-trip fee $1.78 per MNQ (FEES.md); the stop-fill model was measured on NQ.
- Per-book locks used in the O6 scoring (x_locks, closed-trade): R = $1,000; no new entry if the book's day realised P&L <= -2R or >= +5R; day give-back arm at 2R keeping 50 percent; stop the book for the rest of the ISO week at -12R. With one trade a day per cell the day tests are moot for this cell alone.
