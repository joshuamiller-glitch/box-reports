## 1. Rule (NT8, OnBarClose on a 1-minute NQ series)
All inputs are back-adjusted NQ 1-minute bars (MergeBackAdjusted, volume roll). Time ET. A 1-minute bar exists only for minutes with at least one trade (Databento ohlcv-1m semantics; NT8 time bars the same by default); every lookback below counts BARS (index), not clock minutes, and runs across the 17:00-18:00 break, weekends and holidays without reset. In NT8 stamping the bar is stamped with its END time; "open time" below is end time minus 1 minute.

**1.1 Inputs.** C[i] = close of 1-minute bar i (back-adjusted). d[0] = 0, d[i] = C[i] - C[i-1] (1-bar change; across a break or roll this is the adjusted change).

**1.2 Vol-normalised return (z) for each lookback L in {15, 30, 60, 120, 240} bars:**
- W = max(L, 120) = 120 for L = 15, 30, 60, 120 and 240 for L = 240.
- sigma_W[i] = SAMPLE standard deviation (divisor W-1) of d[i-W+1 .. i] (the current bar included; d[0] = 0 is part of the window while it is inside).
- z_L[i] = (C[i] - C[i-L]) / (sigma_W[i] * sqrt(L)). Defined only for i > max(L, W) (i >= 121 for L = 15..120, i >= 241 for L = 240); before that the lookback does not vote. No clipping; sigma = 0 would give an infinite z (does not occur in the data).

**1.3 Vote and count.** vote_L[i] = +1 if z_L[i] >= +0.5; -1 if z_L[i] <= -0.5; 0 otherwise (|z| < 0.5, or undefined). cnt[i] = sum of the five votes (a NET count, -5..+5). "k = 4 of 5" therefore means cnt >= +4: either 5 long votes, or 4 long votes with the fifth lookback neutral (|z| < 0.5). One opposing vote cancels one agreeing vote: 4 long + 1 short = 3 (no signal); 3 long + 2 neutral = 3 (no signal); ties and zero votes count as 0. Short mirror: cnt <= -4.

**1.4 Signal (edge-triggered), evaluated on every 1-minute bar i whether or not a trade is open or the clock is blocked:**
- Long signal on bar i iff cnt[i] >= 4 and cnt[i-1] < 4 (for i = 0 the previous state is "not >= 4"). Short signal iff cnt[i] <= -4 and cnt[i-1] > -4. The first bar of a run triggers; while cnt stays >= 4 nothing fires; the signal re-arms when cnt falls below 4 (to 3 or lower) and later reaches 4 again. A flip from +4 to -4 needs the count to pass through the intermediate values, so a long and a short signal never occur on the same bar.
- The edge state is NOT reset by a trade, a blocked hour, a break or a data gap; it is a pure function of the bar series. Signals that arrive while the book is busy are dropped (1.6), not queued: after the exit the trade can only start from the next fresh edge.

**1.5 Entry.** Market order at the OPEN of the 1-minute bar after the signal bar (signal bar i, entry bar i+1), valid only if bar i+1 exists and starts exactly 1 minute after bar i (no entry across a gap, a break, a halt or a missing minute). Signals whose bar OPEN time is 15:00-17:59 ET are discarded (signal-time block); there is no block for signals at other hours (overnight signals 18:00-23:59 and 00:00-14:59 trade). The lock engine additionally removes entries stamped (bar end time) 15:00-16:54 and 18:00-18:05 ET (in the raw list 266 of 7,900 entries: 15 + 251). Long and short are symmetric (49.8% / 50.2% of entries). 10 lots as 10 x 1-lot market slices 2 s apart in the direct pricing (S10x1_g2), as the other DS3 books.

**1.6 One trade at a time.** The book holds one position: a signal whose entry bar is <= the exit bar of the open trade is dropped. A reversal signal while in a trade is ignored (the position is not reversed; it exits only by target, stop or the 16:55 flat). Of 63,218 replay signals (53,769 library events) only 7,900 library entries survive the busy rule, the signal-time block and the entry-bar checks: 12.5%.

**1.7 Bracket (DS2 bracket, fixed 60/60; stopm 60, tgt 1.0R, read from te_core.gen_entries):** stop-market 60 pt and limit target 60 pt OCO from the entry (bar-open) price; stop exits worked as touch-join for 3 s then market (XJ3, P9 mechanism, unvalidated live). Flat 16:55 ET (market at the close of the 1-minute bar ending 16:55; early-close days at the final bar). The 16:55 flat applies only to trades alive before 18:00: a trade entered 18:00-23:59 or 00:00-14:59 runs until the target, the stop or the next 16:55 (maximum hold in the sample 1,372 min = 22.9 h; median 52 min, 90% 336 min, 99% 913 min). Replay convention when both levels fall in one 1-minute bar: the stop is taken (the library resolves it on the 1-second tape); this and the one-minute exit offsets are the exit-side residual in section 2.

**1.8 Sessions / mix:** by entry-bar end hour: 6.5% 00-02 ET, 21.4% 03-08, 9.7% 09, 39.0% 10-14, 0.2% 15-17, 23.2% 18-23. Exits 49.5% target, 46.5% stop, 4.1% flat (16:55). Raw trades 40.9 per week; after the lock engine 27.4 per week in WF (median 26, 25-75% 20-33, 5-95% 15-47); 18.1 in the 2023-04..2024-01 window.

**1.9 Lots / dollars (the lots rule as scored):** fixed 10 lots (prereg: unsized cell), R = lots x stop x $20 = 10 x 60 x 20 = $12,000 (engine size = lots/14). Locks (exact 1-s mark-to-market engine, firmlib.Ctx.run, te_core.spec_for(10)), per-book DS2 locks: day -2R = -$24,000 and +5R = +$60,000 (a new entry is skipped once breached), give-back arm +2R = $24,000 with 50% kept, week floor -12R = -$144,000, combined day cap off. ## 3. Data, roll, holidays
Continuous front NQ, back-adjusted; z uses price changes over L bars and 1-bar changes, so the ADJUSTED history matters only through the roll bars (the roll-day differences in section 2 are this effect). Flat 16:55-18:00 ET. Holidays and half days: no bars -> no signals; early closes flatten on the final bar. A missing minute shifts every later lookback by one bar (bar-count lookbacks): NT8 must build the series without filling empty minutes.

## 4. NT8 implementation notes (UNTESTED in NT8)
- Native building blocks: Close[0] - Close[L] for L = 15, 30, 60, 120, 240; the 1-bar change series (Momentum(1) or Close[0] - Close[1]); a standard-deviation of that change series over W = 120 / 240 bars. NT8's StdDev indicator may use the POPULATION divisor: if so, multiply its value by sqrt(W/(W-1)) (or implement the sample std in the strategy; I could not inspect NT8 here, treat the divisor as unverified until a test shows the same sigma as pandas.rolling(W).std() on the same bars). With the population divisor the entry list differs by 2.6% (section 2). There is no NT8-native "ensemble": the vote count, the edge state and the busy flag live in the strategy (custom NinjaScript strategy, one data series, OnBarClose).
- Order handling: at OnBarClose of the signal bar submit one market order for 10; attach stop 60 / target 60 OCO from the fill price (ATM or managed approach with SetStopLoss/SetProfitTarget in ticks 240/240); flat by 16:55 ET. Same bracket container as ST/HM.
- Do not shortcut the edge: the signal is the FIRST bar of cnt >= 4, not "cnt >= 4 and flat". After a stop-out while cnt is still >= 4 there is no new entry until cnt drops below 4 and returns.
- Do not use 5/10-minute series or session templates: the rule is index-based on 1-minute bars (unlike the PB3/ST cells).
- Start the strategy with 4+ days of 1-minute history (section 1.10).

## 7. What could kill it
- Selection: the cell is the best of 133 TR1 bracket cells by the WF mapped weekly mean; verdict CANDIDATE; the TR1 best-of-N null at the cell level (p 0.46 PRE, 0.25 HOLD, 0.70 SPAN2 for the best real cells) does not reject the null for the TR1 family as a whole; the cell's own placebo rank is 1.00, so the timing is not random, but the placebo does not test the selection.
- Second holdout fails: 2023-04..2024-01 is -7.5k/wk with a 473k maxDD; the 2023 raw result is -2.77 pts/trade against +2.4..+2.6 in the next three years, and the loss is the short side (-10.1k/wk). The WF figures (2024-02 on) contain no such stretch; a repeat of 2023 at 10 lots would be a drawdown of the order of 470k.
- Pairing: against ST on the days both trade, the ensemble entry is 2.3 pts/trade worse (CI excludes 0) on the direct basis; 71% of its entries have no ST entry within +/-15 min (the unpaired entries are not scored against anything; standalone the book earns), so the book is additive in coverage, not better in timing.
- Pricing: the P&L depends on the S10x1 slicing and the XJ3 stop-join (both unvalidated live); 12.3% of trades are on the mapped basis; the 1.5x / 2x cost lines stay positive (19.0k / 17.8k).
- Rule details that must be reproduced exactly: sample std (divisor W-1; population gives 2.6% entry mismatch), threshold 0.5, net count (4 long + 1 short = 3), edge trigger (not level), the bar-count lookbacks (a filled-gap chart shifts them), the exact-1-minute entry-bar check, the signal-time block 15:00-17:59 ET, the busy drop (no queue, no reversal).
- Roll days: signals on roll days differ by a bar in about 1.3% of the cases between two adjusted-price constructions (112 of 142 entry mismatches); the live chart's adjustment will differ again.
- Concentration: 39% of entries are 10:00-14:59 ET, 23% start 18:00-23:59; the profit sits in wide-range sessions (quiet +3.8k vs wide +14.0k, mapped).
- Capacity/sizing: the practical ceiling is 29 lots by the sweep model; at the 27-contract DS3 line the book is 5-8 lots and the WF maxDD at the cap line rises by about +20k.
