## 1. Rule (NT8, 15-minute series from the 1-minute bars, time = ET)
**1.1 Bars.** 15-minute buckets = floor(epoch minute / 15) over the 1-minute bars PRESENT (clock-aligned :00/:15/:30/:45, identical in UTC and ET); OHLC from those bars. Bar j is complete when its last present 1-minute bar has closed. "Previous bar" = j-1 in the sequence of buckets that exist.

**1.2 Channel (Donchian, n = 20), the level as coded.** For bar i: ub[i] = highest High of the 20 bars i-20..i-1; db[i] = lowest Low of the same 20 bars (the bar itself is excluded; NaN until 20 bars exist). **The orders for bar j use the channel row of the PREVIOUS bar, ub[j-1] = max High of bars j-21..j-2: the high of the bar that has just completed (j-1) is NOT in the level.** In NT8 terms, at the close of bar k = j-1: level_up = MAX(High, 20)[1], level_dn = MIN(Low, 20)[1] (values one bar back). Using the channel that includes the just-completed bar (ub[j-1]+bar j-1) is a different strategy: the replay of that variant reproduces only 60% of the library entries (39.8% mismatch, 5,598 trades), so this detail is a hard requirement.

**1.3 Stop-order placement (what, where, when, until when).** At the close of every completed 15-minute bar k (= j-1), for the bar j that follows, provided bar j starts exactly one minute after bar k's last 1-minute bar (no gap; across the 17:00-18:00 break and weekends no order is placed):
- BUY STOP (long) if Close[k] <= ub[k]: price = ceil(ub[k]/0.25) x 0.25 + 0.25, i.e. the channel high rounded UP to the tick plus ONE tick above (exact tick multiples of the level are +1 tick; others +1 to +2 ticks).
- SELL STOP (short) if Close[k] >= db[k]: price = floor(db[k]/0.25) x 0.25 - 0.25.
- Quantity 10 (one slice, stop-market). Good-for-bar: the order is valid only for the 1-minute bars of bar j (from bar j's first minute to its last present minute); it is cancelled at the end of bar j if not filled and replaced by the new levels computed at the close of bar j. No other cancellation (no price invalidation; the level may change every bar as the channel rolls). With neither side's close beyond the level, both a buy stop and a sell stop are live in the same bar (162,621 orders in 3.7 years = 173 per day, about half per side; 4,812 fills).
- The setup is skipped when the minute before bar j's first minute opens 15:00-17:59 ET (orders for bars starting 15:01-18:14 are not placed; the bar starting 15:00 is allowed by this rule but the lock engine refuses entries stamped 15:00-16:54, and 18:00-18:04, see 1.6).
- Not placed (library) when a trade is open at the time the order's validity ends (window end <= exit minute of the open trade); see 1.4 for the mid-bar re-placement.
- A close-break (Close[k] > ub[k]) places NO buy stop for the next bar (the entry that a close-based book would take is not taken); an intrabar break in bar k that closed back at or below ub[k] DOES leave the order in place.

**1.4 Fill rule (library, 1-second path) and OCO.** Fill = first 1-second record of bar j's minutes whose High >= buy-stop price (Low <= sell-stop price): touch is a fill; fill price = the stop price exactly (no gap logic: a bar that opens through the stop is still booked at the stop price in the scored series). No OCO in the library: when both orders of the same bar can fill the SHORT is processed first (sort key: bar start, then side -1 before +1); after a trade is taken the other side's order of that bar is still live and can fill later in the same bar after the first trade has exited, but a fill at or before the exit minute of the open trade is rejected (no entry, no re-arm: the level having traded already means the order is spent for this bar). One trade at a time. Consequences for NT8: (a) while a position is open place no entry orders; (b) when a trade exits MID-bar, re-place the stop order(s) for the remainder of that bar ONLY IF price has not traded through the level since the bar start (a stop placed behind the market would fill immediately; the library has no such fill); my replay shows that placing orders only at bar start (no mid-bar re-placement) loses 3.3% of the library entries (158 trades, 4.5% full mismatch); (c) true OCO (earliest touch wins, the other cancelled) differs from the library in 1.2% of entries (2.5% full mismatch) because the library takes the short first even if the long level traded earlier in a two-sided bar. The scored series follows the library; the difference is small but is not zero.

**1.5 Bracket (DS2 bracket, fixed 60/60; stop and target OCO from the fill price) and flat.** Stop-market 60 pt and limit target 60 pt (target/stop 1.0) from the fill price (the stop price); the library bracket starts at the second after the fill (so a 60-pt move inside the entry minute counts: 45 of 4,759 trades, 0.95%); stop exits fill at the stop level, or at the previous second's close when that is beyond it (gap); the stop is tested first within a second. Flat at market at the close of the 1-minute bar opening 16:54 ET (16:55 stamp; early-close days at the last bar), only for trades alive before 18:00 (a trade entered from 18:00 runs to the next 16:55). Exits: target 50.5%, stop 45.2%, flat 4.2%; median hold 58 min, 75% 174 min, 90% 382 min.

**1.6 Lots and locks (as scored).** Fixed 10 lots (R = 10 x 60 x $20 = $12,000, the stop is 60 for every trade, so risk-based lots would also be 10). Exact per-book lock engine (te_core.spec_for(10)): day -2R = -$24,000, +5R = +$60,000 (entry-time test on realised net of trades closed before the entry), give-back arm +2R with 50% kept, week floor -12R = -$144,000. The engine also refuses entries stamped (entry-minute open + 1 minute) 15:00-16:54 and 18:00-18:04 ET. Raw 4,806 trades (to 2026-10-23) -> 3,782 after locks and windows (21.3 per week in WF). Entry hours (raw): 09-10 ET 24%, 11-14 ET 22%, overnight (18-08) the rest; long 52% / short 48%.

**1.7 Warm-up.** 21 completed 15-minute bars (about 5 hours of 1-minute history). No recursion. Recommended NT8 setting: load at least 3 trading days; sensitivity measured zero (section 2).

## 3. Data, roll, holidays
Continuous front NQ, back-adjusted; the 20-bar extremes are on adjusted prices, so the roll treatment moves order prices by ticks (37 of 54 entry mismatches). Holidays and half days: no bars -> no orders; a break in bar continuity (previous bar's last minute +1 != this bar's first minute) places no order. Early closes flatten on the final bar.

## 4. NT8 implementation notes (UNTESTED in NT8)
- Strategy on a 15-minute series built so that buckets are :00/:15/:30/:45 (use a 1-minute primary series and build the 15-minute bars in code, or NT8 Minute 15 anchored to the 18:00 session open, which lands on the same boundaries).
- OnBarClose of bar k, if flat: level_up = MAX(High,20)[1], level_dn = MIN(Low,20)[1]; if Close[k] <= level_up submit a buy stop at ceil(level_up/tick)*tick + 1 tick, if Close[k] >= level_dn a sell stop at floor(level_dn/tick)*tick - 1 tick, quantity 10, valid for the bar only (managed approach: EnterLongStop/EnterShortStop with isLiveUntilCancelled = false, or unmanaged with explicit CancelOrder at the next bar close); the two orders are independent (the library has no OCO; adding OCO changes 1.2% of entries).
- On fill: attach stop-market (fill - 60 x side) and limit target (fill + 60 x side) as OCO children (ATM or OnExecutionUpdate); flat at the 16:54 bar close.
- While in a position: no entry orders; after an exit inside a bar, re-place the orders for the rest of that bar (see 1.4) only when price has not yet traded through the level since the bar open.
- Order churn: about 173 submissions and cancellations per day (the library has no order-rate constraint); the cost of this is an execution-side question.
- Guard 15:00-17:59 and the bar-continuity rule in code.

## 7. What could kill it
- Fill quality of the real stop-market (largest uncertainty): the whole +16 pts/trade versus the close-based break comes from entering at the level instead of after the bar closes. The D3LS print model says 87% of fills are at the stop price and the mean slip is 0.06 pt, but it is a 1-lot proxy on a quiet-market tape; the live copier's stop-join record on NQ is mixed (join rates are 8/20 live vs 0.78 replayed in the DS2 file) and no live stop-entry test exists. This is a candidate for the execution server's own stop-fill test (D3XT / T-BRACKET-PM).
- Level definition: the order uses the channel of the previous bar (not including the just-completed bar); a coder who "fixes" it to MAX(High,20)[0] gets a different strategy (60% overlap).
- Selection and family: one of 74 D3TR2 cells; the cell is the best of 9 widening neighbours' parent (PASS in 6 of 9; one mechanism); most trend-family PASS cells are worse per trade than ST10 (paired -8.2 pts); the 2023 span is only +4.7k (0.83 Sharpe).
- Trend beta: it shares the long-regime character of ST10/KB (corr 0.45/0.30); the 2024-26 trend regime rewards almost any 60/60 breakout bracket.
- Locks: 21% of raw trades are removed by locks and entry windows; the lock engine is not re-derived here.
- Order churn and OCO: 173 order submissions per day; the mid-bar re-placement and two-sided short-first rule (1.4) change 1.2-3.3% of entries if implemented differently.
- Roll days (37 of 54 entry mismatches).
- Concurrency/capacity: 10 lots one position; WF Sharpe is flat to the equal-risk 10.8 lots, higher size not studied here.
