## Plain sentence
A 1-minute-bar rebuild of the live stack reproduces the six books' entry signals (85-98% of the reference entries) but not the live stack's weekly P&L (weekly correlation 0.66 with Q1-A ts2 over 2023-04..2026-10, 0.69 after one calibration iteration; the ordered bar is 0.80), because the live exits (Trade-Health / TH-L2 loss cuts and take lines, which turn about half of the 60-point stops into roughly 30-point exits) are decided on 1-second and order-book information that 1-minute bars cannot show; per the stop rule I computed no 2013-2023 stack figures, and the question "was 2023-26 an unusually good window" is NOT answered by this method.

## What was built (APX) and what was checked

## Verdict under the ordered stop rule
Weekly correlation 0.66 (v1) and 0.69 (v2) is below 0.80. STOPPED: no 2013-2023 weekly P&L, maxDD, share of profitable weeks, contributions or DIS-V bootstrap rerun on a 2013-2026 pool were computed. APX would overstate drawdowns (maxDD 2.1-2.4x the reference over 2023-26) and understate level in the recent regime, and its error is not a fixed bias, so a pre-2023 APX series could not be read as "the live stack".
