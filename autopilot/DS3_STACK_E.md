## What was run
Sibling signals on k-minute bars of the 1-min axis (live-bar regeneration identical to live rows and engine daily series, diff 0.0); 46,592 trades (live + 12 sibling row sets, 2023-02..2026-02-06) replayed on raw mbp-10 (stke_direct.py, copy of te_direct: S10x1_g2 + XJ3 + 0.5) = basis D for BOTH the live book and its sibling (paired, same pipeline); basis M = library mapped conservative price. REPL = sibling replaces the live book at the same lots/weights; MIX = 0.5 live + 0.5 sibling (lot-matched); ADD = both at full lots. Windows cut at label day 2026-02-06; UNTOUCHED not read. PRE 75 wk, HOLD 2025-07-01..2026-02-06, X 2023-04..2024-01 (44 wk). MDE = 2.8 x SD(paired weekly diff)/sqrt(n) beside each mean. Tail sets on the base stack: T_PRE20 (20 worst PRE weeks, PRE-only), Q (worst 20% of the window), T_ALL20 (20 worst of 148 weeks, fixed on the full cut window).

Disclosures: (1) before the prereg only identity tests, baseline WF 45,947/wk and row counts were seen. (2) A mapped-basis PRE pass (basis M, no HOLD) was scored after the prereg and before the D replay finished; it did not change any definition. (3) Two orchestrators were launched (second 'rev'); static month lists made the first re-run 8 finished months; all 37 months were complete and readable before the duplicates were terminated (DECISIONS). (4) Governor: PRIO_ALLOW += stke_direct.py, backup replay_governor.py.bak_pre_stke_20261028; the lead restarted it. (5) The thinned-live placebo does not re-run the lock engine on the drawn trades. (6) 2x-cost run applies 2x to the replaced book (live and sibling) and 2x sweep cost to the stack. (7) Direct replay coverage: rows after 2026-02-06 are not replayed (never read); coverage by basis D over all rows is in the JSON (cov).
