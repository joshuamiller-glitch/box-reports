# DS3_THL2_B_MAP - descriptive map of the order book, per in-trade state (L2H, 2026-10-29)

Research only; UNTOUCHED never read. Rows = 10-second decision points of the 10 static-bracket books (ST HM WT KB SM PE PB DN MV MA; CR excluded: dynamic exit), merged profit (upnl>0) and under-water (upnl<=0) path, label days 2023-04-03..2026-02-06. Windows, RECENT FIRST: **2026 = 2026-01-01..2026-02-06 (about 5 weeks: ~25 trading days, a few hundred trades per book; MDE for any decile spread is several pts, read it as a sign check only)**, **L6M = last 6 months 2025-08-01..2026-02-06**, HOLD = 2025-07..2026-02-06, PRE = 2024-02..2025-06, X = 2023-07..2024-01 (X in the csv). Recent windows overlap (2026 within L6M within HOLD). Deciles: edges fitted on X+PRE rows of the side (so HOLD is cut with the same edges); a NaN bin is dropped.

**Outcome columns.** `rem` = mean remaining P&L from the decision point to the bracket exit (pts, MEASURED exit cost, gross of nothing else; hold-to-bracket); `ptgt` = P(target first); `adj` = rem minus the mean rem of rows in the same (book, side, progress-to-target bucket) from X+PRE, i.e. the feature effect AFTER removing where in the trade the row sits (a row near the target has a high rem whatever the book does; read `adj` first). **Confidence intervals** are trade-clustered: the standard error is a ratio-estimator over per-trade sums (all seconds of a trade form one block), 95% = 1.96 SE; in-trade seconds of a trade are strongly autocorrelated, so the effective n is the trade count, not the row count, and nearby deciles share trades. A single decile is never a finding; the top-minus-bottom spread and its sign agreement across PRE / HOLD / books is the reading.

Files: map_core.csv/.parquet (all numbers: pooled and per book, X/PRE/HOLD, every decile) in l2vast/ds3/out/thp/wf/l2h/. Code l2vast/ds3/code/l2h/h_map.py, h_mapreport.py.

**Coverage note.** True wall_tgt / wall_stop (size AT the target / stop) is finite in 19,021 / 16,844 of 39.5 M rows (0.05%): the 10 visible levels cover about 2.5 pts, the bracket is 33-60 pts away. Walls therefore enter below only as depth within 4 / 8 ticks ahead of or behind the price (a proxy for the wall between the price and the target / stop) and as the size at the best.
