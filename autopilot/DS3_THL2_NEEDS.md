# DS3_THL2_NEEDS - features a winning L2 policy would need that D6 does not compute (L2H, 2026-10-29)
No hypothesis reached CANDIDATE, so no winning feature is missing from D6 (imb5, imb10, wall_tgt, wall_stop, delta60 + path set): the set was sufficient for every rule that was tested and none of them beat hold. What the test showed about the existing features:
* **wall_tgt / wall_stop carry nothing in mbp-10.** Finite in 0.05% of profit-side seconds (19,021 of 39.5 M rows) and 0.04% for wall_stop; the 10 visible levels cover ~2.5 pts, the brackets are 33-60 pts. If D6 reads a deeper live ladder (NT8 depth beyond 10 levels, or MBO), the right test is H1-true (size AT the target at 50/70% of the way) and H2-true (size between price and stop); the research library cannot test it without an MBO deep-book rebuild of the book at the target price. Request to whoever owns the MBO replay: size resting at the trade's target and stop price, plus level age, sampled every 10 s in-trade, from MBO (not mbp-10).
* Normalised depth, if anyone wants to re-test H8 later (not recommended): `depth_rel` = total resting size, 8 ticks each side of the touch, divided by the median of the same quantity at the same ET hour over the prior 20 label days (min 5 days), 10-s sampling, side-free. D6 does not compute it; the H8 gate adds +0.28 [-0.24,+0.81] PRE and +0.18 [-0.57,+0.92] HOLD over the plain path rule, i.e. nothing.
* Nothing else is requested: sweep counts (H7), queue consumption/refill (H4), iceberg flags (H5), pulled size (H6), OFI30 (H9) and microprice offset (H10) were tested and did not carry a policy.
* **ES book imbalance** (es_imb5: (bidES5 - askES5)/(bidES5 + askES5) over 5 ES levels, sided with the NQ trade, 10-s sampling; D6 does not compute it) carries the largest descriptive reading in map B (loser L6M adjusted decile spread +7.85 pts, t +4.0) but NO rule built on it (H12) beats hold; do not add it to D6 on this evidence.

## L2SL (selective loss-health) additions, 2026-10-30T00:36Z
Features NOT computed by D6 that the loss_policy_candidate.txt lines use (D6 computes pct, imb10, pulled_sup10; all windows end at the decision second, side = supporting side of the trade):
* **pulled_sup5**: size cancelled over supporting-side levels 1-10 in the last 5 s (long: bids, short: asks), MBO/mbp-10 cancel events, window ends at the decision second
* **pulled_sup30**: same as pulled_sup10 over the last 30 s
* **medOwnLevel**: median of the own-side (supporting side: long bids / short asks) L1..L10 sizes at the decision second
* **pulledShare5**: pulled_sup5 / (pulled_sup5 + sum of own-side L1..L10 sizes now); same for pulledShare10, pulledShare30
* **newAdvExt5**: 1 if the 1-s adverse excursion in the last 5 s set a new maximum adverse excursion of the trade (exceeds the running max over entry..T-5 s); newAdvExt10, newAdvExt30 likewise
* **icebergAgainst**: 1 if iceberg_flag_ahead: size traded at the best AHEAD (against a loser: asks for a long, bids for a short) in 10 s >= 10x the displayed best size (refill rule of l2feat)
* **sweepAgainst10**: count of trade-driven touch clears (>= 2 levels consumed) by the adverse aggressor in the last 10 s
* **ownDepth10Rel**: sum of own-side L1..L10 sizes divided by the median of that quantity at the same ET hour over the prior 20 label days (min 3 days) (l2feat cum_behind10_rel)
* **leadCount2of4**: icebergAgainst + (sweepAgainst10>=1) + (ownDepth10Rel<=0.5) + (imb10<=-0.3)

## EINT2 additions (2026-10-30): features D6 lacks for the calendar / day-type / time-in-session size rules (policy section `size`, file l2vast/ds3/out/missed/eint2_policy_candidate.txt)
All are computable at the signal second from 1-minute NQ (and ES) bars plus a user-supplied date list; none needs L2.
* **et_min**: ET minute of day of the signal (evening 18:00-24:00 negative: minus 1440); **session**: asia 18:00-02:00, london 02:00-08:30, pre 08:30-09:30, nyam 09:30-12:00, nypm 12:00-15:00, close 15:00-16:54.
* **mins_in_session**, **mins_to_session_end**: minutes since the session start / until its end (boundaries -360, 120, 510, 570, 720, 900, 1015 in et_min).
* **mins_rel_<anchor>** (signed, signal minus anchor, minutes): anchors 830e (08:30 on CPI/NFP dates only), 1400e (14:00 on FOMC dates only), and generic daily 830g, 1000g, 1500g, 1600g. 830e/1400e need the date list standS/event_calendar_core.csv (121 events, VERIFIED, to 2026-12-10; the user must keep it updated); earnings dates are NOT available.
* **cal_* flags** of the label day: monthend (last label day of the month), monthend3, monthstart, opexday (third Friday), quadday (third Friday of Mar/Jun/Sep/Dec), opexweek, quadweek, hol_before / hol_after (adjacent weekday without exchange session), early_close (last RTH 1-min bar before 15:00 ET), ev_fomc / ev_cpi / ev_nfp / ev_any, ev_post_fomc / ev_post_any (day after), weekday.
* **on_ratio**: (high - low of 18:00 prior evening to 09:29 ET) / median of the same quantity over the prior 20 label days; usable from 09:31. **gap_r**: (09:30 RTH open - prior 16:00 RTH close) / median prior-20 RTH range, side-aligned; usable from 09:31. **or30_ratio / or60_ratio**: opening range (09:30-10:00 / 10:30) over its prior-20 median, expanding terciles of prior days; usable from 10:01 / 10:31. **fhd_r**: (close of the 10:29 bar - 09:30 open) / median prior-20 RTH range, side-aligned (thresholds +-0.15); usable from 10:31. **esag30 / esag60**: sign of ES move agrees with NQ over 09:30-10:00 / 10:30. **vix_terc** (prior-day VIX, expanding terciles), **corr_terc** (ES-NQ 20d correlation), **prev_daytype**.
* Policy grammar: a `size <TAG> <SESSION|*> <condition> x<mult>` row (x0 = skip, last match multiplies); D6 has no size-by-condition property today, so every row is a `#!` option.

## EINT1 additions (2026-10-30): features D6 lacks for the signal-quality, confluence and HTF `size` rows (file l2vast/ds3/out/missed/eint1_policy_candidate.txt)
All computable at the signal second from 1-minute NQ bars (and the list of other books' signals); no L2 needed. Definitions are exact in l2vast/ds3/code/eint1/ei_feat.py; thresholds = trailing-12-month tercile/decile boundaries of the book before the cutoff (ei_nightly_refit.py recomputes them).
* **sig_kelt1** = side*(E - EMA20)/ATR14 (1-min); **sig_kelt3** = side*(close - EMA14)/ATR14 on completed 3-min bars; **sig_slope** = side*(EMA20 slope per bar)/ATR14; **hma_slope** = side*(HMA21 slope per bar)/ATR14; **sig_hma_sp** = side*(HMA9 - HMA21)/ATR14; **sig_st1_age / sig_st5_age / sig_st10_age** = minutes since the Supertrend(10,3) direction on 1/5/10-min bars last flipped; **sig_st1_dist_atr** = side*(E - Supertrend line)/ATR14; **sig_pb30_atr** = side*(30-bar extreme in the profit direction - close)/ATR14; **pb_speed** = sig_pb30_atr / max(1, bars since that extreme); **sig_lastbar_rng_atr** = (H-L of the last closed 1-min bar)/ATR14; **body_abs / body_signed** = |C-O|/(H-L) and side*(C-O)/(H-L) of the last closed 1-min bar; **bar_vol_rel** = volume of that bar / median volume of the previous 120 bars; **atr_pts** = ATR14 (Wilder, 1-min); **atr_ratio** = ATR14 / median ATR14 of the previous 20 label days. All use the last 1-min bar CLOSED at or before the signal second.
* **z_vwap_eth / z_vwap_rth**: (E - VWAP)/sd of price about VWAP since 18:00 ET / since 09:30 ET (side-signed); **sd_pd_hi_atr, sd_pd_lo_atr, sd_sess_hi_atr, sd_sess_lo_atr** = side*(level - E)/ATR14 for prior-day high/low and current-session high/low.
* **HTF**: daily table from completed label days (18:00 ET roll): **htf_tr20/50** = side*(prior-day close - SMA20/50 of prior daily closes)/ATRd (ATRd = mean H-L of the prior 14 label days); **htf_pd_pos** = side*((E - prior low)/(prior high - prior low) - 0.5); **htf_d_open_atr**, **htf_wk_open_atr** = side*(E - label-day open / label-week open)/ATRd; **htf_vwap_atr** = side*(E - VWAP_ETH)/ATR14; **htf_on_range_atr** = overnight (18:00-09:29 ET) range so far / ATRd; **htf_on_pos** = side*(position of E inside that range - 0.5); **htf_fh_atr** = first-hour (09:30-10:30) range/ATR14, usable from 10:30; **htf_gap_eth** = side*(label-day open - prior close)/ATRd; **htf_gap_rth** = side*(09:30 open - prior 16:00 close)/ATRd, usable from 09:31 ET; **htf_pd_range_atr** = prior-day range/ATRd; **htf_on_ratio** = overnight range / median of prior 20 days.
* **Confluence**: **n_same_<w>m / n_opp_<w>m / n_same_eng_<w>m** (w = 1, 3, 5) = number of OTHER books' entries (all 11 / engine 6) in the same / opposite direction in [t - w min, t] including the same second (D6 must see sibling signals within one evaluation pass; the later-firing book sees the earlier one, the first-firing book never does); **stk_n_opp / stk_n_same** = number of other books currently positioned opposite / same direction at the signal second. Rows `size ... n_same_3m == 0 x0.5` etc. Same-second net-one-position (netOne_0s) needs an engine-level combiner: one order at the summed lots, other books standing down.
