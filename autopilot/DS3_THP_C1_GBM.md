## Protocol as run (exactly the prereg unless stated)
Decision grid = 1-min bar closes (dp1m, ldp1m), exit at bar close = upnl - 0.995 (market +1 tick, MEASURED); tighten = stop at running extreme (profit) / current upnl (loss, ratcheting) minus 10 pts, exit at the level -1.01, detected at 1-min closes (approximation, biased slightly against tighten). Base arm = hold-to-bracket / hold-to-stop on the MEASURED basis (target correction 1.4/1.5/4.2/4.3 by year). A trade is scored by the model in force at its entry; fit trades have closed_ts <= refit time; fit_end_ts <= entry_ts in the decisions files (fit_end_ts = the refit timestamp, an upper bound of the last closed_ts used). Warm-up (fit only) 2023-04-03 .. 2024-03-29; scored OOS refit blocks 2024-04-01 .. 2026-02-06, the same span for every config; HOLD/PRE are reported as sub-splits of the chain (halves 2024-04..2025-06 / 2025-07..2026-02), not extra looks. Pooled over the 11 library books with book_code as a feature (CR has no target distance: the classifier/logit configs leave CR on hold, coverage 95.6%). Thresholds fixed, not tuned: take/cut when E[rem] < -0.995 (exit cost), < -2, < -4, < -8 (sensitivities), and P3 take+tighten.
Grid actually run (per side): reg (E[base]-upnl) on windows 1w 2w 1m 3m 6m 12m YTD ewma5/10/20 x steps 1m,1w,1d; clf and logit the same windows at 1m,1w and 3m/12m/ewma10 at 1d; intraday (18:00, 09:30, 12:00 ET re-fits) for reg 1m/3m/12m/ewma10 + clf/logit 3m/12m/ewma10 (profit) and reg 3m/12m (loss; reduced for compute). Ensembles (ALL, SL=1m+12m, SHORT, LONG; weights softmax(2 x trailing 20-day mean delta)), regime-keyed GBM (vol_tag x session group, 3m/12m weekly), CUSUM-triggered re-fits (k=0, h=4 on daily standardised deltas, base monthly; about 52-60 extra re-fits per window), meta-labelling (3-fold day-blocked OOF scores -> logistic sizes full / half; 3m,12m x monthly,weekly). Not run (declared): online learners, optimal-stopping/hazard/HMM/quantile (other agents), the GRU, intraday for every window, daily for 1w..YTD with clf/logit.
Multiplicity: 575 (profit) / 535 (loss) real configs x policies counted in the reality check (plus the 79/57-rule classic family x 3 re-selection windows x 2 steps).

## Drift of thresholds and feature importance (focus_P.json, focus_L.json)
The mean predicted E[rem] of the profit GBM 3m weekly moves from -0.5 (2024Q1) to -3.1/-1.5/-3.4/-2.9/-5.5/-6.1/-2.0/+0.2 (2025Q4/2026Q1), the share of rows below -0.995 from 0.43 to 0.61, the 10th percentile from -25 to -17; the logit 3m model is far more unstable (mean E +4.6 to -22, take share 0.30-0.74): the threshold itself does not stay put between refits, which is the drift cost of short windows. Importance (gain share, GBM 3m weekly) is dominated by DAY-LEVEL regime features in every quarter: rg_esnq_corr_prev1m, rg_vix_prev, rg_prior_day_range, rg_prior_rth_range, rg_esnq_corr20d, rg_prior_rv_rth (each 5-9%), with upnl and stk_real_day_pts next; path features (dd, vel30, eff120, delta60, secs_ext) never reach the top 8 in either side. In 2024 the keys are vix_prev and esnq_corr; by 2025Q3-2026Q1 prior-day range and rg_prior_rth_range take over. These day-constant features let a 3-12 month model memorise days, which is a plausible source of the failure (no re-run without them was done: next experiment). The logit 3m model keys on sess_now, R_pts, d_sess_behind, atr14, mfe, pct.

## Caveats
* Exits are tested on a 1-min decision grid, not every second; tighten is close-detected. * Multi-exit rates are high (63-72% of trades for c=0.995) because noise in E[rem] crosses -0.995 often; c=8 cuts the rate to 30-50% and gives the same sign. * Loss-rule baseline uses the approximate lc_* port. * Meta, regime, CUSUM and placebo runs cover only the windows listed. * The decisions files hold the acted-on rows (hold rows have delta 0); placebo=1 rows are included for the shuffled-label reg runs, random exits (placebo 2) are summarised, not stored per trade. * The classic-rule baseline here is a 1-min re-implementation (79/57 rules), consistent with but not identical to the 192-rule kernel of THPB. * JADE delta uses jade_pts - 1.01 (stop walk) against the MEASURED base.
