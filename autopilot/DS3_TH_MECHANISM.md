## Plain sentence
After an adverse excursion of 30 points about 64% of trades are "armed", and of those about 72% finish at the 60-point stop and 25% at the target, so the cut turns an armed trade into a fixed -30 exit that gains about +26 points on the 72% that would have stopped and loses about -90 points on the 25% that would have reached the target: it pays only if the share of armed trades that go on to the stop is above about 77% (break-even), the observed share is 70-75% in every quarter (2026: 73-75%, 2025: 70-72%, 2023-24: 70-75%) so the cut is close to a zero-sum bet per trade (mean -1.2 points per lot per cut in 2023-26; 2025 -3.1, 2026 -0.04, taken rows), and the large year-to-year swing in the stack comes from the amplification of a small per-cut difference by the PE book's 7 lots and by a second channel, faster exits freeing slots for extra trades (2026: 998 extra trades worth +113k and cut rows +200k of the +388k stack gain; 2025: cut rows -262k and extra trades -48k); the stop-continuation after -30 is somewhat higher in 2026 (74% vs 70-71%) and slightly more efficient markets (ER of 5-minute closes) go with a higher stop share across quarters (rank correlation +0.55, n = 14, descriptive), but nothing tested identifies a variable that predicts the quarter-by-quarter gain, and the cut helped (positive stack gain) in 6 of 12 measured quarters, with the interval above zero in 2 (2023Q2, 2026Q2) and below zero in 2 (2023Q4, 2025Q1).

## 2. The cut as a bet (live rule -30 / 5 s / 30x via the L2SL fire table)
On the 26,662 live-session rows the rule fires on 12,995 (49%; 57% of TAKEN rows). Per fired cut the stored delta versus the plain exit is +26.2 points where the plain trade stopped (mean over 10,269 matched cuts), -90.4 where it would have reached the target (2,925), -26.2 where it ended flat (374); break-even share of cuts on trades that would have stopped = 90.4 / (26.2 + 90.4) = 77.5%. Observed share of cuts that helped (delta > 0), TAKEN rows: 0.71-0.78 in every quarter (2025Q1 0.712 [0.68, 0.74] lowest; 2026Q2 0.775 [0.75, 0.80] highest), i.e. at or just below break-even: mean delta per lot per cut by quarter -1.6 to +1.7 except 2025Q1 -6.2 [-10.3, -2.5]; by year 2023 +0.3, 2024 -1.3, 2025 -3.1, 2026 -0.04 points per lot (taken). This single number difference of about 3 points per cut per lot, times 3,700 cuts, times PE's 7 lots, is what turns the stack between years.
Direct sum of those per-lot deltas at the final lots (TAKEN, library-exit frame): 2023 -117k, 2024 -184k, 2025 -638k, 2026 +47k, total -892k; the engine's realized effect on the same trades is +93k (section 3). The difference (about 1.3 points per lot per cut) comes from engine mechanics that this diagnostic does not model: the 70/30 split (the resting 30% add is cancelled on a cut), entries shifted by the PE confirmation / OTE-62 rows, the stop-cost frame (stops 2.45 vs market cut 0.80), and the lock stage; it is reported, not decomposed.

## 4. Why is 2026 different? (descriptive; n = 14 quarters, no multiplicity claim)
* Size of the arm relative to the market: the 5-minute true range grew from 11.6 points (2023) to 27.3 (2026) and the median RTH daily range from 185 to 347 points, so -30 points is 16% of a day's range in 2023, 14% in 2024, 11% in 2025 and 9% in 2026 (stop 60 points: 33%, 28%, 23%, 17%). The cut is shallower in relative terms each year while the P(stop/armed) has not trended (0.714, 0.716, 0.709, 0.739): shallower arms fire on more trades (the arm is reached in 18 minutes instead of 89) without changing the break-even.
* Continuation after the arm: cont is -0.3 (2023), +2.5 (2024), +4.2 (2025), +1.2 (2026) points (taken). 2026 is a "continuation" year relative to 2025 by about 3 points, but not relative to 2023.
* Regime variables (Spearman across quarters, n = 14; not corrected for multiplicity): P(stop/armed) vs ER of 5-minute closes +0.55; vs arm/daily range -0.34; vs 10:00-11:00 ET reversal rate +0.09 (reversal rate by year: 0.51, 0.49, 0.47, 0.52 for 2023-26: no pattern); vs 1-minute autocorrelation +0.02; vs realized vol +0.28. Continuation vs vol of vol +0.49, vs 10-11h reversal rate -0.41. With the 12 quarters that have a stack gain: engine gain vs P(stop/armed) +0.33, vs continuation -0.37, vs 1-minute autocorrelation +0.55, vs 10-11h reversal rate +0.44 (all inside what 12 points produce by chance: single-test 95% critical |rho| about 0.58). The only association that is not tiny is P(stop/armed) vs ER of 5-minute closes (the ER range is 0.221-0.238), and it does not carry to the stack gain (+0.22).
* PE stop distance: the library stop is 60 points for PE as for every book (not shortened for PE); PE differs by lots (7 vs 1-4) and by session mix, so PE carries 78% of the full-window stack gain and the largest swings (-359k 2025 vs +22k 2026 on the direct sum, +242k vs -65k in the engine).

## 5. Risk implication
* Quarters in which the cut helped the stack (engine ON minus OFF, $/week, 95% block-bootstrap CI over weeks, carrying weeks dropped): 2023Q2 +9.5k [+5.9, +12.7]; Q4 -7.1k [-12.3, -1.8]; 2024Q1 +2.6k [-2.0, +7.2]; Q2 -1.4k [-11.4, +8.4]; Q3 +8.7k [-0.1, +17.8]; Q4 -0.1k [-9.9, +10.0]; 2025Q1 -20.0k [-32.6, -9.4]; Q2 +7.5k [-0.7, +16.4]; Q3 -3.5k [-11.7, +4.3]; Q4 -6.5k [-17.4, +2.6]; 2026Q1 +7.2k [-8.8, +24.9]; Q2 +15.6k [+1.9, +28.3] (2023Q3 and 2026Q3 lack complete non-carrying weeks). Positive in 6 of 12 quarters; interval above zero in 2, below zero in 2. Direct per-cut sum (TAKEN rows, library frame) was positive in 3 of 14 quarters.
* Regime-contingent view: the cut costs most when adverse excursions revert (2025Q1: share of cuts that helped 0.712, mean -6.2 points per lot, -$20k/week at the stack level, 2025 as a whole -$4.8k/week with the carrying weeks dropped); it pays when they continue and trades are profitable enough that freed capacity is productive (2026Q2: 0.775, +0.8 points per lot, +$15.6k/week). The break-even is a knife-edge: a 3-point swing in the share of armed trades that stop moves the stack by about +-$20k/week at current lots. With the arm unchanged at 30 points and the market's range doubling, the number of cuts rises, so the sensitivity rises.
* Net: the cut is a leveraged bet on whether adverse excursions of 30 points continue; its drawdown benefit in the full history (DS3_ATTRIBUTION, maxDD 175k to 75k) comes from reducing the largest loss clusters, while the profit sign has flipped across years. The data cannot say which side 2027 will be on.

## 6. What additional data would settle it
The TH-L2 live log does not exist on the box. Needed: (1) per live cut, a record with entry/arm/exit seconds, price at the cut, the 5-s pulled-support value and its median-level normaliser, the lots, and a post-cut shadow track of the price for the next 60 minutes and to the original stop/target (so the counterfactual outcome of every cut is known without a replay); (2) the D7 live fills for the entries that follow a cut within 60 minutes (the freed-slot channel); (3) at least two full quarters with at least 400 armed trades per quarter (the quarterly SE of the stop share is about 1.4 points at 1,000 armed trades, comparable to the 3-point break-even swing); (4) a flag for the same-second arm (touch vs close-based adverse), because this study uses the 1-second high/low touch while the live rule uses the stored per-second upnl. With (1)-(3), the quarterly P(stop | armed) and the per-cut delta become observable live and a switch rule (for example, raise the arm in quarters when the share is below 0.77) could be tested forward.

## Caveats
* The path statistics use the library's plain 60/60 bracket and the 1-second high/low touch; the live cut uses the stored per-second adverse (close-based) plus the pulled-liquidity condition, so counts differ (64% armed vs 49% fired).
* The direct per-cut diagnostic does not reproduce the engine's realized sign (section 2); the engine decomposition (section 3) is the stack-level truth and the per-cut statistics are the mechanism diagnostic.
* Quarterly regime correlations have n = 12-14 and are inside chance; no variable is established.
* Forward-safe throughout (the arm uses seconds up to the decision second; no time-shift placebo was needed).
