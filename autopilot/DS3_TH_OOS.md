## Plain sentence
The premise that TH-L2's parameters were fitted on the same 2023-26 data is not what the record shows: the -30 / 5 s / 30x numbers are the user's D7 setting (the box's own L2SL research chose a different cell that failed its holdout), and on the data the L2 family was studied on (2023-04..2026-02-06) the rule is worth about nothing (-$1.2k/week, CI -5.3k to +3.0k, 46% of weeks positive); its entire 2026 gain arrives after the research cap (2026-02-07..2026-10-30: +$18.1k/week, CI +8.3k to +27.5k, 25 weeks, 72% of weeks positive), so the +19.8k Shapley figure is a genuine post-cap result for these frozen parameters but not a validated edge: it is one regime episode (2024 +2.6k, 2025 -4.8k, 2026 +13.7k per week), the rule behaves like a fixed -30 adverse-excursion exit (the arm alone gives +16.6k of the +18.1k after the cap; the 5 s / 30x liquidity condition adds only about 1.5-1.8k/week), the surface is a cliff in the arm (arm 24 instead of 30 turns +18k/week after the cap into +7.7k and the full-window gain from +2.0k to -2.9k) and flat in the multiple, a walk-forward re-fit would have picked a later cut (arm 45) and earned about +4.7k (2024), +0.9k (2025), +9.1k (2026) per week over TH-off instead of the frozen cell's +2.6k, -4.8k, +13.7k, and in the PE book 78% of the net full-window gain sits in five weeks; the PE 45-s confirmation has no consistent OOS contribution (+0.3k/week overall, sign flips by year, top five weeks 463% of its total).

## Caveats
* The "after" period is 25 weeks in one regime (2026 volatility, large trending days); the confidence intervals are block-bootstrap on weeks; the conclusion that the gain is regime-specific rests on 2025 being negative and 2024 near zero.
* The research cap is not the live freeze: the user may have seen later data; no TH-L2 live log exists on the box.
* Re-fit selection by Calmar is one rule fixed in advance; selection by mean gives (rank correlation 0.91) similar cells.
* The leave-one-out and Shapley numbers differ by construction (interaction with lots, locks, ER, zones).
