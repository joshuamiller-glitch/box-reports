## 3. Open-trade event study (open_trade_{JN05W2,JN05W2J,JN05W2J_NOCUT}.md)
Method: every 5th open second of each trade (1.39 M / 1.42 M / 1.55 M samples; 1,875 / 1,964 / 1,752 trades), features over the trailing 10 s: own_pull (own-side near cancels + modify-away), own_big (>=8-lot cancels), opp_add (opposing-side near adds), adv_flow (absorption: adverse aggressor volume with price held within 1 tick), adv_flow_mv (adverse aggressor volume with >=2 ticks against us), qdep (own best queue halved), flick (young-cancel share). Flag = top decile. Outcomes in ticks, + good for the position: hold_value (exit price minus mid now: negative = a cut now would have been better), fwd30, fwd120, mae30. Outcomes are RESIDUALISED on session x trailing-60 s range decile (the first run without this showed p=0.003 everywhere because MBO activity is volatility; the volatility control removes it). Stat: flagged minus unflagged by day-block t plus a circular-shift placebo (300 draws).
Result: hold_value difference ~0 in every flagged cell (|t| <= 2.8, placebo p >= 0.24 in the all-sessions rows): a cut on any of these flags would not have beaten holding. fwd30 / fwd120 differences |d| <= 2 ticks, none with placebo p<=0.05 AND |t|>=2 AND consistent sign across bases. Where cells pass the placebo it is the mae30 column (own_pull -1.7 ticks, opp_add -1.5, flick -2.6 in all sessions; -6.8 / -6.5 / -5.6 in nyam; flagged ~8% of open seconds), i.e. after an own-side pull or opposing add the next 30 s see a slightly bigger adverse swing — but it comes with no change in final hold value, so as a cut trigger it costs winners as often as it saves losers. Family count (JN05W2 / J / J_NOCUT): 104 / 108 / 104 cells, placebo p<=0.05 in 28 / 22 / 25 (expected ~5; almost all mae columns), |t|>=2 in 16 / 13 / 13 (expected ~5); cells that also have |t|>=2 AND the hold or fwd columns: 1-3 per base, signs not repeated across bases (e.g. own_big nypm fwd120 +5.2 only on JN05W2, n=542). Verdict: NULL for loss cut / early cut / near-zone hold. $ per week at the user's lots: none.
