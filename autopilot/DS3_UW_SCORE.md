## Mapping and data caveats
Levels: UW gex-levels files are computed at 20:15Z of the date (end of day): only the PRIOR day is used for any decision (no same-day levels). NQ-mapped level = (index level - UW index spot at T) x prior-5-day median NQ/index ratio (NQ = 1-s state mid; SPX x ~3.70, NDX x ~1.003). UW 'price' for NDX/SPX is a spot estimate with day-level glitches (NQ-NDX basis jumps 200-1,300 pts on ~15 days): distance features inside +-50 pts are unreliable; walls at hundreds of pts are fine. No intraday history of levels exists (strike data for past dates is end-of-day): intraday walls only from the live recorder going forward.
