## 3. Shared library and validation (zen_lib.py and companions)

Location: `l2vast/ds3/code/zen/` - `zen_lib.py` (API documented in its header), `zen_lines.py` (line registry), `zen_lines_cluster.py`, `zen_struct.py` (S1/S2 registries, vectorised grid, reality check), `zen_features.py` (Addendum 4 feature vector + M5 context), `zen_gate.py` (OOF models), `zen_bars.py` (bar builders / calibration / K4), `zen_lib_test.py` (+ `zen_lib_test.log`).

Basis: library bars (back-adjusted tick-built); 1-min OHLC fill path, STOP first in a bar that touches both, gap through the stop fills at the open, targets at the limit (no improvement), market entry = open of the bar after the closed signal bar, limit entry needs a 1-tick trade-through (stop live in the fill bar, target not), scale/BE/trail moves apply from the next bar; net = nominal + min(P9 year x exit-type correction, 0) - $4.84/20 pts (2x-cost = T-PBX formula); multi-unit positions in unit-points (1 unit = 10 lots).

Validation evidence (all in the test logs; 63 tests, 0 failures):
- **Reproduction of a known T-PBX number:** PBX_PB_ST10_SWZ_T3_f60_T1 (60/1R, mapped conservative, 10 lots): WF-PRE mean 2,261 trades ref **-1.042** vs zen_lib **-1.040** pts/trade (per-trade corr 0.9982); WF (to 2026-02-06) 3,226 trades ref 0.359 vs 0.360 (corr 0.9987); entry bars map 1:1 onto the library bars (99.98%, open price diff 0).
- **Conservatism vs the 1-s path** (same 2,261 entries, `time_1s` through the same kernel): 1-min path is lower than 1-s by 0.05 (60/60), 0.05 (20/40), 0.13 (15/20), 0.08 (10/20) pts/trade; same exit reason 99.6-100%. The screen therefore understates tight-stop books by ~0.1 pt/trade - small against MDEs of 2-4 pts, and the direct replay corrects it.
- **No look-ahead (signals use only closed bars):** every indicator, regime R1-R8, swing, FVG and OB zone (formation, edges, death time, first touch), structural swing ladder and every L1 line is IDENTICAL when all bars after a cut are deleted (cut 2025-01-02) and unchanged up to K when every later bar is scrambled; **the 60-feature Addendum 4 vector at 150 touches is unchanged when all later bars are deleted**.
- **Fills conservative:** hand-built cases (target at the limit, stop first on a tie, entry-bar exposure, gap at the open, limit-fill bar target not live, BE next bar, short mirror, break-even 20/40 = 66.7% before cost).
- **Grid identity:** the vectorised 135-stop x 64-target grid equals one-pair-at-a-time `simulate()` on 60 random pairs (counts and sums exact); a full 86,252-candidate pool grid takes < 1 s for one skip variant (sources 5 s).
- **Placebo sanity:** a random "real" set of 3,111 trades vs 40 random-in-regime draws: effect +0.21, MDE 0.68, rank 0.73. MDE prior formula reproduces the T-PBX prereg value (2.67 at n = 2,902). Learned-gate module: noise AUC 0.47 (stop rule fires), planted signal 0.77.

## 7. Best-of-N accounting and the gates

- Placebos on EVERY row: (a) random-in-regime = same regime, window, bracket (structural brackets re-derived at the random minute), random entry minute, per-side take rate, same one-open-trade rule, 100 shared-seed draws (counter-based RNG, so cells see the same draw); (b) random direction on the same minutes. In the grid screens the placebo is the exact EXPECTATION over the candidate pool (no sampling noise); the top 30 are re-run with the full draw machinery.
