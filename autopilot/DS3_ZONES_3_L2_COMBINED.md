## 1. Method points that change how to read the numbers
* Engine path: zone masks enter D7 v1.2 as `extra_skip` / `extra_mult`; B73/WT through the OTE-62 row kernel with the zone distance; U7 fixed-point warm-started from the base mask (verified identical to the cold start, max |diff| 0.0 on 2 random masks); a cell that changes no base-taken trade is stored as a no-op (identical to the base).
* **Volume trap.** A zone family that fires on 40% of the rows cuts maxDD by roughly 40% by itself (TAPEALL D20: 35,047 rows fired, 7,567 base trades removed, cut L6M/HOLD/PRE/X' +47/+39/+24/+65%). The ranking therefore uses the same-count random-skip placebo (random base-taken rows, same number per book x session, 200 draws; `rank` = share of draws with an average cut over L6M/HOLD/PRE/X' >= observed; computed on the no-interaction proxy that removes the removed trades' base P&L, finalists get engine placebos) and not the raw cut. Proxy vs engine fidelity is checked on the stage-1 cells (proxy_avg vs avg columns in an_*.csv: they differ by lock/U7 interaction; engine numbers are the reported cut columns).
* Mean deltas: inside MDE in 266 of 268 cells (L6M); outside: TVP void sess D20 (+4.5k vs MDE 4.2k, a mean GAIN) and TCP D40 (-9.9k vs 9.4k, a mean LOSS).

## 4. The ONE learned rule over price + L2 zones (section 8 of the prereg) - base D7 v1.3 labels
Walk-forward: every ISO week re-fit on the trailing 13 weeks (rolling) and, beside it, on all prior weeks (expanding); HistGradientBoosting depth 3, 70 iterations, min leaf 100; target = the row's base trade hits its TARGET (type 1; base rate 0.39); 88,490 engine rows, 51,423 with a prediction. Arms: A price lines + meta only; Bt price + tape zones; Ct tape zones + meta only; (book zones are missing: arm B with the book is NOT done). Shuffled-feature placebo: feature rows shuffled inside book x session x month, same fit, 10 replicates (30 prereg; reduced for memory, disclosed).

|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| A price roll | 0.609 | 0.619 | 0.640 | 0.612 | 0.612 | 0.2281 |
| A price expanding | 0.619 | 0.628 | 0.639 | 0.622 | 0.629 | 0.2252 |
| Bt price+tape roll | 0.604 | 0.608 | 0.629 | 0.596 | 0.617 | 0.2303 |
| Bt price+tape expanding | 0.624 | 0.631 | 0.637 | 0.623 | 0.623 | 0.2252 |
| Ct tape only roll / exp | 0.607 / 0.620 | 0.610 / 0.628 | 0.623 / 0.635 | 0.591 / 0.622 | 0.620 / 0.627 | 0.2299 / 0.2255 |
| Bt shuffled features (mean of 10; min-max) | 0.612 (0.608-0.614) | 0.617 (0.615-0.618) | - | 0.604 (0.600-0.606) | 0.636 (0.633-0.639) | - |
**Plain reading: the discrimination (AUC ~0.60-0.63) is the book x session prior (the shuffle keeps book / session), not the zones.** The shuffled-feature model scores the same AUC as the real price+tape model (0.612 vs 0.604 on L6M); adding the tape zones to the price lines does not raise AUC (0.604-0.624 vs 0.609-0.619) and the rolling 13-week fit is slightly worse than the expanding fit. Calibration (arm Bt roll, deciles of predicted P): predicted 0.18..0.61 vs observed 0.20..0.50: well calibrated in the middle, over-confident in the top decile (predicted 0.61, observed 0.50). Pooled distilled 6-leaf regression tree on the predictions: splits only on book (92% of importance) and session (10%), i.e. no zone enters the tree. A zones-only residual tree (target minus the book x session hit rate, 6 leaves, fit on rows to 2025-10, tested on HOLD): leaves based on camH3 within 21 pts (hit rate -8 pts in train, +1 in test), TCP 60-pt count >= 2 (-5 / -4 pts), TVP count>5 (-6 / -4 pts) -> only 2 of the 6 leaves keep their sign out of sample, effects <= 4 hit-rate points on <= 860 rows; nothing usable.
Policy cells on the engine (bottom 10 / 20 / 30% of predicted P skipped, or skip bottom 20% + half the next 20%; edges from the previous 13 weeks of out-of-sample predictions; D7 v1.3): all 44 cells in autopilot/ZL3_lrn_v13.csv. They are volume effects: Bt roll skip20 cut L6M/HOLD/PRE/X' +41/+36/-14/+47%, while the 10 SHUFFLED-feature models with the same rule cut L6M +11..+66%, PRE -32..+51%; the real model's rank among the 11 is mid-pack on every window. No learned cell is RISK-KEEP (none beats the shuffled-feature placebo). **Therefore the learned rule has no zone content to map to D7: the per-line table (line | D | freshness | action) is EMPTY for the learned rule; the only supported statement is the shipped one (set-line rows halved).** The unrestricted book x session prior (HM/KB/SM/PE/MV/PB x session hit rates) is a sizing question for the book/session owners, not a zone rule.
