## Reading
* **The shipped layer's L6M / HOLD cut is a constant across every lock setting** (+33..+73% vs no layer; zone-only layer 0..+20%): it is the take lines (the zones HALF layer adds about +10-15%). It does not depend on where the combined lock trips (8R..20R, none) nor on arm / keep / floor.
* **The PRE harm of the shipped layer is lock-dependent and large:** with the combined lock at 8R..16R the shipped layer's PRE maxDD is -45 to -90% WORSE than no layer (comb 8: -90, 10: -84, 12: -54, 16: -12, 20: -21), while with the combined lock off it is 0..+27% (arm / keep / floor variants: -17..+29). So the 'cut' that appears only in some lock frames is, for the shipped layer, the PRE side: the take lines remove profit that the combined lock then no longer protects against in PRE-like regimes, i.e. the lock and the take lines interact. X' improves everywhere (+4..+60%, except comb 20 where zone-only is -82%).
* **Zone-only layer** is mildly positive on L6M/HOLD in every setting (0..+20%), PRE +5..+33% except comb none (-2..+14%), X' unstable at comb 20 (-82%) and comb none keep 0.75 (-12%): a lock-sensitive layer on X'.
* **LP A40 on top of the shipped layer** adds +0..+10 points of cut in most settings and changes X' by -30..+30 points depending on the lock: no stable effect; at comb 20 it flips PRE from -21 to +17 and X' from +21 to -8.
* The per-book parameters (arm 1.5-3R, keep 25-75%, floor 8-16R) move the none-layer maxDD by +-15% and the layer cuts by +-10 points: second-order against the combined-lock multiple.
## What this means for 'LOCK-ONLY' cuts
They appear at the stack-lock multiple, not at the per-book arm/keep/floor: the same layer swings PRE from +27% to -90% between 'lock off' and 8R. A zone/take layer must be judged in both frames and at the lock multiples 10-16R before its PRE or X' side is believed.
