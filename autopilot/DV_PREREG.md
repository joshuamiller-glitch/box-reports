JL2 gets the same table; JL2 thresholds for the candidate (JL2 average weekly profit) shown as a second set of columns so candidates are on one scale.
Snooping columns (RC, SPA with B = 2000, DSR, BH 10%) are computed per FAMILY over all engine cells of the family that cover the window, differences vs JL2; trial count N = engine cells of the family in TRIALS_LEDGER.csv (variants only; controls excluded) as the stated lower bound and N including replay screens as the stated upper bound; both shown.
PBO: only for fitted choices; matrix = weekly diffs vs JL2 of all variants of the family (same windows); S = 12, 14, 16 blocks (all C(S,S/2) combos, 16 -> 12,870), purge 1 week from the train side, metric = Sharpe of weekly diff; reported beside WF.
No threshold is tuned after seeing results; the calibrated pass rule is W-PR2's (DS3_PASS_RULE.md).
