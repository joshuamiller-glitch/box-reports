## ANSWER FIRST
1. **No recipe in X12, X16, X17 or X18 passes. Nothing goes to W-DYNQ's menu** (/mnt/l2/l2vast/_dynexit/x12x18_candidates.json is empty on purpose). 17 live books (Asia 3, NY AM 7, London 3, NY PM 4), 407 recipes each (6,900 recipe-book cells) plus 20 random-timing null families (34,700 cells), all charged.
2. **Attribution (does the signal tell you about the rest of the trade, vs a random bar at the same open profit in R and time in trade): no.** 116 of 6,038 recipe-book cells have a control-adjusted t above 2 (about 139 expected by chance one-sided); 13 above 3.5. Average raw gain of exiting at the first fire is negative in nearly every family (about -1 to -3 pts per fire) on both loss and profit side. Exit map (optimal stopping on open profit x time x vol, monthly refit on past trades): worst family, -2 to -3 pts per fire, it learns cell noise.
3. **The walk-forward "PASS" flags are luck.** The pick rule (2/3 of periods shallower, income within 10%) is met by 3 of 7 session x step cells for the real families (Asia 13w X17, NY AM 13w X17 earlier run; ALL 4w/13w tighten and X18), but 20 random-timing exit families put through the same pick pass in 15-20 of 20 (Asia), 2-11 of 20 (NY AM), 9-13 of 20 (London, NY PM). Chained out-of-sample max drawdown of the real families is no better than the random median in any session: e.g. London 2w best real -42% vs random median -27% (min -59%), NY PM 13w best real -35% vs random median -23% (min -48%); NY AM and ALL real families are drawdown-neutral or worse. Asia: random early exits beat the informed ones on income (+11% vs -3% to -30%), i.e. the fixed Asia brackets lose to almost any shorter holding; this is not a signal.
4. Drawdown reductions that exist (London -33% to -48% chained at -5% to -15% income, NY PM -20% to -40% at -2% to -9% income) are shared by random exits (see 3), so they are a shorter-holding effect, not information. They pass the first clause in under half of forward periods (47-60%), not the 2/3 required.
5. Best of the clock rules: flatten at 08:25 ET (London books) has adjusted z +5 but raw gain -0.45 pts per fire and zero drawdown effect; flatten before scheduled events (2024-10+, ~650 fires): raw -3 pts, adjusted -0.4 to -1.1: no. 10:00 and 15:55 flattens: negative, NY AM -$170k.
