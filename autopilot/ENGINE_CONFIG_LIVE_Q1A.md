## 1. The differences most worth checking first (details in section 3)
1. **No-new-entry windows (09:00-09:59, 18:00-18:05, 15:00-16:54 ET).** DECISIONS.md 2026-10-20 records them as live (effective 10-19) and DIRECTIVE_O7_PIN_20261104.md still speaks of "the 09:00-09:59 block". The engine blocks 09:00-09:29 and 15:00-16:54 only through zero session lots (the 'pre' and 'close' sessions) and does NOT block 09:30-09:59 or 18:00-18:04: the Q1-A run takes 1,754 trades entered 09:30-09:59 (HM 398, KB 590, MV 570, PE 196; net +$154k, 2023 +22k, 2024 +67k, 2025 +76k, 2026 -10k) and 773 trades entered 18:00-18:04 (HM 169, KB 402, PB 30, PE 172; net -$101k), together 11.8% of 21,397 trades and +$54k net. If D7 still blocks them, the engine overstates trade count and the recent results change by that amount. **Verify against DS7.cs.**
2. **Stop cost.** D7 spec says "Native stops"; the engine runs stop exits at 2.45 pt worse than nominal (join-style, d7_v13t.py:376 STOPREL = stopcost - 1.01) from the 10-20..23 live reconciliation (n = 21), not the native 1.28 (n = 8).
3. **Lock R unit and the day boundary.** The engine's R for the per-book locks is the planned lot x 60 pt x $20 (PE $8,400, KB $2,400, HM and SM $4,800, MV $2,400, PB $1,200; 2R day gate and give-back arm, 12R week floor, +5R day profit gate) times the running-max ER multiplier only when > 1, with the label day rolling at 18:00 ET; DECISIONS.md 2026-10-18 records the D6 give-back "from 00:00" and size 8 per book. The exact DS7.cs R definition is not on disk.
4. **TH-L2 loss cut** is a reconstruction of -30 / 5 s / 30x from the stored per-second order-book table (not the live 1-second evaluation), with the market exit cost shifted to 0.80.
5. **Zone HALF D20 lines, PE 45-s confirmation pattern, OTE-62 B73, the 70/30 dip gating** are mappings (status U\* / R), not source code.

## 3. Possible engine-vs-live differences, with the evidence
1. **Entry windows (row 24).** Evidence: Q1-A taken trades by entry minute ET: 09:00-09:29 0, 15:00-16:54 0, 09:30-09:59 1,754, 18:00-18:04 773 (script: scratchpad window count; data ref_tr.pkl = Q1-A rec trades). DECISIONS.md 2026-10-20 00:53Z and 2026-10-20 "ANSWERS Q3/Q6" record the windows as live from 10-19. The 2026-11-04 O7 spec says O7 is "exempt from the 09:00-09:59 block". Net effect on the Q1-A total is small (+$54k of $2,520k) but the count (2,527 trades) and the 2026 portion matter for window results.
2. **Stop cost (row 21).** Engine 2.45 (join-fill reconciliation, n = 21 stops, StopJoin +0.52 better n = 8, StopJoinFallback -4.46 n = 12); D7 is described as "Native stops" (native measured 1.28, n = 8, pre-A/B). The user said Native "is a model input" (DS3_BETTER_BASE.md: Native worth +$0.2-0.3k/wk).
3. **Locks (rows 13, 14).** R base: planned lot x 60 x 20; live R is not on disk. Time base: 18:00 ET label-day roll vs the D6 "give-back from 00:00". The 12R week floor never binds on this base (DS3_TAIL_STRESS.md: floor-only = no lock).
4. **TH-L2 (row 10).** Reconstruction: arm on per-second close adverse, pulled support from mbp-10 bins vs median level; live: every tick, evaluated once per second on the live L2 feed. DS3_TH_OOS.md: the cut's value is concentrated in the post-2026-02 period and in the PE book; a live per-cut log would settle it (DS3_TH_MECHANISM.md sec 6).
5. **ER stale case (row 7).** Live ER has no session-break logic (4.4% stale cases after the 17:00-18:00 break and weekends). Library ER origin: thp aux 5m.er20; gap handling not audited here.
6. **Zones (row 8) and OTE-62 (row 9).** POI-library freshness at the signal second; "fresh POI within 20 points AHEAD" definition from the box; the live zone code is not on disk. Density of lines changes with price level (DS3_ZONES_3_S5: signals with >= 2 lines within 40 pts fell 35% -> 9% as NQ doubled).
7. **PE 45-s confirmation (row 16) and 70/30 (row 15).** The confirmation pattern and the dip-gate details are research mappings. A mismatch changes PE (7 lots) most, and PE is 78% of the full-window TH gain.
8. **One position per book (row 25) and busy rule.** A different live rule (allowing a second position per book, or blocking across books) changes net exposure and the cap analysis.
9. **Lot table (row 3).** PB all-session row in ss2_lib.LIVE vs the dumped asia-only row; K7 NY-PM 2 lots rests on the 10-31 reply.
10. **Back-adjusted frame (row 28).** Library prices are back-adjusted additive; round-number and level features were computed in the RAW frame where noted (poi README: RND in the raw frame); gaps at rolls are handled by the library.
11. **Fees (row 20).** $4.84 RT per contract is the box's constant; the engine also deducts SLIP 0.5 pt per contract; D7 live commissions and slippage may differ.
12. **Day lock and daily gates counting.** The engine counts realised P&L of closed trades of the same label day before the entry time (oc_lib.py:84-92); live counts "daily ±R caps" on its own session accounting (open P&L included or not unknown).
13. **X1-X5, O6/O7, S7 (not live)** are outside the core and excluded; the live account total includes X1-X5 at 2 lots (DIRECTIVE_BETTER_20261101.md) which this base omits.
