# ENGINE_KFIX_IMPACT - one page for the user (BASE-4, 2026-11-06). Research only, nothing live, no PASS claim.
## What was wrong, in plain words
When the model decides that the TH-L2 loss cut would have closed a trade early, it needs the trade's result "with the cut". It gets that by taking the outcome the trade would have had without the cut and adding a stored "cut minus no-cut" difference. The stored difference is measured against the plain textbook exit of the signal (stop or target on the original entry). But for trades with the 70/30 entry or a dip entry the model's own no-cut outcome can be different (a different entry price, so a target that is reached when the textbook trade hit its stop). The two were mixed. For most trades the mix-up is small. For some it is large: one blind-week trade was booked at +$7,716 although the cut was taken 18.75 points against the trade. A fix already existed in the code (called kfix, written on 2 November as an option) but was never switched on, and for the blind week it silently did nothing because the table it reads stops at February.
## What it changes in the headline numbers (research history, 935 days, Q1-A stack, locks on)
- Total P&L: base V0 (engine as run) -$15k (2,520k -> 2,505k, -0.6%); base V2 (09:30-09:59 and 18:00-18:04 entries skipped) +$57k (+2.3%). The sign depends on the base, because the lock rules react to a few big trades that move.
- Drawdown and ratio: HOLD maxDD 122k -> 131k on V0 (+7.5% deeper), 91k -> 101k on V2 (+10.7%); HOLD Calmar 0.185 -> 0.167 (V0), 0.260 -> 0.238 (V2). Last six months and the earlier windows get slightly shallower on V0, MID gets deeper. These moves are smaller than the usual lock-timing noise (about 17-18% of the drawdown).
- The cut trades themselves: 11,651 on V0; 22% change; 56 flip sign; 105 move by more than $1,000; the worst single swing is $17k each way. Gains and losses of the swings cancel (+$360k and -$360k), so the cut as a whole is neither better nor worse on average, only noisier.
- TH-L2 is still worth money in every case. Its value per week moves by -5% to -10% on V0 and by 0% to +28% on V2.
- Blind week: the engine's number falls from +$34.9k to +$27.9k, so the gap to the live +$104.1k widens to +$76.2k (the cut problem, and the entries the engine never sees, remain the explanation).

## What to treat with care
Pages that quote the stack's maxDD, Calmar, exposure caps, risk frontier, the Shadow lot table, the PE concentration (PE falls from 58% to 56% of the total) and the TH-L2 gain (+$18.1k/wk post-cap on V0 would be about 9% lower) use cut trades: read them as "about 10% soft" until the stack is re-run with the fix. Nothing was changed in any published number; this page is a measurement of the effect. The decision to adopt the fix (new truth base) is yours/the lead's.
