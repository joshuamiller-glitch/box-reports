## Per-rule effect series (from stored decisions, recomputed)
Exit rule r on trade i: fires iff its exit second is after entry and before the baseline exit; effect_M = rp - (pts_lib - fill_cost) where rp = stored absolute exit pts (measured fills); effect_N = effect_M - fill_cost*(baseline exit is a target fill) (no target correction in the baseline arm, as in L2SJ). Entry rule: effect = net pts per planned lot of the L2SE cell minus baseline net (unfilled = -baseline), N with the target correction set to 0. Filter/size rule: effect = (W-1)*baseline net, fires iff W != 1. Rules are restricted to the home books of their report (pooled rules: all six).
## Weights (walk-forward, weekly)
Re-fit every Monday 00:00 UTC 2023-07-03..2026-02-02. For (rule, book) use trades whose BASELINE close (closed_ts) is in [t-91 d, t) and where the rule fired (n >= 20 else weight 0): mean mu, day-clustered SE (label day). Per side group (profit, loss, entry, mult = filter+size) empirical-Bayes shrinkage toward 0: tau2 = max(0, var(mu) - mean(se^2)) over eligible pairs of the group (needs >= 5 pairs), w = max(0, mu*tau2/(tau2+se^2)) in pts per fired trade; MDE = 2.8 se printed beside. M ensemble fits on M effects, N ensemble on N effects. Trades entered in week t..t+7 d use the weights of t. Pruned version: w < 0.1 pts set to 0 (no refit of the others).
## Votes and precedence on one trade (fixed)
(1) skip: any firing positive-weight filter rule with W=0; (2) entry mechanic: the firing positive-weight entry rule with the largest weight (one only); (3) size: product of positive-weight firing multipliers, clipped [0.5, 2]; (4) exit overlay on the entry clock as in L2SJ: the EARLIEST valid exit of any positive-weight profit/loss rule (ties: larger weight); bracket wins ties. A secondary quorum aggregation (exit only if firing weight >= 50% of the side-group total positive weight) is reported as sensitivity, not headline.

## Mechanism and predicted effect
Mechanism: many weak, strongly correlated exit/entry rules (take-at-70% family dominates the exit side); shrinkage by trailing MDE removes most weights; the benefit is the residual rule-specific information. Prediction: honest-set ensemble ~ 0 +/- MDE in L6M/HOLD on N (the M gain is the target-fill correction on market exits), all-rules set positive in 2026/L6M on M only (selection on looked data), placebo (b) ranks around 0.5-0.8; total OOS gain below the stack MDE (~50-60k $/wk). ## AMENDMENT A1 (2026-10-30, before any stack statistic of the ensemble was computed)
After the first weight fit (only counts of active weights per side group were printed; no engine or proxy performance number of any ensemble existed) the prereg formula tau2 = max(0, var(mu) - mean(se^2)) was found to centre the prior on the cross-sectional MEAN of the rules, not on 0, and to give tau2 = 0 for all profit and loss rules in most weeks because the rules are strongly co-moving (same trades): that is not the "toward 0" shrinkage the directive asks for. PRIMARY is therefore the James-Stein / empirical-Bayes estimator centred on 0: tau2 = max(0, mean(mu^2) - mean(se^2)) over eligible pairs of the side group (needs >= 5 pairs), w = max(0, mu*tau2/(tau2+se^2)). The original formula (variance about the cross-sectional mean) is reported as sensitivity 'EBVAR'. Everything else unchanged.

## AMENDMENT A2 (2026-10-30, written after the first stage-A engine output was seen; labelled post-hoc)
First engine output (version 1, as preregistered) showed the multiplier side (union of ~26 lot-matched skip/double/halve rules, product clipped [0.5,2]) lowers the stack in L6M (-24k/wk), while loss-exit (+14k) and entry (+12.7k) rules help on M. The multiplier union does not preserve average risk (each rule is lot-matched alone, the product is not), which is a design defect, not a finding. The preregistered variant stays the headline row 'ALL' / 'HONEST'; a post-hoc variant 'MATCHED' is added next to it: after combining, each book's multipliers are rescaled by 1/(mean multiplier of that book's trades entered before the week, causal expanding, >= 50 trades), skip still 0, product clipped [0.25, 4]. Matched rows are tier-capped at CANDIDATE-RECENT (post-hoc) and flagged.

## AMENDMENT A3 (2026-10-30, post-hoc, labelled; after inspecting the last re-fit weights)
The last-re-fit weights showed L2EA 'keep'-type filters with mean effects of +17..+63 pts per fired trade: the L2EA multipliers are lot-matched, so a rule that keeps 11% of trades has W = 9 on kept trades (NON-EXEC, >5x lots per the L2EA report) and its weight is measured at 9x lots while the combination clips the PRODUCT to 2x: effect measure and applied action disagree for the multiplier side (a defect of the preregistered text).
