# EVENTCAL prereg (W-CAL, Part 3 of DIRECTIVE_UW_3PART_20261113) - written before any re-run scoring
Question: how much of the T-UWREGIME forecast gain of iv_term, flip, gamma, charm, vanna, tide (and the other W-UR1 groups) survives when the benchmark also contains the macro calendar and VIX. Code: code/th_cal/ (copies of W-UR1 ur_forecast.py machinery; W-UR1 files untouched).
Panel: frozen copy of W-UR1 panel.parquet (552 days 2024-10-02..2026-11-12) + event flags (event_flags.parquet; scheduled releases, known in advance; UNVERIFIED rows excluded) + VIX family and VX curve as of the PRIOR trading day close (vix_daily.parquet).
Benchmarks: IV = ATR + IV level/rank + day of week (W-UR1 'strict', BENCH=iv). CAL = IV + session event flags (any, hi, mac, earn, chair, auc for the TARGET session) + day flags (ev_day, hi_day, earn_prev, earn_amc_today) + VIX set (log VIX, VIX3M/VIX-1, VIX9D/VIX-1, log VVIX, VX2/VX1-1, VX1-VIX).
Procedure identical to W-UR1: expanding ridge, min 120 d, refit every 10 trading days (2 weeks), readings R0..R3, shifted-time null (20 draws, k in 8..60), family null = q95 over draws of the MAX gain across groups x readings per session-target, 90% CI by 5-day block bootstrap. Extra non-UW groups scored on the IV benchmark: cal:events_sess, cal:events_day, vix:set (to measure what calendar and VIX add by themselves).
Gain reported: gain_full, gain_L6M (from 2026-05-01), gain_L3M (from 2026-08-01), H1 (< 2025-11-01), H2. Survival of an input = YES vs CAL (gain>0, CI low>0, gain>family q95). Relabel rule: if an input is YES (or weak) vs IV but gain vs CAL <= 0 or not > its null q95 in the same session, label it "calendar proxy" for that session. No PASS claim; a lead needs both recent and older windows (L3M/L6M and H1) positive.
Event-day split: gain recomputed on days with ev_day=1 vs ev_day=0 (same out-of-sample predictions; no refit).
Files: /mnt/l2/l2vast/_calendar/fc/forecast_{IV,CAL}*.parquet, fam_*.pkl.
