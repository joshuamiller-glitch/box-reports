## Definitions
- Data: NQ back-adjusted 1-min bars (NQX), label days 2023-04-01 .. 2026-10-16 (bars end 2026-10-16; the research cap 2026-10-31 is not reachable on this 1-min series, the 1-s path continues to 10-30). Quarters 2023Q2..2026Q4 (2026Q4 partial: Oct only).
- Event = first trigger per (anchor, side, label-day, session): sessions Asia 18:00-02:00, London 02:00-09:30, NY-AM 09:30-12:00, NY-PM 12:00-16:55 ET (session of the SIGNAL bar). Levels are used as of the PREVIOUS bar (causal; moving levels such as VWAP, bands, mid never use the signal bar). Overnight/Asia/London ranges only after they are complete; session-specific anchors only after they exist (IB after its window); zone lines only while fresh (untouched).
- Triggers: touch_reject = bar extreme through the previous-bar level and the close back (W=0); failed_break = a close beyond then the next close back; mancini_X5_W15 = break >= 5 pt then a close back within 15 min; exhaust_N4 = 4 same-direction 1-min bars into the level (within 0.5 ATR); stretch_k = close at >= k x ATR60 from the session VWAP (first per session); tod_stretch_k = move since session open >= k x median of the prior 20 label days at the same minute (15-180 min into the session).
- Forward return: signed in the FADE direction (side x (close[j+h] - close[j])), j = signal bar, in NQ points, h = 5/15/30/60/120 min; blank if beyond the 16:55 flat or the label day. NO costs and NO stops/targets (cost reference: fee 0.24 + market exit 0.80 = ~1.0 pt round trip, stop slip 2.45 on stops). Events overlap in time across anchors (same market move counted under several anchors): cells are NOT independent.
- Touch count = first bar per (anchor, side, session) whose extreme reaches the previous-bar level; reject rate = touch_reject events / touches.
- Multiple comparisons: this table has ~733 anchor x trigger x session cells; with that many the best cell looks good under the null. Read patterns by family and by time, not single cells.
