Question: is dealer gamma a causal predictor of the 2026 NY-AM reversion (the lag switches detect it only 5-29 days late)? Answer: NO. Dealer net gamma at the open does not explain the 2026 fade P&L, and where there is a pattern it points the wrong way (the fades earn MORE on negative-gamma days in 2026).
Data and caveats: l2vast/pred/store/C1_gex.parquet, NY-session row at h = 0 (OI snapshot >= 300 s before the 09:30 open, causal by construction), NQ options (CME) with dealer sign convention A (long calls, short puts: assumed, not observed), no intraday gamma, 94% of label days known (2023-02..2026-10-16), ES / SPX / NDX gamma signs agree with the NQ sign only 0.51-0.62 (so the sign itself is a noisy construct). Flags published in `code/zones3/fade/fade_cond.py`: `Cond().flags(trades)` now returns G_KNOWN, G_POS, G_NEG, G_ZGD (trailing-250-day tercile of the signed ATR distance to the zero-gamma level), G_WALL (tercile of the distance to the nearest GEX wall), plus _L / _A suffix versions for the London / Asia open; `conditioned_report` prints G_POS / G_NEG / G_ZGD / G_WALL cuts.

## (3) Did the predictor itself shift? (share of positive-gamma days at the NY open)
2023-25: 80.1% positive (673 known days), 2026: 69.3% positive (202 days), 2025H2+2026: 80.6%. By quarter: 2025Q3 100% / 2025Q4 97% (the quarters right after the Oct-2025 break), 2026Q1 38%, Q2 94%, Q3 71%. Mean net-gamma fraction 2023-25 +0.163, 2026 +0.088. So 2026 is NOT 'positive-gamma days becoming common': if anything 2026 has more negative-gamma days (31% vs 20%) and the positive share was highest in the quarters just before and after the regime change.

## (1) Does the gamma sign predict the fade books' daily P&L? (mean $/day on G_POS days minus G_NEG days; 7 fade series + 6 live NY-AM books; t-stat from a permutation)
Whole sample (679 pos / 196 neg days): ONRTH -243 (t -1.7), VWAPF -241 (-0.9), ORBF -103 (-0.5), three-book bundle -588 (-1.5), VB2 NYPM T1 +15 (0.4), OR15 NYAM T2 +92 (0.6), live NY-AM sleeve +405 (0.8). Nothing significant; the sign of the seed fades is NEGATIVE-gamma days. 2026 alone (140 pos / 62 neg): bundle +666/day on positive vs +2,456 on negative (diff -1,790, t -2.3), ONRTH -546 (-2.0), VWAPF -1,206 (-2.1), ORBF -37, OR15 NYAM T2 +738 (+2.2: the only book that earns more on positive-gamma days in 2026), live sleeve -158. In 2023-25 the same differences are small and unsigned (bundle +97, t 0.2; ONRTH -85, VWAPF +242). Quarterly mean $/day of the bundle on negative-gamma days: 2026Q1 +2,789, Q2 +451, Q3 +1,990, Q4 (to 10-16) +4,722 vs on positive-gamma days +166, +1,107, +686, -928: the 2026 money is on the negative-gamma days (n = 62 days). Other predictors: zero-gamma distance top vs bottom tercile: ONRTH t +2.18 (p 0.026), bundle t +1.97 (p 0.065), VWAPF t 1.51; wall distance: no cell with p < 0.13.
FAMILY TEST (the harness-placebo analogue: shuffled-day labels, counts kept, 2,000 draws; 7 series x 3 predictors = 21 cells): observed max |t| = 2.18 vs null median 2.08 / p95 2.99, family p = 0.41. The single cells with raw p < 0.10 (ONRTH zero-gamma-distance p 0.026, ONRTH sign p 0.087, bundle zero-gamma-distance p 0.065) do not survive the family correction.

## (3b) Mirror: live trend books in NY-AM by gamma sign (mean $/day, pos minus neg)

So the live trend books lean slightly positive-gamma in 2023-25 (the gamma-pinning story pointing the other way from the fades), but not significantly and not in 2026; no switch is proposed (reported, not applied).

## Conclusion
Dealer gamma sign / zero-gamma distance / wall distance are not a usable causal predictor of the 2026 NY-AM reversion: p = 0.41 against the family null, the positive-gamma share did not rise in 2026, and the 2026 fade profits sit on the negative-gamma days (n = 62). The lag switches (trailing fade P&L, first-hour range share, opening-range hit rate) remain the only readable regime indicators (they detect the Oct-2025 change in 5-30 trading days). A gamma-regime row is added to FADE_ROWS_CAL.md labelled NO.
