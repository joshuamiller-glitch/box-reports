## 1. Inputs (from D7's own tape; nothing else)
* Trade prints of the traded NQ contract (exchange timestamp to 1 s or better, price, size) -> (a) 1-second bins (hi, lo, last print), (b) 1-minute bars: bar [B-60, B) holds the prints with timestamp >= B-60 and < B (open = first print, high, low, close = last print, volume = sum of print sizes IN THE SAME interval). Clock: ET wall clock (zoneinfo America/New_York); epoch minutes/seconds UTC in the log; **label day** = ET date of (wall clock + 6 h): clocks >= 18:00 ET belong to the NEXT calendar date (Sunday 18:00 -> Monday).
* No other input (no L2, no options, no second instrument). Prices in the RAW frame of the contract D7 trades; every rule uses price differences inside one contract (section 7 gives the roll adjustment). The research used a back-adjusted frame; only differences matter (offsets are in the unit-test rows).
* **Volume definition (found while building this spec):** the research minute feed took PRICE bars from the 1-s table stamp as the END of its 1-s bin (minute = (ts - 1 s) // 60 s, verified bit-exact against the research bars) but VOLUME from the stamp as the START (ts // 60 s): one second of volume sits in the next minute. The live definition above (price and volume by print time) is the correct one and is what the spec requires. Effect, measured by re-running the streaming engine with print-time volume on the old research bars: VB2NYPM_T1 9 of 524 signals changed (knife-edge band touches), VB2NYAM_T3 5 of 892, PDASIA_T2 and OR15NYAM_T2 none. ZL-1 has since fixed fl_data.py (volume now binned by (ts - 1 s)//60 s) and rebuilt the lists: VB2 NYPM T1 523 trades (windows +212/+128/+384/+238/+17/+38/+20, still STRICT), VB2 NYAM T3 893 (HOLD +356, MID -20); the research and the live definition now agree. The stack numbers in section 11 and the live-equivalent comparison in section 10 were computed on the OLD-volume lists (differences of 1 signal per VB book; not recomputed).

## 2. Common state (updated at every 1-minute bar close B, in this order)
1. **Clock:** (label day, hm = ET minute of day) of the bar's OPEN time. A new label day resets the day state (below) and stores the prior day's RTH range.
2. **ATR (5-minute Wilder, 14), as-of the signal bar.** 5-minute buckets = (epoch minute // 5) (clock aligned :00-:04, :05-:09, ...). A bucket's high/low/close accumulate from the minute bars that exist in it. The bucket is COMPLETE when its :x4 minute bar closes, or when the first bar of a later bucket arrives (a missing :x4 minute does not wait). On completion: TR = high - low for the first bucket ever seen, otherwise max(high - low, |high - prior bucket close|, |low - prior bucket close|) (the gap rule: the prior bucket close is the previous COMPLETE bucket regardless of overnight/weekend/holiday gaps, no reset); ATR = TR for the first bucket, else (13 x ATR_prev + TR) / 14. The ATR used at bar t is the value after the buckets complete at or before t, i.e. it INCLUDES the 5-minute bucket that ends with t when t is a :x4 minute. State: ATR, prior bucket close, the open bucket's high/low/close. Warm-up: the seed decays as (13/14)^n per bucket (1e-9 after one trading day); replay at least 3 label days before the first live signal (section 7).
3. **Closes/extremes:** the last 6 closes (c[t], ..., c[t-5]), the last 5 highs and lows (bar t included), across day boundaries.
4. **RTH prior-day levels:** during every label day accumulate high/low/count of bars with hm in [09:30, 16:00); at the day rollover the prior-day levels are (high, low) if count > 30 else UNDEFINED (no PD signals that day). No special case for early-halt days (their partial RTH range is the level).
5. **RTH session VWAP and sigma** (VB2 books): anchored at the first bar of the label day with hm in [09:30, 18:00), bar included, accumulate over every bar that exists from there: tp = (high + low + close) / 3; cv += v; cpv += tp v; cp2 += tp^2 v; den = max(cv, 1); VWAP = cpv / den (defined when cv > 0); **sigma** = sqrt(max(cp2 / den - VWAP^2, 0)) = the volume-weighted standard deviation of tp around the VWAP (not the std of closes; typical NY-PM sigma 20-120 pt); band U = VWAP + 2 sigma, L = VWAP - 2 sigma (as of the bar INCLUDING bar t); pos = the number of bars accumulated before bar t (pos = 0 for the first). The band values of the PREVIOUS bar (bar t-1 of the same accumulation) are kept.
   **VWAP convention:** one session VWAP from 09:30 ET for both NY-AM and NY-PM (NY-PM does NOT reset at 12:00); the VWAP of Z2's NY-PM cells resets at 12:00 and is a different trade set (4.4% same-bar overlap, ZL-2 audit 4).

## 3. Book definitions (frozen; every rule is exact; reference code `fade_books.py`, classes VB2NYPM_T1, PDASIA_T2, VB2NYAM_T3, OR15NYAM_T2)
All books: evaluated at the close of bar t after section 2 has been updated with bar t; require ATR defined and > 0 (otherwise the bar is skipped and no event is consumed); **one event per side per label day per book** (the event is consumed when the entry condition is met, even if the shape filter below drops the trade); the signal is logged at signal_sec = open of bar t + 60 s = B.
**Shape filter (all books):** side = -1 (SHORT) for an upper-level event, +1 (LONG) for a lower-level event. ext5 = max of the last 5 highs (SHORT) / min of the last 5 lows (LONG), bar t included. stop = ext5 + b x ATR (SHORT) or ext5 - b x ATR (LONG) (b = buffer of the table). R = |stop - close_t| (close_t = the bar close); stop_pts = R; tgt_pts = k x R (k = target multiple of the table); the trade is dropped unless R >= 5.0 and tgt_pts >= 4.0. tstop_s = time stop x 60. Flat 16:55 ET. lots = 1.0.
**VB2NYPM_T1** (touch-and-reject of the +-2 sigma band, NY-PM): bar t has hm in [12:00, 15:00) and pos >= 30 and sigma > 0 and VWAP defined. Upper event (SHORT) if high_t >= U_t and close_t < U_t and close_{t-1} < U_{t-1} (the band of the previous bar); lower event (LONG) if low_t <= L_t and close_t > L_t and close_{t-1} > L_{t-1}. b = 0.5, k = 0.5, T = 60 min.
**VB2NYAM_T3** (exhaustion at the +-2 sigma band, NY-AM): bar t has hm in [09:30, 12:00) and pos >= 30 (the 31st bar of the session, 10:00 on a normal day) and sigma > 0. Upper event (SHORT) if close_t < open_t and high_t >= U_t - 0.25 ATR_t and the four previous closes rise in sequence (c[t-1] > c[t-2] > c[t-3] > c[t-4] > c[t-5]); lower event (LONG) if close_t > open_t and low_t <= L_t + 0.25 ATR_t and c[t-1] < c[t-2] < c[t-3] < c[t-4] < c[t-5]. (No touch of the band is required beyond the 0.25 ATR proximity.) b = 0.5, k = 1.0, T = 120 min.
**PDASIA_T2** (failed break of the prior day's RTH extremes, Asia): Asia bars = hm >= 18:00 or hm < 02:00 of the label day, counted from 0 (k = number of Asia bars before bar t); active from k >= 5 (the 6th Asia bar, 18:05 on a normal day) and only if the prior-day levels are defined (PDH = prior label day RTH high, PDL = low). Short event if close_{t-1} > PDH + 0.1 ATR_t and close_t <= PDH; long event if close_{t-1} < PDL - 0.1 ATR_t and close_t >= PDL. b = 1.0, k = 2.0, T = 15 min. One event per level per Asia session.
**OR15NYAM_T2** (15-minute opening range failed break, NY-AM): the range = high/low of the first 15 bars (k = 0..14) with hm in [09:30, 12:00); active only if the 16th bar (k = 15) opened no later than 18 minutes after the first (a bar-gap guard). From k >= 15 until 12:00: short event if close_{t-1} > OR_high + 0.1 ATR_t and close_t <= OR_high; long event if close_{t-1} < OR_low - 0.1 ATR_t and close_t >= OR_low. b = 1.0, k = 2.0, T = 120 min.
**No-overlap:** one open position per book (a signal while the same book is open is logged and marked skipped by the register runner = the harness no-overlap rule: the new first fill must come after the previous exit). The four books are independent and may be open together; no rule uses an outcome, a later bar or another book. Raw signals vs simulated trades: 935 vs 824 for OR15, 524 vs 522-524 for VB2 NYPM.

## 4. Order of evaluation inside D7 (per second s; this order is the unit-test contract)
(a) append the prints of second s to the 1-second bin and to the open 1-minute bar (prints with timestamp < B belong to the bar that ends at B); (b) if s is a minute boundary B (s % 60 == 0): finalize bar [B-60, B), then in this fixed order: section 2 steps 1-5 (clock/day rollover, ATR bucket, closes/extremes, RTH range, VWAP/sigma), then the books VB2NYPM_T1, PDASIA_T2, VB2NYAM_T3, OR15NYAM_T2, each producing zero or one event per side; (c) for every kept signal: write the log row (signal_sec = B) and arm the order: first fill at the last print at or before B + 1 s, then the resting stop and target at E1 -/+ stop_pts / tgt_pts, the time exit at E1's second + tstop_s (market), flat 16:55 ET; (d) every second while a shadow position is open: stop first, then target (the target is only eligible from the second after the first fill), then the time stop, then flat; (e) close the shadow position, write the fill row. Everything is evaluated at minute boundaries only: no per-second trigger in any of the four books.

## 5. State and cost
Common: ATR (1 float), prior bucket close, 3 open-bucket floats, 6 closes, 5 highs, 5 lows, label day, RTH high/low/count, prior-day high/low, VWAP accumulators (cv, cpv, cp2, pos), previous-bar bands. Books: per-day done flags (2 booleans each); PDASIA_T2 an Asia bar counter; OR15 the range high/low, the bar counter, the open time of the first bar and an active flag. O(1) per bar, under 1 KB, no history arrays. Day reset at the label-day rollover (18:00 ET).

## 7. Roll, holiday, restart handling
* **Roll (contract switch).** All state is held in the frame of the contract D7 trades. At a switch, once, between the last bar of the old and the first bar of the new contract, add delta = (new contract price - old contract price at the SAME minute close) to every stored PRICE of the old contract: the last 6 closes, 5 highs and lows, the open ATR bucket (high/low/close) and the prior bucket close, the current day's RTH high/low and the stored prior-day levels (PDH, PDL), the OR15 range, and the VWAP accumulators (cpv += delta cv; cp2 += 2 delta cpv_old + delta^2 cv; bands and VWAP += delta). ATR itself and sigma are differences and do not change. `fade_books.Ctx.roll_adjust(delta)` implements exactly this; `test_fade_shadow.py` rolls at 60 random minutes with random +-20..400 pt deltas (also mid-session) and the signals after the roll equal the single-frame replay in 60/60 cases. About 4 rolls a year (15 contract switches in the 2023-2026 tape).
* **Prior-day levels in the raw frame:** PDH/PDL are the RTH extremes of the previous label day measured in the contract that traded that day, re-based by the roll adjustment above if the contract changed since.
* **Holidays and early halts:** books run on every label day on which the bars exist. A label day with <= 30 RTH bars gives no PD levels the next day. Early-halt days (13:00 or 13:15 ET): rules unchanged; a VB2NYPM_T1 or other open position is flattened at the halt (the shadow P&L closes at the last print; the research exits at the first 1-s print at or after the time stop, which can be after the reopen: small, rare). Full closures: no bars, no row.
* **Restart:** replay the bars of the previous 4 calendar days (at least 3 label days) before the first live signal, with signal output suppressed for signal_sec < restart time; `test_fade_shadow.py` restarts at 120 random minutes with a 4-day warm-up and the signals of the next 1,500 minutes equal the full-history replay in 120/120 cases. A restart inside a session reproduces the VWAP/sigma accumulators from 09:30 and the Asia/OR15 counters from the session start; do not backfill across a restart longer than the anchor.
* **Data gaps:** bars that do not exist are not synthesised; thresholds counting bars (pos >= 30, Asia k >= 5, OR15 k >= 15, RTH count > 30) count existing bars. ATR/bucket logic is clock based.
* **Known rare divergence:** the research table includes a 5-minute bucket in the ATR at its last EXISTING minute even if that is not the :x4 minute; a streaming implementation cannot know that. The reference code completes the bucket at :x4 or at the next bucket's first bar; the signal lists above show no difference over 3.7 years.

## 8. Unit-test rows from history (recomputed independently from the raw 1-second print table, raw contract frame)
