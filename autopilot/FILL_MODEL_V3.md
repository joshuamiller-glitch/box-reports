**By lot count (V3 split model; live - model):** 1 lot +0.48 (n 105, sd 1.9); 2 lots +0.21 (30); 3-4 lots -0.63 (32); 5+ lots +0.38 (20). **Without the 70/30 add** the same rows read +0.48 / -1.32 / -1.26 / -1.12: the multi-lot live entries are 1.1-1.3 pt better than market-only because 30% rest at the dip.
**Slicing (1 lot per dt s):** dt = 1, 2, 3 s give the same mean error (-0.09 / -0.23 / -0.24 pt over all lots, MAE 1.57 / 1.60 / 1.68): price drift over the 2-3 s of slicing is not detectable against the 2-pt noise, so dt = 2 s is kept for timing only (directive). Walking the same-aggressor tape for the whole size is worse (MAE 1.70). Using the touch instead of the first print: -0.73 pt (live 0.7 pt better than the pre-trade touch), MAE the same.
**Timing:** the first print falls median 0.19 s (p90 1.05 s) after signal+1 s; 114 of 136 timed live first fills are in the first second (ZL-2). **Partial fills:** a live qty is the filled total; the model fills every market slice (no partials observed); the dip add is the only unfilled-risk component (model: converted to a market slice at 900 s, option `limit_unfilled='drop'`).
**Noise:** the residual sd of a single entry is about 2 pt (about $40 per lot): the model gives the mean, not each fill; a Monte-Carlo option is a bootstrap of the residuals (not implemented, stated).
## 2. Targets
85 of 86 live targets: model level = average live entry + nominal; the model fill time (first print at/through the level) is within 2 s of the live exit second on 92%, median +0.5 s earlier than the logged second (live fill latency), 6% earlier by more than 2 s (P7 multi-slice average prices put the level slightly off), 1% later. All 86 live fills are at nominal (mean deviation 0.004 pt). **The 12 without a print through the level in the exit second**: touch only (11) or none (1): at the level the tape traded >= the lots in 11 of the 12 (K7 1-2 lots with 1-8 contracts, P7 4 lots with 5, P7 7 lots with 15, M7 3), the 12th (P7 7 lots, level 0.07 pt away) is the multi-slice average. So a touch with enough volume at the level fills (no queue loss detected); a touch with less volume than the order is untested (no such case in the week).
## 3. Stops (n = 2, not a calibration)
Two live stops, both shorts on 11-03 (odd date = arm A Native per D6_LOG_WRITER_SPEC; the directive's 'K7 JoinThenMarket 3 s' is the even-date arm): M7 entry 28063.50 exit 28144.25 (80.75 pt), K7 entry 28072.00 exit 28155.00 (83.00 pt). The nominal 75 inferred by ZL-2 does not fit the tape: with 75 the level was first printed 35.6 s (M7) and 6.9 s (K7) before the logged exit second; with 80 the trigger print is in the exit second for both (M7 28143.50, K7 28154.00 gapping through 28152.00) and the market fill is exactly the opposite touch of the row after the trigger + 1.00 pt for BOTH (the figure `stop_extra` = 1.0). So: nominal stop 80 pt for K7 and M7 (inferred; other books unknown), Native exit = touch + 1.0 pt, i.e. 0.75 (M7) and 3.00 (K7) pt beyond the level, mean 1.9 pt versus the historical 1.28 (native) / 2.45 (join) figures. JoinThenMarket (K7, even dates) is implemented from the DIRECTIVE_PLAN36 description but has no live observation in this week; applied to the two stops it would have given M7 a join fill (-1.75 pt vs live) and K7 a market fill at 3 s (-0.75), i.e. it cannot be validated; labelled so. n needed to separate native 1.28 from join 2.45 at 95% is the register's job (D6 log).
## 4. Cuts and market exits
99 live cut-type exits (ThL2Cut 53, PolicyCut 39, BookLock 3, PolicyTake 2, ThL2Trail 2). Model: decision second d = logged exit second - 1, market 1 s later at the opposite touch of that row + 0.57 pt. Error (live - model): mean 0 by construction, sd 1.94, MAE 1.35 pt (touch at the exit-1 second would be 2.0 pt worse on average, first print at/after the exit second +1.13, mid at the exit second -1.18). By book: K7 +0.05 (sd 1.4), P7 +0.01 (2.3), A7 +0.09 (2.3), M7 +0.05 (1.6); the O7 cuts (2) are thin (cost 5.0 pt, error -3.9). By spread bucket the extra over the touch is flat (0.7 / 0.7 / 0.2 / 0.9 pt for spreads <=0.6 / 0.6-1 / 1-2 / >2 pt), by lot count flat (0.56 / 0.41 / 0.79 / 0.50 pt for 1 / 2 / 3-4 / 5+ lots) - no size slope detected up to 10 lots.
## 5. Module
fillsim.py: `Path(tape, table)`; `entry_fills`, `target_exit`, `stop_exit(arm='native'|'join3')`, `market_exit`, `simulate_trade(P, sig_sec, side, lots, book, stop_pts, target_pts, cut_dec_sec=None, cut_type=None, flat_sec=None, params=PARAMS)` -> entry avg, slices, exit time/type/price, points. The earliest of target / stop / cut / flatten wins; a resting add that has not filled by the exit is cancelled and the trade re-evaluated on the market part. Inputs: tape arrays (ts ns, px, sz, side) and a per-second table with bid_px, ask_px (ts = bin END; `Path.book(sec)` = last finite row <= sec). Unit tests (test_fillsim.py, 8): first print rule and slice timing, short bias sign, split add and its cancellation on an early exit, target touch, stop native (never better than the level; short symmetry), join fill vs market, cut price with per-type extra, earliest exit wins. Parameters are one dict `PARAMS`; every choice above is a key.
## 6. Tie-out against the 187 live trades (model entry + model exit; live cut decisions supplied as exit second - 1; stop 80)
* Entry error (live - model): all +0.23 pt, sd 2.0, MAE 1.45; by book table above.
* Target exits (85): price error 0.004 pt, time error median +0.5 s.
* Cut exits (99): price error sd 1.94, MAE 1.35 (per type in 4).
* Stops (2): price 0.00 / 0.00 pt, time +1.1 / +1.4 s.
* Exit-type agreement: Target 85/86 -> target; cuts 95/99 -> cut; stops 2/2 -> stop. The 4 disagreements are cut trades where the model's own (better-priced) entry reached the 60-pt target before the live cut (3 K7 trades, model +60 vs live -44.6 / -34.8 / +27.0 pt, +6.7k) and one P7: with the live entry price as the fill price the target is not reached first. This is the one place where the fill-price noise (about 2 pt, 7 pt on the worst K7 entry) flips a trade; the module therefore also accepts a supplied fill price.
* P&L (points x $20 x qty, no fees): live 105,244, model 106,727 (+1.4%); live pnlNet 104,095. By book model/live: A7 -1,010/-1,415, H7 -913/-775, K7 22,349/14,845 (the 3 flips), M7 6,525/6,738, O7 672/660, P7 79,360/85,302, U7 580/624, X3 -1,008/-910, X4 -49/-175, X5 221/350. ThL2Trail (2 trades, trailing exit) is not modelled (model +41.6 vs live +26.1 pt: it exits at a cut, the trail logic belongs to the exit rules).
## 7. Limits and assumptions
(a) In-sample calibration on one week; entry noise sd 2 pt; the 70/30 dip-3 add and 900 s timeout are the engine config's values, fitted only through the bias of multi-lot entries (dip 2: -0.43, 3: -0.08, 4: +0.03 pt, MAE 1.55 / 1.54 / 1.75). (b) Stop nominal 80 pt, n = 2; arm assignment by date parity is from the log spec, not observed. (c) 10 live trades have pnlNet implying a different lot count than qty (ZL-2); the tie-out uses qty. (d) The live csv times are bar-aligned for 149 of 187 entries; true seconds only for late slices. (e) Cuts: the decision second is taken as exit second - 1 (live latency 1 s from the directive), which the data support (touch at the exit second has the lowest error) but cannot prove. (f) No fees in the tie-out. (g) Single-lot entries are 0.48 pt worse than the model and multi-lot are unbiased: the model uses no per-book offset (K +0.75, A +0.84 would be a per-book bias option, not enabled).
