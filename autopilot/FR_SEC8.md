## 8. RECOMMENDATIONS, IN MY OWN WORDS, RANKED

**1. Run live: the pair, one size step down, with the latency measurement on from day one.** The only low-assumption way to cut drawdown is size. At 8/8 there is a 70% chance of a >30% drawdown and 29% of >40% over a year; 6/6 puts the 40% case at <= 10% for a cost of ~25% of net (all-in $2.06M vs $2.74M over the window; weekly Sharpe unchanged). That is a risk decision for you, but nothing else on the table buys as much for as little assumption. Keep the day lock and week floor (insurance-flagged, cheap). Do not re-fit, do not touch the EMA200 filters (ST off = -93% net), and do not add overlays: about 150 were tried. The pair is a regime-bound edge (no gross edge 2013-22, nothing in price statistics explains why), so treat the 2023-26 Sharpe as best-case; watch the REGIME tracker and the HM 8-week Sharpe early-warning (fired 2026-09-06 with 72% of the loss already booked). Expect fill-timing luck: about 0.2 of the 1.90 Sharpe is probably fill-second luck (random 0-2 s delays give 1.71 +/- 0.15).

**2. Paper-run the FVG family in a separate NT8 account, starting now, at no capital.** It is the only item that survived family-level nulls, pre-registered replication, tick replay and a causality audit, and the only one whose live weeks can confirm it inside a year (IMP3 15-24 weeks, VC 13, E1 17-21, P1 31-33). Run IMP3 tf3 m1.5 as the primary and VC k1 m1.125 as a second arm. Go live only after the paper fill rate and R/trade sit inside the frozen bands over >= 300 orders (stop if the VC fill rate is < 70%, IMP3/E1/P1 < 60%). When live, start at 10% share, move to 20% only after the forward promotion rules are met, never above 20% before 26 live weeks, and prefer the constant-risk blends (80/20 or 70/30 with the pair) over additive: on the 52-week bootstrap they reduce P(DD>$200k) from 0.46 to 0.02-0.07, and they beat the pair even if the sleeve edge is zero because they hold less pair risk. Additive sleeves only help if the sleeve edge is above ~40% of the backtest. Do not read the Sharpe of 4-5 for pair + VC as real: it rests on limit-fill assumptions, and the lesson of the whole program is that the first live numbers will be lower.

**3. Forward-test, at zero capital, and kill on the rules.** K4 family (as one signal; arbiter is the forward weeks), ES dip-buy (trade count: ~2 years for 13 more trades), GC fade ensemble (R5 sign test at 8 complete weeks), and the index-fade cluster (Q34, BM1/BJ2, SW4, SW20, Q12, Q20 as ONE hypothesis; REGIME tracker flags it). FA3 says weekly P&L cannot confirm 18 of 21 leads within a year, so these are kill-watches: if a lead is below its band after 6 weeks, retire it. Do not allocate capital to any of them on the strength of the backtest.

**4. Stop spending on search.** The on-disk cell supply is exhausted (sweeper out of unclaimed cells; 25 left need data we lack). Further batches only manufacture LEAD labels: the program-wide gate-3 count is at chance and a LEAD label alone is ~25 expected false positives. Specifically stop: pair-side overlays, filters and tilts; ML on bars; exits and management; lock/floor variants; option-regime and gamma sleeves; ICT concepts other than the FVG retrace; 5-min trend families; cost-limited fades (passive entry does not rescue them: adverse selection); extra cross-instrument and market-structure studies; and any new long-only rule judged on the old random-sides null. Stop buying data: no more mbp-10, no GC/CL ticks until the GC fade passes 8 forward weeks, no re-buy of NQ L2. The only continuing spend is the ~$6 weekly extension (plus daily and crypto bars, ~$0.03). Keep the ~$1,060 headroom.

**5. If you want to keep one research line, make it execution and the live feedback loop, not new signals.** Live fills calibrate slippage and the latency gate (the pair's biggest known fragility), and decide whether the FVG limit fills are real. After 4-6 weeks of live fills, re-run the pair and the FVG replay with measured delays and slippage; that re-run is worth more than any further backtest family. If NQ L2 lands, run the pre-registered L2 families once, report-only; do not extend them.

**What would change this advice.** A FVG paper run with fill rates and R per trade outside the bands (retire the family and stop all sleeve work); a REGIME flag below -1.10 twice (assume the 2023-26 environment is ending and cut size further); live latency above 5 s median (fix before any trading); or the NQ L2 data showing a stable depth effect (reopen the L2 lines only then).
