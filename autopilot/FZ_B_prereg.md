## Bases, units, honesty rules (from CF-A/CF-B/CF-ENG lessons)
Bases: M0 = market at the signal (1 lot), S73 = the 70/30 plain 3-pt dip split (the floor; the rules are always compared with it and with M0), W0 = pure wait (full lot at the 3-pt dip). Fill bases: N (primary, selection), M (measured; must also be >0 in PRE and X'), L = LIVE-CAL (N with stop-class exits 2.45 instead of 1.01 pt per lot-fill; reported for every look row).
Units, always together: (i) per PLANNED lot (equal weight per signal), (ii) per CONTRACT of final position (sum pts / sum lots of the entered trades), (iii) trades per week, (iv) kept vs skipped P&L, (v) lock-engine stack $/wk (ENGINE books ST HM WT KB SM PE; PB DN MV MA are non-lock and labelled) for the selected finalists with skipped signals removed from the engine (fill index -1). A result is trusted only if it holds in planned-lot AND contract units AND (for finalists) on the stack. Moved stops/targets/deeper limits are STRUCTURE: the real test is the random-level placebo with the same distance distribution; size/stop-risk-matched rules are reported at equal exposure (sum m*y / sum m) and as the raw change.
"add" for a take-only/size rule = (mean of entered trades) - (mean of all signals of the same scope), i.e. the gain per entered trade; the total effect (gain_wk) is the entered sum minus the all-signals sum per week and is usually negative for filters that skip positive-mean trades - both printed.

## B3 STOPS AT THE LOCATION
Masks = B1 pooled-selected POI-type cells (POI / POICLS / POITF, D<=10), per base M0 and S73. When K: stop = entry-to-POI-near-edge distance + buffer b in {2,4,8} pts (hybrid with the fixed 60 = the tighter of the two, identical because dist+b<60); lots flat or risk-matched (lots = min(2, 60/stop)); target options: unchanged (60), RR1 (= stop distance), RR2 (2 x stop), liquidity target (nearest fresh POI of any class ahead at >= 5 pts, minus 2 ticks, else 60). ## B5 COMBINATIONS (CF-COMBO method)
