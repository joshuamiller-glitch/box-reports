## Why it does not pass (evidence)
1. **G1 is capped by trades the engine takes and live did not (engine-only), not by the cut rule.** WS3 census: engine 242, live 187, matched 143; engine-only 99: class A1 29 (live was inside a trade of that book when the engine entered: exit-timing cascade), A2 1, C 8 (after a live BookLock), **B 61 (live was flat, the signal passed every engine gate, live did not trade it: HM 29, K7 9, M7 9, P7 8, A7 3, U7 3)**. G1 ceiling if every live trade were matched 0.654; with class B removed (diagnostic only) 0.831. Even with the live EXIT times forced (oracle) G1 is .544 and class B stays 61: it is not an exit or fill problem.
2. **HM: live H7 is silent after Mon 11:10 (3 trades, 0 matched); the engine takes 29 class-B HM trades.** Tested and rejected: five VWAP-reset anchors, 48 HMA/EMA parameter sets, the H7 SkipBot3 slot, locks, caps, blocks (BLIND_DEFECTS_BASE4.md FOLLOW-UP). Needs the live HM parameters/gates or the D6 skip log.
3. **P7/K7/M7 class B are not explained by the day locks as coded.** lockinfer.py marks each live book to the tape per second and tests -2R / +5R / give-back(2R, 50%) for R = 1200 x lots (1..10): the R values that do not contradict the live entries (P7 R >= 7 lots, K7 >= 3) lock none of the class-B signals; the R values that would lock them (P7 R <= 6 lots, K7 R <= 2) contradict live entries after the lock. The skipped signals (P7 Tue 02:16-08:29 after a +16,700 label day, K7 Tue after a -3,840..-5,090 day, M7) have an unknown cause; candidates for the user's log: dollar-R of the day lock per book, cooldown after a profitable/lost run, session guards, U7/K7 shared state.
4. **Live-only trades (44) the engine lacks:** R0 no signal row 14 (M7 8 = MV rows still thinned, ZL-1 building the unthinned MV rows; H7 3; K7 1; P7 1; U7 1), R1 row exists but candidate False 13 (P7 9 + K7 4: the P0 skip slot and the PE confirmation rule; removing them adds more engine-only trades than it recovers: G1 .515-.528), R3 passes but engine busy 14, O7 3 and X 7 (no engine book / X panel outside the lock engine).
5. **G3 cannot be reached by the trigger alone.** ZL-2: the live default rule (arm 30 / MULT 30 / 5 s / N 1) agrees with the live cut/no-cut decision on 79.6% (strict) / 87.1% (lenient) of 186 trades; the fit-selected rule (arm 25 / MULT 60 / 3 s / N 2 / extreme within 5 s) 84.4% / 88.7% (a labelled hypothesis row, not the Gate 0 rule). Our sequential engine cuts 127-139 trades against 92 live: the pulled-support condition is satisfied on nearly every armed second (12 of the 100 live trades that reached -30 were held with support >= 100x). Something outside the stored rule disables or delays the live cut (what the live code reads for depth, what disables it).
6. Fill noise: about 2 pt per fill (Z1), so no trade-level price gate is tighter than that; cut timing: 60 of 92 live cuts have an engine cut pair in the same trade, about 30 within 10 s.

## A1 CASCADE DIAGNOSTIC (lead order; BASE-4; ws3 on tree v3, live default rule; PRE-PROOF; table /mnt/l2/l2vast/ds3_base4/defects/v2/a1diag.csv)
A1 = engine-only entries made while live was INSIDE a trade of that book: 30 (K7 13, P7 11, M7 3, A7 3); 26 are the same side as the live position, 4 opposite. Question: exit-timing cascade or a position-rule difference?
- **Exit timing is the lever.** For 13 of the 30 the engine trade immediately before is the MATCH of that very live trade and it exited EARLIER than live (by 32 s to 8,724 s, median 1,252 s): engine exit types policy_cut 9, target 3, thl2_cut 1, while the live exit of the same trade was Target 8, PolicyCut 2, ThL2Trail 1, Stop 1, ThL2Cut 1. I.e. in 9 cases the engine cut a trade that live held, and the book became free early; the other 15 A1 entries are descendants of such chains (e.g. K7 Mon 20:09 -> 22:36 PolicyCut: engine cut at 20:11:34, then re-entered at 20:30, 22:00 and 22:33 while the live trade was still open; K7 Tue 01:48 -> 02:55 Stop: engine cut at 01:51 and re-entered 02:03, 02:30, 02:48).
- **Two roots are a P0 skip-slot effect, not exits:** K7 Sun 19:06:03 and P7 Sun 18:51:03 (11 lots) have no engine trade before them: the engine skipped the live book's first trade of the night with the old-panel slot (K7 SkipBot3 / P7 SkipTop3, which live did not apply), so a later signal enters while live is inside the skipped trade.
- **No evidence of a one-position-per-book rule difference or an add-leg double position:** ws3 gates strictly by fill-model exit times; every A1 entry occurs after the engine's own previous trade ended; the 4 opposite-side entries sit inside the same chains.
- **Conclusion:** the cascade comes from the engine exiting a trade (mostly by the TH-L2/loss-line cut) that live held - the same 8-12 'held' trades ZL-2 could not separate with any rule variant (pulled support >= 100x, adverse -38 to -82 pt, live did not cut). Fixing that one live behaviour (why the live cut does not fire on those trades) would remove up to ~28 of the 30 A1 trades and most of the G1/G3 gap from exit timing; the P0 slot accounts for 2.
