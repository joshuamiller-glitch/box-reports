# HELD_TRADES_DIAG - what separates the live trades that reached -30 and were not cut from the ones that were cut (ZL-2, 2026-11-06; hypotheses for the user's live-code check, no tuned rule)
Data: the 186 live trades of the blind week with a tape series (live csv + P2 1-s tables), those whose adverse (last print vs the live average entry) reached <= -30 before the live exit: **100 trades, 88 cut (ThL2Cut or PolicyCut) and 12 held** (the 12 'held' = the 8-12 unexplained trades of DS3_THL2_LIVE_FIT.md / DS3_TH_V3_LIVE_CALIB.md). The 55 cut trades the lead mentions are a subset (BASE-4's matched pairs); all 88 are used here. One week, 12 held trades: nothing below is a finding; each line is a question to put to the live code. Code l2vast/ds3/code/v2/held_diag.py (tables /mnt/l2/l2vast/ds3_v2/held_diag3.pkl). Mann-Whitney AUC = P(held value > cut value); with 12 vs 88 only p < 0.01 means anything, and a 12-trade difference cannot support a rule.

## 3. Reading and what to ask the user to check in the live code (not a tuned rule)
No single tested variable separates the 12 held trades from the 88 cut ones. The consistent weak signals are (a) thinner pulled flow at the arm (held median 266 contracts vs 470; AUC about 0.2 on every definition), (b) held trades more often had other positions open (10 of 12) and more often entered in the old panel (4 of 12 vs 13 of 88), (c) a deeper worst adverse by construction (nothing cut them). Hypotheses 1, 3, 6, 7 and the entry-slice variables are rejected as separators. Questions for the live code / log:
1. For the 12 trades (table, section 1): what did the cut evaluation return at every second from the first -30 second to the exit - 'pulled_own_10s', 'med_own_level', the arm state, the decision? The D7_LOGGING_REQUEST th_cuts.csv logs only fires; the v1.5 TH log (every evaluation) answers this directly. Without it the 12 trades stay unexplained.
2. The measure of pulled liquidity: our table sums cancel size over all 10 levels on the supporting side over the last 5 s; held trades sit at 131-412 contracts (5 s). Is the live threshold absolute or relative to a median level that is computed differently (NT8 DOM depth, how many levels, which side, queue-size units / per-order sizes)? A live median level near 5-10 (instead of 2) would put the live threshold near 150-300 contracts, which is where the held trades lie.
3. Is the cut suppressed or serialized while another position is open (10 of 12 had one), e.g. a shared account-level order lock, an exit order already working (the target limit order, JoinThenMarket stop timers), or a cut that waits for an order-state flag? Which orders were working on those trades at the first -30 second?
4. For the 4 P0 trades (before Mon 08:20): did the old panel's TH-L2 loss cut run at all while th_profit / tighten was ON (profit-mode state may take the exit path)? For the 8 P2 trades: which policy lines were active (a policy hold / take line, and the order of PolicyCut vs ThL2Cut evaluation)?
5. For the two stops (K7 Tue 01:51, M7 Tue 02:28) and the K7 BookLock trade: the stop was JoinThenMarket (K7) / the lock closed it - confirm no cut order was rejected or suppressed during the 3-s join phase or after the halt flag.
