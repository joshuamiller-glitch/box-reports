## How to read it
- One row per group appears in the Monday table (`| LM <group> | OK / LOOK / NO DATA | n metrics with data, n LOOK, top metric |`); `live_monitor.py --full` prints all 38 metrics. States: **NO DATA** (the file is absent or empty), **TOO FEW** (below the sample column), **OK**, **LOOK**, **LOUD LOOK / ALARM-LOOK** (beyond the p99 of DIS-V, or a CUSUM alarm).
- Baselines are descriptions of the research distributions (p10 / p50 / p90 over rolling windows of the 187 research weeks, or over the 14 quarters for TH-L2), the DIS-V simulated distribution for drawdowns, or cited figures; they are not tests. "Sample before an alarm means anything" is the minimum n at which the sampling error of the metric is smaller than the gap between the baseline and the alarm band; below it the monitor prints TOO FEW rather than a state.
- A LOOK means: read the named page (DS3_TH_MECHANISM.md for group 1, DS3_FWD_DD.md / DS3_CONCENTRATION.md for 2, LIVE_STOP_JOIN.md / DS3_LIVE_RECON.md for 3, Q4_NQ_STRUCTURE.md / DS3_FADE_RANKED.md for 4, DS3_SIZER_Q*.md for 5, SHADOW_PACK(_2).md for 6) and decide whether anything needs the user. The monitor does not decide.

## Weekly one-page layout (what the Monday table plus `--full` gives)
1. TH-L2: share of cuts on would-be stops (vs 0.775 break-even), cuts per week, extra trades, per-cut value.
2. Concentration: PE share of the trailing 13 weeks and of the worst week, top-5-week share, trailing 13/26/52-week maxDD against DIS-V, time under water.
3. Execution: join fill rate, stop slippage by arm, entry slippage vs L2 mid, target fill, STOP A/B n per arm.
4. Regime: fade bundle trailing 4/8/13 weeks, h5 CUSUM, R_F40, 10-11h lag-1 autocorrelation, first-hour range share, NQ/ES vol ratio, gap-fill rate (each with its pre-break and post-break ranges).
5. Sizer: ER ladder share below 0.30 and lots per book.
6. Shadow lines: SPRT state and expected weeks to decision.

## Notes and limits
- **Sample sizes dominate.** TH-L2 needs a quarter (about 300 closed cuts) before the share of cuts on would-be stops (research 0.70-0.75, break-even 0.775) can be told from its baseline; the 60-minute shadow track is the only way to know the would-be outcome, so the cut log is the one new field set that has to be written before group 1 means anything. The execution metrics need 30-60 events each; the STOP A/B needs about 200 stops per arm (months at today's stop rate). The CUSUM alarm lags a regime shift by about 50 trading days (Q4_NQ_STRUCTURE.md detector table: h5 alarm at 49-52 days at 2-3 false alarms a year), so group 4 is a slow instrument; it is a watch on the old-regime signature, not a switch.
- **Concentration baselines are in-sample descriptions** of the 187 research weeks (PE is 53-61% of P&L and 104k of the 122k HOLD maxDD, DS3_CONCENTRATION.md); DIS-V percentiles come from the stationary bootstrap of the same weeks, so a live window above the p90 is "deeper than 9 in 10 resampled years", not evidence that anything broke.
- **Regime baselines.** The pre-break / post-break split is at the Q4 break date 2025-10-23 (post-break statistics start 40 days later to leave out the transition).
