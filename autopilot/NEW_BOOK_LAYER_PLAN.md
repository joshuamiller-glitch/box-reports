## 3. Fit protocol (the standing selection rule)
- Fit each layer setting on the TRAILING 6 MONTHS (202605-202610, 3M beside), one layer at a time, grid of 6-10 settings per layer (e.g. take pct 0.5-0.95 x secs 5-30; lock pct 0.5-0.8 x X; cut d at 0.5-1.0 x stop; day cap 1-4 stops; NTZ windows from the by-minute P&L of the new book), everything else at the ADD baseline ('off' = the ADD row, reference only; all live layers stay ON).
- Metric order: stack ulcer first, worst week, max DD, then mean $/week at base and x1.75, R per week; a setting must also keep trade count >= 80% of baseline.
- Test the chosen setting on prior years 2023 / 2024 / 2025 (amber 15-30% worse than the ADD row, red > 30% worse = not delivered) and on the 2-week rolling pick (pick on the trailing 26 weeks -> next 2 weeks) with date-shift nulls (the same 30 shifts as the ADD controls, layered).
- Then JOINT: top 3 settings of each layer combined (grid of combos, at most ~40), picked on L6M, tested the same way. Output = one setting block per lead book + per-layer deltas, with the layer-by-layer table (what each rule added or removed in $ and ulcer).
- Cheap pre-fit: the exit-path layers L1-L4 are first screened with the path-dependent exit harness (th_grid gexit, verified vs tape walk) on the cell's own trades so the engine only confirms the top settings (engine budget ~48 set-months per layer on the L6M months, ~290 for six layers for one book, then prior years only for the winners: 37 months each).
- Labels as always; no label without the 30 controls (layered controls = the same layers on the date-shifted events).

## 5. Addendum 1731Z: DAY-DONE is a SETTING, and the clock order
- **DAY-DONE as a stack-relative setting (refit jointly with the other layers, not a fixed 22 / -$4,200):** (a) close count = a multiple m of the stack's typical closes per trading day, scanned around the value that 22 represents today (m = 22 / today's median closes per day from JL3; scan roughly 0.6-1.6 x of it); (b) loss threshold in R of the stack's own dollar stops (R = the stack's typical per-trade dollar stop at today's lots), scanned around 2R (1.5-3R); (c) scope: all books vs per session (a session-only DAY-DONE for the session being added); (d) day loss / profit caps for wider-stop books as multiples of the new book's own dollar stop (1-4 stops), because a 120-pt stop at 1 base lot is 2,400 $ and the engine default cap is 4,800 $ (two stops, one stop at 2 live lots). These are layers L5/L10 in section 1 (L10 = DAY-DONE close count / loss threshold / scope), fitted on the trailing 6 months, tested on prior years + 2-week roll, jointly with L1-L9 (not one at a time only).
- **Clock order of decisions:** layers and books are decided in clock order Asia -> London -> NY AM -> NY PM; after each session is decided the day-state (closes so far, day P&L, DAY-DONE trip times) is REBUILT from the chosen books of the earlier sessions before the next session is fitted. NY is last, so every NY phase-3 result (WP10, XA3 and their layers/DAY-DONE settings) is PROVISIONAL until Asia and London are final; the JL3 day-state table used by the grid account-rule scoring is the today's-stack version.
- **Basis of every engine row to date:** vs TODAY's stack (JL3) with today's DAY-DONE setting: they answer "add / swap ONE book in today's stack", not "the rebuilt stack" (stated at the top of NEW_BOOK_ENGINE.md).
