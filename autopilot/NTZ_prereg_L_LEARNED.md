# NTZ-1 PREREG group B3 (learned walk-forward policy from POI features) - 2026-10-30
Registered before any learned model was fitted or scored.
Model: LightGBM classifier (depth 3, 7 leaves, 120 trees, lr 0.05, subsample 0.7, colsample 0.5, min child 60, L2 5) of y = the signal's original bracket exited at the target (P(target before stop)), pooled over the 10 books with book / session / side as features. Walk-forward: MONTHLY refit (expanding history, first fit after 13 weeks of data), trained only on trades CLOSED before the first signal of the test month (closed_ts < t_sig of the month); out-of-sample p-hat for every later signal. (The CF-COMBO "3-month weekly" form is a different layer; monthly keeps the 4 feature sets x 11 draws affordable on a loaded machine - stated.)
Feature sets: S = signal quality (side, td, sd, ATR15, ET minute of day, weekday, VIX of the previous day, HTF-against flags: price vs 4h EMA50, 1h Supertrend, 1d EMA200) + book + session; SB = S + order book at the signal second (wall ahead/behind, density, ABS/ICE/sweep bits, imbalance, delta30, pull, absr, rel_tot8); P = S + POI aggregates (per class x tf-group {ltf <= 3m, mtf 5-30m, htf >= 1h, na}: nearest fresh POI ahead, nearest fresh POI behind, number of POIs ahead, number behind; plus confluence totals ahead <= 20 and behind <= 10); PB = SB + the POI aggregates. The POI contribution is P vs S and PB vs SB.
Policies (thresholds = quantiles of the OOS p-hat of the PREVIOUS 3 months, >= 800 values, so no future information): skip10 (skip the lowest 10 %), skip20, size_dn20 (50 % size on the lowest 20 %), size_ud20 (50 % lowest 20 %, 150 % highest 20 %). Cells = 4 feature sets x 4 policies x 2 bases (MKT, SPL) = 32 cells x scope ALL + 10 books.
Selection on PRE u X' OOS predictions only (same rule as prereg B: gain per signal on N > 0 in PRE and X', top 3 per scope); one recent look; model-quality block (AUC, Brier vs unconditional, calibration deciles) per window; extracted rules in words and the best 1- and 2-feature distillation (qv.distill, fit on PRE u X').
Placebo: POI aggregate rows shuffled across trades within book x session x month (time-aligned), full re-fit and re-score, 10 draws for sets P and PB; the real P-vs-S and PB-vs-SB gain differences are ranked against the draws. Random-fire placebo for the policy cells as in B.
Tiers/validity as prereg B. Cell count 32 x 11 scopes; cumulative multiplicity as prereg B.
