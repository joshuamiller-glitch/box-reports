# NYAM_SHADOW_SPEC - implementation-ready spec of the three NY-AM fade books for the SHADOW-ONLY forward register (D7 v1.4/1.5) (CAL, T-NYAM-REGIME / P1, 2026-11-04; modelled on BT_SHADOW_D7_V14_SPEC.md)
Status: SHADOW ONLY. D7 computes the signals below and writes log rows; it never places, changes or cancels a real order. Size of the register = 0.5x of the study = 1 lot per book (study 2 lots). Evidence: autopilot/DISCOVER_D.md, DISCOVER_REGIME.md (family D3/D4 + RTH ORB fade): the books are a LEAD-REGIME bundle (NY-AM mean reversion since Oct-2025; negative/flat 2023-2025Q2), NOT cleared by gate v2; the register decides whether the 2026 regime persists.
**Reproduction (verified, diff 0):** `l2vast/ds3/code/zones3/nyam/verify_nyam.py` replays the streaming reference implementation (`nyam_books.py`, the exact code of sections 2-4) on the study feeds and diffs the signal lists with the study trade lists: ONRTH 543/543, VWAPF 3,273/3,273 raw signals, ORBF 914/914, 0 only-in-one-list, 0 field mismatches (sec, side, stop_pts, tgt_pts, tstop_s).
**CORRECTION found while building this spec (read first):** the original study lists for ONRTH and VWAPF (DISC_D / DISC_R) skipped whole days that contain a >100 pt 1-minute close jump anywhere in the window, a look-ahead filter (it removed 2 ONRTH and 16 VWAPF signals on 9 days such as 2025-02-07, 2025-04-07, 2025-04-23, 2026-04-02, 2026-06-12). The spec has NO jump rule; the study lists were rebuilt without it (`out/nyam_study_corrected.pkl`) and are the reference. Per-trade net points 2026 (n, mean, sd): ONRTH 118, +13.1, 59.2; VWAPF 424, +8.0, 60.2; ORBF 211, +7.8, 59.6 (these set the SPRT: H1 = half the mean).
**Decision needed (ORBF clock):** the study trigger is the FIRST 1-second bin whose high/low breaks the 09:30-10:00 range (a per-second evaluation). ## 1. Inputs (from D7's own tape; nothing else)
* Trade prints of the traded NQ contract (exchange timestamp >= 1 s, price, size) -> (a) 1-second bins (hi, lo, last print) and (b) 1-minute bars (open epoch s, o, h, l, c, v = sum of print sizes). Clock: ET wall clock (zoneinfo America/New_York); epoch seconds UTC for the log; label day = ET date of (t + 6 h), weekend rolled to Monday (RTH 09:30 of a label day is on that calendar date; clocks >= 18:00 ET belong to the PREVIOUS calendar day).
* No other input (no L2, no options, no second instrument). Prices in the raw contract frame (all rules use price differences inside one contract). The research used a back-adjusted frame; only differences are used (section 6 gives the offsets).

## 2. Definitions (frozen; every rule is exact; reference code `nyam_books.py` classes OnRthFade, VwapFade, OrbFade)
**Book ONRTH (overnight-extreme fade at the open, ON_RTH x0 w60, 60/60, T60).** ON range = max high / min low over the 1-minute bars existing in [18:00 ET previous calendar day, 09:30 ET); requires >= 30 bars (else no signals that day). During bars opening in [09:30, 10:00) (index j = 0,1,... over the bars that exist): for each direction independently, an UP-break starts at the first bar with high > ON high (excursion tracker = the max high from that bar on), and at every later bar update the tracker; at each bar (including the break bar itself) if close < ON high: if j - j_break <= 60 and excursion >= 0 -> SHORT signal at THIS bar's close (signal_sec = bar open + 60), first signal per direction per day, else reset the break; DOWN-break mirrored (low < ON low, close > ON low -> LONG). Bracket stop 60 / target 60 pts from the first fill, time stop 3,600 s after the signal, flat 16:55.
**Book VWAPF (NY-AM VWAP fade, d10, T120).** Session = bars opening in [09:30, 12:00). After each completed bar j (bars that exist, j = 0,1,...): cum_vol += v; cum_pv += v*(h+l+c)/3; VWAP = cum_pv / max(cum_vol, 1); dev = c - VWAP. Flags: below = True once dev <= -10 (never cleared except by a signal); above = True once dev >= +10. Signals only when j >= 30 and bar open < 11:45: if below and dev > 0 -> SHORT at this bar's close (below cleared); elif above and dev < 0 -> LONG (above cleared); at most one per bar. 60/60, time stop 7,200 s after the signal, flat 16:55.
**Book ORBF (RTH 09:30 30-minute-range fade, 60/60, T240).** Range = max high / min low over the 1-second bins in [09:30:00, 10:00:00); valid only if >= 60 bins exist and the first bin is at or before 09:32:00. From 10:00:00 until 14:00:00 (exclusive) the FIRST bin with high > range high (up-break) or low < range low (down-break; a bin that breaks both counts as DOWN) triggers one fade for the day: up-break -> SHORT, down-break -> LONG; signal_sec = that bin's second (decision after the bin closes). 60/60; time stop at 14:00:00 ET (tstop_s = max(60, 16,200 - (signal_sec - 09:30:00)) seconds, i.e. 270 minutes after 09:30), flat 16:55.
Common: one open position per book (a signal while the same book is open is logged and marked skipped by the register runner = the harness no-overlap rule); the three books are independent and may be open together; no rule uses the trade outcome, a later bar, or another book.

## 3. Order of evaluation inside D7 (per second s, this order is the unit-test contract)
(a) append the prints of second s to the 1-s bin (hi, lo, last) and to the open minute bar; (b) ORBF: if s in the range window accumulate (bins, high, low); elif 10:00:00 <= s < 14:00:00 and the range is valid and no ORBF signal yet today: test the break with this bin and, if triggered, emit the signal with signal_sec = s; (c) if s is a minute boundary B (s % 60 == 0): finalize the bar [B-60, B), then ONRTH (range accumulation or window logic) and VWAPF (cum sums, flags, signals) with that completed bar; signal_sec = B; (d) write the log rows for the signals of this second (log row time = D7 clock when written; fill model starts at signal_sec + 1 s).

## 4. State and cost
ONRTH: 2 floats (ON high/low), bar count, per direction (break index, excursion, fired) + day flags; VWAPF: cum_vol, cum_pv, bar index, 2 flags; ORBF: range high/low, bin count, first-bin second, fired flag. O(1) per second, a few hundred bytes; no history arrays. Day reset at 18:00 ET of the previous calendar day for ONRTH (overnight window) and at 09:30 for the others.

## 5. Roll, holiday, restart handling
* Roll: all three books use the contract D7 trades. If D7 switches contract inside a book's lookback (ONRTH 18:00 -> 09:30, ORBF/VWAPF the session), add the same-minute close difference (new - old) to the older bars (additive adjustment, as the research path) before computing ranges; about 4 days a year. No jump rule exists (a real >100 pt minute move is a valid signal day).
* Holidays: books run on every label day on which the 09:30 RTH session opens. Early-halt days (MLK, Presidents, Memorial, Juneteenth, Jul 4, Labor, Thanksgiving: halt 13:00 ET; Jul 3, day after Thanksgiving, Dec 24: 13:15): the rules above are unchanged (VWAPF ends 12:00, ONRTH 10:00), open positions are flattened at the halt (the research flat time 16:55 is never reached; shadow P&L closes at the last print). Full closures: no row. Good Friday NFP sessions (abbreviated, 09:15 end) skip the books (< 60 bins / < 30 bars rules).
* Restart: warm up with the prints since the day's anchor (18:00 ET for ONRTH, 09:30 for the others) before the first signal; do not backfill across a restart longer than the anchor.
* Data gaps: bars/bins that do not exist are not synthesised; thresholds counting bars (ONRTH >= 30, VWAPF j >= 30, ORBF >= 60 bins) count existing bars.

## 6. Log (ZL-2 helpers `code/zones3/discover/shadow_register.py`; directory forward_register/nyam/)
Per book `<book>_signals.csv` (ONRTH, VWAPF, ORBF) written at the signal by `log_signal(dir, book, sec, side, signal_price, stop_pts, tgt_pts, tstop_s, lots, session='nyam', version='nyam_v1', flags=...)`: sig_id, ts_signal_utc, book, session, side, signal_price (last print at or before signal_sec, raw frame), stop_pts 60, tgt_pts 60, tstop_s, lots 1.0 (0.5x), definition_version, regime_flags, logged_by. `regime_flags` carries the context as `key=value;` pairs (no commas): ONRTH on_hi, on_lo, level, excursion, brk_bars; VWAPF vwap, dev, bar_idx; ORBF rng_hi, rng_lo, brk. `<book>_fills.csv` written when the shadow position closes by `log_fill(dir, book, sig_id, ts_exit, exit_price, exit_type, net_pts, usd)`. Runner (`nyam_forward_weekly.py --regdir forward_register/nyam`) replays each signal through the harness fill model (entry = last print at or before signal_sec + 1 s, LIVE costs) and reconciles any logged fills (max |logged - replay| pt). ZL-2's `reconcile` (MultiHarness, slicing model) gives the same numbers for 1 lot.

## 8. Forward runner and monthly read
* `nyam_forward_weekly.py [--regdir forward_register/nyam | log.csv] [--tape tape_1s.parquet] [--sleeve live_nyam_daily.csv] --start 2026-11-04`: Monday run; per-book week and cumulative n / hit / $ at 1 lot, bundle, vs the live NY-AM sleeve, SPRT (per-trade LLR, H0 mean 0 vs H1 = half the 2026 study mean, alpha = beta = 0.10, bounds +-2.20), verdict EARLY before week 13 (about 17 trades a week in the bundle: the SPRT needs hundreds of trades, expect INCONCLUSIVE at week 13; CONTRADICTED if z of the mean vs H1 < -2 after week 13), appends a block to autopilot/NYAM_FORWARD_WEEKLY.md and one `NYAM FORWARD week N` line to MORNING.md; writes forward_register/nyam/nyam_daily.csv for the monthly read. `--selftest` replays a dummy log built by the streaming code over 2026-10-05..10-16 and requires 0 difference to the corrected-study daily P&L at L=1.
* `nyam_regime_monthly.py`: first Monday of the month (or --force), one `NYAM REGIME` MORNING.md line: fade bundle trailing 20/40-day $, first-hour range share 20d vs 250-day median, NQ opening-range +-0.25% hit rate 40d, live NY-AM sleeve 20/40-day $, state FADE-ON / FADE-OFF / MIXED (read only).
* Weekly scheduler: `nyam_weekly_loop.sh` (nohup) runs both every Monday 13:00 UTC.
