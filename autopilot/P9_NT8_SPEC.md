# P9 NT8 SPEC - execution policy for DualStrat (ST/HM) from the real-book replay
Author trackP2, 2026-10-20. TEXT ONLY: no file other than this spec was edited. Source for the code references: outputs/h2/DualStrat.cs (line numbers are approximate). Replay source: outputs/l2vast/pairP2/p9_replay.py, results in /mnt/l2/l2vast/pairP2/p9/, scoring p9_score.py / p9_final.py / p9b.py / p9_xj.py / p9_tail.py. Numbers are 14 lots (engine scale 1.75x the 8/8), NQ, 45 months, 11,612 bar-open entries.
STATUS: research result, NOT validated live. Defaults below keep today's behaviour (Market entry, Native stop); nothing changes until a Sim A/B (section 5) passes.

## 0. What is being implemented (one paragraph)
Entries (both books): instead of one 14-lot market order at the bar open, either (EI) join the own-side touch (improve by one tick when the spread is more than one tick) for 10 s then market the remainder, or (S14x1) send 14 single-lot market orders 2 s apart. Stop exits: instead of the native StopMarket executing at the trigger, wait 3 s with a passive limit join, then market. Flips, time-block flattens, session flat, give-back/cap/week locks, ProtectRejected, desync: unchanged (market).

## 1. ENTRY mechanics
### 1.1 Timing and reference (unchanged)
Signal stays at bar close (Calculate.OnBarClose, OnBarUpdate; the entry call is the first tick after the bar closes = true entry instant = engine bar open; replay t_dec = that instant, order arrival +5 ms). All gates in OnBarUpdate stay as is (enabled, InTrade, ExitPending, EntryOrder != null, DayHalt, WeekHalt, TradingBlockedNow incl. the live windows 18:00-18:05 and 15:00-16:54 via GuardAsia/GuardNy/FlattenAtHHmm settings, MaxTradesPerSession, OppositeEntry). The execution mode only replaces what SubmitEntry (line ~519) sends.
### 1.2 Bid/Ask tracking (required)
OnMarketData (line 682) returns on anything but MarketDataType.Last. Add: for MarketDataType.Bid / Ask store bestBid, bestAsk, bidSize, askSize and tick time (e.Time live: NinjaTrader.Core.Globals.Now as the existing code does). Join logic uses the last stored Bid/Ask at the moment of submission; if either is older than 2 s or missing or spread <= 0, use Market (log reason).
### 1.3 EI (inside-join) EXACT
At the entry instant for a BUY: spread = ask - bid. If spread > 1 tick: limit = bid + 1 tick (inside). If spread == 1 tick: limit = bid (join the bid). SELL mirrors: ask - 1 tick if spread > 1 tick, else ask. Order: SubmitOrderUnmanaged(0, action, OrderType.Limit, Qty(14), limitPrice, 0, "", Tag+"_Entry"), TimeInForce Day. NO re-peg (replay used one placement, no cancel/replace); the limit is not chased. Timer: 10.0 s of tick time from the submit (OnMarketData handler of the same book checks now - submitTime >= StEntryJoinSeconds on every Last/Bid/Ask event; if the market is silent for 10 s the next event fires it; a 1 s housekeeping check like lastFlattenCheck is acceptable). At the timer: CancelOrder(entry); wait for terminal state (>= 400 ms as PumpExit does); the remaining quantity (Qty - PosQty) goes at market as Tag+"_EntryFB" (flag fallback = true). Partial fill before the timer: the filled part is kept (OnExecutionUpdate already averages EntryPx over partials), the remainder is market at the timer. Missed-fill rule: NONE - never skip; the replay always ended filled (fallback share EI 11%, EL 18%). If the market order is rejected: existing Rejected handler path (ResetTrade when PosQty==0).
EL (touch join) = same with limit = own-side best bid/ask always (fallback share 18%, saving $187/trade vs EI $211): not recommended.
### 1.4 S14x1 EXACT (slicing)
Slice k = 0..13 sends SubmitOrderUnmanaged(..., Market, 1, ...) at t0 + 2.0*k s (tick-time timer from the first slice; gap 2 s is the replayed gap; gaps 2-10 s were NOT replayed - do not change without a re-replay). Sequence completes at +26 s (replay median t_done - t_first = 26.0 s). All 14 slices are always sent (no skip). Slice orders are named Tag+"_Entry" (suffix index in the log only) so that the existing OnExecutionUpdate weighting (EntryPx, PosQty) works unchanged; b.EntryOrder != null for the whole 26 s so no second signal for this book is accepted (as today).
### 1.5 Bracket with slices (the open design point)
Replay: EACH slice carries its own 60-pt stop / 60-pt target (1.0R) from its own fill, i.e. 14 independent brackets; the book result is the quantity-weighted average. Two NT8 options: (A, exact) 14 OCO pairs of qty 1 (28 working orders), each pair placed at that slice's fill; needs a list of sub-legs in Book (StopOrder/TargetOrder become lists). (B, simpler, NOT replayed) one OCO pair on the net position: place at the first slice fill with qty 1 and stop/target from that fill, then after every later slice ChangeOrder(qty += 1) and re-price both to AvgEntryPx -/+ 60 (the pair is unprotected for zero ticks but the levels move to the average). Today PlaceBracket is called only when the entry is complete (PosQty >= Qty, line ~630); waiting for slice 14 leaves up to 26 s unprotected and must NOT be used. Recommended first build: (B) with a Sim shadow of (A) in the log (compute what the per-slice levels would have been) and compare outcomes; if (B) deviates from (A) by more than 0.3 pt per trade on average, build (A).
### 1.6 Interactions
- Live windows / FlattenAtHHmm: decided at the signal bar only (TradingBlockedNow). A slice sequence that is running when a window starts keeps going (<= 26 s). If TimeBlock/FlattenAtHHmm fires (RequestBookExit) during slicing: cancel the unsent slices FIRST, then the existing cancel-stop/target + market exit.
- Per-book daily +5R/-2R and weekly (MaxDaily*, CheckLimits): realized-only, evaluated at signal time and on realized fills: unchanged. A limit hit mid-slicing: cancel unsent slices, RequestBookExit(b, "DayHalt").
- Combined cap/give-back/week floor (CheckComboLock, 2 s, realized + open): uses b.Unreal on the partial position: unchanged; a lock mid-slicing cancels unsent slices then flattens.
- ST/HM independence: each Book owns its slice/join state; NT8 nets the account. OppositeEntry = AsIs today: with slicing the window in which ST and HM can sit on opposite sides of the NET lasts 26 s instead of ~0; recommended: while a book is slicing, treat it as 'entering' for the OppositeEntry test (existing code already tests o.EntryOrder != null) and use OppositeEntry = Skip during the Sim test.
- Flip while slicing: a new signal is not accepted (EntryOrder != null). An OppFlip_/other RequestBookExit cancels the unsent slices first.
- Copier-leader: UNVERIFIED. The copier-leader Sim mirrors the live account; whether the follower replicates fills (14 x 1 lot) or the strategy's order intents is not known to me. Mandatory Sim copier test before any live use: check follower fill counts, price and that the follower bracket matches.
- Disconnect/desync: the existing DS_Desync routine (line ~957) cancels stop/target and squares the net; with slices also cancel unsent slices.
## 2. STOP EXIT (XJ) mechanics
### 2.1 What 'trigger' means
Today: native StopMarket (OCO with the target) at StopPx, executed by the broker/exchange when the market trades at/through it. Replay: trigger = first TRADE print at/through the stop level (exit order decision time; +5 ms). New mode (JoinThenMarket): the strategy detects the trigger itself in OnMarketData: Last print <= StopPx (long) / >= StopPx (short). The native StopMarket is NOT cancelled; it is replaced at placement by a CATASTROPHE stop at StopPx -/+ StCatastropheStopPts in the same OCO as the target (b.Oco), so the protective order stays server-side.
### 2.2 Action at the trigger
1. Cancel the target limit only when the exit order is accepted (not before). 2. Submit Limit exit for the full PosQty at the OWN-SIDE best (a long exits by SELL limit at the best ASK; a short by BUY limit at the best BID: passive join, queue behind existing size; no price improvement, no re-peg). 3. Timer 3.0 s of tick time: at expiry CancelOrder (wait terminal, >= 400 ms), then market for the remainder (name Tag+"_StopFB"). 4. Partial passive fills: the rest goes market at the timer. 5. If the native catastrophe stop fills first, nothing else to do (OCO). 6. If the 3 s timer expires and price has already recovered above the stop, the exit still goes out (the replay exits in all cases; there is no 'hold the position' variant). 7. Trigger can only fire once per trade (flag in Book).
Variant XS0 (touch-cross 2 s then market; saving $442 vs XJ $525 per stop exit) is the fallback if the passive join proves unsafe.
### 2.3 Why the catastrophe distance: tail numbers (replay, 4,306 STOP exits, XJ minus market exit, points per exit; +ve = XJ better)
Distribution: p1 -22.9 | p5 -10.9 | p25 -0.5 | p50 +1.18 | p75 +3.0 | p95 +17.0 | p99 +36.2 | worst single -70.1 | best +103.9 | mean +1.84.
Worst 1% (stop+flatten pooled, 64 trades; STOP-only 43): mean -41 pts pooled (-31.5 STOP-only) = about -$11.5k per trade at 14 lots; by book HM 30 / ST 13 (STOP-only 43); by year 2023: 2, 2024: 12, 2025: 14, 2026: 15.
By year (n, mean, p1, p5, min): 2023 528, +2.26, -19.4, -9.7, -33.8 | 2024 868, +1.14, -24.1, -10.9, -43.3 | 2025 1316, +2.14, -23.4, -11.8, -49.6 | 2026 1594, +1.85, -22.7, -10.7, -70.1.
By book: ST 2447, +2.36, -16.2, -7.2, -43.3 | HM 1859, +1.17, -26.4, -14.8, -70.1 (HM has the fatter tail and half the gain: roll out ST first).
How far price travels BEYOND the stop within the 3 s wait (max adverse excursion after the trigger): median 1.9 pts, p90 7.9, p95 11.7, p99 50.4. Frequency beyond 15 / 20 / 25 pts: 3.53% (152) / 2.48% (107) / 2.04% (88) of stop exits.
Cost of a catastrophe stop (assumed to fill at the level plus 0.5 pt; trades where the excursion reaches the level are exited there instead of at the XJ outcome): at +15 pts: -0.361 pt per stop exit (about -$435k over 45 months at 14 lots; mean -10.2 pts on the 152 triggered trades, whose XJ outcome averaged -65.3 pts, i.e. most of those spikes bounce back); at +20: -0.365 pt (107 triggers, -14.7 each); at +25: -0.385 pt (88 triggers, -18.8 each). The cost is nearly flat in the distance because fewer but worse triggers offset; XJ+catastrophe keeps about +1.47 pt per stop exit (+$412) of the +1.84 (+$515).
Choice: +20 pts (2.5% trigger rate, protects against an outage at -1.33R rather than unbounded, costs 0.365 pt; +15 triggers more often for the same cost, +25 costs more per trigger). Re-tune only with live trigger data. In a feed/platform outage the position is protected at StopPx -/+ 20 only.
### 2.4 Flatten/flip exits UNCHANGED
Flip exits (OppFlip_/'Entry' exits), TimeBlock, SessionFlat (1655), ComboLock/DayHalt/WeekHalt, LossExit (TH, off), ProtectRejected, desync: all stay RequestBookExit -> market via PumpExit. Replay: XJ on these exits is -1.21 pts mean (median +0.2, n 2,118); P9b chose market 97% of the time. Target exits unchanged (resting limit).
## 3. PROPERTIES (DualStrat naming style; [NinjaScriptProperty] [Range] [Display(Name=..., Order=..., GroupName=...)]; set in OnStateChange SetDefaults)
New group "07. Execution (P9)" (per book St*/Hm*, Order 0-9 each; globals Order 20+). Defaults keep the live behaviour.
- enum DsEntryMode { Market, JoinThenMarket, Slice } and enum DsStopExit { Native, JoinThenMarket } (like DsOppositeEntry, DsVwapAnchor).
- StEntryMode / HmEntryMode: DsEntryMode, default Market. Display "ST Entry mode (Market = live today)".
- StEntryJoinInside / HmEntryJoinInside: bool, default true (EI; false = EL touch join).
- StEntryJoinSeconds / HmEntryJoinSeconds: double, Range(1, 60), default 10.
- StSliceCount / HmSliceCount: int, Range(1, 20), default 14 (slice size = Qty / SliceCount, remainder in the last slice; replay Qty=14 -> 14 x 1).
- StSliceGapSeconds / HmSliceGapSeconds: double, Range(0.5, 10), default 2.0 (replayed value).
- StStopExitMode / HmStopExitMode: DsStopExit, default Native.
- StStopJoinSeconds / HmStopJoinSeconds: double, Range(0.5, 10), default 3.0.
- StCatastropheStopPts / HmCatastropheStopPts: double, Range(5, 100), default 20 (used only when StopExitMode = JoinThenMarket; 0 not allowed).
- ExecMaxFallbackPct: double Range(0,100) default 40 (kill: over the last 20 JoinThenMarket entries; replay EI 11%).
- ExecRevertCostTicks: double default 1.0 (kill: rolling 30-trade mean realised entry cost minus shadow-market cost above this reverts to Market).
- ExecLogCsv: bool default true (section 5 log).
Implementation hooks: SubmitEntry (~519), PlaceBracket (~530; per-slice/net), RequestBookExit/PumpExit (~543-575; add 'cancel unsent slices'), OnMarketData (~682; Bid/Ask + join/slice/stop-join timers), OnExecutionUpdate (~615; entry weighting exists), OnOrderUpdate (~584; names Tag_EntryFB, Tag_StopFB), TradingBlockedNow (~1008; unchanged).
## 4. NOT implementable / not measurable in the NT8 Strategy Analyzer, and platform limits
- The Analyzer fills market orders at tick prices without book depth and limits when price touches/trades through, with no queue: the walk cost, the join fallback share and the 3 s stop bounce cannot be validated there; a sliced/timer strategy also needs Calculate.OnEachTick + tick replay and still has no impact model. All validation is Sim/Rithmic live or this replay.
- Rithmic/NT8 have no native iceberg or time-sliced order for this: slicing and the join timers are client-side (strategy-side timers on tick time); they stop if the platform/VPS stops. Commission per contract is unchanged; order count rises (14 entry orders per trade, plus a cancel/replace-free join).
- Exchange-side stop-with-protection or stop-limit that waits 3 s does not exist natively: the delay can only be done by the strategy (hence the catastrophe stop). A native StopLimit gets about +$317 per stop exit (XS4) vs +$525 (XJ): the gain is the delay.
## 5. Test plan, logging, acceptance, kill switches
Phase 0 (no orders): log shadow decisions on the live account: at each entry the would-be join price/slice schedule and at each stop touch the would-be join, plus the actual fills. Phase 1: new Sim account (14/14, like the A/B Sim precedent) running JoinThenMarket entry + Native stop; Phase 2 adds StopExit JoinThenMarket with the catastrophe stop; Phase 3 adds Slice. Each phase: matched against the copier-leader Sim (market) on the same signals for >= 40 entries / 30 stop exits; then a 1-lot-per-slice live pilot only after the copier test.
Log per order (CSV, ExecLogCsv): book, signal bar time, mode, slice index, order name, submit ts (Globals.Now) and tick-time, side, qty, order type, limit price, bid/ask/last and sizes at submit, spread in ticks, accepted/working ts, each fill ts/price/qty, cancel ts, fallback flag + market fallback ts/price, stop trigger ts/trigger print, passive limit price, fills, fallback, final average exit, catastrophe-trigger flag, native stop fill, position quantity over time, time-to-bracket-attached per slice. Plus for reconciliation: the first print of the entry minute and the engine fill (first print +1 tick adverse).
Acceptance (all measured live/Sim vs the market shadow on the same signal): entry: mean saving per entry >= 50% of the replay value at 14 lots (EI $211, S14x1 $315; ST $227/$322, HM $188/$304), fallback share <= 25% (replay EI 11%, median wait 0.24 s, 69% filled within 1 s); no leg/position unprotected for more than 2 s; stop exit: mean saving per stop exit >= +$250 (replay +$515) after >= 30 exits, catastrophe stop trigger rate <= 4% (replay 2.5%), worst single stop exit not worse than -$20k vs the market shadow (replay -$19.6k).
Kill switches: (1) master: all three Mode properties back to Market/Native (one switch ExecMode off); (2) automatic revert to Market when the fallback share over the last 20 joins exceeds ExecMaxFallbackPct or the rolling realised entry cost exceeds the shadow market by ExecRevertCostTicks; (3) revert StopExit to Native on any catastrophe trigger in the first 30 JoinThenMarket exits or any stop exit worse than -25 pts vs the shadow; (4) disconnect: existing desync path plus cancel of unsent slices/timers; (5) day/week/combined locks always win (they cancel unsent slices and flatten at market).
