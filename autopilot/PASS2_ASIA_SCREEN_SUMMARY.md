## 1. What outcome stream the screen used (exactly)
BOOK-ALONE, no live book. For each book the combiner rebuilds the trigger's raw signals from the current module code on the book's own bar, and takes each event's outcome from the grid's tape walk (gcore2.walk1): entry at the first print one second after the signal bar closes plus measured slippage, the book's own fixed bracket (stop and target), flat at session end, 4.84 USD per lot round trip, 1 lot, one position at a time, a skipped event frees later signals. NO engine, NO live book on or off, NO stack rules (DAY-DONE, caps, give-back lock, lots table), NO layers. Dollars are therefore screen dollars; the engine ladder rows of BEST_BOOKS include the stack rules and differ (checked: median trade difference 0 USD, 72% within 25 USD, correlation 0.965 on one book).
The nine LIVE-book streams (section 6) are the opposite by nature: the realised trades of the JL3 exact-engine run (all live layers and the stack on); they are "beside-stack" outcomes of the live book itself.
L-id books whose description says "long" were scored on the both-side stream (evcheck: Asia all 50 identical to the current event files).

## 2. Counts (49 or 50 books; definitions fixed before counting)
Passing means ALL of: (a) C1: the book's search-aware luck test p <= 0.10 (the whole C1 search repeated on condition rows shuffled within weeks, 10 repeats, so p <= 0.10 means the real best beat all 10), AND at least one kept C1 setting that keeps >= 60% of the trades and >= 5 per week, beats the matched random-skip null (p <= 0.10) and beats the reference book's score (SCORE = $/wk divided by ulcer) in at least 2 of the 3 prior years 2023 / 2024 / 2025 (never used for the choice). (b) C2 gate: the component's best beats max-T q90 AND a kept C2 setting passes the same null and prior-year test. (c) C3 time window: same, with the guard that the dropped events lose money in both halves of L6M.

|---|---|---|---|

| (c) C3 time-of-day window | 5 (ranks 11, 24, 33, 40, 43) | max-T alone: 6 books; hold-out alone: 16 books | |
IMPORTANT, plain: with a luck test of this kind a book with NO real effect passes it about 1 time in 11. 49 books screened means about 4.5 passes expected by chance for the luck test; 3 books passed. For C2 and C3 the max-T test is a 10% test: about 5 of 49 expected by chance, 3 and 6 observed. The counts are therefore NOT more than what luck alone gives. Nothing in this screen is evidence of a real effect; it is a candidate list for the exact-engine joint stage. (The luck test has only 10 repeats, so it cannot tell p = 0.09 from p = 0.01; a longer run is needed for any book that goes forward.)
