## Flag-file interface (agreed with W-P2A; PASS2_COMBINER.md section 6)
/mnt/l2/l2vast/_pass2/flags/<setid>.parquet, keys ts (signal t_close s + 2) and side (original side) = the engine events file; flag_<settingid> int8 (1 take); optional state, er, q, nconf_<id>, bits, flip_<id> (flip==1 trades the OPPOSITE side, same bracket: HOOKED). Hook params: p2.flags, p2.flag_col, p2.flip_col. Events with no flag row are taken and counted (counts.jsonl in /mnt/l2/l2vast/_pass2/logs). Null files flag_null_<id>_<seed> work as flag_col unchanged (matched random skip).

EQUAL AVERAGE RISK (definition, code/th_pass2/p2_equalrisk.py): risk of a taken event = lots x initial stop distance (pts) x $20; average risk = mean over the events the book takes (same event set for sizer and flat reference); the sizer is rescaled by k = flat_mean / sizer_mean and rounded (>= 1 lot) with the residual ratio reported; dollars also shown per $ of average risk, and the realised downside (mean loss x losses) beside it. A sizer that wins only by taking more risk fails.
## Cost of one book's joint stage (set-months)
Measured: one exact set-month with the hook = 25-30 min wall at box-1 load ~100 and nice 10 (single core); W-FASTLADDER measured ~255 core-s exact vs ~115 core-s fast per set-month. The hook adds no measurable engine time (per event: dict lookups; per print: manage() only when cut / no-progress / take / lock are set), so a pass-2 setting costs the same as a plain book set-month.
Joint stage per book = J joint settings x 6 months (L6M, L3M inside) + top 3 x 37 months (prior years) + 30 date-shift controls x 6 months (+ the 2-week roll reads the same monthly results, no extra runs). With J = 100 settings: 600 + 111 + 180 = about 890 set-months per book (J = 50: 590; J = 200: 1,490). At the fleet rate of 3,000-3,800 set-months/h (HEALTH.md) that is about 15 min per book if the whole fleet were used; top 50 per session = about 44,500 set-months = about 13 h of whole-fleet time (about 6 h on the fast path once all pass-2 templates are in FAST_KINDS). Screen-only components (flags, windows, gate, sizer tiers on a file) are screened outside the engine at no engine cost.
