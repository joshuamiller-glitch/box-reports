# PE_EA_STACK_IMPACT - what an unthinned (exit-aware) PE book does to the headline stack numbers (BASE-4, 2026-11-06). One page for the user. Research only, no PASS claim, nothing live, nothing published was changed.
## What was tested
The published PE book takes a signal only if the PREVIOUS PE trade, run to its nominal 60-point stop or target, has already ended. The live PE book (P7) is free again as soon as ITS OWN trade is closed, often earlier because the TH-L2 cut or a lock closes it. ZL-2 built a private tree (tag pe_ea) where PE takes every valid signal (61,765 rows instead of 8,497) and the engine applies its own one-position rule; I ran the Q1-A stack on it (935 days to 2026-10-30, locks on, bases V0 = engine as run and V2 = 09:30-09:59 and 18:00-18:04 entries skipped; kfix off = published convention, kfix on = the cut-delta correction of ENGINE_KFIX_IMPACT.md). Prereg l2vast/ds3/prereg/PE_EA_STACK_scope.md (sha256 3f35868e...). Sealed/published trees: size-and-time manifest of 17,279 files unchanged before and after, md5 guards OK.

In words: the unthinned PE book trades 35-39% more often, makes roughly the same or less money in total (down on three bases, up 1.8% on one), and makes the worst stretch clearly deeper (HOLD drawdown 32-62% deeper, Calmar lower in all four). The other books are unchanged except through locks (lock exits 120 -> 141 on V0). Cut share of PE trades stays about 49%. TH-L2 value per week (cut on minus off): V0 post-cap +12.8k -> +13.8k, full history +1.5k -> +1.2k (kfix off); the cut stays positive.
Per year (V0, kfix off): 2023 +72k -> +99k, 2024 734k -> 679k, 2025 744k -> 598k, 2026 970k -> 963k: the extra rows help in 2023 and hurt in 2024-25; ZL-2 found their nominal outcome flips sign by year, so this is not an expectation.

## Caveats
Tie-out FAILED: with the extras dropped the pe_ea run gives 2,534,661 (V0) / 2,474,700 (V2) instead of the published 2,520,149 / 2,462,264 (+0.58% / +0.50%). Cause: dropping extras by skipping them is not the same as not having them; for 34 PE trades the 30% resting add leg is gone in the pe_ea run (their P&L is about 0.70 of the published one) and 2 trades are missing, because the entry kernel looks at neighbouring rows before the skip. So the comparison above is published tree vs pe_ea directly; about 0.5% of the total difference is this artefact. Also: zone-layer POI streams are the published ones (not rebuilt for the extra rows); tags and profit statistics are NaN for the extras (the engine does not read them); extras have not been tested for cap-period fidelity; 98% of the extra ids have loss rows (the rest never trade below 0); PE ids are no longer in time order. A baseline that includes unthinned PE would be a new truth base: that decision is the lead's/yours; nothing published was changed.
