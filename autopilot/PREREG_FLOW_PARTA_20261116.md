# PREREG T-FLOW PART A (W-CF2, 2026-11-16, written BEFORE any real-outcome result of the new library is read)
Library: order-by-order features from the NQ MBO history (2023-04-02..2026-11-13; per-second tables cf2_mbo3 built by code/th_confl_cf2/mbo_sec3.py; book rules of code/mbo_book).
Families (measure registry in code/th_confl_cf2/flow_lib.py): 1 arrivals (count, size) at levels L1 (touch), L1-3 (within 2 ticks), L1-10 (within 9 ticks); 2 cancels (count, size; touch, 1-3, 1-10, deep; cancel-to-add ratio; liquidity pulling toward the side price moves to); 3 trades (aggressor volume, trade counts, imbalance, mean size, shares of 1-lot and 10+ aggressor orders, speed, same-side run length); 4 sweeps (aggressor orders crossing 2+ / 3+ levels; refill half, full, failed within 60 s); 5 hidden size (refill events, trade volume above displayed touch size); 6 absorption; 7 queue (touch size, orders, mean order size, depth 1-3 / 1-10, orders, flicker); 8 order size mix; 9 price impact (ticks per 100 contracts, change over the window); 10 stop-run signature N = 5, 15, 60 s; 11 book depth at 20/40/58/60 points from the mid on the stop side and the target side, level and change over 60 / 300 s.
Windows 1, 5, 15, 30, 60, 120, 300 seconds ending the second before the entry second (window sums over seconds [T-W, T-1]). Forms: own side, other side, difference (own - other) for side-specific measures (own = bid side / buy aggressors for a long, ask side / sell aggressors for a short); single measures (impact, imbalance, stop-run, depth at stop/target) have one signed form. Raw value and its percentile among the same measure at the SAME ET clock minute (+-2 minutes) over the previous 20 trading sessions (needs >= 100 reference values). Tails: LOW = value (percentile) <= 0.20, HIGH = >= 0.80; raw tails use the 20th / 80th percentile of the whole minute panel of that measure.
Latency: every item scored with the value taken at the entry second shifted by 0, 1, 2, 5 seconds earlier; an item that loses its sign or falls below the nonsense bar at 2 s is flagged "needs < 2 s". Wait variants (+30/+60/+120 s after the signal second) are produced for passing items only.
Outcome: taken JN05W2 trades to 2026-11-13, net $ per trade at base lots (engine fee), R per trade; cells ALL and sessions 0,1,3,4; books x sessions only for passing items. Statistic: weekly-cluster t of the lift (yes minus no). Years 2023..2026, L6M = 2026-05..10.
Nonsense bar: the same library evaluated at the signal time shifted back 11, 23, 37 calendar days (same clock second, whatever day it lands on), same flags, same tests; bar = 99th percentile of |t| of those placebo items in the same cell. An item PASSES a cell when |t| > bar, the sign is the same in at least 3 of 4 years, and the L6M sign is the same. Passing items are clustered by flag correlation >= 0.9 (one representative per cluster is the unit of count).
PLANTED-EDGE CHECK (before any real result is read): outcomes = real $ shuffled within book x session x month (destroys real structure) plus planted effects on 4 library items (+150, +80, +40 and 0 $/trade on the yes side, items spanning families), run through the whole pipeline including latency and percentile; PASS requires: each planted non-zero item recovered with lift within +-35% of the planted value and |t| above the bar at lag 0, ranked inside the top 1% of items or in the cluster of the top item; share of non-planted, non-neighbour items beating the p99 bar <= 2% in the shuffled outcome; the 0-effect planted item not above the bar. If it fails the pipeline is fixed and the check repeated; real results are not read before a PASS.
No spend. Output: cols_cf2/flow_*.parquet (sig_id keyed), FLOW_STATS csv, page contribution to DS3_FLOW.md.
