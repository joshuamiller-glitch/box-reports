Date 2026-10-17. Worker ICT/FV. Code ap_fv16*.py (sleeve pipeline = ap_ict_stage2/verify reused as in FV14; ICT core extended with YM from the i/m1_YM.npz 1-min cache: PV $5, tick 1.0). Up to 8 processes. Disk guard: 60 GB free required (152 GB free at start; outputs are MBs). Clean-only selection 2023-08..2025-07; H1/H2 read once for selected cells only; UNTESTED never. Roll handling: back-adjusted series (as the ICT study sleeves); IMP3-line tests (I2, I4) use the FV roll exclusion list on NQ.

## A. Family SEMIV (I1), 36 setups per instrument
At slots 10:30, 12:00, 13:30 ET (decision on the previous minute close, entry next open): SI = (RS+ - RS-)/(RS+ + RS-) over the trailing W minutes of squared sampled price changes (sampling s minutes). W in {60,120,180}, s in {1,5}, threshold |SI| >= th in {0.2,0.4,0.6}, mode {continue = sign(SI), fade}. Stop 1.5 ATR(15m), time exit 120 min, exit XT (stop + time).
## B. Family OPENTYPE (I3), 32 setups per instrument
Decision at 10:00 ET from the 09:30-10:00 bars against the prior label-day high/low (PDH/PDL). Exclusive types in this priority: ORR (open-rejection-reverse: 30-min high > PDH and close < PDH => direction -1; low < PDL and close > PDL => +1), OD (open-drive: |close-open| >= 0.6 range30, range30 >= 0.8 ATR15, close in the extreme 20% of the 30-min range => sign(close-open)), OTD (open-test-drive: close beyond open by >= 0.4 range30 after an opposite excursion of >= 0.2 range30 from the open => direction of the close), OA (everything else; direction sign(close-open)). Setups: type (4) x action {with, against the type direction} x stop {1.0, 1.5} ATR(15m) x hold {180, 330} min = 32. Exit XT. (The prior-day value-area part of the idea is replaced by PDH/PDL, which are on hand in the ICT level set; disclosed.)
## C. Family IB (I5), 24 setups per instrument
IB length L in {30, 60} min from 09:30; IB range R must be >= 0.5 ATR(15m). Scan 5-minute closes from IB end to 15:30. Action BREAK-WITH: n consecutive 5-min closes beyond the IB edge (n in {1,2,3}), entry next open with the break, stop below/above the lowest/highest point of the confirming window -/+ 0.05 ATR (0.75-4 ATR bounds), target tgt x R (tgt in {1, 1.5}); action FAILED-BREAK-AGAINST: first 5-min close beyond an edge, then n consecutive closes back inside within 6 checkpoints, entry against the excursion, stop beyond the excursion extreme +0.05 ATR, target tgt x R. First event per day, flat 16:55. Exit X3. 2 x 3 x 2 x 2 = 24.

## D. Gates (identical to ICT stage 1b/2 and verify)
Screen: clean weekly Sharpe >= 0.5 and n_clean >= 40; own timing-destroyed null 200 replicates per cell (p95); family best-of null per (family, instrument) from the same 200 replicates (per replicate max over the family's cells, p95); clean additive uplift @20% >= +0.10. Selected = all four. Full verify() for selected cells: H1/H2 once, shares 10-50%, vol-matched and R-sized framings, bootstrap, shift null, plateau +-25% exact, 2x slippage, strict fills, odd/even months (alt-month leg), worst-decile correlation <= +0.30. Cross-instrument leg: the same family is run on NQ/ES/RTY/YM; a survivor must also be clean-positive (Sharpe > 0, uplift > 0) on at least one other instrument. H0 leg (2023-02..07, sleeve-alone Sharpe) computed for survivors. ERA flag: price-only rules are run on NQL-style 2013-22 by the 1-min cache for survivors only.
Cells: (36 + 32 + 24) x 4 instruments = 368 cells; chance expectation at the ICT study screen rate (6-10%) is 22-37 screened and at most 1-2 selected.

## E. Axis tests of the IMP3 line (counted selection rounds, NQ)
Incumbent comparison: IMP3 tf3 m1.5 (ap_fv_imp.imp3g(3, 1.5), 1-min model) in R units (net R per trade, clean weekly Sharpe, uplift @20%, trades) through the same simulator and roll exclusion.
- I2 VOLCLOCK, 6 cells: bars close when cumulative volume since the last close >= V = k x 3 x (trailing 27,600-row mean 1-min volume) (k in {0.5, 1, 2}; also forced close at the label-day change), ATR14 on these bars, then the identical IMP3 rule (3 same-direction bars, net >= m x ATR, m in {1.125, 1.5}, limit at 50% of the net move, stop beyond bar-1 extreme +0.05 ATR with 0.75-4 ATR bounds, 2R, life 20 bars converted to minutes, flat 16:55, hold 360). Control: the same code with 3-minute clock bars (must reproduce the incumbent within sampling error). Null: depth permutation (as IMP3).
- I4 TIMESYM, 18 cells: IMP3 tf3 m1.5 impulses (duration 9 min fixed by the definition); entry at the market (next open) at offset k x 9 minutes after the impulse end (k in {0.5, 1, 1.5}) iff retrace >= r (r in {0.25, 0.4, 0.5} of the net move) and the stop is not yet breached; stop {IMP3 rule, 1.0 ATR from entry}; 2R target. Because every impulse lasts 3 bars the duration-permutation null of the idea text is degenerate; the null is a random-offset null (offset redrawn U(1,60) minutes per impulse, same rule), plus the timing-destroyed null.

I2 and I4 are counted as two further selection rounds in the program count (cells 24 on the NQ line).
