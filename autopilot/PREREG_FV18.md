# PREREG FV18 - VC arm: live spec, same-basis insurance table, combinations, fill stress (written before the FV18 runs)

Date 2026-10-17. Code ap_fv18*.py on the FV17 tick-built series (fv/fv17_audit.pkl: bars rebuilt from raw NQ trades, tick replay). No tuning; VC k1 m1.125 is the VC arm (k0.5 shown), incumbent = TB3 m1.5 (tf3 m1.5 time bars), S2 = TB3 m1.125.
1. fv/LIVE_SPEC_VC.md (exact rules, NT8 OnEachTick design with BarsRequest warm-up, Rithmic-vs-Databento calibration procedure, order load, pre-live checklist with UNVERIFIED flags) and an extension of fv/EXEC_NOTE_FVG.md.
2. One basis table for VC k1 m1.125, VC k0.5 m1.125, S2 and the incumbent. Bases: A0 no latency; A1 +1 s; A60 +60 s; B = +1 s and +1.4 ticks extra adverse on every stop exit (the measured mean stop-market slippage in FV1; the replay already fills stops at actual prints, so B is deliberately conservative / double counts); C = +60 s and +1.4 ticks. The earlier 1-minute-cache replay of the incumbent (-139.6k worst week) is shown as a reference row, with the top-3 worst weeks and their dates per basis, and the mean of the three worst weeks as a stabler tail measure, to explain the -206k vs -139.6k gap. Live limits for VC at 20% use basis B.
3. Pair + sleeve combinations under basis B: additive 20%; constant-risk 80/20 and 70/30 (combined = (1-s) pair + s x kappa x sleeve with kappa = clean weekly std(pair)/std(sleeve), i.e. equal weekly risk); IMP3 + VC two-arm books (pair + 20% IMP3, + 20% VC, + 10% IMP3 + 10% VC, + 20% + 20%; constant-risk 80/10/10). Overlap of IMP3 and VC trades: share of VC trades with a same-side IMP3 trade whose fill time is within +-5 min and share of overlapping holding intervals; daily and weekly P&L correlations; effective number of independent arms N_eff = 2/(1+rho_weekly).
4. Fill stress for VC under basis B: queue bounds q50 (base), q100, through-by-1-tick only; 30% missed fills (random, mean of 30 seeds); combined (through1 + 30% missed); sweep of random missed share d in 0..0.95 and an adverse sweep (the best-R filled trades removed first, the documented touch-only pattern). Minimum fill rate = lowest base fill x (1-d) at which uplift@20% >= +0.10 on clean, H1 and H2 simultaneously.
