# PREREG FV19 - stress of the FVG family (IMP3 tf3 m1.5 and VC k1 m1.125); no new selection (written before the runs)

Date 2026-10-17. Worker ICT/FV. Up to 12 processes (load 3 at start; 154 GB disk free). Code ap_fv19*.py. Specs are fixed: IMP3 = time bars tf3, m 1.5, 60-min expiry (ap_fv_imp.imp3g); VC = volume bars k 1, m 1.125, 60-min expiry (ap_fv16_ax.volclock). Statistic = clean (2023-08..2025-07) weekly Sharpe of the R-sized sleeve (qty = min(24, 9,600 / (stop x PV)), i.e. R units), plus net R/trade. 1-min simulator (K.run_cell, X1 bracket 2R, flat 16:55, hold 360, FV roll-exclusion days dropped for NQ/ES/RTY). No parameter is chosen here.

1. Nulls, 1,000 worlds each, NQ then ES, GC, RTY: (A) depth permutation (entry depth and stop distance permuted jointly among the cell's setups); (B) entry fraction redrawn U(0.25, 0.75) per setup with the original stop distance rule (VC and IMP3 rebuilt each world). Reported: real statistic, null mean/p95/p99/max, rank, empirical p, and a Gumbel/normal-tail extrapolated p for the real value. Enlarged family null (NQ): the 149 cells of FV10 + FV16 axis (125 + 24) with the depth permutation, 1,000 worlds, statistic = best clean Sharpe per world. Program level: the real value is compared with (i) the family null p95/p99/max, (ii) a Bonferroni/Sidak correction over N_prog = all cells examined by this worker in the program (ICT stage 1: 3,878; IMP3 line 149; FV13 45; FV14 79; FV15 79; FV16 368; FV12 about 50; about 4,650 in total), using the extrapolated tail p-value; other workers' cells are outside this count and are said to be so.
2. Surface (no selection): IMP3 time bars tf in 1..6 minutes x m in {1.0, 1.125, ..., 2.0} (9) x expiry in {15, 30, 45, 60, 90, 120} min = 324 cells; VC k in {0.25, 0.5, 0.75, 1.0, 1.25, 1.5, 1.75, 2.0} x m (9) x expiry (6) = 432 cells (NQ). Per cell: trades, net R/trade (all and clean), clean/all Sharpe, uplift@20% clean. Reported: fraction of cells with positive net R, with net R >= incumbent, percentile of the VC and IMP3 reference cells on the surface, surface flatness = coefficient of variation of net R across the grid, mean absolute difference of net R between grid neighbours relative to the median, and the fraction of the grid within +-25% of the reference cell.
3. Walk-forward by quarter on the same grid(s): for each test quarter from 2024Q3 to 2026Q4 the cell with the highest weekly Sharpe over the previous four quarters is applied; out-of-sample concatenated weekly Sharpe, net and net R/trade versus the fixed spec in the same quarters; also the frequency with which the fixed spec equals the choice and the dispersion of chosen parameters. Done for the IMP3 grid, the VC grid and the union.
4. Fill-model Monte Carlo on the FV17 tick-built setups (raw NQ trades, bars rebuilt from ticks): per order a queue position q ~ U[0,1] times the trailing 15-minute traded size at the limit price (price traded through fills immediately; at-price trades fill once cumulative size exceeds q x size), per-order scenario latency L in {0.1, 0.5, 1, 2} s (same q draws across L), 200 draws per L, plus 1.4 extra ticks on stop exits (basis B). Sequential one-position acceptance within each month. Reported per L: distribution (mean, p5, p50, p95) of net R/trade, clean/H1/H2 uplift@20% and fill rate, for VC and the incumbent.
5. Decomposition (tick-built basis B, +1 s): net R and USD by ET weekday and by entry hour, by year, and concentration = share of total net USD from the top 5% of trading days and from the top 5% of trades (and the same share after removing the top 5% of days).
