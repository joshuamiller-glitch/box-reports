## B. FVG in the pair's trend

Gate for a variant to be a lead: uplift >= +0.10 on clean/H1/H2 at 20%, AND mean net R per trade above the unfiltered sleeve's, AND filtered clean Sharpe above the p95 of 200 random thinning draws with the same retained fraction (timing-neutral thinning), plus the P1 gates.
