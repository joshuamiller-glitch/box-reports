## A. Range-state sizing of E1 (walk-forward)
Motivation: 41% of E1's P&L comes from the 20% highest-range days. State variables (known at placement): S1 = trailing 20-label-day mean daily range / trailing 120-day mean (slow regime); S2 = ATR(15m) at the signal / median ATR(15m) at the same clock minute over the previous 20 label days (intraday vol vs time-of-day norm). Multiplier per trade from the expanding-window tercile of the state among ALL PRIOR trades (min 500 prior trades, else 1): high tercile / mid / low = (1.5, 1, 0.5) [mapping W] or (1.25, 1, 0.75) [mapping N]; normalised so the mean multiplier over clean trades = 1. Cells: 2 states x 2 mappings = 4 + 2 controls (inverse mapping on S1 and S2 with W). Gate: sized E1 minus base E1, additive uplift at 20%, >= +0.05 on clean, H1 and H2 AND weekly-Sharpe of the sized sleeve above the base on all spans; own null: state labels permuted within month x member (100 reps), real clean improvement above p95; report worst week, CVaR5, max DD change (sizing up in high range must not worsen the worst week by more than the base share).
