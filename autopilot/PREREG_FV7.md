## 3. Limit-order setups beyond ICT (same machinery, 20 fixed cells, NQ, all sessions, 2R, hold 360, flat 16:55, roll rule, life 20 bars)
A DISP50: limit at 50% of a displacement bar (range >= d ATR, close in the bar direction), stop beyond the bar extreme by 0.05 ATR (floor 0.75 ATR): tf {3,5,15} x d {1.5, 2.0} = 6. B ORMID: opening range (09:30 to 09:45 / 10:00) high/low; after the OR the first 5-min close beyond one side -> limit at the OR midpoint in that direction (one per day), stop = opposite OR extreme, life 60 min: OR {15, 30} = 2. C VWAP: session VWAP (from 18:00 ET, 1-min volume weighted); after a displacement bar (>= 1.5 ATR) closing on that side of VWAP, limit at VWAP in the displacement direction, stop 0.75 ATR beyond VWAP: tf {3,5,15} = 3. D EMA20: EMA(20) of the tf closes; after a displacement bar (>= 1.5 ATR) with the close beyond the EMA, limit at the EMA, stop 0.75 ATR beyond: tf {3,5,15} = 3. E IMPULSE3: three consecutive same-direction bars with net move >= m ATR (m in {1.5, 2.5}); limit at 50% of the 3-bar net move, stop beyond the first bar's extreme by 0.05 ATR (floor 0.75): tf {3,5,15} x m = 6. Screen: clean 1-min Sharpe >= 0.5 and >= 100 trades -> tick replay (P1 machinery); selection-corrected depth-permutation null across the 20 cells (100 reps) and the usual gates only for cells that pass; independence from E1 (weekly and daily correlation, share of taken trades starting within 15 min of an E1 trade on the same side, partial uplift over pair+E1).
## 4. Pair entries as limit re-entries at the retrace
For every pair trade (13,089 rows), at the flip signal (last 1-min row before the entry) define the impulse = the last 3 completed tf5 bars in the trade direction (net move from the first bar's open to the third bar's close, must be >= 0.5 ATR(15m), else the trade stays a market entry); place a limit at 50% of that impulse (buy below / sell above the market), life 10 tf5 bars (50 min). Filled: new entry at the limit, stop and target 60 pts from the new entry (the pair's bracket), exit at the first of stop / target / the pair's original exit time; not filled: variant SKIP (trade dropped) and variant FALLBACK (market entry at expiry with the original bracket). Evaluated at the trade level on the pair's own rows (net points per trade vs the original, locks ignored for both so the comparison is like for like) in the 1-min model, then tick replay of the fills for the better variant: report fill rate, mean net points of filled vs missed trades (adverse selection), weekly Sharpe of the no-lock pair series with and without the change on clean/H1/H2. Gate: improvement in weekly Sharpe >= +0.10 on clean/H1/H2 and mean net points per taken trade higher; otherwise NO. Max status FOUND-PENDING (H1/H2 non-virgin for FVG logic only; the pair-side variant is new on those spans: a single pre-declared cell, read once).
