## 1. Continuation complement (tf in {3,5,15}, m 1.5)
Impulse extreme E = high (low) of the signal bar. For each IMP3 signal, one OCO pair is placed at the bar close, both valid for the limit's life (20 bars): the limit at the 50% level P, and a continuation stop-entry at E + 1 tick (E - 1 tick). Whichever trades first wins and cancels the other; both are cancelled if the limit's stop level trades first. Continuation trade: entry at the first print at/through E +- 1 tick (price taken as that print: actual slippage), stop at P (the 50% level, distance about half the net move, >= 0.75 ATR), target 2R. Cells per tf: V0 limit only (reference); V1 limit + continuation stop-entry (the ONE combined rule); V2 limit + market at expiry (if neither triggered and not invalidated: market at the first print after expiry, stop distance = the limit trade's |P - S|, target 2R); V3 continuation leg alone; V4 expiry-market leg alone = 12 new cells (V1-V4 x 3 tf). Gates for the combined rule V1 (and V2): uplift >= +0.10 on clean/H1/H2 at 10/20/30/50% with worst week within the share; additivity: V1 uplift minus V0 uplift >= +0.05 on every span at 20% and the continuation leg alone has clean Sharpe above its own depth-permutation null; independence of the legs (daily and weekly correlation of V3 vs V0; the legs are mutually exclusive per signal by construction; share of V1 trades that are continuation); plateau +-25% on the shared IMP3 parameters in a 1-min numba OCO model (tf, m, bars, offset, floor, cap, life, entry fraction, R, hold: 20 neighbours, >= 75% of neighbours keep >= 75% of the all-in uplift at 20%); family-level null: depth permutation (entry depth and stop distance permuted among setups, extremes kept) across the 109 cells of FV8 plus these 12 = 121 cells, 60 replicates, 1-min model, IMP3 V1 tf3 clean Sharpe must exceed the p95.
