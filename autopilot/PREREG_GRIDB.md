## Method (harness = W-GRIDA): trailing 6M pick (3M beside), 2-week roll from 2023-10, older years amber 15-30 / red >30, neighbours agree, nulls = random-clock bars, shifted labels (11/23/37 d), random entries same count and side mix; random-skip null on every confluence cell; naked first (60/60 + scaled brackets, tape fill after bar close + 1 s, flat at session end, 4.84 RT).
Native variants: '_native' = clock-minute lookbacks (time windows / time-EMA) beside the bar-count transplanted version; bar-native families use the bar's own measuring stick (counts, bar duration, delta, bricks).
Timestamp rule: every trigger uses bars 0..i only (bar KNOWN at t_close = known_ts); causality test on every module. Contract rolls: iid change -> additive back-adjust of earlier bars when the bar frame carries iid (loader.py does; harness frames without iid see unadjusted roll gaps: quarterly, ~4 per year).
Caveats stated in advance: levels/profile/VWAP/session triggers overlap W-GRIDD (levels) and W-GRIDA (VWAP bands) by design (different construction on the bar series; duplicates counted in trials); absorb/exhaust cannot fire on constant-range price bars; speed triggers cannot fire on constant-duration time bars; footprint rows exist only for vfp_* (6 rows, 2026-04..10).
