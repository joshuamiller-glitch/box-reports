# PREREG ICT STUDY (written 2026-10-16, BEFORE the first scored run; code: code/census/night_ins/ap_ict_*.py; outputs: outputs/autopilot/ict/)

Mechanism and prior: ICT concepts claim that price is drawn to resting liquidity (equal highs/lows, prior-day/week/session extremes), that
imbalance zones (FVG/OB/gaps) are revisited and respected, and that time windows matter. Literature and every prior NQ intraday family in LEDGER.md
say: level-based entries on 1-minute NQ lose to 1 tick/side costs unless the level carries information beyond "price was here". Prior of any single
concept producing a FOUND: ~1%; prior that at least one level family shows significant respect vs random levels (published as a table, not a trading claim): 30%.
DEVIATION LOG (decided, not asked): (1) bars are built from the 1-MINUTE series on the box (the same back-adjusted NQ series the pair uses, 2023-02-09..2026-10-13;
ES/RTY 1-min from data/databento/*/series_*.npz aligned by minute); 1-second data is not used (not on the box as bars; no purchase). tf in {1,3,5,15} min are clock-aligned
from 18:00 ET. (2) ultraZen / BOS / FSD (item 19): no source, parameter export or trade list exists in outputs/strats (README only); an invented port would be a different strategy,
so item 19 is recorded VOID/UNPORTABLE (nothing scored). (3) Inside-window microstructure (sub-minute order) is unknowable at 1-min; same-row ambiguity resolved pessimistically (stop first).

## Data / clock / fills (all modes)
NQ primary, ES, RTY replication. ET clock via hm (minute of day), label day changes at 18:00 ET. Spans: warm-up 2023-02-09..2023-07-31 (debug only), CLEAN 2023-08-01..2025-07-31 (all selection),
H1 2025-08-01..2026-02-06, H2 2026-02-07..2026-10-13 read ONCE after selection (stage 2 files), UNTOUCHED never. Signals evaluated at tf-bar close; market entry at next 1-min row open +1 tick adverse;
limit entry fills only if a 1-min row trades THROUGH the limit by 1 tick (fill at limit, no slippage, no target check in the fill row, stop checked); stops fill at stop -1 tick adverse (gap: open -1 tick);
target limits fill on touch without slippage. Stop-first inside a row. One position per cell. Flat at 16:55 ET row open, max hold 360 min. Commission $4.36/contract round trip.
2x slippage = 2 ticks/side. Sizing: qty-equivalent = min(24, 9600/(stop_pts*20)) so each full stop = R = $9,600 (library standard); setups with stop < 6 pts or > 150 pts are skipped. Daily P&L by exit label day.
Pair baseline: B.Cfg(wk_floor=8, wk_loss_pb=None) computed in a CLEAN process (b11_nt8 imported first): weekly Sharpe clean/H1/H2 = 1.46/2.83/2.30.

## Concepts, rules, grids (a "setup" = detector parameters; a CELL = setup x exit x session; every cell is counted)
Exits: X1 bracket (stop structural, target 2R); X2 trail (initial structural stop, trail 2 x ATR14(tf) from the running extreme, no target). Pool-target concepts (15,16) use X3 = target at the nearest opposite pool >= 1R away, else skip.
Sessions (ET, signal-bar close in the zone): ALL, ASIA 20:00-24:00, LON 02:00-05:00, NYAM 07:00-10:00, NYPM 13:00-16:00, LONCL 10:00-12:00 (6). Fixed-window concepts have their own window (no session factor).
ATR = Wilder 14 on tf bars. Structural stop floor 0.75 ATR(tf), cap 4 ATR(tf), else skip.
1 STRUCTURE: fractal k {3,5,7}; BOS (close beyond last confirmed swing in trend direction) and CHoCH (close beyond swing against trend); displacement = break-bar range > d x ATR, d {1,1.5,2}; tf {1,5,15}; entry market at next open in the break direction; stop beyond last opposite confirmed swing. 54 setups.
2 FVG: 3-bar imbalance, min size {0.25,0.5,1} ATR; entry limit at CE (50%) or far edge (full fill), direction of the gap, life 20 tf-bars, stop beyond candle-1 extreme; tf {1,3,5,15} -> 24 setups. IFVG: gap closed through within 30 bars then retested from the far side (limit at the broken edge, opposite direction), tf {3,5,15} x min {0.5,1} -> 6.
3 BLOCKS (tf {3,5,15}, displacement d {1,2}, entry {open,50%}): OB (last opposite candle before a displacement bar: range >= d ATR and body >= 60% range), MB mitigation (body of the swing-extreme candle of the leg reversed by a CHoCH displacement), BREAKER (OB closed through within 30 bars, retest from the other side, opposite direction), PROP propulsion (candle opening inside a same-direction OB and closing beyond it; limit at its open), REJ rejection (wick >= 60% of range, range >= d ATR; limit at 50% of the wick); 5 x 12 = 60 setups.
4a LIQUIDITY SWEEP-AND-REVERSE: pools EQ (swing-high/low fractal-2 pairs within tol {1,2,3} ticks over win {10,20,50} bars; tf {1,5}; x=4 ticks, N=2; plus x {2,8} x N {0,6} at (tf5,tol2,win20)), PDHL, PWHL, ASIAHL (last completed 20:00-24:00 range), LONHL (02:00-05:00), SESS (running label-day high/low from 18:00, excluding the last 30 min); sweep = high exceeds the pool by >= x ticks {2,8} then close back inside within N {0,3} bars, tf {1,5,15}; market entry reversal, stop beyond the sweep extreme +1 tick; each pool once per day. 22 + 5 x 12 = 82 setups (judas swing / turtle soup by session = these cells viewed by session; item 18).
4b PAIR STOP PLACEMENT (M5): see pair section.
5 PREMIUM/DISCOUNT + OTE: dealing range = last confirmed fractal swing low->high (or high->low), leg displacement >= d ATR; OTE entry limit at retracement {0.62, 0.705, 0.79}; stop beyond the swing; tf {3,5,15}, k {3,5}, d {1,1.5} -> 36 setups. PD filter (longs only with position < 0.5 of the range, shorts > 0.5) is evaluated as a twin of the 6 best clean cells of concepts 1 and 2, and as a pair filter (M4).
6 GAPS: NDOG/NWOG (previous label-day last close to 18:00 open; Sunday for NWOG) x {fill: at 09:30 ET enter toward the gap CE if price is outside the zone, target CE; edge-fade: limit at the near edge, stop beyond far edge +0.25 ATR(15m)} x tf {1,5} x stop {1,2} ATR(15m) = 16 setups; BPR (overlap of opposite FVGs within 10 bars; entry limit at overlap mid in the later FVG direction) tf {3,5,15} x min {0.25,0.5} = 6; volume imbalance tf {3,5,15} x min {0.25,0.5} = 6 (28 total).
7 SMT: NQ new N-bar high/low (N {20,60}) or new running label-day extreme while the partner (ES, RTY) does not (partner high <= its own N-bar high) ; confirmation = signal bar closes in the reversal direction; reversal entry market, stop beyond the NQ extreme; tf {1,5}; scope {rolling, session} -> 16 setups.
8 DAILY BIAS sleeve: enter 09:30 ET open in the bias direction (prev-day close vs open | NDOG direction | price vs 00:00 ET open at 09:30 | all three agree), stop {1,2} ATR(15m)x4 (ATR of 15m bars x 4), flat 15:55 -> 8 setups. Bias as pair tilt: M4.
9 AMD / POWER OF THREE: overnight range 18:00-09:30; after 09:30 price sweeps the overnight high/low by >= x {2,8} ticks within win {60,120} min then crosses back through the 09:30 open in the opposite direction (tf {1,5} close); stop beyond the sweep extreme -> 8 setups.
10 CBDR/ASIA SD: range = CBDR 14:00-20:00 or ASIA 20:00-24:00; breakout after 02:00 (first tf-5 close beyond range) with stop at range CE and target at SD k {1,2} x range beyond the extreme, or fade at the 2 SD level (limit, stop 0.5 range) -> 8 setups.
11 KILL ZONES: session factor on every free-session cell; pair size tilt by kill zone (walk-forward) in the pair section.
12 SILVER BULLET: FVG formed and entered inside 03:00-04:00 / 10:00-11:00 / 14:00-15:00 ET, tf {1,3,5} x min {0.25,0.5}, entry CE limit -> 18 setups.
13 MACROS: sweep-and-reverse (as 4a, SESS pool and 30-min rolling extreme) with signal inside 09:50-10:10, 10:50-11:10, 11:50-12:10, 13:10-13:40, 15:15-15:45, tf {1,3}, x {2,6} ticks -> 20 setups. Pair pause/tilt by macro window in M4.
14 TIME REVERSALS at 08:30, 09:30, 10:00 and the 00:00 true open: condition A = prior 30 min swept the prior-120-min extreme and reversed (close back inside) ; B = price crossed the 00:00 open and reclaims it; enter at T open (market), hold {30,60} min, stop 1 ATR(15m) -> 12 setups.
15 '2022 MODEL': sweep (pool SESS|PDHL|EQ) -> MSS (close beyond the last confirmed fractal-3 swing within W {10,20} bars) -> FVG (>= 0.25 ATR) in the displacement -> limit at CE inside a kill zone (sessions NYAM, LON, ALL) -> X3 target nearest opposite pool else X1; tf {1,3,5} -> 18 setups x 3 sessions.
16 SILVER BULLET MODEL: concept 12 with X3 draw-on-liquidity target; tf {1,3,5} x window 3 -> 9.
17 UNICORN: BREAKER overlapping an FVG formed by the same displacement; limit at overlap mid; tf {1,3,5,15} x d {1,1.5,2} -> 12.
18 TURTLE SOUP / JUDAS: reported as concept-4a/9 cells by session. 19 ultraZen/BOS/FSD: VOID (no source).
Cell count (NQ): ~420 setups; free-session cells x 12 (6 sessions x 2 exits), fixed-window cells x 2 exits -> about 4,300 M1 cells; ES, RTY replicate only clean-selected cells.

## Use modes
M1 standalone (weekly Sharpe, trades, win rate). M2 additive sleeve: C = P + s*S, s {0.10,0.20,0.30,0.50} of pair risk (t3_score.score_one). M3 constant-risk: C = (1-s)P + sS (INSURANCE/SURVIVES via the T3 rules). Both on clean/H1/H2/all-in.
M4 pair conditioning at the flip (feature -> filter {skip misaligned} or tilt {1.5 aligned / 0.5 misaligned; and 1.25/0.75}), through B.Cfg(entry_ok|sizefn), live rules on: features (at entry bar, causal): SMT-confirm (ES, RTY; N {20,60}), PD position (aligned = longs in discount), FVG support/resistance (aligned FVG of tf5 within 1 ATR(15m) in trade direction behind), OB alignment, last-BOS/CHoCH direction (tf5 k5) aligned, recent sweep (sweep of SESS/PDHL/EQ within 30 min in trade-aligned reversal direction), daily bias agreement (prev-day candle | NDOG | 00:00 open | composite), kill zone membership (tilt by zone, walk-forward), macro-window membership, distance-to-nearest-pool-ahead (<1 ATR(15m) = blocked). ~30 features x {filter, tilt1.5/0.5, tilt1.25/0.75} = ~90 cells.
M5 pair stop/target placement (re-simulated exits on the pair's own entries, see below): 108 cells.

## M5 / pair-stop analysis (answers question i)
(a) Descriptive, on all 13,089 pair rows and the bracket-stop rows: P(a pool lies between entry and the 60-pt stop) and P(pool within 10 ticks beyond the stop) among stop hits vs target hits vs random-level null (pool set shifted by +-U(1,3) ATR15(day)); stop-hit rate by pool-inside quintile; reversal-after-stop (price returns to entry within 60 min) with and without a pool near the stop; Mann-Whitney / bootstrap CI.
(b) Pool-aware stop re-simulation: pair rows keep their entry bar and side; new stop and target: mode {WIDEN: stop = beyond the nearest adverse pool in (60, 100] pts else 60; TIGHTEN: stop = beyond the nearest adverse pool in [25, 60) else 60; SNAP: both}; buffer beyond pool {2,4,8} ticks; pool set {ALL, HTF (PDHL, PWHL, ASIA, LON), LOCAL (EQ, SESS)}; target {fixed 60, = stop distance}; sizing {constant contracts 8, constant risk: 8 x 60/stop clipped to [0.5,1.5]}. Exit after entry = first of new stop, new target, the row's ORIGINAL flip/time exit (if the original exit was an Entry/TimeBlock exit), else (original bracket exit but new levels not hit by then) continue to the next entry of the same book or the 16:55 flat. Control = same machinery with stop 60 / target 60 (must reproduce the live pair net within 3%; else the comparison is made against the control, reported). Locks via B.run on the substituted exits.
Nulls for M5: random-level (pool set shifted +-U(1,3) ATR15, 30 replicates) and stop-distance permutation among the same book/year (timing-destroyed). Gate: the usual 7 on the pair-Sharpe uplift (variant - control).

## Pair questions
(ii) SMT at the flip: for each pair entry compute SMT-confirm for its side (NQ made a new N-bar extreme in the reversal direction within the last L {5,15,30} bars while partner did not; or partner made it and NQ did not = reverse flag); report win rate (target hit share and net pts) confirm vs not, by book, by span; test: difference in win rate, Fisher / bootstrap CI; null: SMT flags permuted across trades within book x year x hour (timing-destroyed) 1,000 draws; M4 filter/tilt cells.
(iii) KILL-ZONE TILT WALK-FORWARD: zones ASIA, LON, NYAM, LONCL, NYPM, OTHER by entry time and book; multiplier set {0, 0.5, 1, 1.5}. Walk-forward rule W: for each trade, zone score = expanding mean net pts per trade of the same book and zone over trades closed before the entry (min 40 trades, else 1); multiplier = 1.5 if score in the top tercile across zones of that book, 0.5 if bottom tercile, 1 otherwise (variant W0: bottom -> 0). Static rule S: multipliers chosen per zone on clean only to maximize clean weekly Sharpe from the 4-set (4^6 greedy coordinate search), frozen, read on H1/H2. Both through B.Cfg(sizefn). Normalized so the mean multiplier on clean = 1 (reported also un-normalized). Null: zone labels permuted among trades within book x week; gate = uplift >= +0.10 on clean, H1, H2.

## Level-respect tables (published regardless of outcome)
For each level family on NQ, ES, RTY (2023-02..2026-10 and by year): PDH, PDL, PWH, PWL, ASIA H/L, LON H/L, CBDR H/L, EQH/EQL (tf5 tol2 win20), swing H/L (tf5 k5), FVG edges and CE (tf5 >= 0.5 ATR), OB edges (tf5 d1.5), NDOG/NWOG edges and CE, 00:00 open, 09:30 open. First touch per level instance (price reaches the level from the prior side, active to end of label day). Outcome: respect = price moves A away in the reversal direction before it moves A beyond the level (A = 1 x ATR14 of 15-min bars at the touch); also sweep rate (breaks beyond by >= 4 ticks then closes back within 3 x 1-min rows... i.e. wick-sweep) . Respect rate = respect/(respect+through). Random-level null = the same procedure on the level shifted by +-U(1,3) x ATR15 (random sign per instance), 20 replicates. Table: n, respect rate, null mean/sd, z, p.

## Selection and gates (clean only)
Per cell: >= 100 trades in clean else 'small-sample' (listed, not selectable alone as FOUND but still screened), standalone clean weekly Sharpe >= 0.5 after costs = SCREEN. SCREEN cells get: (1) timing-destroyed null: 100 replicates (same side, same stop distance, same RR/trail, same session, random entry rows among allowed-session rows in clean, market fills) -> standalone clean Sharpe above the p95; (2) random-level null: 30 replicates of the same rule with its levels shifted +-U(1,3) ATR(tf) (limit/entry/sweep/break levels shifted at creation; market-entry concepts with no level are exempt from (2) and flagged) -> real clean Sharpe above the p95. Only cells passing (1) AND (2) are SELECTED; plus the clean additive uplift at 20% share >= +0.10. If nothing is selected, H1/H2 are not read for that concept (verdict NO on clean). Selected cells proceed to stage 2: H1, H2 read once; additive uplift >= +0.10 on clean, H1, H2 at each share 10/20/30/50%; worst week not worse than the share; bootstrap 90% CI (3,000 resamples of weeks) of all-in uplift > 0; plateau: +-25% neighbours (numeric parameters; non-numeric neighbours = adjacent grid values) keep >= 75% of the uplift in >= 75% of the neighbours; 2x slippage keeps uplift sign; worst-decile-week corr <= +0.30; NT8-codable spec and margin. Circular-shift null reported. ERA flag from the 2013-2022 scaled re-test on selected cells only (series_NQ_long.npz). Chance expectation: the whole NQ grid is re-run on 20 random-level replicates (levels shifted, same seeds) and the number of cells passing SCREEN and the best clean Sharpe in the replicates give the expected chance passes; reported per concept next to the real count. Verdict classes per concept: FOUND (all 7 gates + replication per AUTOPILOT amendments) / LEAD (passes clean selection incl. both nulls, fails a later gate) / NO.
Library: every scored cell's standard-size daily series for the clean-screened cells and all selected cells, via lib_save.save_book / save_family. GAPMAP: claim/close cells in outputs/GAPMAP_UPDATES.md.
