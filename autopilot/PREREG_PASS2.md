## 0. Fixed inputs (not tuned)
- Books: top own-merit books per session from BEST_BOOKS_ASIA / BEST_BOOKS_NYAM (rank = SCORE L6M = L6M $/wk / own L6M ulcer; distinct streams, twins merged per DEDUP_TWINS). Start with the top 50 per session, then continue down the list with no cap (top 100, 200 ... as the cost table allows). Live books (P7, K7, U7, H7, A7, O7, M7) are ordinary rows and enter on the same terms. The list is frozen per batch of 50 at the moment the batch is queued (hash of the list file in the DECISIONS line of the batch); a later refresh of BEST_BOOKS does not change a batch already queued.
- Book = the cell as it stands at its first look: bar, trigger, side, fixed bracket, 1 lot, market entry at signal + 2 s, flat at session end. "Reference row" = the book exactly so, with no component ("none" / "off").
- Windows (data end 2026-10-31): L6M = 2026-05..10 (26 weeks), L3M = 2026-08..10 (13 weeks). Prior years 2023, 2024, 2025 (all months landed). Pick window end 2026-10-31, refit date 2026-11-22 section.
- Drawdown-first order everywhere: own ulcer index (primary), own max drawdown, own worst week; then dollars ($/wk, 1 lot net of $4.84 per lot per trade). Equal-risk judgement for sizers.
- Bar rule: every indicator is computed on the book's own bar type and size (bar-native); a higher-timeframe condition uses a larger bar of the SAME bar family (same type, k x size, k in {3, 5}); a time-bar indicator is never transplanted onto an alt bar. No 1-minute bars as a deliverable in NY AM / NY PM / London (1-minute rows stay reference only; Asia is judged on the book's own bar type like the rest; a book whose bar is 1-minute time is reference-only in NY and London and is not ranked).
- Labels (standing list, same definitions as BEST_BOOKS): CONFIRMED, LEAD, WEAK-BUT-POSITIVE, BEST-AVAILABLE-NOT-PROVEN. "none" / "off" rows are reference only and never carry a label of their own.

## 1. Stage S - SCREEN (fast walker, one component at a time on one book)
Every component below is a column of settings on a book. Each setting is scored on the fast screen (production driver fast path where its hook passed identity, else the screen walker). No component is dropped: its best setting is carried with its evidence even if weak. Number of settings kept per component into the joint stage ("top few"): K_filter 5, K_gate 3, K_time 3, K_entry 3, K_exit 4, K_intrade 3, K_sizer 2, plus the reference "off" for each. A kept setting must at least have L6M and L3M ulcer not worse than the reference or be the best of its component (the best is always kept, label WEAK-BUT-POSITIVE or BEST-AVAILABLE).

### 1.1 Grids (counts are the number of settings per book per component, an upper bound; hooks that do not exist at screen time reduce it and the page says which)
C1 FILTERS / CONFLUENCE (entry = trigger AND k-of-N conditions). Condition library (W-P2A builds from the existing level / momentum / tape / book / cross-market feature code), each condition has 2-3 pre-set thresholds:
 (a) level proximity / reaction: distance to supply-demand zone edge, session levels (Asia / London / prior day / prior week H-L), VWAP and +-1/2 sigma bands, volume-profile POC / VAH / VAL / HVN-LVN, pivots, round numbers; distances in units u = trailing 50-bar median range of the book's bar (u in {0.5, 1, 2});
 (b) higher-timeframe agreement: sign of the larger same-family bar's trend (k=3 and 5 x size; slope of a 20-bar mean, close vs its 20-bar mean);
 (c) momentum / state: squeeze (on/off, release), MACD-type (sign, cross, histogram slope), CCI (Josh's manual set), the grid's own momentum modules; computed on the book's bar;
 (d) tape / volume state: trailing volume vs median (>1.0 / 1.5), delta sign, arrival imbalance;
 (e) order-book state (where L2 exists for the month; otherwise not applicable and said so): top-of-book imbalance, wall near entry, spread;
 (f) cross-market agreement: ES, RTY, megacap basket, NYSE TICK sign (same-clock bars).
 Single-condition screen first (about 150-250 conditions x thresholds per book). The pool for combining = the top N_pool = 12 single conditions by screen gain over the matched null (pre-set: positive on L6M and L3M; if fewer than 12 qualify the pool is the qualifying ones plus the best of the rest up to 6). Combinations: any k of any N <= 12 subset of the pool, k from 1 to N; enumerated as all subsets of size 2-3 with k = all, k = majority, k = 1, and the greedy forward chain (add the condition that most improves the ulcer-first score, stop when none helps) for sizes 4-12 with k = all and k = N-1. Cap 300 settings per book, screen ranks them; K_filter = 5 kept.
C2 MARKET-TYPE GATE: state metrics ER (efficiency ratio over 20 and 50 bars of the book's bar), ADX-type (14), squeeze on/off, range-vs-own-median (day range so far over its 20-day median, and the previous session's); thresholds at the 25 / 33 / 50 / 67 / 75 percentile of the metric in the trailing 60 sessions (rolling, no look-ahead); action in {trade only in trend state, only in range state, trade the book's side in trend and flip or skip in range, off}. About 160 settings. "Switch a session between a trend book and a range book by state" is tested in the stack stage (W-COMBO), here the gate is a per-book on/off and a side choice.
C3 TIME-OF-DAY: 30-minute blocks of the session on / off. Settings: drop one block (n_blocks), keep one contiguous window (start, end on the 30-minute grid, at least 2 hours), drop the first or last 1-3 blocks, day-of-week drop-one (5) and drop-two (10). About 60-80 settings. A block counts only if its sign is the same on both halves of L6M (odd / even weeks) - guard against one-month blocks.
C4 ENTRY METHOD: market at signal + 2 s (reference); limit at signal price -/+ {0.25, 0.5, 1.0} u with time-out {2, 5, 10} bars; dip entry (enter after a pull-back of {0.5, 1.0} u within {5, 10} bars, else skip); split (half at signal, half at a dip {0.5, 1.0} u); book-confirmed entry (imbalance >= tercile threshold for {1, 3} s after signal; L2 months only). About 30 settings. Unfilled orders are lost trades (counted as skipped for the null and for the trade-count floor).
C5 EXITS: all 68 recipes / 7 families (D8_EXIT_SCREEN.md grid; code/th_grid/gexit.py) on every pass-2 book, in the exact engine once W-XB1 / W-XB2 recipes pass identity; before that the screen walker only and flagged SCREEN-ONLY. K_exit = 4 kept: the best 3 overall and the best of the family not already represented that has the lowest ulcer. The fixed bracket is the reference row.
C6 IN-TRADE MANAGEMENT and RE-ENTRY: early loss cut at {0.4, 0.6, 0.8} R if no progress after {2, 4, 8} bars; early take / lock (move to break-even at {0.5, 0.75} R, take half at {0.75, 1.0} R); no-progress exit (flat after {6, 12, 24} bars if MFE < {0.25, 0.5} R); re-entry: cooldown after a loss {0 (reference), 15, 30, 60 min, rest of session}, no re-chase of the same side after a stop within {30, 60} min. About 80 settings (one of each kind per setting, kinds combined in the joint stage).
C7 SIZER (judged at equal average risk, mean lots over L6M = 1.00 by construction): ER-trend sizer (lots 0.5 / 1 / 1.5 / 2 by ER tercile, and the inverse), signal-quality sizer (lots by the count of agreeing conditions from C1 or by tape-score tercile), a 2-step ladder. About 40 settings. A sizer never enters the trade-count floor and is not subject to the random-skip null (its null is C7's own, see 1.2).

### 1.2 Nulls on the screen
- Filter-type components (C1, C2, C3, C4 where it loses fills, C6 no-progress / re-entry) must beat a MATCHED RANDOM-SKIP null: same book, same number of trades kept (the setting's own kept count n_k out of n), n_k trades chosen at random from the book's trades. Draws: 200 for every screened setting (stage 1), 1,000 for any setting that is among the top 10 of its component on the screen, 10,000 for any setting that goes into the joint stage (cheap: the null needs only the book's per-trade P&L vector). Matching also on the week mix: random skip is drawn within weeks so that the number of trades per week matches the setting's per-week kept counts (the "block-matched" draw), the plain draw is shown beside. Score compared on the drawdown-first order: p_ulcer (share of draws whose L6M ulcer index per $/wk is as good), p_mean, p_ALL on all landed months; the PASS rule for a setting is p_ulcer <= 0.10 AND p_mean <= 0.50 on L6M, and not worse than the null median on L3M. For sizers: null = random permutation of the lots vector over the same trades (same lot counts), 1,000 draws.
- Exits and entry methods: the null is the SAME-bracket random entry (the existing W-SHORT stage ladder, 30 -> 200 -> 1,000 draws) with the recipe applied.

### 1.3 Minimum trade count per week (propose and justify; not a tuning knob)
Evidence: own-merit top 50 distinct books (BEST_BOOKS 18:12Z): Asia trades per week (1 lot) min 6.7, 10th percentile 8.6, median 17.3, max 28.8; NY AM min 2.6, 10th percentile 4.8, median 5.7, max 12.0 (the live NY AM books run 1.5-1.9 per week, so a low rate is normal there). Rule: after any filter-type setting (C1, C2, C3, C4 fills, C6 no-progress / re-entry, and combinations) the book must keep, over L6M and over L3M separately, at least (i) 60% of its own reference trades and (ii) a floor of F per week: Asia F = 5 trades / week, NY AM F = 2 / week (about 80% of the 10th-percentile book in Asia is not needed: F is set below the 10th percentile so that the top 90% of books keep their identity; a book whose own reference rate is below F keeps 60% of it), London and NY PM F = 2 / week until their own tables exist (then 40% of their 10th percentile, appended as an amendment); and (iii) at least one trade in 60% of the weeks of L6M where the reference traded. Failing a setting on any of these means it is shown with its numbers and NOT carried to the joint stage as a "kept" setting but its best passing neighbour is (no component dropped). Reason for 60%: a gain that survives while 40% of trades are removed needs the null above to be fair; deeper skipping makes the book a different, rarer book that must pass its own first look.

## 2. Stage J - JOINT (the verdict; exact engine, production driver)
Per book: all components together around the screen's kept settings (state N: per component K as listed, plus off), judged in the exact engine (never the screen walker alone).
- Design (state N): J1 = 96 joint settings: reference; 7 single best (one component at a time at its screen best); 28 pairs among the 7 component-bests (21) and the 7 second-bests; 60 further settings from a space-filling sample (Latin hypercube over the product of kept settings, 5x3x3x3x4x3x2 = 3,240 combos) whose hash seed is the book id; all with the trade-count floor of 1.3 applied jointly (a combination that violates it is replaced by the next sample, no new draws beyond 2x). J2 = 24 refinements: neighbours (one step in one component) of the best 4 of J1 by the ordering below. Total J = 120 settings; 24 of the J2/J1 best (the "roll set") go into the roll test.
- Ordering within a book (drawdown first): L6M own ulcer, then L6M max drawdown, then L6M worst week, then $/wk; L3M shown beside and a disagreement is stated (the pick is by L6M; the pick must have L3M ulcer and $/wk not worse than the reference or the disagreement is printed and the label capped at WEAK-BUT-POSITIVE).
- Prior years 2023 / 2024 / 2025 (only where the year's months are landed): the pick's mean and its ulcer are compared with the book's OWN recent level (own L6M $/wk and SCORE) per the standing rule: worse by 15-30% = AMBER, > 30% = RED (RED = not delivered as a pick, shown with its numbers); lines at 15% and 30% printed. Run for the top 3 J settings by the ordering (so a RED does not end the book).
- Persistence >= 0.58 (share of non-overlapping 14-day windows with own P&L > 0; L6M and all landed).
- 30 date-shift engine controls on the FINAL setting of each book (clock shifted by a random whole number of weeks 4-80, whole component set applied to the shifted entries, the same procedure at the same cost); the real result must beat the 30 shifted results on the first-order statistic (ulcer per $/wk, L6M): count of shifted controls at least as good <= 3 of 30 (p <= 0.10) for a CONFIRMED or LEAD, <= 6 of 30 (p <= 0.20) for WEAK-BUT-POSITIVE. Because J settings were chosen on the real data, the shifted controls ALSO re-run the selection step on the shifted data for 10 of the 30 (J1 only, 96 settings): the selection-corrected null.
- 2-week roll: walk the fitted settings through history: at each 2-week step (Nov 2026 back to 2023-04) refit on the trailing 6 months over the roll set (24 settings + reference) with the same ordering, apply the pick to the next 2 weeks, chain; report the rolled series' own ulcer / $/wk / worst week against the reference row and against the fixed final setting; the roll counts as passed when its ulcer is not worse than the reference's by more than 15% and its mean not lower. Same procedure on 10 shifted-label books (null). Cost: PASS2_COST.md.
- Labels per book (applied to the final setting, same definitions as BEST_BOOKS and tightened by the pass-2 evidence): CONFIRMED = L6M and L3M positive, persistence L6M >= 0.58 and all landed >= 0.58, prior years none RED, controls p <= 0.10 and roll passed, screen/joint component evidence below the corrected FDR of section 3, and the joint beats the reference row on L6M ulcer by at least 10% without lowering $/wk. LEAD = same with prior years not all landed or one of controls / roll / FDR missing but none failed outright. WEAK-BUT-POSITIVE = L6M $/wk > 0 and ulcer not worse than the reference but it fails persistence, controls (p <= 0.20 only), L3M or a prior year at AMBER. BEST-AVAILABLE-NOT-PROVEN = the best setting by the ordering even if it does not beat the reference (shown so the book is never "improved" by silence). RED prior years = shown, not delivered. "none" / "off" = reference rows only.
- Every layer of the live D7 stays ON for a live book; new books get their OWN fitted layers in the stack stage (W-COMBO); pass 2 does not remove a live layer.

## 3. Calibration for pass 2 (family-wise FDR given how many settings were tried)
1. Count the tests. For each session the number of settings screened is M = sum over books of the component grid sizes (about 700-900 per book) plus the joint settings (120 per book); stated in the page header (a computed number, not an estimate once the screen ends).
2. Selection-corrected null per (book, component): the best-of-K statistic. For K = the number of settings screened in the component, the null distribution of the BEST gain over K draws of random-skip sets (same sizes) is built from 200 best-of-K replicates; a component result counts only if its best gain beats the 90th percentile of the null best-of-K (the "max-T" form), i.e. the multiplicity inside a book is paid.
3. Across books: Benjamini-Hochberg at q = 10% per session over the p-values of the book-level best-of-K tests (family = all books x components of the session), with the existing R3 ladder's convention (stage advance on either p_L6M or p_ALL; 30 -> 200 -> 1,000 -> 10,000 draws); the FDR line printed as "null families at threshold / real families at threshold" like BEST_BOOKS (target <= 16.5%).
4. Whole-procedure null: the entire pass 2 (screen -> joint -> labels) is run on 20 null books per session: random-entry books with the same bar, bracket, side, trade rate and week mix as 20 of the real books (drawn from the same ranks), so the share of null books that earn each label is measured. The page reports it: the real label rate must exceed the null label rate; the labels on the real list are reported with the null rate beside them. If the null books earn CONFIRMED / LEAD at >= 25% of the real rate, every label is capped one step and the page says so.
5. The date-shift controls (section 2) and the 10 selection-repeating controls are the book-level calibration; together with item 4 they stand in for a global p-value. No threshold is moved after seeing results.

## 4. Gates summary (order of checks; a failure is shown, never silent)
G0 identity of hooks (inert hook == plain book trade for trade; active hook vs the screen walker within the pre-set tolerance of PASS2_HOOKS.md) before a hook is used at scale; G1 trade-count floor 1.3; G2 random-skip null 1.2; G3 joint drawdown-first ordering; G4 L6M pick, L3M beside; G5 prior years AMBER / RED; G6 persistence >= 0.58; G7 30 controls; G8 2-week roll; G9 FDR / whole-procedure null; G10 label.

## 5. Order of work and dependencies (unchanged by results)
1. Per-component screens in the order C5 (exits, once W-XB1/W-XB2 identity passes) and C1 (filters) first by expected size of effect, then C6, C4, C2, C3, C7; components whose hooks are not ready are run on the screen walker and flagged SCREEN-ONLY (they enter the joint stage only after their hook passes identity).
2. Joint stage per book as soon as that book's screens are done (books are independent; no waiting for the rest of the list).
3. Stack from EMPTY per session with the upgraded books (W-COMBO forward selection, joint refit of account-level rules); only then compared with today's D7.
4. A session is processed in clock order Asia -> NY AM (then London, NY PM) as its first looks finish; sessions share the engine by the rule in PASS2_COST.md.

## 6. What the file does NOT allow
No change of the book's bar, trigger, side or bracket in pass 2 (that is pass 1, already ranked); no selection of the window after seeing results; no 1-minute deliverable in NY or London; no transplanted time-bar indicator on an alt bar; no engine run of a hook that has not passed G0; no change to the live layers; nothing is deleted.

(c) The other 20 of the 30 date-shift controls (final setting, no re-selection) are exact-engine for every book as written. Nothing else changes.
