## 0. Question and prior
Do commonly used pullback levels (moving averages, VWAPs, bands, pivots, prior-day/overnight extremes, Fibonacci, round numbers, FVG/OB) get RESPECTED by NQ/ES/RTY price beyond a random-level null, and if so is any rule built on them (level x trigger x HTF trend x stop x target x session) a standalone edge, a sleeve that raises the live pair's weekly Sharpe, or a better way to time/size the pair's own entries? Mechanism proposed in the folklore: resting institutional/algorithmic orders cluster at widely-watched levels, so the first test of a level inside a trend finds liquidity and bounces. Counter-mechanism: levels are self-similar (a shifted level is as good), and 1-tick/side costs on 1-5 minute stops erase small bounce edges. Prior that any cell clears ALL FOUND gates: 3%; prior that at least one level family shows a statistically real bounce excess vs random-level null on clean: 35% (anchored/session VWAP and prior-day/overnight levels most likely; EMAs least).
Cost knowledge: ledger shows E5 pair limit entries NO, G11-A entry-location conditioning NO, G11-C swing stops NO, C11 ES RSI2 dip (daily) FOUND-PENDING. This study is the intraday level-conditioned generalisation; it is NOT a re-skin of those (level-conditioned retrace entries, bar sizes 1-60, triggers and HTF trend are new).

## 1. Data, clock, fill model (all cells)
- 1-minute bars: data/databento/{NQ,ES,RTY}/series_*.npz (2023-02-02..2026-10-12; columns o,h,l,c back-adjusted, raw_o/raw_h/raw_l/raw_c, adj, v, delta, minute (UTC epoch min), day (ET trade-date label, session starts 18:00 ET), hme (ET minute of day)). ERA run: outputs/autopilot/c3/h23/series_NQ_long.npz (NQ 2013-01..2026, back-adjusted, raw_o/raw_c, iid). Signal bars 1,3,5,15,60 min are built from 1-minute bars with buckets aligned to the 18:00 ET session start (session minute // n); a bar is usable only after its last 1-minute bar completes. HTF = next timeframe up: 1->5, 3->15, 5->15, 15->60, 60->240; HTF values used are those of the last HTF bar COMPLETED at or before the signal-bar completion minute.
- Levels and indicators use only information up to the close of the signal bar i (level value at bar i; SAR/Supertrend use the value that was in force going into bar i, i.e. computed through bar i-1). Round numbers and pivots are computed on RAW prices (raw = adjusted - adj) and mapped back with the bar's adj.
- Entry: market order at the OPEN of the first 1-minute bar after the signal bar completes (so signal-bar close -> next minute open). Intraday only: every position is flat at the open of the first bar with ET time >= 16:55 (pair also flat at 17:00); no entry at or after 16:40 ET or after a data gap > 3 minutes; one position at a time per cell (signals during a position ignored).
- Stops/targets are evaluated on 1-minute bars. Stop fills at min(stop, bar open) for longs (max for shorts); if the stop and target are both reachable in the same 1-minute bar the stop is taken first (conservative). Target (limit) fills at the target price when the bar high/low reaches it. Stop and target prices are fixed at signal time from the signal-bar close, ATR(14) of the signal-bar series and the level (R = |close - stop|).
- Costs: 1 tick per side on every entry AND exit (market or limit, conservative) + $4.36 per contract round trip (NT8 $34.88 per 8-lot). 2x slippage test = 2 ticks per side. 1 contract: NQ $20/pt, tick 0.25; ES $50/pt, 0.25; RTY $50/pt, 0.10. Execution venue: NinjaTrader Brokerage via Rithmic, NT8 strategy on 1-min data (bars of n minutes via NT8 minute series), entries at market on next bar open, stops/targets as OCO brackets (stop-market, limit); margin intraday ~ NQ $1,000-2,000/contract day-trade margin (broker dependent); position size per sleeve scaled to pair risk.
- Pair: B.Cfg(wk_floor=8,wk_loss_pb=None) computed in a CLEAN process (t3_score imported first; b10_core never imported before b11_nt8); asserted 1.46/2.83/2.30 weekly Sharpe clean/H1/H2.
- Spans: clean 2023-08-01..2025-07-31 (all selection), H1 2025-08-01..2026-02-06 and H2 2026-02-07..2026-10-13 read ONCE after selection; UNTOUCHED never. 2023-02-02..2023-07-31 (H0) is used only for warm-up and, where stated, replication. Selection-stage code masks entries to the clean span.

## 2. Grid (NQ is the selection instrument; ES and RTY are confirmation)
### 2.1 LEVELS (41 variants; each defines a long-support level L_long and a short-resistance level L_short, equal for symmetric levels)
EMA{9,20,21,34,50,100,200} (7); SMA{20,50,100,200} (4); HMA{20,55} (2); VWAP_DAY (volume-weighted, reset 18:00 ET), VWAP_RTH (reset 09:30 ET, defined 09:30-16:00) (2); anchored VWAP: AVWAP_WEEK (anchor Sunday 18:00 session), AVWAP_MONTH (first session of the month), AVWAP_SWING (anchored at the last confirmed swing low for longs / swing high for shorts; pivot = 5 bars each side on the signal-bar series, confirmed 5 bars later) (3); BB_2 (Bollinger 20,2: lower band for longs, upper for shorts; BB mid is identical to SMA20 and is not repeated) (1); KC_1 and KC_2 (Keltner EMA20 -/+ 1.0 and 2.0 x ATR10; Keltner mid is identical to EMA20) (2); KIJUN (Ichimoku 26-period (HH+LL)/2) (1); PSAR (0.02,0.2) (1); FIB382, FIB500, FIB618 (retracement of the last confirmed swing pair: long = swing high H minus f(H-L) where L is the confirmed swing low preceding H; short mirrored; pivots 3 bars each side confirmed 3 bars later) (3); PD_BRK (long: prior-session high; short: prior-session low; the retest of a broken level) and PD_OPP (long: prior-session low; short: prior-session high) (2; prior session = previous 18:00-17:00 ET trade date); ON_BRK and ON_OPP (same with the overnight Globex high/low 18:00-09:30, valid from 09:30) (2); pivots classic PIV_PP, PIV_S1R1 (S1 for longs, R1 for shorts), PIV_S2R2 (from the prior session H/L/C) (3); Camarilla CAM3 (L3 long / H3 short) and CAM4 (2); ROUND50 and ROUND100 (long: nearest multiple below the previous close; short: nearest above) (2); SUPERTREND (10,3) line (1); FVG_TOP (most recent unmitigated bullish FVG upper edge for longs / bearish lower edge for shorts; FVG = 3-bar gap low[k]>high[k-2]; mitigated once a close is beyond the far edge), FVG_MID (zone midpoint) (2); OB_TOP (last opposing candle before a >=1.5 ATR 3-bar displacement; level = that candle's body edge nearest the move origin; unmitigated until a close through its far end) (1). Total 41. These FVG/OB are my own mechanical definitions (the ICT worker's code was not available; if it appears its definitions are a robustness check only).
### 2.2 BARS: {1,3,5,15,60} minutes.
### 2.3 TRIGGERS (evaluated on the completed signal bar i; direction long needs approach from above: close[i-1] > L[i-1]; short mirrored). band b in ATR(14) units; touch = low[i] <= L[i] + b*ATR for longs (high >= L - b*ATR for shorts). first-touch = no touch in the 10 prior signal bars; any = no such restriction.
 TCB(b,fa): touch and close[i] on the trend side of L (close-back); b in {0,0.25,0.5}, fa in {first,any}: 6 variants. Core = TCB(0.25,any).
 PIN(w,b=0.25,fa): TCB plus the rejection wick (long: min(o,c)-low; short: high-max(o,c)) >= w of the bar range, w in {50%,66%}, fa in {first,any}: 4 variants. Core = PIN(50%,0.25,any).
 ENG(b=0.25,fa): bullish engulfing (c>o, previous bar bearish, c>=o[i-1], o<=c[i-1]) with a touch in bars i-1..i and close above L (mirror for shorts), fa in {first,any}: 2 variants. Core = ENG(0.25,any).
 RSI(k,thr): RSI(k) of the signal bars (Wilder) was below thr (long; above 100-thr short) within the last 3 bars and now turns (RSI[i] > RSI[i-1] long), price touched L within band 0.25 ATR in bars i-2..i and close[i] is on the trend side; k in {2,7,14}, thr in {30,50}: 6 variants. Core = RSI(2,30).
 STO: stochastic %K(14,3 smoothed) < 20 (long) in the last 3 bars and %K crosses above %D(3) at bar i, with the same touch condition: 1 variant.
 CNT(N): N in {2,3,5} consecutive down-closes (long) ending at bar i-1, touch of L (band 0.25) at i-1 or i, bar i closes up and on the trend side: 3 variants.
 Trigger menu = 22 (4 core + 18 others).
### 2.4 TREND filters (direction of the trade must equal the trend direction; HTF values as of the last completed HTF bar)
 TR0 none (both sides allowed); TR_E50 HTF EMA50 slope sign (default); TR_E200 HTF EMA200 slope sign; TR_ST HTF Supertrend(10,3) direction; TR_ADX15/20/25 HTF ADX(14) > thr AND HTF EMA50 slope sign gives direction; TR_LVL HTF close vs the same level computed on HTF bars (centre of L_long/L_short); TR_PST, TR_PHM, TR_PAGREE = direction of the live pair's open ST / HM / (ST and HM agree) position at the signal time, from ROWS_corrected_engine (NQ pair; for ES/RTY the same NQ pair state is used; not defined before 2023-02-10; flat = no trade). Menu = 11.
### 2.5 STOPS (price from signal bar i; skip if stop distance < 0.3 ATR or > 6 ATR): SPB pullback extreme = lowest low (highest high) of the last 5 signal bars incl. i, minus (plus) 1 tick; SATR1, SATR1.5, SATR2 = close -/+ m x ATR; SLV25, SLV50 = L -/+ 0.25 / 0.5 x ATR. Menu = 6.
### 2.6 TARGETS: R1, R1.5, R2, R3 (multiples of R = |close - stop|); SWING (nearest confirmed swing high above close (pivot 3/3 on signal bars, within last 60 bars) at least 0.5R away; skip if none); HTFLV (same on the HTF bars); VWAPB (long: VWAP_DAY + 1 sigma, short: -1 sigma, sigma = volume-weighted std dev of price around the day VWAP; skip if not at least 0.5R away); TM30, TM60, TM120 (exit at the open of the first minute >= 30/60/120 minutes after entry; protective stop active); TRAIL (exit at the next open when a signal-bar close crosses the pullback line: the level itself for EMA/SMA/HMA/KIJUN/ST levels, EMA20 otherwise; protective stop active). Menu = 11.
### 2.7 SESSIONS (by the entry minute, ET): ALL; RTH 09:30-16:00; GLOBEX (not RTH); ASIA 20:00-24:00; LONDON 02:00-05:00; NYAM 07:00-10:00; NYPM 13:00-16:00; LDNCLOSE 10:00-12:00 (kill zones as defined by the ICT study). Menu = 8.

## 3. LEVEL-RESPECT TABLE (published even if nothing survives)
For each (level, bar, core trigger, instrument): events = trigger firings with trend TR_E50, direction by trend, ignoring the position/one-at-a-time rule (all firings), session ALL. Outcome from the open of the next minute: BOUNCE if price reaches +1.0 x ATR(signal bars) in the trend direction before -1.0 x ATR, within 12 signal bars (n x 12 minutes) and before 16:55; LOSS if -1 ATR first (same-minute double touch = loss); otherwise UNRESOLVED (excluded; share reported). bounce rate = BOUNCE/(BOUNCE+LOSS). Null A (random-level): the same procedure with the level replaced by L + o x ATR[i], offset o drawn per replicate uniform from +/-[0.5,2.0] ATR with random sign, 40 replicates per (level, bar, instrument); z = (rate - mean_null)/sd_null and the percentile of the real rate in the 40. Null B (timing-destroyed): a random bar with the same trend direction and session, same number of events, 40 replicates, gives the unconditional bounce rate (drift baseline). A level is RESPECTED if z >= 3 and rate - mean_null >= 1.0 percentage point on clean for the core trigger on at least 2 of the 3 instruments or on NQ at >= 3 of 5 bar sizes (reported as a table; the verdict here is descriptive). Computed on the clean span; H1+H2 columns are filled in at the single post-selection read; 2013-2022 NQ column as ERA information.

## 4. SCORING OF FINALISTS (the gates; both framings)
Sleeve P&L = the cell's 1-contract daily net P&L scaled so its CLEAN weekly sigma equals the pair's (100% share = pair-equivalent risk), as in t3_score. Framings: additive C = P + s S and constant-risk C = (1-s) P + s S, s in {10,20,30,50}%. FOUND needs all of (AUTOPILOT.md as amended): (1) this pre-registration; (2) clean-only selection; (3) additive weekly-Sharpe uplift >= +0.10 on clean, H1 and H2 separately at 10/20/30/50% and worst week not worse by more than the share; (4) standalone all-in weekly Sharpe above p95 of BOTH (a) a timing-destroyed null and (b) the random-level null, plus bootstrap 90% CI of the all-in additive uplift > 0 (circular-shift null reported); (5) plateau at EXACT +/-25% of every numeric parameter (level length, band, wick, RSI thr and k, count, ADX thr, stop mult, target R, minutes; integers rounded to nearest, min 1; categorical bar size not varied): >= 75% of neighbours keep >= 75% of the all-in additive uplift (20% share), and survives 2x slippage (uplift >= +0.10 clean/H1/H2 retained); (6) worst-decile-week correlation with the pair <= +0.30; (7) NT8 spec and margin; plus ERA flag (positive pre-2023 / gross-positive, cost-negative / negative; 2013-2022 NQ long series; net at fixed cost and at price-scaled cost reported) and REPLICATION (>= 2 of: cross-instrument ES and RTY 2023-2026 same sign and >= 50% of the NQ uplift; H0 2023-02..07; pre-registered alternating-month split: whole search repeated on odd months, read on even, then reverse, the odd-selected best cell must have positive even-month uplift of >= 50% of its odd-month uplift; newest live weeks). Small-sample flag if < 100 trades in the pair window (report 2013-2026 NQ Sharpe beside).
Nulls: (a) TIMING-DESTROYED: 1,000 draws; K = the cell's all-in trade count; entry minutes drawn uniformly from the cell's session-eligible signal-bar completions (same long share), stop distance = the cell's median stop distance in ATR, target = the cell's median realised winner/loser structure approximated by the same R multiple (R targets) or the cell's median hold in minutes with the same stop (swing/HTF/VWAP/time/trail targets). (b) RANDOM-LEVEL: 100 replicates re-running the cell with L shifted by a per-replicate offset in +/-[0.5,2.0] ATR (stops of type SLV shift with the level). Both reported as p95 of standalone all-in weekly Sharpe and as rank of the real cell. Bootstrap: 3,000 weekly resamples. Insurance metric set (CVaR5/10, maxDD, worst week, Sortino, weekly Sharpe, MAR, %neg weeks) on clean/H1/H2/all for pair, additive 20/30%, const 20/30%.
FALSE-DISCOVERY ACCOUNTING: the whole stage-1 grid is re-run on 30 random-level worlds (per-(level,bar) offsets re-drawn per world); reported: distribution of best-of-search clean weekly Sharpe vs the real best, mean count of cells per world with clean Sharpe >= the finalist-list threshold, and the empirical conditional pass rate of gate 3 for the top-3 cells of 10 null worlds read on H1/H2. Expected false FOUNDs = (cells above the screening threshold in null worlds) x (conditional pass rate of the later gates).

## 5. PAIR-SIDE
(a) RE-TIMED ENTRIES. For each live pair row (ROWS_corrected_engine, 13,089 rows) replace the market entry at the flip by a resting limit at the pullback level: level in {EMA9(1m), EMA20(1m), EMA20(5m), VWAP_DAY}; limit price = min(original entry price, prevailing level) for longs (max for shorts) updated each minute from the previous bar's level; filled at the limit when the bar low (high) reaches it; CANCEL (trade skipped) if not filled within N minutes, N in {5,15,30,60} (16 cells). Exit variants: X_ABS = the original absolute stop/target prices (stop = E-60, target = E+60 of the ORIGINAL entry price) and X_REB = a fresh +/-60 bracket from the fill; in both, rows whose original exit was not a bracket exit (reversal 'Entry' exits and TimeBlock) exit at the original exit price/time, and the row is cancelled if that exit occurs before the fill. Total 32 cells. The engine is B.run (live rules: per-book day locks, combined caps, week floor 8R) on patched rows; the 1-minute bracket simulation is validated first by replaying all rows at N=0 (market) and requiring >= 95% agreement of pts on bracket-exit rows. Gates: weekly-Sharpe uplift >= +0.10 clean/H1/H2 vs baseline, worst week not worse, bootstrap 90% CI > 0, plateau (N x0.75/x1.25, EMA length x0.75/x1.25), 2x slippage, null = random-depth limit (each trade's limit depth drawn from the empirical depth distribution of the cell, 200 reps), ERA flag not applicable (pair history 2023+), NT8-codable (the live DualStrat would need a limit-entry state; stated).
(b) PULLBACK-DEPTH SIZE TILT. Depth d = (side-signed) distance of the entry price from the level in ATR(1m, 14) units for level in {EMA20(1m), EMA20(5m), VWAP_DAY}; positive = entry extended in the trade direction. Multiplier from the EXPANDING (causal) percentile u of d among prior entries (first 100 entries multiplier 1): m = 1 + sg*a*(2u-1) with a in {0.25, 0.5} (range 0.75-1.25 / 0.5-1.5, mean about 1) and sg in {+1 (bigger when extended), -1 (bigger when shallow/pulled back)}: 3 x 2 x 2 = 12 cells, no normalisation beyond the percentile definition. Via B.Cfg sizefn. Gates as in (a) with null = the multiplier vector permuted across entries (200 reps) and plateau a x0.75/x1.25.
