# PREREG - SIGNAL x BAR GRID (W-GRIDA, 2026-11-17), frozen before any scoring
Cells: (bar row, trigger module, variant, params, bracket, mode both/long/short) x session (London, NY AM, NY PM; Asia after). Fills: first print >= bar close + 1 s, measured slippage (BAR_SLIPPAGE_TABLE, 2 lots, ALL window, by session x bar group), exit prints as th_swap/sim.py, flat at session end, cost 4.84/lot RT. Brackets: plain 60/60; sym 8u/15u/30u; asym 10u/20u, 20u/10u, 15u/30u with u = trailing 50-bar median range at the signal bar, clipped 10..120 pts.
Weekly bins (since 2023-04-02). Rolling selection: roll every 2 weeks from 2023-10-01; pick = max trailing-26-week net points subject to >= 30 trades ('free'); 'agreed' pick additionally needs positive trailing-13-week and >= 50% of neighbours (adjacent bar sizes of the same bar type, adjacent grid parameters of the same variant) positive over the trailing 26 weeks. Score = the next 2 weeks (out of sample). Statistics per pool: OOS mean pts/week (1 lot), R per trade (net pts / stop pts), ulcer of the weekly OOS equity (pts), persistence (share of rolls > 0), per-year flags: year mean worse than doing nothing by (loss / pooled mean) > 15% AMBER, > 30% RED.
Pools: ALL per session (best pick overall = the headline) and trigger family x bar family blocks. Nulls: random-entry replicas of the whole grid (same count per session instance and side mix, tags rand1..randD), shifted labels (pick window lagged 2/3/5 weeks ~ 11/23/37 days), random-clock bars (rc rows). A pool counts only by EXCESS over the null distribution of the BEST pick of the same pool; percentile among the D null draws is reported; missing controls = NOT FINISHED. Screen is naked (no layers); verdict only from the engine with layers (DIRECTIVE_BAR_LAYERS).
Trials counted: all cells in the grid (reported on the page); correction = best-pick null (no White/SPA here); PBO not computed at screen level.
