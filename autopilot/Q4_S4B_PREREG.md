# Q4_S4B prereg (S4b, 2026-11-04 ~19:15Z): NQ tape / L2 microstructure by quarter and around the 2025-10-23 break. Written BEFORE any outcome statistic.
**Data:** 1-s L2 tables (data/l2vast/tables/<SYM>/1s/*.parquet): NQ 2023-02..2026-10-30, ES and RTY 2023-08..2026-10. Windows (ET, per label day = the ET date of the 09:30 open): PRE 09:00-09:29:59 (only used as a pre-open-known predictor and for the surge baseline), OPEN 09:30-09:59:59, MID 10:00-11:29:59. Instruments: NQ, ES, RTY.
**Series (per day and window):** (1) spread (mean spread_t, ticks, clipped 0-50); (2) top-of-book depth mean(bsz1+asz1); (3) 5-level depth mean(sum bsz1..5 + asz1..5); (4) mean trade size sum(trd_vol)/sum(trd_n); (5) large-print share big1 (>= 10 lots) = sum(big1_buy+big1_sell vol)/sum(trd_vol); (6) big2 (>= 25 lots) share; (7) sweep share = sum(swp_vol_buy+swp_vol_sell)/sum(trd_vol); (8) aggressor imbalance magnitude |sum(buy-sell)|/sum(trd_vol); (9) aggressor imbalance persistence = lag-1 autocorrelation of the 10-s signed aggressor volume within the window; (10) cancel / add pressure at level 1 = sum(sz_can_b1+sz_can_a1)/sum(sz_add_b1+sz_add_a1); (11) the same for levels 2-3; (12) refill rate = sum(refill_n)/sum(trd_n); (13) refill-fail share = sum(refillfail_n)/(sum(refill_n)+sum(refillfail_n)); (14) trade rate = mean trd_n per second; (15) absorption frequency = share of seconds where max over sides of [30-s rolling sum of depl_{a,b}_trd / max(best size, 1)] >= 10 (the l2feat flag); (16) depth resilience after sweeps = mean over seconds with a sweep (swp_n_buy+swp_n_sell > 0) of depth5[t+5 s] / depth5[t-1 s]; (17) OPEN only: 09:30 trade-rate surge = trd_n in 09:30:00-09:34:59 / mean 5-min trd_n in PRE.
**Test 1 - which series shifts at the break date (2025-10-23) and is NQ-only:** AFTER = label days 2025-10-23..2026-10-30; BEFORE = the same number of label days immediately before (2024-10-xx..2025-10-22). Per series x window x instrument: Welch t of the difference of daily means; NQ-only = the NQ shift in standardised units (difference / pooled day SD) minus the ES (and RTY) shift in the same units (difference-in-differences), with a permutation p over 200 placebo break dates drawn from 2024-01..2025-06 (same AFTER / BEFORE lengths shrunk to the available data), ranking |DiD z| of the real date among placebos. n_tested = 17 series-windows (16 x 2 windows + the surge) x 3 instruments for the shifts + 17 x 2 for the DiD = 85.
**Test 2 - does any series predict (causally) the day's fade-bundle P&L or the OR-break outcome:** predictors = PREVIOUS label day's OPEN and MID values of NQ series (16 x 2 = 32) and the SAME-day PRE-window NQ values of the pre-open-known series (spread, depth, trade size, large-print share, sweep share, rate = 6): 38 predictors; targets = CAL fade_cond table 'fade_bundle' (three-book NY-AM fade bundle daily $) and 'hit' (+1 = the 30-min opening range break reached +0.25%, -1 = failed, NaN/0 = none); Spearman rank correlation (bundle) and win-rate difference of top vs bottom tercile (hit), whole sample and 2026-only, block-permutation p (5-day blocks, 2000 draws). n_tested = 38 x 2 targets = 76 primary tests (whole sample); the 2026-only and pre-break-only splits are reported but not counted as new tests. Bonferroni on 76 and BH q reported.
