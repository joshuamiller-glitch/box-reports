## 2. Mismatches ranked by likely dollar impact on the blind-week gap (live +104,095 vs engine +49,856; gap 54.2k, exits +50.4k)
Reading: the gap is carried by ThL2Cut-vs-target flips on matched trades. None of the code-fact mismatches below is large enough to explain it; the cut evaluator is mechanically faithful and its disagreement is data-side (A1-A4).
1. **A-evaluator data-side approximations (not code mismatches, but the only items of the right size, plausibly 10-50k).** Engine has 85 sim cuts vs 92 live; 10 FALSE_POS (live held, sim cut; live P&L of those $33k) vs 17 MISS. I tested one named cause: live `traded` window uses the coarse Globals.Now clock (all prints with now>=t0 count, DS7.cs 1565), engine uses k-1,k. Rebuilt the stream with quantised clocks (private copy): FALSE_POS 10 -> 9 (1 ms/15 ms) -> 8 (250 ms) -> 7 (1 s); FP live P&L $33.1k -> 21.1k/16.3k/7.9k; MATCH 51 -> 52/50/49; MISS 17 -> 20/22/25. So clock coarseness explains at most a minority of the FPs and trades FPs for MISSes; it does not close the gap. Remaining candidates untested: 10 vs 20 DOM levels, Rithmic Last event granularity vs Databento T, feed offsets (ts_recv vs Globals.Now) at 1-s throttle phase, MedLevel cold seed (warm file stream7_warm is byte-identical on med, never a real warm-up). T-record pre-trade-book assumption CHECKED OK on raw data (buy-aggressor px<ask0: 0 of 11,708; sell px>bid0: 0 of 12,210).
2. **M6 cut basis (low-moderate, ~1-3k, direction unknown).** zl2_cut_model (ws9.py:241-249) passes ONE epx = final fill average incl. the later 30% add, AdvExt initial = that average from the first fill. C# resets EntryPx/AdvExt/AdvExtTime at every fill (3050-3053), so before the add the unreal is measured from the first-slice average and after the add the adverse extreme restarts. Also a mild look-ahead (add price known before the add fires).
3. **M4 add confirmation proxy (moderate lots effect, ~2-5k).** 2 of 8 gates (fillsim.py:60-72 micro_with / delta_turn). BASE-9 lists 5 of the 7 remaining lot mismatches as add fired/not fired (P7 Tue 20:03 5 vs 7, Thu 02:02, Wed 07:25, Thu 10:39). Uncertain sign.
4. **M10 P7 Confirm tie (low-moderate, P7 7 lots, NY-AM only).** ws9.py:211 skips the 45-s bar that closes in the same second as the 1-min signal (every 3rd minute signal). In NT8 the 45-s series is a later BIP than BIP0, so that bar is evaluated after the hold starts; entry could be up to 45 s earlier. UNVERIFIED, not tested.
5. **Hole / outage fitted to live (not a code mismatch; see 4).** Contributes the U7/M7 08:20 live entries.
6. **M1 stop-mode map (zero blind-week $ in the engine: ws9_r9.pkl has 0 stop exits).** fillsim.py:13 JOIN_BOOKS=('K7','H7') follows the answers text; DS7.cs gives K7, M7, U7, P7 one shared property (JoinThenMarket default) and leaves H7/A7 unassigned (enum default). The live M7 `_Stop` at +80.75 pts (native stop 20 pt past 60) only fits a join-mode M7, contradicting the answers (M7 Native). Matters for the next week (stops will occur): ~1-3 pt x lots x $20 per stop, plus the 3-s join window.
7. **M2 policy stop distance (~0-1k).** Policy arm 28.5-30 pt instead of exactly 30 on gate books when the fill is not at the signal price.
8. **M3 add disarm (small, 0-1k).** Add can fire after 09:00 block start / lots-0 session / session roll in the engine; C# disarms.
9. **M5 U7 NTZ snapshot order (small).** U7 on BIP3 runs after the 15-min BIP2 (DS7.cs 654-655): at :00/:30 the OTE lines already include the new 15-min bar; exact9.py:87 snapshots before it for all books.

## 3. Verified, pre-existing flaws in comments (no numeric effect)
ws9.py:129 docstring of er_at says include_current=False means the bar ending at t_sig is NOT closed; the code (searchsorted right - 1) INCLUDES it. Tested against live: of 7 boundary-minute entries where the two readings give different lots, fresh/inclusive (the code default) reproduces live qty 5, lagged 1 (P7 Sun 20:45: 4 live, 4 fresh, 11 lagged). So the default is empirically right and the docstring is inverted. er_incl=True would be a one-bar LOOK-AHEAD (idx=count); it is off by default.
