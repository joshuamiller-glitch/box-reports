## 0. Verdict

The weaknesses are in the INPUT MODEL (what NT8 saw vs what the mbp-10 file can tell), 4 outcome-informed conventions in the headline, one look-ahead (<=100 ms) in the fill book, and an unexplained structural difference in cut labelling that the literal port cannot reproduce. T-REPLICA "independent QA finds no mismatch/look-ahead/calibration" is therefore NOT satisfied as written: M1, M2, M3 below are real, L1 is a (small) look-ahead, and C1-C4 are live-informed constants.
