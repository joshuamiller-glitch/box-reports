# REFIT1122_PREREG - W-RF, PRIORITY 12 (the 2026-11-22 joint refit), frozen 2026-11-14 ~16:55Z BEFORE any refit score is computed
Research only. Exact engine for every dollar that goes into a verdict; first-order composition only to RANK. All layers ON in every arm (off/none rows = reference only). No spend. Shadow only. No PASS claims.

## 1. Method (frozen)
- Refit dates every 14 days from 2024-01-01 to 2026-10-19 (Z.wf_biweekly convention: training = 26 weeks ending 7 days before the refit date, purged; OOS = the next 14 days). Rolling method R = pick at each date, trade the next 2 weeks, roll; the realised series is the concatenation of the OOS pieces.
- Arms: LIVE_FROZEN = LPB_BASE exact (live panel: P7 day cap 8,400, 7/4/4 table, P7 cut 40/5x/w5/p3, ATR>=1.6x skip, P7 post-stop 30 min blackout), no layer change ever. JN05W2_FROZEN = JN05W2 exact (LPB + F3 + E1 + C22 + BR3S + FLAT45K). BEST_SINGLE_FIT = the same pick procedure run once on the 26 weeks before 2024-01-01 and frozen (and, labelled IN-SAMPLE hindsight, once on the whole 43 months). R = rolling.
- Candidates (stage 1, no new runs): per layer top-K / pool cells from existing exact results and the owners' multiplier files: lot-level layers (SIZER, LOT, SIGQ, REG, STACK, COOL, ZONE, A06, B02) from _th_joint/work/pools.npz and the registered topK.json (down-only cells; a cell that zeroes a whole book-session is a turn-off = reference only); trade-list/hook layers (ATR-skip, blackout, context cut F3, WL_E1, C22, breadth BR3S, K7 flat cut, profit rules, near-target, re-entry, full-stop and event rules) from the 43-month exact cells on the JN05W2 base (runs_rf/*), each with its scope (the book-session units whose trades differ from JN05W2), plus survivors of priorities 2-11 as their cells reach full coverage.
- Scope granularity: every candidate may apply to ALL units or to one session group (Asia / London / NY AM+PRE / NY PM+close) and to P7-only / non-P7 books, i.e. session by session.
- Stage 2 (first-order composition on exact trade tables): the composite trade list of a combination = for each (book, session) unit the trades of the chosen exact cell, times the lifted multiplier product (key book + signal second; unmatched = 1; integer-lot rounding Z.round_mult). Ranking key per layer and jointly (coordinate descent from the current setting, 3 passes, shortlist 4 per layer): trailing-window maxDD at EQUAL MEAN to the base (cell mean scaled to the base mean), then worst week, then full stops/wk, then mean; tolerant gate: trailing mean >= 85% of the base trailing mean and (when the pick has a PRE value) rule 2a labels; a lead must hold in both recent (L6M/L3M) and older (HOLD, PRE, X') windows or it is labelled RECENT-ONLY and reported with both numbers.
- Stage 3 (exact): the winning combination per roll is built as one exact set (multiplier table + hook list) and re-run in the TP engine for the months of its OOS pieces; cached by setting hash. Realised exact WF = concatenation of exact OOS pieces. Where a roll's exact run is not yet available the first-order value is shown, labelled FIRST-ORDER.
- Windows: L1M, L3M, L6M (from the program's Z.WM, data to 2026-10-30), HOLD, PRE, X', GAP (202507..202510). Metrics in order: max DD (weekly cumulative), worst week, full stops/wk, best-5 reference weeks kept, winning reference weeks turned losing, then mean $/wk. Also every DD with week 2026-W44 removed from both series.
- Placebos beside every number: (P1) stale-fit refit: the same procedure but the training window shifted back 11 / 23 / 37 days; (P2) random layer settings: at every roll the same number of layers changed as the real pick, settings drawn at random from the same pools, 5 seeds; verdict "real beats placebo" only if the real realised OOS maxDD cut and d mean both exceed the placebo's best of its draws.
- Trader test: every rule in the final candidate is also written as what a competent discretionary trader would do; a rule that is only a curve fit is labelled so.
- Costs: measured fills (NQ +0.23 entry / +0.44 stop / +1.36 pts at 08:30; $4.84 RT) as in the exact engine.
