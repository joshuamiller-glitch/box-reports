## Section 2: flow persistence (S2_*.csv)

By ET hour (2013-22 / 2023 / 2024-26): overnight 18-02 ET 0.90-0.94 / 0.97-1.01 / 0.98-1.00; RTH 08-15 ET 1.02-1.05 / 1.05-1.08 / 1.03-1.05. **Reading:** a weak metaorder footprint exists only in the cash session and only since about 2019; flow went from slightly mean-reverting (pre-2018, probably market-maker inventory flipping) to slightly persistent. Nothing at the 15-60 minute scale.

## Section 3: price impact and decay (S3_*.csv)
Slope of NQ return on same-minute signed volume, bp per 100 contracts: 1.5 (2013), 1.6, 2.3, 2.4, 1.4, 2.5, 2.2, 3.9 (2020), 2.6, 3.9 (2022), 2.1 (2023), 2.1, 2.8, 2.6 (2026): flat in bp, i.e. impact in dollars per contract of net flow rose from $0.09 (2013) to $1.46 (2026) only because the index rose 9x. By ET hour: 4-6 bp per 100 in the thin overnight hours, 2.0-2.5 in RTH (both eras). Decay: the cumulative return over the next 1/5/15/30/60 minutes divided by the same-minute impact is 0.94-1.00 in 2013-19 and 0.99-1.10 in 2020-26: impact is permanent, essentially no reversion within an hour in any year (a small 3-6% giveback in 2013-17 at 5-15 minutes, gone since 2020). Short-gamma versus long-gamma days: lambda 3.4 vs 2.1 bp per 100 (section 4) because vol is double.
