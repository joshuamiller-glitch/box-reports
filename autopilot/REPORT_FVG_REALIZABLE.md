## 1. Method
Per-order independent tick outcomes (fill, stop touch, exit, expiry) are computed once for every order, as before - an order's own path does not depend on other orders. The change is the order-state machine that decides, in forward time, which orders exist and which fills are taken: events (placement, fill, expiry/stop-touch, exit) are processed in time order and a decision at time t uses only events at or before t. Rules: (a) ffw: first-fill-wins OCO - orders may coexist while no position is open, the first fill cancels the others, no placement while a position is open; (b) ooat: a signal is placed only when there is neither a working order nor a position; (c) nro: a new signal replaces the working order (no placement while in a position); (d) stk2: at most 2 positions plus working orders, each fill independent. Two scoring layers: tick-level (raw NQ trades 2023-02..2026-10, fills q50 = a print through the limit or at-price after 0.5 x trailing 15-minute size, stop at the first print through, target one tick through; IMP3 tf3 / S2 / VC rebuilt from raw ticks, the other cells from the 1-minute cache setups mapped to raw prices) and exec_engine ECON (1-minute, one tick through, 1 tick stop slippage). R-sized sleeves (R = $9,600 per full stop, qty = min(24, R / (stop x $20)), $4.36 commission), uplift@20% = weekly-Sharpe change of pair + 0.2 x sleeve (non-vol-matched, as in FV10-FV19), spans clean 2023-08..2025-07 / H1 / H2. 'Indep. fill rate' = share of placed orders that would fill if alone (the old 'fill rate'); placements/day counts orders actually placed under the rule.
Cross-check: the 1-minute own machine and exec_engine agree to a median |net R| difference of 0.0046 per cell-rule (max 0.025; larger gaps only for stk2 where the engine counts a fill rejected at the cap and my machine caps positions plus working orders); the MECH reference log is identical on all 3,432 signals (REF_LIMIT_SPEC.md section 12).

## 5. Why the placement-order basis was positive and first-fill-wins is negative
Independent orders: IMP3 21,720 fills, net R -0.0393, win 0.335 (2R break-even 0.333); VC 58,020 fills, net R -0.0234, win 0.337. An unconditional resting order at 50% of a 3-bar impulse has zero gross expectancy and loses the costs. The placement-order basis accepted 11,279 (IMP3) / 18,577 (VC) of these fills with net R +0.119 / +0.207; first-fill-wins accepts 12,037 / 23,912 with -0.037 / -0.020. The two sets overlap in 7,829 / 10,190 trades (net R +0.127 / +0.197); the whole difference is in the trades only one rule takes:
- taken only by the placement-order rule: 3,450 / 8,387 trades, net R +0.100 / +0.219;
- taken only by first-fill-wins: 4,208 / 13,722 trades, net R -0.341 / -0.182;
- independent fills the placement-order rule skipped (placed while an earlier order was pending or in a position): 10,441 / 39,443, net R -0.210 / -0.132 (the rule threw away the losers);
- orders first-fill-wins cancelled that would later have filled: 4,923 / 17,752, net R -0.080 / -0.028.
The mechanism, measured directly on the 4,208 (IMP3) and 13,722 (VC) trades that first-fill-wins takes and the placement-order rule drops: every one is displaced by an earlier-placed order that fills LATER; in 83% / 69% of the cases the two orders are on the same side, and then the displacing order has the DEEPER limit in 99.2% / 99.5% of the cases (median depth gap 10.5 / 15.0 points). Nested same-direction limits: the shallower, later-placed order fills first (before the displacer in 79% / 73% of the pairs) and loses on average (-0.43 / -0.32 R in the same-side pairs) because price is still moving through it; the deeper order fills only if price continues through the shallow level and then recovers, earning +0.15 / +0.32 R. The placement-order rule always keeps the deeper, later-filling order and discards the shallow one that would already have filled: a hindsight choice of the better of two nested entries, available only because the future fill is known. Fill delay itself does not explain it (net R of independent fills by delay is flat near -0.02 to -0.08 in every bucket, tables below); the effect lives in the selection among overlapping orders. For VC (more setups, more overlap) the artifact is larger in volume terms (39,443 skipped fills at -0.13 R), which is why VC looked better and why its depth-permuted null looked 'depth-independent': permuting depth does not remove the selection, so the null stayed positive.
