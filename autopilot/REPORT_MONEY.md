## Drift correction and chance expectation (added after the coordinator review)
- The library holds no trade sides, so the drift- and clock-matched null (ap_null_dm / ap_bj_pipe) cannot be re-run per book; it is approximated at the series level: each book's weekly P&L is regressed on the weekly % return of its underlying (equity-index factor for NQ/ES/RTY/YM/multi, own instrument for GC, CL, ZN, FX, BTC, ...), the drift-adjusted Sharpe uses alpha = mean - beta x mean(factor), and the rank is the share of best-of-N edge-destroyed nulls below it (code ap_money2.py). The proper per-cell re-run is still required for the final verdict of any top-50 book (owners).
- Effect: small. Intraday NQ books have weekly correlation 0.0-0.15 with the factor (the K4 family 0.14), so the drift term removes 0.0-0.2 Sharpe; ranks move by <= 0.06 for the top 10 (RY5_E1_K4 1.00 -> 1.00, T3S1 G6 0.96 -> 0.95, G3 0.93 -> 0.90, B_WT_30 0.89 -> 0.94). - Chance expectation of verdicts among ZERO-EDGE books (sign-randomised weeks, common signs across the 31,686 books, 300 replicates): books that pass every computable gate (H1 and H2 net > 0, Sharpe above the 0.92 null proxy, bootstrap lower bound > 0, H0 net > 0) = 515 per replicate (1.6% of the library) - i.e. about 515 books would be labelled PAIR-GRADE? at the computable-gate level by chance alone; of these 5.5 per replicate have a Sharpe at the pair's level (>= 1.86) and pass the gates; 14.3 zero-edge books per replicate have Sharpe >= 1.86 at all (12 by the single-replicate count shown in the table), and 0.09 per replicate exceed the best-of-N p95 (rank >= 0.95). (gates 5-6 untested), about 8.8 would read BELOW (they fail H0, H1 or H2 net), and all 14 read NOISE under the best-of-N rule (only 0.09 per replicate clear rank 0.95). The actual library has 176 non-duplicate books with Sharpe >= 1.86 (chance 14, 12x) and 1,431 books at the computable-gate level (chance 515, 2.8x): there is excess edge in the library, concentrated in correlated clusters, but a single book at the pair's Sharpe is not separable from chance; only the best-of-N p95 (about 2.6) separates.

## Caveats
- Library books are daily USD at a stated size; "net at the DD-matched size" is linear scaling (no contract rounding, no margin check, no cap on the number of contracts); books on instruments other than NQ are sized in their own dollars.
- H1/H2 are not virgin for any library book (families read them once at their own verdict stage); books selected inside a family by clean Sharpe carry selection bias that the best-of-N null across the whole library partly, not fully, accounts for.
- The sign-flip null answers "could this Sharpe appear among N unrelated zero-edge books"; it is not a per-book timing-destroyed null (gate 3 proxy) and not a plateau/slippage test.
- The library daily series carry NO slippage for the T3S1 books (pts exactly +-60, commission only; a3a74): their Sharpe falls by 0.2-0.3 per tick/side (G6 2.68 -> 2.28 at 1 tick/side).
- Books that trade NQ intraday ("NQ_INTRADAY" netting class) would need the pair account layer (netting against ST/HM lots) to be run in the same account; the column says yes/no.
