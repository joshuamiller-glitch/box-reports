## Method (as registered)
Customer sign convention: aggressor buy = customer buys = dealer short; hedge flow = cust_sign x delta x size x mult/fut mult in NQ/ES contract-equivalents (+ = dealers buy futures). Black-76 on the per-(date, expiry) put-call-parity forward, IV per trade, exact expiry time. Families NDXF = NQ.OPT+NDX+NDXP (trade NQ), SPXF = ES.OPT+SPX+SPXW (trade ES). Fill model: entry at next minute open, 1 tick/side (0.5 pt round trip) + $4.36/contract, 2x slippage for the stress. Baseline B.Cfg(wk_floor=8, wk_loss_pb=None) clean process: weekly Sharpe clean/H1/H2 1.46/2.83/2.30. Selection on clean 2023-08..2025-07 only (>= 100 trades, positive weekly Sharpe in both clean halves for sleeves; dSharpe(30%) > 0 in both halves for tilts), top 3 per family; H1/H2 read once; UNTOUCHED never.

## Honest limits
OPRA flow sign is a 56%-accurate tick rule (no quotes bought; a quote/NBBO schema would be the fix and is not covered by the user's $2,500 rule), SPXW sign is a bar-close proxy, SPX/SPXW flow is mapped to ES not to a cash index, the cell-4 dealer inventory is flow-implied (no OI bought for OPRA), and 2022-2026 contains one 0DTE regime. These make a null result less informative about true dealer flow than about what an NT8-reachable public tape can show. GLBX NQ.OPT/ES.OPT (exact sign) are too thin intraday to carry the sleeves. A cure attempt (NBBO-signed 0DTE flow from cbbo/tcbbo for NDXP/SPXW, maybe $1-3k) is the one untested lever; not pursued because the zero IC of even the exact-sign GLBX series and the cross-family inconsistency give no sign of signal to unlock.
