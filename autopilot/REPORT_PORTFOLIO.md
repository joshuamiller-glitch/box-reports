## 0. Read this first

- **The sleeve series are non-virgin.** Every sleeve OOS series (METHOD-2, 12/3/3 tilt, 2024-02..2026-10) was selected as a family on the same window the portfolio layer scores, and the live pair's window (weekly Sharpe 2.1 over these 142 weeks) is its best period. Even the walk-forward portfolio numbers below (weights fitted on trailing 52 weeks, applied to the next 13) are therefore optimistic; the top-k and greedy sets additionally pick sleeves on the full-window OOS Sharpe / OOS income. The 50% and 0% sleeve-edge rows are the honest read.
- **Weekly basis.** All series are weekly $ (ISO weeks, 142 weeks 2024-02-01..2026-10-16). Drawdowns (DD52) are on the weekly path, max over rolling 52-week windows, as in SI1 (pair 6/6 = 0.75 x the NT8-faithful 8/8 series ap_m2c_pair.npz; its weekly DD52 is $183.7k; the $210k of the directive is the daily-path figure). "Pair-6/6 DD" below means this $183.7k, always <= $210k.
- **Pair base.** The live pair on the NT8-faithful TH-off engine with the live account layer (per-book 5R/-2R, combined day lock cap 8R / arm 2R / give-back 67% from 00:00, combined week floor -8R, lot netting). The live chart has ST TH ON and HM TH OFF; the scoring base stays the TH-off engine for both (no TH-on ST path was built). 6/6 is scaled linearly from 8/8 (SI1 convention); 8/8 = the series itself.
- **Two sizing conventions.** (M) DD-matched: each portfolio is scaled so its OOS DD52 equals the pair-6/6 DD of the same weeks; because sleeves diversify, the matched scale is >1 and also levers the pair (e.g. 7.4 per book instead of 6), which by itself raises income even at 0% sleeve edge. (H) pair-held: the pair stays at 6/6 and the sleeves are added at the same capped contracts; this is the SI1 convention and the one used for the recommendation. Both are reported.
- **Sleeve sizing cap (coordinator rule).** The packs imply 238 NQ-eq for BM13 at the $210k match (tiny per-unit-risk backtest DD). Every sleeve is capped at 10 NQ-equivalent gross contracts (upper bound: all weighted cells open at once) and at a 30% risk share (pair floor 40%). The cap is applied to all contract/risk numbers marked "capped" (cap flag in the allocation lines).
- **Not modelled:** FIFO lot netting among the NQ sleeves (the series are standalone; netting can only lower gross contracts and shift P&L), integer rounding effects on the account locks, sleeve-sleeve execution conflicts, and any live fills. Contracts for non-NQ sleeves are given in NQ-equivalents of the library R-risk basis (R = $9,600 per full stop = 8 NQ); convert with the sleeve pack (livepack/<id>/CELLS_WEIGHTS.csv). ## 2. Methods (all with long-only risk shares; pair floor 40%, sleeve cap 30% each)

| method | definition |
|---|---|
| EQ equal-risk | equal risk contribution (risk parity) on the Ledoit-Wolf covariance of the vol-normalised weekly series, then projected on the caps |
| IV inverse-vol | weights proportional to 1/sigma on natural-size books = equal vol shares (identical to 1/N in risk-share terms; differs from EQ because EQ uses correlations), projected on the caps |
| MV min-variance | Ledoit-Wolf covariance, SLSQP with the caps |
| MI max income | maximise mean subject to 52-week max DD <= the pair 6/6 DD of the same fit window (budget = pair-6/6 weekly SD; 12,000-candidate constrained random search + local refinement) |
| MD min DD | minimise max DD subject to mean >= pair 6/6 mean of the same fit window |
| CV CVaR5 | maximise mean subject to CVaR5 (worst 5% of weeks) no worse than pair 6/6 of the same fit window |
Each method twice: **OOS** = fit on trailing 52 weeks, apply to the next 13, rolled (7 folds, weeks 53..142 = 90 OOS weeks, 2025-02..2026-10) and **IS** = fit once on all 142 weeks and applied to them (in-sample, labelled). Weights are vol-normalised on the fit window; the budget is the pair-6/6 weekly SD of the fit window. "OOS $/wk at pair-6/6 DD" = mean weekly $ after rescaling by L = DD52(pair 6/6, same weeks) / DD52(portfolio). Fixed-share sets ("PAIR + x" at 10/20/30%) use the stated shares (methods do not apply); "(free)" sets let each method choose pair/sleeve weights. Top-k = sleeves ranked by their own 142-week OOS Sharpe; greedy = add the sleeve that most raises the walk-forward DD-matched income, stop when none does (run per method).
