## 3. Bar construction and the W42 recompute
Construction in the engine (ap_i5_engine.get_clock): bars are built from 1-MINUTE rows. Renko: brick 8/16/32 ticks (2/4/8 points); anchor = the close of the row that started the bar, a new bar starts at the next minute after a row whose close is >= 1 brick beyond the anchor with the trend or >= 2 bricks against it, reset every trading-day label. Range: R = 12/20/32 points; a bar ends at the first minute row where running high - low >= R. A bar can therefore close at most once per minute and overshoots; fills are evaluated on the same 1-minute rows (rule ffw, price-priority fills, ECON >= 1 tick through).
NT8 native: Renko with a fixed brick size (ticks) and the standard 2-brick reversal, built tick by tick (bars close exactly on the brick); Range with a fixed range (ticks), bar closes exactly when the range is reached; neither resets per session. Neither equals the simulated bars: the strategy REQUIRES CUSTOM BARS (flagged in the packs).
Rebuild from the trade prints (20.4M NQ prints 2026-08-02..10-16, bars with the same rules at tick resolution, back-adjusted, session-reset): bars per day, tick-built vs 1-minute: Renko U8 9,673 vs 806 (12x), U16 2,615 vs 492 (5.3x), U32 667 vs 236 (2.8x); Range R12 about 2x, R20 1.5x, R32 1.2x the bars. Dollar weekly totals at the same frozen weights are not comparable (40-100x more trades): for W42 the matrix path gives +$30,825 / +$50,867, tick-built bars with 1-minute-engine fills -$3.36M / -$0.27M, with tick-kernel fills -$2.42M / -$0.20M, with placement at the completion print -$1.08M / -$0.15M. Reading: the tick-kernel fill model is not the cause (it is less pessimistic than the 1-minute engine on the same signals); the 1-minute bar rule is. In the 1-minute construction a bar cannot form faster than the 1-minute row, so the A9 'unconfirmed momentum' signal is rare and its retrace limit sits inside a one-minute window; at tick resolution the same rule fires 10-60x as often on noise and loses the costs. The W42 gain of A9_RANGE (+0.47 R per trade on 230 trades) is specific to the approximation. Caveat: the tick-built version is the same rule at finer time resolution, not NT8's exact bar algorithm, and its ES confirmation still uses 1-minute ES rows.
