2. Multi-timeframe votes lose money (-4k to -16k/wk for single-book cells) and the unanimous/majority triggers raise maxDD by 30-420k; vote-sized VC only for both books is +2k (placebo .81).
3. Book agreement/opposite sizing: all 12 cells within noise (|delta| <= 7k vs MDE 14k), holdout deltas -6.7k to +1.8k, nothing agrees across neighbours.
4. Pyramiding (+30 pts add, stop at entry) is flat to negative (paired -0.23 [-0.59,0.13]; +45 pts add paired -0.52 [-0.80,-0.25]); the +20 add is +0.09. Dead.
5. Scale-out (take 33-50% at 1R, trail the rest 20-30 pts to 2R) is the only effect: paired +0.9 to +1.3 pts/trade mapped (+0.70 to +0.94 after direct exit costs), CI excludes 0 in all four cells and in the holdout sign; full-sequence maxDD is better only for SO_f50_tr20 (-19k with direct exit costs); the other three are +25k to +77k worse. It is classed DEAD only because placebo rank is 0.27-0.59: ALL 500 random scale-out rules (random trail 10-45) are also positive (share > 0 = 1.00, median +9.9k/+12.6k), i.e. the gain belongs to the shape 'let part of the winners run beyond 1R', not to the specific trail. Under the frozen rule that is DEAD; I flag it to the lead as the one direction worth a new V2 prereg with a shape-level placebo (e.g. exit-leg timing randomised) and a full direct replay including the 2R target fills, because the effect is paired-significant after direct exit costs.
6. Stack T2.5 (T15_COMBO + PY_t20_f50 and neighbours): -8.4k / -17.1k / -7.2k per week WF, DEAD (Track 1's COMBO is itself NOT PASSED and holdout negative).
