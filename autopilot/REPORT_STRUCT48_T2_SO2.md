## Verdict
**PASS: none. CANDIDATE: none. DEAD: all 8 cells.** Nothing registered in paper_all/. Scale-out ('let part of the winners run past 1R') has no measurable positive effect on the real book: the paired mean effect is between -0.24 and -0.03 pts/trade (14-lot equivalent) in all 8 cells on 5,027 taken WF trades, every paired 95% CI includes 0 (best CI upper bounds +0.11..+0.51), the holdout paired effect is negative in all 8 (-0.27 to -0.51), and full-sequence maxDD is worse in 6 of 8. The shape-level placebo ranks are 0.97-1.00 for every cell (so that condition is met), but the base conditions (mean up, holdout sign) fail; the placebo is not what kills the cells here.

## Detectable effect (80% power, paired, session blocks, 10,000 draws)
MDE 0.41-0.76 pts/trade depending on the cell (0.41 and 0.49 for the f50 1.5R cells, 0.48 / 0.60 for f50 2R, 0.60-0.76 for the f33 cells; about $4-7k/wk). ## Direct replay: what was replayed and how the legs are priced
- All 12,242 Set A rows 2024-02..2026-10 (148 = 1.2% unreplayed, dropped from cells and baseline alike), exec_engine_l2 on the mbp-10 books (prop queue, 5 ms). Seven bracket arms (qty, target ratio): (7,1.0),(5,1.0),(9,1.0),(7,2.0),(9,2.0),(7,1.5),(9,1.5), each with its own entry sweep and STOP/TIME/SESSION exits re-executed by XJ stop-join with the leg's size; 22,176 leg-B trailing-stop and force-close exits (XJ, qty 7 and 9) at the tape trigger second; 122,420 partial-exit alternatives for the placebo grid.
- Two-leg entry: leg A (nA = 7 or 5 lots) swept first, leg B pays the deeper part of the 14-lot sweep (entry price = (14 x avg14 - nA x avgA)/nB). Measured: leg A enters 0.34 pt (f50) / 0.44 pt (f33) better than the 14-lot average, leg B 0.34 / 0.25 pt worse; the pure split cost vs Set A (both legs held to stop-60/target-60, A_split) is -0.007 (f50) and +0.022 (f33) pts/trade, i.e. splitting is free in this model.
- Per-leg brackets: leg A stop 60 / limit +60 for nA lots; leg B stop 60 / limit at the 2R (or 1.5R) target for nB lots; leg B trailing exit and forced exits from the tape trigger second via XJ with nB lots. 334-368 trades touched +1R on the tape while the leg-A limit was NOT filled by the engine (trade-through rule): they are priced from the leg's own bracket arm (counted: A_unfilled). Cap-target tape fallbacks 11-96 per cell.
- Baseline = Set A through the single 14-lot replay path (A_xj: weekly WF mean $19.7k); results are also reported against official Set A (weekly_vs_official in rows: -$3.0k to -$7.3k per week, maxdd_vs_official in rows).

## Shape-level placebo (verdict placebo) - construction, validation, ranks
Only the partial-exit leg is randomised (B leg real): (i) random subset of trades (same count) with the matched elapsed time, (ii) same trades at a random time of their first-leg window, (iii) both; 500 draws each; the pooled rank is the verdict placebo, per-family ranks in the rows. Row-delta construction: every row's partial exit was replayed once at q = 0.1, 0.3, 0.5, 0.7, 0.9 of its window and draws are recombined from those fills. Validation: 20 draws with continuous random times replayed in full (2025-03/04, ~575 trades per draw): the grid understates the partial-exit leg by 0.63 pt on average (mean |diff| 0.67, max 1.36, event correlation 0.85) because it stops at q=0.9; this does NOT meet the 0.1-pt criterion on the leg (it is ~0.16 pt on the trade mean, about MDE/3), so ranks are reported both uncorrected and bias-corrected and the verdict uses the lower. Family means: (i) -0.2 to -0.6 pts/trade, (ii) -8 to -12 pts (the trades that reached +1R are by hindsight those whose price is highest at the end of the window, so a random earlier time is far worse), (iii) -0.2 to -0.9. Ranks 0.97-1.00 everywhere. What it tests: whether banking at the +1R price level beats banking at the wrong time or on the wrong trades: yes, by a wide margin; it does not test whether scale-out beats Set A (that is the paired test, which says no).

## 50-contract rule and peak lots (whole book)
Engine rule (prereg): a new entry whose 14 lots would take concurrent lots (all positions and legs, remainders running after the next same-book signal) above 50 is skipped, both legs; 2 passes. Peak concurrent lots 46-49 in all cells (Set A baseline 28). Skipped entries: f50_tr20 (2R and 1.5R) 0, f50_tr30 1 (both targets), f33_tr20 4 (2R) / 3 (1.5R), f33_tr30 9 (2R) / 7 (1.5R). Cells with >= 1 skipped entry are flagged 'not tradeable as specified': f50_tr30_t2R, f50_tr30_t1.5R and all four f33 cells; SO2_f50_tr20_t2R and SO2_f50_tr20_t1.5R fit the cap as specified.
