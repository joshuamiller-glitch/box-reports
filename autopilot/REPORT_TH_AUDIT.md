## 0b. TH COOLDOWN verdict (Table 4 / 4b, section 10): is the conversion of smaller cuts into more attempts, targets and income real?

1. **The mechanics are real.** Tick port, WF 31 months, PAIR, live layer (day-loss -2R): TH off takes 6.5 attempts/day, 3.01 targets/day and is halted by the day-loss rule on 34% of days; loss-side TH takes 12.9 attempts/day, 3.52 targets/day (+17%) and is halted on 20%; at -4R: 14.2 attempts, 3.84 targets, 3% halted.
2. **It does not become income.** Income per calendar day: TH off $2,209 vs loss-side TH $1,773 at the live -2R, and $2,767 vs $1,215 at -4R: each extra attempt is a ~-23-pt TH cut plus ~$119 of friction, so attempts rise 2x and targets only 17%.
3. **Relaxing the limit helps TH OFF, not TH.** Net gain from -2R to -4R: TH off +$367,738 (MAR 3.2 -> 6.1), loss-side TH $-367,734. Conversion (TH gain minus TH-off gain, 10-day-block bootstrap 90% interval): tick port WF -735,472 [-1,176,175, -119,261] (P>0 = 0.02); B3 tick-mode logic -159,212 [-773,582, 494,129]; B3 sec-mode logic -115,460 [-696,203, 559,304]; in-sample tick port -544,143. No engine or window shows a positive conversion with an interval above zero; the only positive point estimates (B3 in-sample) are within noise.
4. **Profit-side and both-on are worse still:** 15-23 attempts/day but only 0.4-0.5 targets/day (profit-side TH removes the target); net at -2R: profit-side +$0.39M (tick port) / +$0.11M (B3 tick logic), both on -$0.52M / -$0.64M, and none of them improves with a looser limit in the port. No profit-side sensitivity variant beats TH off in the tick port; 'min profit 40' beats it in the B3 tick-mode logic (+$0.21M at -2R) but not in the port, and it loses ground as the limit loosens.
5. **The random-exit control behaves the same way** (it moves -$0.10M..+$0.10M from -2R to -4R across engines, the same size and sign scatter as TH's own response, so nothing here is specific to TH's information) and the grid agrees: of 240 structural loss-side cells + the live cell, 0% beat TH off for ST and 6-9% for HM at either limit; only ~54% of ST cells and 17-23% of HM cells gain from a looser limit (section 10, 4.5).
6. **Verdict: the TH cooldown conversion is NOT real** - TH buys more attempts and targets, not more money, and the gap to TH off widens as the limit loosens. Caveats: the combined lock is validated on one live firing (model 04:30:10 vs live 04:30:11) and is otherwise approximate; each window/engine is one noisy realisation (CIs are wide: the B3 engines cannot reject zero conversion); relaxing the day-loss limit for the TH-OFF books (-2R -> -3R/-4R: +$0.07M to +$0.37M WF across the three engines, +$0.19M tick-port in-sample, -$0.07M B3 sec in-sample) is a separate lead that this audit did not test for significance.

### 2.2 Definitions

* **Arms** (all at LIVE values): off; **LOSS-side only** = DualStrat ManageLoss (ST 15/3/30, HM 10/3/30, WT 10/2/30; momentum 30 s/4 pts, stall 30 s/pullback 4 pts, efficiency 30 s/0.35, eval 1 s - all confirmed in DualStrat.cs:171-185); **PROFIT-side only** = STStrat JADE module (section 2.4); **both on**.
* **Windows.** "In-sample (IS)" = whole B3 span 2023-02-09..2026-02-06, fixed live values, no selection. "Walk-forward (WF)" = the 31 B3_TH test months 2023-08..2026-02: (a) fixed live values on those months (what REPORT_B3_TH calls ON_live), and (b) the setting re-selected each month from the grid on the previous 6 months (section 2.5). "EXT" = 2026-02-09..10-16 (port/kernel only; no B3 data). "Live window" = 2026-10-04 18:00 .. 10-16 04:30 ET.
* **Costs.** *B3 native* = 10 lots, $2.49/side, zero slippage (REPORT_B3_TH). *Standard (live)* = 8 lots, $4.84 round trip per contract (FEES.md) + 0.5 pt round-trip slippage per trade (project convention), applied to every trade. Per-book daily rule 5R/-2R (R = 60 pts) applied sequentially on the full span, exactly as `b3_common.apply_daily`; "day-rule blocked" counts the trades it suppressed (the only lock in a standalone backtest; ComboLock exists live only since 10-15). Sharpe = weekly mean / weekly SD x sqrt(52), weekly = ISO label weeks. maxDD on daily net. Pair = ST + HM summed (8 lots each, no lot netting or opposed-position cost; B3_TH's pair uses its own opposed-cost layer).
* **Random-exit control** (B3_TH method, `b5_common.run_cell` kind=rand): each trade, with probability = the real arm's cut share, is given a target adverse depth drawn from the real arm's realised cut depths and exits at market at the first print reaching it (loss side); for the PROFIT side, which has no adverse depth, each trade with probability = the real arm's share of profit-TH exits is given a hold time drawn from the real profit-TH hold-time distribution and exits at market then (as in nq7_th_overlay matched-random exit). 20 seeds per cell; "rank" = share of the 20 controls that the real arm beats (net, MAR). Controls are produced by the validated kernel in each engine's mode (also for the "B3 real" rows).

### 2.4 Profit-side Trade Health (JADE) - parameter table, implementation, ambiguities

The profit-side object is a **separate class** (`st_th.TradeHealth`, st_th.py:42-116) from the loss-side `st_lossth.LossHealth` (st_lossth.py:30-), though it shares the condition machinery. It exists **only** in the Python research port of STStrat.cs (st_th.py docstring: exact port of ManageTradeHealth / ThEvaluateConditions / ThUpdateTrail, STStrat.cs 2589-2790). It exists in **no** DualStrat code: DualStrat.cs:37 lists "profit-side Trade Health" under "WHAT IS DELIBERATELY NOT HERE", it has no profit-side fields (so "extract from DualStrat.cs defaults" is impossible), and the live logs contain no profit-side exit name. It is implemented in the B3 engine for tick exit mode only (`st_run5.py:345,469`, th=True, th_params) - the `sec` branch has no profit-side TH - and I ported it into the kernel (same one-print-at-a-time emulation as the loss side; fills as the loss side). The instruction to port it onto the pair's books "using the one-second row emulation" was met with the print-level emulation instead (strictly finer; the 1-s row emulation `ap_th_port.th_scan` has only the loss side).

* The tick port and the B3 tick-mode logic are strictly causal (400/400 unchanged under truncation and poisoning).
* **B3 sec mode contains a 1-second look-ahead by construction**: stop/target of a second are decided on that second's high/low before its prints reach TH, so a trigger can be pre-empted by a stop touched later in the same second (poisoning only the same second changes 45% of exits in the test; poisoning from the next second changes none). It is a bias toward hard-level exits and TH looks slightly *worse*, not better, for it. Real-data impact: switching it off moves ST dNet by -$13k (B3nat, 21-lag mean -402k vs -389k), < 0.1 SE. It cannot explain any reversal.
* The B3 `recov` (later-target) flag and the lazy-start reconstruction use future ticks only for diagnostics / state rebuilding from past ticks; no decision depends on them (kernel replicas agree 99.8% with the real engine).
* **Walk-forward leakage:** `wf_select` trains on the 6 months before each test month only, using trade lists produced by one continuous run (state carries over the boundary, which is allowed). No leak found. The leak is elsewhere: (a) "modal walk-forward setting (fixed)" rows in REPORT_B3_TH use the modal over all windows, an in-sample figure; (b) the LIVE cells (ST 15/3, HM 10/3, WT 10/2) come from earlier research that overlaps the test months, so ON_live is in-sample (98.5th grid percentile); (c) TH_TICK's 192-cell fit on the 195 live exits (small).
* Zero entry latency (fill on the bar's first print) is a mild optimism shared by TH-off and TH-on in the B3 engine; for the TH EFFECT it is a favourable draw (3.4).

### 4.0 The account layer: what is modelled, how it was validated

Source: DualStrat.cs CheckLimits (848-872) and CheckComboLock (808-845); parameters from SetDefaults (ComboProfitCapR 5, ComboGiveBackArmR 2, ComboGiveBackPct 67, ComboLockFromHHmm 0, loss floor / week floor / max trades off; per-book 48,000 / 19,200 / 57,600 at 8 lots = +5R / -2R / -6R, R = $9,600).

* **Modelled:** (a) virtual, independent books (OppositeEntry AsIs; broker net P&L = sum of the books, so no lot netting, unlike the DDMIN layer whose FIFO netting is not the live behaviour); (b) per-book REALISED day profit +5R and day loss -lR, evaluated when a trade closes, halting the book from the next bar until the next session (label day = ET + 6 h); (c) per-book realised week loss -6R, halting to Monday; (d) the combined lock on realised + unrealised P&L of both books, checked every 2 s on the Databento print tape (price = last print), cap +5R of $9,600 and give-back (arm when the day peak >= 2R, lock when the day falls to 33% of the peak), on a lock all open trades close at that print and nothing more is taken that session; (e) the session windows, flatten, entry rules of the chosen engine (kernel session). Trades inside a session are a causal chain, so applying a halt or lock = truncating the chain after that moment (exact).
* **Not modelled / approximate:** lock fill latency and slippage beyond the standard 0.5 pt + fees; the live 10-18 .. 10-20 StopJoin / StopJoinFallback exits; restarts and carried state; the 18:00-18:05 and 15:00-16:54 entry windows of the 10-20 live schedule (the kernel sessions use the research / ET windows of section 3.2); the combo lock existed live only since 10-15, but is applied to the whole history here (the user asked for the live layer); the chart-level combo week floor is off in DualStrat.cs, so only the per-book week -6R is applied; week-loss bookkeeping uses pre-lock trade results on the lock day.
* **Validation of the combo lock against live:** the only ComboLock firing inside the print archive (<= 2026-10-16) is HM/ST 2026-10-16 04:30:11. Fed the 13 live trades of that session (live entry prices and times, the two locked trades left open), the model's 2-s scan fires a GIVE-BACK lock at 04:30:10 (day peak $28,025 at 01:05, floor 33% = $9,248, day $9,080) = 1 s before the live lock, for the right reason.
