## 1. Re-derivation of the time-stop / exit_ts rule
* 1-minute bars (shared evaluator, unchanged): bar k covers [ts_k, ts_k+60). The deadline D = entry_ts + tstop_s. The simulator can only observe a bar at its close, so the time exit is the CLOSE of the bar covering D: first k with ts_k+60 >= D, price c_k, exit_ts = ts_k+60; tested AFTER the flat, stop and target tests of the same bar (stop first, then target, then time). Correct for 60-s bars.
* 1-second bars (the bug): the same formula with a 1-s bar fires on the first bar for tstop <= 60 s (ts_k+60 >= D is true immediately) and 59-60 s early for longer holds, while exit_ts is written as ts_k+60. Confirmed: planted hold 30 s exits at 60 s with the original, hold 60/300/1800 s realise only the entry bar for 60 s. This is CHAR-C's D1.
* Variant rule: for bar_s < 60, a time stop is a market order issued at D, filled at the open of the first bar with ts_k >= D (price o_k, exit_ts = ts_k). Placed after the flat test and BEFORE the stop/target test of that bar (a deadline bar's range is after the order, so it must not trigger the stop/target); a stop or target touched in an EARLIER bar is honoured first. For bar_s = 60 the original line is kept untouched. I re-derived this independently and agree: for W-second bars the first observable print at or after D is the open of the first bar with ts >= D; CHAR-C's sim1s (close of the bar covering D, ts+1 >= D) is the other defensible convention: on 4 real 1-s frames the two differ by at most 5 s in exit_ts and the P&L correlation is >= 0.999 (table below), so either is fine; the shared pages should state one.
* bar_s detection: median gap of the first 100,000 sorted bar timestamps < 30 s -> sub-minute. Measured medians on the owned 1-s tapes: ES/NQ/RTY/GC/CL 1.0 s, ZN 2.0, 6J 3.0, NKD 14.0 (p90 97 s): all classified sub-minute, NKD with the smallest margin.

## 2. Own planted-path tests (14 tests, all PASS; script t1_planted.py)
Dense 1-s tape with a +0.01/s drift, long, no stop/target hits: hold 30 / 60 / 300 / 1800 s -> variant exit_ts = entry + h exactly and pnl = drift - cost (the original exits 60 s for h = 30). Same-bar stop + target (long and short): stop first, exit at the stop level minus stop_extra, exit_ts = that bar (pnl -215.0 vs expected -215.0 both sides). Stop before the deadline honoured (exit 30 s); target before the deadline honoured with fill at the level; flat before the deadline exits at the flat bar open. 60-s bars, holds 60 / 300 / 1800 s: variant == original (same tuple), exit at the close of the covering bar, exit_ts - entry = h. 
Behaviours to know (all by design, none a defect): (a) a deadline bar that also touches the stop exits by TIME at its open in the variant (market order issued at the deadline), whereas a 1-minute frame exits by the stop in the covering bar: planted case pnl +0.10 pts vs -0.50 pts; negligible at 1-s resolution but not identical semantics. (b) exit_ts is stamped at the bar open for 1-s frames (and at ts+60 for 1-min frames).

## 5. Findings (notes for the owners, none blocks acceptance)
* N1 (medium): the bar-width test is implicit. A 1-s series with a median gap >= 30 s (thin instruments or overnight-only slices; NKD is at 14 s, p90 97 s) silently falls back to the 60-s branch and the old bug returns without any message. Recommend an explicit bar_s argument or an assertion/warning when the median gap is between 5 and 60 s.
* N2 (low/medium): the deadline clock starts at entry_ts, not at the fill bar. If the first print is later than entry_ts + tstop_s (sparse tape, short tstop) the trade enters and exits on the same bar with zero hold: planted case entry bar at +45 s, tstop 30 s -> exit on the entry bar, pnl = -cost. CHAR-A's tick books set entry_ts = the first print at/after ts0+1, so their frames are aligned (checked in the tick_books_fix docstring); other callers must do the same or measure the clock from the fill bar.
* N3 (low): bar widths >= 2 minutes keep the 60-s branch (wrong by design); only 1-min and sub-minute frames are supported. 
* N4 (low): the two conventions (variant: open of the first bar >= D; sim1s: close of the bar covering D) coexist on pages; state one.
