# REXIT_C3_PREREG (W-RX): exit score, walk-forward, actions, controls - frozen before any C3 scoring (C1/C2 preregistered separately in REXIT_PREREG.md)
Rows: every 5th closed minute in trade (j = 5, 10, ..., 480) of every trade of the source (raw stage 0 or JL2), per book. Target rem_R clipped +-3. Features (all causal): the signed readings of code/th_rexit/conds.py SIGNED + UNSIGNED_Z + 3 squeeze-on flags, the 16 cluster votes now, count of clusters against now, count flipped since entry, path state (open R, best R, give-back R, ln minutes in trade, minutes since best, distance to stop and target, atr_rel, range used, level distances), missing-block indicators (L2, L3, alpha, markets, tape); missing readings = 0.
Walk-forward: test blocks of 2 weeks, train = the trailing 26 weeks of trades ENTERED before the block and CLOSED before the block (label known), per book; ridge (closed form on standardised columns), alpha from {100, 1000, 10000} chosen on the last 4 weeks of the training window; boosted check = LightGBM (150 trees, 8 leaves, lr 0.05, min_child 200, subsample 0.5, lambda 50), same walk-forward. Predictions exist for trades entered from week 27 of data on (2023-10 onwards); every comparison below uses this same span. Path-only model (path columns only) is the baseline for the information the readings add. Controls: shuffled outcomes (rem_R permuted inside book x session x checkpoint, 3 seeds) and readings taken from the trade 23 trading days earlier in the same cell (3 shifts 11/23/37 for the IC).
Actions (one per trade, the first row that fires; no later action): thresholds on the predicted remaining R, separate in profit (open > 0) and in loss (open <= 0): theta in {off, -0.05, -0.10, -0.20, -0.30} each, 24 combinations (not both off) per action family; families: X = exit the whole trade at the next second after the decision minute (cost: 1.2 pt slip), H = take half off at the decision (1.2 pt slip) and keep the rest to its real exit with the stop moved to breakeven, B = tighten the stop on the whole trade: in profit to breakeven (entry), in loss to 20 pt below the current price; stop fills at the level with 1.2 pt slip; evaluated on the stored 1-s paths (path arithmetic, no re-entry; the exact engine is part D). Total 72 variants per book per source; all 72 are counted as trials and all enter White RC / Hansen SPA (stationary bootstrap, 2,000, weekly P&L difference vs the unchanged trade), DSR of the best, BH 10 percent on the t of the weekly difference, and CSCV PBO (12, 14, 16 blocks, 1 week purge) on the 72-column weekly-difference matrix.
Controls for the best variant per book: random exit with the same share of early actions per state, the same distribution of minutes-since-entry and open profit at the action (template rows matched by (minutes/30, open R/0.5) inside the same trade, persistent draw bucket 300), 30 draws; realised share vs the real rule printed, > 0.05 apart = invalid and redrawn; percentile of the real delta in mean $, in weekly max drawdown and in worst week. Simple give-back comparator (exit when give-back >= F of best, best >= B pt; F in {0.4,0.5,0.6}, B in {15,25,40}: 9 variants, all counted) on the same rows. Judging: percentile among >= 30 draws, never "beats all". $ at base lots; $4.84 RT column and x1.75 live-lot column by path arithmetic.
