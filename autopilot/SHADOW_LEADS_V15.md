## 1. Common definitions
**Inputs (weekly).** (a) `forward_register/th_eval.csv` of D7 v1.5 (every live TH-L2 evaluation: timestamp, book, unreal, pulled, MedLevel, secs_ext/newMae, DepthLive, decision; order send/fill times; 60 s of path after each cut), read with `code/gate0/w2/th_eval_io.py` and its aliases (core fields ts, book, unreal, pulled, med_level, secs_ext, depth_live, decision; optional side, entry_px, entry_ts, price, trade_id, order_send_ts, fill_ts, fill_px). (b) the week's live `StratTrades_DS7_<acct>.csv` (slices aggregated to trades by (book, exit time, exit name, side); times = ET wall clock; USD gross recomputed as side x (exit-entry) x qty x 20 because pnlGross is blank). (c) tape, best available, auto-labelled `tape_mode`:
| tape_mode | what is on disk | what it can decide |
|--|--|--|
| PRINTS+L2 | raw-print npz (p_ts_recv, p_px in ticks) + l2pre npz (row, cb, ca, med, live), i.e. the output of code/replica/l2pre.py run on the week's mbp-10 | everything: pulled support at any window (P06/P07 15 s, P05 2 s, P11 3 s), print-driven evaluation after the live exit, F10 on 100-ms samples, bracket touches |
| 1S | `forward_register/ohlcv1s/NQ_*.npz` (ts,o,h,l,c) or tape.csv (sec,open,high,low,close) | prices only: A4, C3, F08, bracket touches, F10 at 1-s resolution (flag; understates path length). NOT the pulled-support leads after a live cut |
| NONE | th_eval rows only (+ the 60 s post-cut path if merged) | A4 (needs only the cut times and the trade list), C3 and F08 while the live trade is open. P06/P07/P05/B3 on trades the live cell cut: status NEEDS_PATH |
**Units.** Prices in index points (NQ tick 0.25), money USD = points x qty x 20. `unreal` in th_eval is points. `pulled` is the logged 5-s window (TH PULL_SECS 5); MedLevel as logged; thresholds are in the units of the C# (LOSS_MIN pt, PULL_MULT x MedLevel, PULL_SECS s; ThL2 evaluated at most once per second per book, only while DepthLive).
**Live definition every cut lead is compared with ("live cell").** ThL2 loss branch + policy loss line, identical inputs: cut when unreal <= -30 pt AND MedLevel > 0 AND pulled(5 s, own side) >= 30 x MedLevel AND the adverse extreme was set within the last 5 s (secs_ext <= 5), evaluated once per second per book while DepthLive; scope books K7 P7 U7 H7 M7 (L2GATE_BOOKS). Live exit kinds counted as a live cut: ThL2Cut, PolicyCut. L2 profit side OFF, L1 profit OFF (hold-to-bracket); U7 keeps its live 30-min same-side blackout after a ThL2 cut. Bracket: stop = STOPPTS (60 pt; O7 40; X3/X4 75), target = stop x TARGETR (1.0; O7 2.25) as in code/replica/sim.py; if live session brackets differ the scorer's hold-through result for that trade is a model value (flag `bracket=model`).
**Fill model for a lead exit.** Decision at an evaluation row time t; market exit fills at t + 1.0 s (replica LAT_FILL) at the tape last price at or before that time (or, with no tape, at entry + side x unreal of the last th_eval row within 3 s). If the lead decides at the same row as the live cut (|dt| < 0.5 s) the lead exit IS the live exit, delta 0. `delta_usd` = lead P&L minus the ACTUAL live P&L of that trade (primary). `delta_model_usd` = lead minus the live exit modelled with the same fill rule (diagnostic of fill-model bias; empty when no live cut to model).
**Statuses per (lead, live trade).** OUT_OF_SCOPE; NO_TRIGGER (lead and live exits coincide, delta 0); OK (lead differs, delta counted); NEEDS_L2 (lead would have kept the trade past the live cut and the pulled series is absent: only a hold-through BOUND is logged in `delta_bound_usd`, nothing enters the sums); NEEDS_PATH; NO_EVAL_ROWS. Undetermined trades are counted (`n_undetermined`) and the evaluation below refuses SUPPORT when more than 25% of in-scope trades are undetermined.
**Not modelled (declared).** Cascade: a changed exit changes later signals, re-entries, lots, caps; the shadow keeps every other live trade as it was. Overlap flag not yet raised if a held trade would run into the next live trade of the same book. Commissions (equal per round trip, gross used). Native-stop slippage of a lock (C3 books the lock price). Intra-bar ordering on the 1-s tape (a bar touching stop and target is booked as the stop).

## 2. The leads: exact rule, scope, counterfactual
Common register fields per decision: lead, week_id, book, side, qty, entry_ts/px, live exit ts/px/kind, live P&L, cf_decision, cf_fire_ts (decision time), cf_exit_ts, cf_exit_px, cf_exit_kind, cf_pnl_usd, delta_usd, delta_model_usd, status, basis (LOGGED / TAPE / LOGGED5_PROXY_LB|UB / tape_prints / rows).

**A-P06** (arm 40 pt / pulled 10x MedLevel / window 15 s / persistence 5 / ext live-type). Cut when unreal <= -40 AND MedLevel > 0 AND pulled(15 s own side) >= 10 x MedLevel AND secs_ext <= 15 on 5 consecutive evaluated seconds (counter resets on any evaluated second that fails; not-DepthLive seconds are not evaluations). Books K7 P7 U7 H7 M7, all sessions. Replaces the live cell (cut and policy line). Counterfactual per live trade: (i) walk the th_eval rows of the trade; first row where the rule holds 5 in a row = decision; (ii) if the live cell cut earlier and the lead has not fired, continue on the tape (print-driven evaluations, pulled from l2pre) until the lead fires or the bracket is touched (hold_through) or the tape ends (open_at_tape_end); (iii) log decision, fill, P&L, delta. Pulled for the 15-s window comes from the tape; without it the logged 5-s value is a lower bound (basis LOGGED5_PROXY_LB) and post-cut continuation is NEEDS_L2.
**A-P07**: identical, pulled mult 5x.   **A-P05 (contrast only)**: arm 45 / 10x / window 2 s / persistence 1 / live-type (upper-bound proxy when only the 5-s pulled is logged: LOGGED5_PROXY_UB).
Compared with: the live cell (section 1). Replica claim being tested (14 units, USD/week vs HOLD, L1M/L2M/L3M/L6M/HOLD/MID/PRE/X'): P06 -611/-5004/2378/5593/4862/1073/1550/-849; P07 -583/-4979/2520/4927/4622/1419/1570/-1282; P05 -1835/941/3638/4588/2161/-428/2664/116.

**A4 BO_both_5** (5-min both-side post-cut blackout). After every live ThL2Cut on a book (cut time = the th_eval row with decision ThL2Cut; PolicyCut sets no blackout in the C#; fallback: live ThL2Cut exit time - 1 s) no entry on either side in that book for 300 s. Counterfactual: every live trade of that book whose entryTime lies in (cut, cut + 300 s) is suppressed; cf_pnl = 0, delta = - its live P&L (gross); logged one row per suppressed trade, plus the count of cuts (`n_cuts` in notes). Needs no tape. U7: same-side entries are already blocked live for 30 min, so only opposite-side U7 entries can appear. Cascade effects (a suppressed entry changes later states) are NOT counted. Claim being tested: L1M..PRE +2426/+1948/+1643/+3390/+1833/-41/+647/+263 per week (all books, full replica including the cascade).

**B3 F10 + P11, K7 only** (AddToPath: both run). P11 = arm 35 / 10x / window 3 s / persistence 3 / live-type replaces the live cut on K7; F10 = path rule: eligible once the adverse extreme >= 20 pt, evaluated at the first print >= 10 s after the previous evaluation; votes (need 3 of 3): momentum (price now vs 15 s ago, >= 8 pt against), stall (adverse extreme >= 30 s old and recovery from it <= 6 pt), efficiency (|net| / path over 60 s < 0.4) on 100-ms samples; market exit. Decision = the earlier of P11 and F10. Counterfactual: as P06, plus F10 on the print/sample path; F10 on a 1-s tape is flagged (basis ...|rows or 1S resolution) and understates path. Claim: 2115/2971/3275/3188/2219/370/190/97.

**C3 lock 70% / +2 pt, K7 only.** Arm when the favourable extreme >= 0.70 x target distance (K7: 0.70 x 60 = 42 pt); then a stop at entry + side x 2 pt; first touch after the arming bar/row exits at the lock price. Only the live trade's open period is scanned; if the live exit comes first the delta is 0. Log arm time, lock price, would-be exit, outcome. Claim: 2201/2492/1877/2577/606/-325/342/23.

**D F08 (P7 only, entry session nyam 09:30-12:00 ET).** At entryTime + 45 min, if the live trade is still open and the favourable progress < 20% of the target distance (P7: < 12 pt, negative included), market exit (fill +1 s). Log progress at the horizon, exit, delta. Clock = StratTrades entryTime of the first slice (the replica measures from its position-entry timestamp; on the one blind-week trigger the two clocks differed by 241 s - to be resolved from the live fill times). Claim (stack basis): 0/174/350/168/232/485/528/321.

## 3. Outputs
`shadow_trades_<week>_<label>.csv` (per lead per live trade, all fields above, per book via the book column) and `shadow_register.csv`, **append-only, one row per lead per week** (re-run of the same week/label/lead appends version+1; older rows never rewritten): week_id, version, label (LIVE|SYNTHETIC|REPLICA), run_utc, lead, th_eval/trades sha16, tape_mode, n_trades_scope, n_eval_rows, n_determined, n_trig, n_pos, n_neg, n_undetermined, delta_usd, delta_model_usd, delta_bound_usd, live_pnl_scope_usd, live_ref_agree (live cell re-evaluated on the rows vs the logged cuts), by_book_json (n_trig, delta, n_undetermined per book), notes. ## 4. Pre-registered success criteria (fixed now, before any live row exists)
Evidence accumulates over weeks; **no single-week decision**; the cumulative record is read only at weeks 8, 13, 26, 52 (LOOKS) and only from label LIVE, latest version of each week. A lead is "SUPPORT_CANDIDATE" at a look only if ALL hold: (1) cumulative determined triggers >= n_min; (2) mean weekly delta >= floor (half of the replica L6M claim) and z = mean / (sd_week / sqrt(W)) >= 2.5 on the shadow's own weekly deltas (2.5 not 2.0 because seven lines and several looks are read, and each lead is best-of-N from the replica search); (3) positive in >= 60% of the weeks that had a trigger; (4) <= 25% of in-scope trades undetermined; (5) the sign also holds with the largest-|delta| week dropped. "CONTRARY" when z <= -1.5 at a look. Otherwise OPEN (or INSUFFICIENT before week 8 / n_min). SUPPORT_CANDIDATE is not an ENTER: it only reopens the lead for a re-run on a proven engine and the full ENTER test (placebo, family, walk-forward, convention sign). CONTRARY closes the lead as forward-negative.

Reading the table: the MDEs reported by A-E are the effect a 2.8-sd test could detect on the replica's weekly delta series; a forward record resolves the P06/P07/P05 claims only after 70-90 weeks, A4/C3 after 35-45, B3 after about 19. So the 8/13/26-week looks can show SUPPORT_CANDIDATE only if the live effect is larger than the replica claim, or CONTRARY if it is negative; INSUFFICIENT/OPEN is the expected reading for most of the first half-year and means "not yet", not "no". F08 cannot reach n_min = 20 before roughly week 20 at the blind-week rate. The prior weekly sd implied by the MDE (MDE x sqrt(26) / 2.8) is about 16.6k (P06), 7.4k (A4), 4.9k (B3), 6.2k (C3); the scorer uses the shadow's own sd once W >= 2.
Direction needed for each lead to read as support: **sign positive** (lead better than the actual live exit) for P06/P07/P05, A4, B3, C3, F08, on a cumulative delta of at least floor x W and the counts above. Sign negative cumulative with z <= -1.5 closes it.
Because the live cut-input reproduction is poor (14%), the first th_eval weeks also test the replica itself: `live_ref_agree` (the live cell re-evaluated on the logged rows vs the logged cuts) must be near 100% before any shadow delta is read; if it is not, the th_eval rows or the scorer's cut semantics are wrong and the week is marked and not read (not deleted).
