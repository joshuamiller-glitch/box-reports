## 2. Exact definitions (the docstrings of shadow2_books.py, i.e. the code that was verified in section 3)
Common: signals only at 1-minute bar closes, signal_sec = bar open + 60 s; ET clock; label day = ET date of (wall clock + 6 h); prices in the contract frame D7 trades (all rules use differences inside one contract; roll handling as FADE_SHADOW_SPEC.md section 7 for the Ctx state, the extra state below holds only differences, ranges and counts). Live fill model (user, 2026-11-04): signal at the bar close, first fill about 1 s later, native stop 2.45 pt worse, market exit 0.80, fee 0.242 pt per lot. ATR is the Wilder ATR14 of the 5-minute buckets as-of the signal bar: Q13 and DIS-K use the FADE_LV definition (prior bucket close enters the true range across gaps; fade_books.Ctx), DIS-B / DIS-C / DIS-E use the definition of the research minute table ds3_fade/minute.parquet (a bucket more than 30 minutes after the previous one has TR = own high-low; shadow2_books.CtxG). The two differ only around daily breaks and weekends.

### 2.1 Q13GAP_T1 - Q13 mid-tercile gap-fill (B1d5 T1)
Q13 B1d5, tercile 1 (middle third of |gap|/ATRd), both directions. Day table: label days whose RTH bars [09:30,16:00) number >= 300, first at 09:30 and last at 15:59 ('qualifying' days, known only at day end; the live logger skips the known CME early-close days, flag day_complete is finalised at the next rollover). gap = open(09:30 bar) - close(15:59 bar) of the previous qualifying day; ATRd = Wilder ATR14 of the qualifying days' RTH ranges (seed = mean of the first 14, as of the previous day); g = |gap|/ATRd; tercile of g within the previous min(250) qualifying days (>= 60 finite values). Event: |gap| >= 2 pt, tercile 1 (q1 < g <= q2), 5th RTH bar (09:34) closed, gap still open by more than 4 pt (gd*(c - prevclose) > 4), ATR(5m) defined > 0. Side = against the gap; stop = extreme since 09:30 (high for an up-gap, low for a down-gap) -+ 0.25 ATR(5m); R = |stop - close| >= 5; target 2R (>= 4); time stop 30 min.

### 2.2 DISK_BO_CH - DIS-K BO COMP_HI NYAM
DIS-K 'BO' event, state COMP_HI, NY-AM. Five-minute buckets of the RTH session (bucket k = [09:30+5k, 09:35+5k)); OR = buckets 0..5 (09:30-10:00) high/low. First bucket k in 6..65 whose close is beyond the OR (close > OR high: +1; close < OR low: -1) is the day's single BO event (one per day); it is a NY-AM event if k < 30 (bucket ends by 12:00). COMP_HI = ratio > 66.7th percentile of the previous <= 120 valid days' ratios (>= 40 finite), ratio = mean RTH range of the previous 5 valid days / mean of the previous 20 (needs 20 prior valid days). A day is valid if all 78 buckets have bars and the RTH session has >= 20000 one-second stamps with prints (nsec = seconds with at least one print in the minute; known at day end). Trade: WITH the break, 60 pt stop / 60 pt target, 3600 s, 2 lots.

### 2.3 DISB_PREL - DIS-B P_REL_cont pre-market
DIS-B P_REL_cont_m1.0 (pre-market). Release move = close of the 08:34 bar - close of the 08:29 bar (both bars must exist; ET clock); a830 = ATR(5m) as-of the 08:29 bar close; Event if |move| >= 1.0 x a830 (>0); side = sign(move) (continuation). Signal at the 08:34 bar close (sec = 08:35). ext = low of the 08:34 bar (long) / high (short); atr = ATR(5m) as-of the 08:34 bar; stop = c - (ext - 1.0 atr) (long) or ext + 1.0 atr - c (short); keep only 0.3 atr <= stop <= 3.0 atr; target 1R; time stop = min(1800 s, 09:26 - signal) = 1800 s; risk lots min(2400/(stop*20), 6). Research filters (known only at 09:30) : >= 300 bars in 04:00-09:30, the 09:30 bar exists, >= 10 earlier valid days, a prior-day 15:59 bar exists.

### 2.4 DISE_PDF - DIS-E prevday-FAILURE NYAM
DIS-E N1 prevday|FAILURE NY-AM. NY-AM = ET minute of the bar open in [09:30,12:00). PDH/PDL = high/low of ALL bars of the previous label day (18:00-17:59 ET; NOT the RTH range). Per bar (A = ATR(5m) as-of, cp = previous bar close, HM10/LM10 = max high / min low of the last 10 bars incl. this one, RF5 = sum of RF (refill-failure events completed in the minute, from the L2 book: a trade-driven touch clear whose book did not heal within 60 s) over the last <= 5 bars of THIS NY-AM session): res = level L (PDH or PDL) with h >= L-0.05A, h < L+A, cp < L, h >= HM10 ; sup = level with l <= L+0.05A, l > L-A, cp > L, l <= LM10 ; fire if (res or sup) and RF5 >= 1 and A finite; first per NY-AM session; side = +1 if res else -1 (follow the break). stop = max(0.75 A, 2.0), target 2 x stop, 3600 s, risk lots min(2400/(stop*20), 6).

### 2.5 DISC_SUN30 - DIS-C Sunday 18:30 with-trend
DIS-C Sunday 18:30 with-trend (NQ). t1 = first bar of the Sunday (ET weekday 6) with ET minute in [18:00,18:05); P1 = its open; needs the preceding Friday to have had a bar before 17:00 ET. Signal at the close of the bar t1+29 min (all 30 minute bars present, i.e. the bar count since t1 is 30) = the '18:30' entry; mv = close - P1 (must be != 0); side = sign(mv) (with the first move); stop = max(1.0 x ATR(5m), 2.0), keep only stop < 6 ATR; target 2 x stop; time stop = min(3600 s, t1 + 7200 - signal); risk lots min(2400/(stop*20), 6). One signal per weekend.

## 5. Log schema and verdict rule
**Register directory** `forward_register/shadow2/`, one pair of append-only CSV files per line, ZL-2 `shadow_register` format (the same helpers as the fade register, `zones3/discover/shadow_register.py`; join on `sig_id`):
`<LINE>_signals.csv` (one row for EVERY signal the streaming book emits, whether or not the position is later skipped for overlap): `sig_id` (`<LINE>_<signal_sec>`), `ts_signal_utc` (ISO), `book`, `session` (nyam / pre / asia), `side` (+1 long), `signal_price` (last print at or before signal_sec, contract frame), `stop_pts`, `tgt_pts`, `tstop_s`, `lots` (research lots: 1 / 2 / risk lots, floats), `definition_version` (`shadow2_v1`), `regime_flags` (key=value; pairs: level, close, ext5, atr, plus gap/g/q1/q2/atrd for Q13, or_hi/or_lo/bucket_k/ratio for DIS-K, relmove/a830 for DIS-B, pd_hi/pd_lo/rf5 for DIS-E, first_move for DIS-C, and book_ld), `logged_by`.
`<LINE>_fills.csv` (written when the shadow position closes, or by the runner replay when D7 writes none): `sig_id`, `ts_exit_utc`, `exit_price`, `exit_type` (stop/target/time/flat), `net_pts` (per lot, after the live cost basis), `usd` (= net_pts x 20 x lots), `note`.

One position per line at a time: a signal while the line is open is logged and skipped by the replay (the harness no-overlap rule). Flat 16:55 ET for the NY lines; the Sunday line is capped by tstop_s at 20:00 ET; DIS-B by 09:26 ET (never binding at 1800 s).
**Verdict rule** (runner `shadow2_weekly.py`, replay with the live fill model on the D7 1-s tape, one position per line): per line the sequential probability-ratio test of BASE-4 (SPRT, alpha = beta = 0.10, bounds +-2.20) on per-trade net points per lot, H0 mean 0, H1 mean = HALF the 2026 research mean with the 2026 research sd (Q13 6.9 / 82.6, DIS-K 5.9 / 57.4, DIS-B 2.4 / 39.9, DIS-E 3.2 / 54.2, DIS-C 2.5 / 40.9 pt). Weeks 1-25: `EARLY (keep logging)` unless the SPRT crosses (CONSISTENT at +2.20, CONTRADICTED at -2.20). From week 26 with >= 30 trades: CONTRADICTED also if the mean is more than 2 standard errors below H1; otherwise INCONCLUSIVE (keep logging). CONSISTENT never promotes anything: every line stays a WATCH line, a CONSISTENT reading is a reason to propose a prereg, not a result. NOW-ONLY = REGIME: a CONSISTENT reading in 2026-27 does not clear the 2023-25 sign (Q13 and DIS-E are flat/negative in MID). Mechanics check (weeks 1-4): the streaming signals logged by D7 must equal a re-run of `shadow2_books.py` on the same bars (set difference empty); the replay reconciliation against logged fills as in fade_forward_weekly.

## 7. Known limits and decisions needed (none blocks logging)
1. DIS-E needs RF, the count of refill-failure events completed in the minute, from the L2 book (a trade-driven touch clear whose book did not heal to <= the pre-clear spread and >= 50% of the pre-clear touch within 60 s; l2vast/TABLES.md). D7 must build it from its own book events; without L2 the line cannot run (the other four need only trade prints). The research RF comes from the 1-s L2 tables; rank-tolerance is not enough here (RF >= 1 is a count gate), so the first weeks need the comparison of D7 RF with the table RF on a day of overlap.
2. DIS-B and DIS-C trade outside RTH: pre-market 08:35 ET and Sunday 18:30 ET. The live system may not trade there; Shadow only logs what WOULD have been taken.
3. Day-validity flags (Q13, DIS-K, DIS-B) are end-of-day knowledge; the logger emits the signal and the runner drops signals on known holiday / early-close sessions (calendar list to be kept with the register). The research Q13 table silently drops early-close days; live, such a morning would produce a signal that must be dropped by the calendar.
4. Lots: DIS-B / DIS-E / DIS-C use the research risk lots min(2400/(stop*20), 6) (mean 4.7-5.3 contracts), DIS-K 2, Q13 1. These are research sizes, not what the live box would trade; the per-lot net_pts column makes any scaling explicit, and the $ columns are at research lots.
5. Frame: Ctx state is rebased at a roll as in FADE_SHADOW_SPEC.md section 7; the extra Q13 state (previous close, daily ATR, gap list) stores differences and is rebased by the same delta for the previous close only.
6. DIS-K COMP_HI and Q13 tercile gates use rolling windows of 120 / 250 days: a cold start before 60-74 qualifying days gives no signals; the 420-day replay warm-up is part of the spec.
7. No PASS claim, no purchases. All five lines are WATCH / REGIME / best-of-F; the forward register does not change any label.
