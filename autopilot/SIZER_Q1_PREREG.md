# SIZER_Q1_PREREG - first question: does the x1.5 side of the live ER ladder earn its keep? (ZL-2, 2026-11-02, written before any Q1 statistic)
Frame: V13T_LIVE (D7 v1.3 exact, take lines off, xcost 0.80, stopcost 2.45, cancel-on-exit, per-book locks only = 'lock none'), single rounding (engine as coded) as the headline, sequential rounding beside it (lots_round='sequential'). Harness: l2vast/ds3/code/zones3/sizer/sizer_harness.py (SIZER_HARNESS.md). Multipliers by d row from the live ladder (ER(20) 5-min last closed bar, x1.5 if ER >= 0.30 else x0.5; NaN -> 1.0); zone HALF (x0.5 on the 7 line set) and integer half-up min-1 lots stay in the engine.
Cells: L = live ladder; A = size-DOWN only (x0.5 where ER < 0.30, x1 where ER >= 0.30); B = x1.5 UP only (x1 where ER < 0.30, x1.5 where ER >= 0.30); C = sizer off (x1).
Windows: L6M, HOLD, MID, PRE, X', F9 (2026-02-07..2026-10-31). Metrics per cell and window: mean $/wk, maxDD (weekly cumulative), worst-20 weeks, ulcer, CVaR5, Calmar; cuts vs L; paired MDE (2.8 SE).
Tests: (1) PA9 (9 lock-reset phases) cut vs L against the final V13T PA9 band tl_band_final.csv (flip 2%: L6M 18.8, HOLD 18.2, MID 21.2, PRE 7.8, X' 14.5), no-harm; (2) equal-risk mean (engine lscale scans, linear fits; cell's fitted mean at L's fitted maxDD per window) and equal-mean-lots (lscale so mean effective lots equal L's 3.20, then the same metrics); (3) weeks 2026 W29-W39 (ISO YYYYWW 202629..202639) dropped from base and cell in every 2026 window, plus the carrying weeks of PRE (2024 W34-51) and X' (2023 W21-41); leave-one-episode-out min cut; (4) per book x session x vol-tercile split vs L (n taken, $/day, MDE) for F9 and ALL; (5) placebos >= 100 draws, phase-averaged over 3 phases (-4, 0, +4): for A and B random multipliers with the same distribution (permuted within book x session), shuffled-day multipliers, AR(1)-matched paths (C is constant, no placebo); rank = share of draws below the real cell on cut and on mean $; (6) single vs sequential rounding for all four cells.
Decision rule for the plain sentence 'should the x1.5 side stay live tonight' (frame always stated): the x1.5 up-size side is SUPPORTED if (a) L beats A on equal-risk mean in at least 4 of the 5 windows L6M/HOLD/MID/PRE/X' AND at equal mean lots L has a higher mean in L6M, HOLD, PRE, AND (b) L's extra mean over A in L6M remains positive with W29-W39 dropped, AND (c) B's mean-dollar placebo rank is >= 0.95 in L6M, HOLD and PRE (PA3, all three kinds); it is NOT SUPPORTED if (b) fails or two or more of the five windows have L worse than A at equal risk; otherwise INCONCLUSIVE (state which test is mixed). The drawdown cost of the x1.5 side (L vs A maxDD in L6M, HOLD, PRE, F9) is reported beside the verdict, never netted into it. Nothing is a lead on one week or one month. No PASS claims; Shadow/analysis only; the user decides.
