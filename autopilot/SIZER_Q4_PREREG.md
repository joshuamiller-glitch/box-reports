# SIZER_Q4_PREREG - ER at the add, ER freshness / boundary fragility, smooth ladder (ZL-2, 2026-11-02, written before any Q4 statistic)
Frame: T-SIZER ts base (run_v13t_ts: V13T_LIVE costs, sequential rounding, lock R scaled only when mult > 1, A06 not modelled, zone half before ER); reference L = live ladder; harness sizer_harness.py. ER(20) on 5-minute closes: closes at the epoch 300-s boundaries, bar k covers [300k, 300(k+1)), stamped at its end; at second s the last closed bar closes at B0 = floor(s/300)*300 (a bar closing exactly at s is included); close prices = the 1-s path close SC of the last second before each boundary (back-adjusted frame); ER = |c0 - c20| / sum_{i<20} |c_i - c_{i+1}|. First step: validate this recomputation against S4b's er_5m_20 on all rows (tolerance 1e-3); if it matches on >= 99% of rows it is used for the add-time ER; otherwise the add-time ER uses S4b's builder.
(1) ER AT THE ADD. The add fills at path index kf (engine R['kf']; 62% of signals have a fill). ER_add = ER with B0 from SS[kf]. Cells on the live ladder L (70% leg sized by ER at the signal unchanged), via BASE-4's add_mult / add_only_if hooks (EXIT_API.md; min-1-lot rule for the add applies): ADDER = add only if ER_add >= .30, add x0.5 if ER_add < ER_sig - 0.1, otherwise x1 (decision order: cancel first, then halve); ADDER_c = cancel only (ER_add < .30); ADDER_h = halve only (ER dropped by > 0.1). Rows without an add fill are unaffected. Placebo: the (add_mult, add_only_if) pair permuted within book x session over the rows with an add fill, 100 draws, ranks on cut and mean in L6M and PRE; exposure_table; same standard as Q2 (cuts and paired MDE vs L in L6M, HOLD, MID, PRE, X', F9, W29-W39 dropped, exact lot scan lscale 0.8-1.6 step .05 for the equal-risk mean, flag rule).
(2) ER FRESHNESS. 100-row audit (seeded random taken rows): S4b's er_5m_20 vs my closed-bar-only recomputation vs ER with the forming bar (c0 = SC at the signal second, then the closes at B0, B0-300, ...) vs ER one closed bar earlier (B0-300 as c0); counts of exact matches, and the maximum absolute difference. Fragility: over all 88,490 signals and the taken signals, the share with |ER - .30| < 0.02 (the share whose side flips under a +-0.02 shift) and the expected flip probability under uniform +-0.02 noise; share whose side differs between closed-bar and forming-bar ER and between closed-bar and one-bar-earlier ER.
(3) BOUNDARY AND SMOOTH. Boundary = taken signals with ER in [0.27, 0.33]. Report the net $ per trade (lots x1 table) and counts of the below (.27-.30) and above (.30-.33) boundary groups by window; the P&L and maxDD effect of moving the whole boundary band to x0.5 (BND_LO) or to x1.5 (BND_HI) versus the ladder. SMOOTH = linear multiplier 0.5 + (ER - 0.25)/0.10 clipped to [0.5, 1.5] (x1.0 at .30), NaN -> 1.0, sequential rounding; scored with the Q2 standard including exposure_table, placebo (multipliers permuted within book x session, 100 draws) and the exact lot scan. Multiplicity: 6 new cells (ADDER, ADDER_c, ADDER_h, SMOOTH, BND_LO, BND_HI). A cell is flagged only under the Q2 FLAG rule (maxDD cut in L6M and PRE with W29-W39 dropped and mean inside the MDE) and is a lead only if the placebo mean rank >= 0.95 in L6M and PRE and the equal-risk gain is positive in L6M-dropped and PRE; the user decides; no PASS.
