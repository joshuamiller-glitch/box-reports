# SIZER_Q5_PREREG - stale-bar / gap-window effect on the ER ladder (ZL-2, 2026-11-02, written before any Q5 statistic)
Frame: T-SIZER ts base (run_v13t_ts: V13T_LIVE costs, sequential rounding, lock R scaled only when mult > 1, A06 not modelled, zone half before ER); reference L = live ladder; ER(20) on 5-minute closes from S4b's bar builder (bars built from the 1-s path by minute key, so minutes with no print create no bar and a 20-bar window can span a gap). Bar end = (key+1)*300 s; last closed bar at the signal second s = last bar with end <= s.
(1) Diagnostics on taken signals (and all signals), by book and by session: age = s - end of the last closed bar; counts with age > 5, 15, 60 minutes; GAP-WINDOW = the 21 closes of the ER window contain a step between consecutive bar ends > 30 minutes (1800 s); counts, net $ per trade (lots x1 table) and total, mean ER, multiplier mix (x0.5 / x1.5, composite with the zone half not split) of these signals versus the others.
(2) Cells (all other signals on the live ladder): GAP_NAN = ER treated as NaN (multiplier 1.0) when the window spans a gap > 30 min; GAP_HALF = multiplier 0.5 on gap-window signals; SESS_ER = ER computed only from the 5-minute bars of the CURRENT session (session start from the ET clock: asia 18:00-02:00, london 02:00-08:30, pre 08:30-09:30, nyam 09:30-12:00, nypm 12:00-15:00, close 15:00-16:00; a bar belongs if its start >= the session start), lookback = min(20, bars - 1) and at least 10 bars, else NaN (multiplier 1.0); ladder x0.5 below .30, x1.5 at or above.
(3) Standard of Q2-Q4: cuts and paired MDE vs L in L6M, HOLD, MID, PRE, X', F9, W29-W39 dropped, exact lot scan (lscale 0.8-1.6 step .05) for equal-risk, placebo 100 draws (multipliers permuted within book x session over taken rows) with ranks on cut and mean in L6M and PRE, exposure_table, flag rule (maxDD cut in L6M and PRE with W29-W39 dropped and mean inside the MDE). Multiplicity: 3 cells. No PASS; the user decides.
