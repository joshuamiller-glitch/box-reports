# SUNDAY_GIVEBACK_RULES (W-SUNDAY-GB, task 2)

**Answer first.** For each EDGE book, the live row against the five protection rules (be50, be70, pl50k50, pl70k50, nt70k25): number of trades that gave back MORE than 50% of their peak open profit, and dollars given back at live lots (before fees). Eligible trades = peak (best favourable excursion on the REPLAYED 1-s tape, entry to exit) >= 30% of that trade's target (tgt_pts of its event row); give-back = (peak - final pts) / peak; dollars = (peak - final pts) x $20 x lots. Windows: all 42 months, L6M (2026-05..10), L3M (2026-08..10), years 2023-2026 (2026 = to 30 Oct). Book tables use the tested book's trades in the mix of its own set (live row = live set, same book); the "Session mix" table under each uses all books of the set. The last column gives the NY-FIB-type count: trades with peak >= 90% of target that closed at a loss (all42 / L6M / L3M). Read: the break-even rules (be50/be70) catch the near-target losers (NY MF1 18 -> 4/5 over 42 months, L3M 1 -> 0) but raise the count and dollars of >50% give-backs because they convert more trades into small-win exits after a big peak; the peak-lock and near-target rules (pl*, nt70k25) keep the count at or below live and remove the 90% losers (NY MF1 18 -> 0). NP-CONF has no live reference run (TL3_DX_NP_CONF_fix_M is not in runs_rf), so its live row is empty. csv: /dev/shm/sun/gb50.csv (sess,tag,code,window,n_trades_gb50,gb_dollars; tag ending _MIX = session mix of that tested-book set; for live in the 4 session mixes tag is the book). Code: code/th_sunday/sun_gb2.py, sun_gb2_tab.py. Live-row session-mix sets are the live sets, so the live mix row repeats across books of one session.
