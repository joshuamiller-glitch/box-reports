## C. Caveats (read before using any number)
- **Exact vs first-order:** first-order (pnl per planned lot x new lots) is a SCREEN ONLY; it cannot see exits, re-entries, locks, slice fills and lot-scaled arms. Measured: ladder L cut -76 exact vs -43 first-order; B -143 vs -73; P7 5/4/4 +2.5% exact vs +29.6% first-order. Rows marked [FIRST-ORDER SCREEN ONLY] carry no verdict.
- **Engine ROLLFIX1:** the base engine flattens at each of the 14 contract rolls (previously 33 trades alive across a roll produced fake Target/Stop outcomes). Rows tagged [EXACT ROLL-FIXED] ran on it; rows tagged ROLL-EXCL excluded the 33 roll-alive trades from scoring (a stopgap that misses follow-on effects). The roll patch does not repair bars/indicators that contain the jump for a few hours (a back-adjust would).
- **Stop fill model bias:** the engine's stop fills are optimistic, so ABSOLUTE means are optimistic and the stop-exit modes (REDO-R9) are unresolved; drawdown ranking between sizing cells is the usable output, absolute $/week is not an expectation. The stop-fill model and stop-exit-mode hooks are OUT of this rebuild.
- **Entries are not live-matched (entry match < .85):** PRE-PROOF; everything is post-hoc and Shadow only; no PASS claims.
- **Held-out live week:** first-order rows only (no losing day in the week for any cell); exact live-week rows are not available (the blind week is not in the S1b pipeline); the user finding stands that live cuts of the 11-08/09 night are bug exits and are excluded.
