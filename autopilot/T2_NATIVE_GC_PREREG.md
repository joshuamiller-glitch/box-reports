## Books (36 signal sets; signals at completed 1-min bars, bar names = START time ET, entry at the next bar open)
G1 London AM fix (LBMA auction 10:30 London = 05:30 ET; the 1 hour DST mismatch weeks are NOT adjusted, stated): pre = close(05:29 bar) - close(04:59 bar) (30-minute drift into the fix, signal at 05:30) or post = close(05:34) - close(05:29) (5-minute reaction, signal at 05:35); trade when |move| >= k x ATR14(5m) at the signal, k in {0.5, 1.0, 1.5}; continuation or fade: 2 x 3 x 2 = 12 sets.
G2 London PM fix (3 pm London = 10:00 ET): pre = close(09:59) - close(09:29), post = close(10:04) - close(09:59); same k and directions: 12 sets.
G3 COMEX 08:20 opening range: OR from 08:20 of 5 / 15 / 30 minutes; entries within the next 90 minutes: first close beyond the range (trend, once per side) or failed break (wick >= 0.1 ATR beyond, close back inside within 10 bars; fade): 3 x 2 = 6 sets.
G4 gold-vs-dollar-yield divergence days: overnight log returns from 18:00 to 08:19 of GC, 6E (euro, the dollar proxy, inverse) and ZN (yield proxy, price) from the owned 1-min feeds (instr2_z3/6E_1m.npz, ZN_1m.npz); trailing 60-label-day OLS of the GC return on (6E, ZN) returns (strictly earlier days), residual z = (actual - predicted)/residual sd; divergence day = |z| >= k, k in {1.0, 1.5, 2.0}; signal at the 08:19 close, entry 08:20 open: trade WITH the residual (continuation) or AGAINST it (convergence to the dollar/yield-implied move): 3 x 2 = 6 sets.
Brackets (63 per set): stop ATR14(5m) x {2, 3, 4, 6} or fixed {$600, $1,200, $2,400}; target {1R, 1.5R, 2R}; time stop {30, 60, 120} min; flat 16:55. **n_tested = 36 x 63 = 2,268** (registered T2_NATIVE_GC before running).
