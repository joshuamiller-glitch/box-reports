## 3. Cuts and rule definitions (G1, single-measure gates)
* Cuts per book from the SEL window (X' + PRE trades only): q10 q33 q50 q67 q90 of the sided measure. Every other window (including the SEL window itself) is bucketed with these fixed cuts.
* Continuous measures (more than 6 distinct values): 8 SKIP rules: skipBot3 (x<q33), skipTop3 (x>q67), keepTop3 (skip x<q67), keepBot3 (skip x>q33), skipBot10 (x<q10), skipTop10 (x>q90), keepTopHalf (skip x<q50), keepBotHalf (skip x>q50). Discrete measures (<=6 values): skipEq_v for each value v. Missing measure => signal is KEPT (the gate acts only where the measure exists); coverage shown.
* Cells = unit x measure x rule. Exact count appended in ADDENDUM 0 (about 430,000).
* Statistics per cell and window: n, skipped n, keep fraction, gain per unfiltered signal (economic), kept mean, skipped mean, lift = kept mean - unfiltered mean (planned lot and per contract), P(target) kept/skipped, MAE kept/skipped, trades/week, on E_MKT and E_73 x M/N/LC.
* SELECTION (PRE+X' columns only): eligible if keep fraction in SEL in [0.30, 0.97]; skipped n in SEL >= 100; lift (E_73 N, planned lot) > 0 in PRE AND in X' separately; lift (E_MKT N) > 0 in PRE and X' separately; lift (E_73 LIVE-CAL) > 0 pooled SEL; week-clustered lift t (delta method) >= 2.0. Rank by that t. Selected per unit: top 5 plus top 2 per family label (CHART, STAT, CROSS, FLOW, DAY). Selection file sha256 logged in DECISIONS.md BEFORE the single recent look (L3M view-only, 2026, L6M, HOLD; plus PRE, X' already known).
* Mechanism and predicted effect: trend-continuation books (KB ST SM HM PE WT DN PB MA) are momentum bets; they win when returns are positively autocorrelated, trends are efficient and aligned across timeframes, volatility expands with the move and flow is persistent. Predicted: the high-trend tercile of ADX/ER/MA-separation/HTF-agreement/TSMOM/flow persistence has a higher P(target) and +1..+4 pts/contract lift vs the book mean; the OFF side (Chop>61.8, ER<0.3, ADX<20, squeeze on, variance ratio<1, high-entropy) is worse; the over-extended top decile of distance-from-MA is worse (exhaustion); MV (mean-reversion) is predicted to prefer the opposite (chop) side. Falsifiers: lift <= 0 in PRE or X' (EINT1 already saw strong-last-year/zero-or-reversed-before); a gate that raises quality but removes more P&L than it adds on the stack (opportunity cost) is reported as such.
