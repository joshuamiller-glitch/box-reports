## What is coded (rule dict keys; None/{} = HOLD, byte-identical)
* B, L1 loss: `l1loss` = literal port of DS7.cs ManageLoss (L3231-3262): ThMinPoints eligibility on the adverse extreme, votes momentum / stall+pullback / efficiency on the 100-ms AddSample series with MaxWindowSecs retention, evaluated once per `freq` s, >= min(req,3) votes -> RequestBookExit "LossExit" (replica request_exit: cancel 0.5 s, market, fill at decision + LAT_FILL). `l2loss: False` = path TH alone (ThL2 loss branch and policy loss lines off); `True` = AddToPath; ReplacePath (path off, L2 on) is HOLD. Combines with the program-A keys (arm, mult, win, pers, ext) for B3.
* D, L1 profit: `l1profit` (dict or list, executed in list order): ph = literal port of ManageProfit (L3278-3330; PhMin eligibility on the favourable extreme, 4 votes, PhTrail behind the extreme, touch -> market "ProfitExit"); chand, atr (Wilder ATR on the replica 1m / 5m bars, completed bars only, ratcheting level, market exit on touch); decay (market exit if pct < x at T min, or the target shrinks to (1-f) of its distance, market if already beyond); scaleout (half lots at market at pct, runner none/trail4/8/15).
* C, L2 profit: `l2profit` thick (the ManageThL2 profit branch with the cell numbers: thick multiple, seconds, trail pts, min pts 30, tighten = ThL2Trail ratchet or take = market "ThL2Take"), be (bracket stop and native order moved to entry + buf), scaleout (runner bracket / 8-pt trail / market when ahead_rel >= 1.5 for 2 s), bookstate (trail live only while thick / thin / pulled / iceberg holds); `policy_profit` / `policy_profit_add` replace / prepend policy PROFIT lines (gated take lines through the replica's own manage_policy; `policy_profit_mode` live = only while the loaded policy version has profit lines, always). `ice_against` feature added to the policy feature set (iceberg refill on the side against us in the last 10 s).
* `cell_to_rule(grids.py cell)` maps B1, C1-C5, D1-D5 cells (C2 `lvl` = index into {0,1,1.5,2} / {0,5,10,30} / {0,1,2,3}, Z1's assumption; C1 `trail` in POINTS as ThL2ProfitTrailPts; the prereg wording "ticks" is flagged). B2 (18 THL_BOOKS cells) and D6/E combos are lists/dicts of the same keys, not mapped here.
* G, `record=True`: one row per open book per wall second (first print of the second), 32 features + ts, book, entry id, px so labels (y = PnL hold h s more - exit now) come from the later rows of the same trade: L1 path (upnl, mfe, mae, pct of target/stop, secs since MAE/MFE, momentum 15/30/60, efficiency 30/60/120, recovery, pullback, elapsed, ATR5, PhMin state) and L2 book (pulled support and multiple at 2/5/15 s, tot_rel, ahead_rel, imbalance x side, iceberg against, sweep-against age, classified-print delta 10 s and prior 10 s, delta turn, median level), lots. The blind week gives 210,022 rows on HOLD.

## Stated conventions / open points
* Latency is the replica's (LAT_FILL 1.0 s, book at the callback); the "0.175 s exchange fill" is the replica-2 convention, not in this engine: pass `prm={'LAT_BOOK':..}` if the lead wants it (HOLD headline would move).
* Stop-type extras (chandelier, ATR, runner trails, book trail, PhTrail) exit at market on the print touching the level (as the live PhTrail), not as resting stops; BE moves the bracket stop (a later place_bracket, e.g. after a StackCut, would reset it).
* Scale-out needs pos >= 2 lots (1-lot A7 books: rule inert). Chandelier / ATR levels ratchet and arm when in profit / at pct.
* The 10-s grid features of the old engine do not exist here: policy lines run on the replica's 1-s throttle (THR_MODE gap).
* Timing: one replay about 25-50 s warm.
