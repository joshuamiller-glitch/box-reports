# TH_RULE_V3 - cut-trigger series, decision/fill timing and calibration protocol for engine v3 (T-TH-REDO Gate 0), ZL-2, 2026-11-06
Frozen before the v3 series build and before any calibration statistic. Supersedes the trigger part of TH_RULE_V2.md (sha256 23c8faefc2575d9531235044485e4b5082cb76af5b6cd45c7cb0b7925b711c6d); the v2 values remain the interim PRE-PROOF default until addendum 1 (selected parameters) is logged. Everything fitted on ONE live week is a hypothesis for the user's code/log check.
## 1. Data and cadence (what 'raw stream, 1-s clock, state up to that second' means here)
* Decision cadence: every integer second T, ts = bin END convention. The state at decision T uses only mbp-10 records with ts_event < T: the 1-s L2 table row with ts = T is exactly the raw records of [T-1, T) aggregated (cancel sizes by side and level group; book sizes at the last record before T). DS3_TH_FIDELITY.md (R10) rebuilt these quantities from the raw records and found them identical to the table on 99.9996% / 99.9999% of 1.96M decision rows; the blind-week table was built from the raw pull (NQ_blind) with the same builder. No 10-s grid, no binning beyond the 1-s decision bin; the event-clock (sub-second) variant is not built (DS3_TH_CLOCK.md).
* Adverse: upnl(T) = side x (print price of second T - E) with E the engine entry price (published rows: stored E; shifted rows: the actual entry price), path price = last print of the second, forward-filled.
* Supporting side = bid for longs, ask for shorts. Pulled support p_w(T) = sum over the w one-second bins ending at T of cancel size (all levels) on that side. Median level med(T) = nanmedian of the 10 own-side level sizes of the book as of T. Pulled multiple m_w = p_w / max(med, 1). Features are NaN (condition false) when the book age exceeds 3600 s.
## 2. Trigger family (all built, none assumed)
Condition C(T; arm, MULT, w, X) = [upnl(T) <= -arm] and [m_w(T) >= MULT] and [X], X in {off; at a new adverse extreme this second (cm(T) > cm(T-1)); new adverse extreme within the last 5 s (stored ne5)}. Persistence N: the decision fires at the first second T_N such that C is true on N consecutive seconds T_N-N+1 .. T_N (N = 1 is the published rule on a 1-s clock). Grid built for every trade: arm {15, 20, 25, 30, 35, 37.5, 40, 45, 50}, MULT {5, 10, 20, 30, 60, 100, 200}, w {2, 3, 5, 10, 15}, N {1..10}, X {off, extreme-now, extreme-within-5-s}: 9 x 7 x 5 x 10 x 3 = 9,450 combinations; first decision second per trade and combination (0 = none). The loss-line policy cut of the live config (0.5 x stop distance, 30x, new extreme within 5 s, from Mon 08:20 ET) is the arm 30 / 37.5 members with X = extreme-within-5-s and w = 5.
## 3. Latency and cut price (engine unit: path prints)
* Decision at T (state up to T) -> market order -> fill in second T+1: the exit second index kx = path index of the decision second + 1 (as the stored engine index), and the cut price is the **print of the fill second** (path price at kx) minus the measured live slip versus that print: ThL2Cut 0.55 pt (n = 53), PolicyCut 0.10 pt (n = 39), pooled 0.36 pt (DEFECTS_ZL2 data, 92 live cuts of the blind week, side x (print of the fill second - live fill)). For reference the same fills versus the MID at the fill second cost 1.05 pt (ThL2Cut), 1.40 pt (PolicyCut), 1.18 pt pooled (spread at the cut 1.21 pt). Cut delta vs the library exit = side x (PX[kx] - E) - slip(type) - (pts_lib - fill_cost_pts). The published engine used the print of the DECISION second minus 0.995: v3 uses the next second's print and the measured slip. Cut type: policy cut when the policy rule fires (arm = 0.5 x stop distance, X = extreme-within-5-s), otherwise ThL2.
## 4. Calibration protocol (acceptance check, hypothesis only)
Target: the 92 live cuts of the blind week (ThL2Cut 53 + PolicyCut 39; live csv data/live/StratTrades_DS7_<acct>.csv sha256 d918fee4..., read after the seal as already logged) against the rule's cut list on the same live trades (live entry price, live entry time, live exit time as the end of the observation window). Metrics: (a) cut/no-cut agreement by trade over the 186 live trades with a tape series and over the 100 that reached -30 (target >= 85%); (b) cut-dollar agreement: sum over live trades of the rule's cut dollars (live lots, rule fill per section 3) versus the live cut dollars of the 92 cuts (target within 10%), with and without the false cuts; (c) cut-time agreement on trades both cut (median and 90% quantile of |rule fill second - live exit second|). Selection on the FIT half (trades exited Sun 11-01 to Tue 11-03 ET), held-out Wed 11-04 and Thu 11-05; the selection criterion is fit trade agreement, ties broken by fit cut-dollar error, then by fewer departures from the published rule (arm 30, MULT 30, w 5, N 1, X off). The number of combinations scored (9,450) is reported. Selected parameters, fit and held-out results are logged as TH_RULE_V3 addendum 1 BEFORE BASE-4 freezes engine v3; they are NOT a finding about the live code.
## 5. Deliverables
(1) 1-s series for every trade of the v3 tree (rows with upnl <= -15; columns ti, ts, upnl, cm, med, p2, p3, p5, p10, p15, ne1, ne5 and the pulled multiples derivable) and the fire matrix for the grid above (arm instant per arm included); (2) the calibration page; (3) a note on the 8 engine-cut / live-held pairs and the 12 live trades that reached -30 and were not cut, under this series (single second vs sustained, pulled multiple >= 100).
