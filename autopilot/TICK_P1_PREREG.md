# T-TICK PART 1 prereg (W-TK1, 2026-11-14 ~03:00Z, before any scoring)
Data: /mnt/l2/l2vast/_tick/tick_feat.parquet (causal, usable end+5 s), NQ 1-min RTH. Sessions through 2026-10-31 (research cap).
Decision times: every 5 min at mso 5..385 (ET 09:35..15:55), slices S1 09:30-10:00 (mso 5-30), S2 10:00-12:00 (35-150), S3 12:00-14:30 (155-300), S4 14:30-16:00 (305-385); plus single readings R15/R30/R60 (first 15/30/60 min) for the rest-of-session day-type targets.
Targets: (a) rest-of-session ER (5-min closes), close location |2(c-L)/(H-L)-1|, mean crossings (sqrt); (b) rest-of-session ln range; (c) NQ forward return 5/15/30/60 min (bp); (d) continuation = sign(past h-min return)*fwd h-min return, h=15/30/60.
Benchmark (price-only): NQ past returns 5/15/30/60, range so far, 1-min ER and ATR over 15/30/60, 14-day mean RTH ln range, time of day, event-calendar flags (nyam/nyam2/nypm any/hi/n), VIX close/change/term prior day (calendar+VIX exist only from 2024-09/10: before that imputed + missing flag; stated caveat for PRE/X').
Model: ridge (lambda 10, standardised, missing->0 plus availability flags), expanding walk-forward refit every 10 trading days (bi-weekly), min train 60 days. Gain = (SSE_bench - SSE_bench+TICK)/SSE_mean (OOS), also IC gain. TICK groups: level, share, cum, ext, div, first, ALL. Windows L1M/L3M/L6M (ET dates from 2026-10-01/08-01/05-01), HOLD 2025-11-01..2026-10-31, PRE 2024-02..2025-06, X' 2023-04..2024-01, full.
Nulls: TICK block shifted by k trading days (time of day kept), k in {11,23,37} + random draws 8..60 (NDRAW 30); per cell q95, and family null = per draw the max over all groups x targets of the slice, q95 of that max. Day-block bootstrap CI (5-day blocks). A cell is called a lead only if gain>0 in both recent (L6M) and older (PRE and X') AND full gain > family q95; else plain 'no'.
No trading in Part 1. Code: outputs/code/th_tick/tk_fc.py.

## Addendum A (2026-11-14 ~03:40Z, after seeing the first walk-forward pass; stated openly)
The first WF pass gave a negative X' gain for every feature family (60-day warm-up leaves X' with a thin, unstable training set), so X' and PRE are additionally scored by blocked 10-day-chunk cross-validation (each chunk predicted from all chunks except itself and its two neighbours; not walk-forward, labelled cv_*), with the same shifted-time nulls. Also reported: abs_* = gain of bench+TICK over the plain mean (the absolute R2), because the price-only benchmark itself has negative R2 on return targets. Single-reading slices R15/R30/R60 use ridge lambda 300 (829 rows). Lead rule: WF gain>0 in L6M and HOLD, cv gain>0 in PRE and X', full WF gain > family q95.
