## Queue model (label: QUEUE MODEL, 1-s aggregates; not order-level)
For a buy limit at L (mirror for sells): the first second s1 whose trade low <= L is the TOUCH; trade low <= L - 1 tick in that second (or any later second while the order rests) = THROUGH (every order at L is filled, ours included); a touch with low == L and no through is TOUCH-ONLY. Queue ahead Q_ahead = phi x Q_start, Q_start = displayed best-bid size at the level when price arrives (end-of-second bsz1 + the volume traded against the bid in that second when the bid sits at L; session median best size if the level is not the best bid), phi in {0 (front of the queue), 0.5 (central), 1 (back)} because the order is placed minutes before price arrives and some displayed orders arrived after it. Depletion D(t) = cumulative volume traded against the bid at L (depl_b_trd in touch seconds) + cancels at the best bid (sz_can_b1 in the seconds the bid sits at L, 100% credited to our queue ahead when phi = 1, proportionally phi otherwise). FILL of the first lot when D >= Q_ahead + 1 or on THROUGH; an N-lot order fills min(N, D - Q_ahead)/N of its size until price leaves (partial fills; entry time = first fill second). Unfilled = no trade (0), as in the pure-wait cell. Exit after a later fill: stop at the ORIGINAL level (hold dies if hit before the fill), target at fill +- target distance, same costs as the cell (limit fill no slip, stop 1.01 / time 0.995 + 0.242 fee, N basis; LIVE-CAL beside). The kernel's own touch-fill must be reproduced as the touch convention (identity check, reported). Table prices are used for the queue (path series is offset by a roll constant: the offset is taken per event at the placement second).

## Predicted
Touch-and-bounce fills are the winners (price rejects the level): the touch convention overstates the LEAD; with phi = 0.5 the queue fill rate on touch-only events is 30-60% and those misses are better trades than the average fill, so the per-planned-lot value falls from +4.6 (filled) / +2.4 (CF2 per planned lot) by roughly a third to a half; at phi = 1 it approaches the +1-tick-through stress (CF2: +2.23 L6M).
