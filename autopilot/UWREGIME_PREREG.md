# UWREGIME_PREREG (W-UR1, T-UWREGIME, 2026-11-13). Research only, no trading in page 1.
Targets (NQ 1-min, roll jumps >1.2%/min removed): per session (asia 18:00-02:00, london 02:00-08:30, pre 08:30-09:30, nyam 09:30-12:00, nypm 12:00-17:00 ET; plus nyam10 10:00-12:00 for the 10:00 reading): ln range, ln realized vol (1-min), efficiency ratio (5-min closes), close extremity |2*CL-1|, mean crossings of session open (5-min), time-of-high and time-of-low (session fraction); pinning (14:30-16:00 ET): log shrink of |P-K| to largest-gamma strike K (NDX/QQQ on NQ, SPX/SPY on ES), benchmark K = nearest round 100/25.
Readings: prior close (UW rows up to 16:00 ET prior day + prior-day EOD level files), 08:30, 09:30, 10:00 ET; each row usable at start+180 s; EOD level files (gex_levels, spot_strike, vol_stats, dp_levels, iv_term) only from the previous trading day. Futures move since prior close maps UW spot to the reading time (no UW intraday info before 09:34 ET).
Benchmark (no UW; VIX not on box): ridge on ln prior-day same-session range, ln ATR14 of same session range, lagged own target, 5d mean; for readings after prior close also ln range so far today and |move since prior close|/ATR. Model = benchmark + one UW input group (<=6 features). Expanding window, min 120 days, refit every 10 trading days (= 2 weeks), ridge lambda fixed. Gain = OOS R2(model) - OOS R2(benchmark) and rank-IC gain; 90% CI by 5-day block bootstrap of daily squared-error differences; shifted-time null: UW inputs taken k trading days earlier, k in 8..60 (30 draws) joint over all groups -> per-group null q95 and family (max over groups) null q95. 'Forecasts yes' = gain>0, CI low>0 and gain > family null q95; 'weak' = gain > per-group null q95 only.
Windows for OOS reporting: full OOS, last 6 months (L6M), last 3 months (L3M), first half/second half. No PASS claims. Page 2 (dials via exact TP engine) only for inputs with 'yes' or 'weak'.
