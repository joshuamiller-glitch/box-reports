# V19_SPEC_F3 - what WB_X_FR_F3 is AS SCORED, for the D7 v1.9 coder (W-B, written 2026-11-12 ~13:20Z). Shadow only. Code: outputs/code/th_works_b/wb_hooks.py (_cell), wb_ctx.py (tag); set json /mnt/l2/l2vast/_th_state/s345/exact_b/sets/WB_X_FR_F3.json.

## 1. The two cut lines (exact parameters as run)
Both are the engine's state-based loss cut (state_base_b.advance), P7 only, all sessions. Fields: arm (points), mult (x MedLevel), win (seconds), pers (seconds), newmae = 0, var = 0 (arm is NOT lot-scaled).
- FRESH line (= "V2"): arm 45 pts, mult 5, win 5 s, pers 10 s.
- RECHASE / FAST line: arm 30 pts, mult 10, win 5 s, pers 2 s.
Meaning of one line, evaluated every second of an open P7 trade (1-s top-10 state): the trade is "armed" when the adverse excursion from the entry fill is >= arm points (side x (mid - entry) <= -arm); the cut condition is true in a second when armed AND own-side pulled depth over the last win seconds (own-side cancelled size net of trades, summed over win seconds, the same pulled quantity as the profit-state features) >= mult x MedLevel (the half-hour median level size at that second, > 0); the exit fires when the condition holds for pers consecutive seconds; decision at the close of second s, market exit at row s+1 at mid(s+1) - side x 0.80 pt (engine state-cut cost). The cut line is chosen ONCE per trade, at the first management second after the fill, and held for the trade (no switching mid-trade). It replaces the live LPB P7 cut (40/5x/w5/p3) which is the rest of the base; every other layer of the live panel base (7/4/4 table, day cap 8,400, etc.) unchanged. "none" is never used: every P7 trade carries one of the two lines.

## 2. The tag (P7_CONTEXT_DEFS.md), evaluated at the SIGNAL second (b.sig_ns, the 1-minute-bar close that produced the signal), fixed at entry
fresh = n_sess <= 2 AND NOT chasing AND gap_min >= 30; otherwise rechase.
- session: by the signal clock, ET: asia 18:00-01:59, london 02:00-08:29, pre 08:30-09:29, nyam 09:30-11:59, nypm 12:00-14:59, close 15:00+.
- n_sess = 1 + number of P7 trades whose ENTRY second lies in [session start, signal second) (same session, same day; asia starting 18:00 ET of the prior evening for 00:00-01:59).
- gap_min = (signal second - exit second of the most recent P7 trade closed at or before the signal second) / 60, any exit name, any side; infinite if none in the data.
- chasing: chase_k > 1.0 (strict). chase_k = ((last completed 1-min close at or before the signal second) - (open of the first 1-min bar of the session)) x side / ATR5, ATR5 = the 5-min ATR(14, Wilder, roll-gap-adjusted bars) of the last 5-min bar completed at or before the signal second (the same ATR as the REG layer, absolute points not atr_rel). chase_k = NaN (no session bar yet, or no ATR) counts as NOT chasing.
## 3. What the tag does in practice (IMPORTANT, ambiguity of scale)
ATR5 is small relative to a session move, so chase_k above 1 is the normal case after the first minutes of a session: in June 2026 (P7, 242 trades) only 19 trades (8 percent) were fresh; every other trade got the fast 30/10x/w5/p2 line. F3 as scored is therefore "fast line on about 92 percent of P7 trades, slow V2 on the first one or two clean trades". If v1.9 wants the same behaviour it must use ATR5 in points exactly as above; with a session-range or daily ATR the tag would change and F3's results would not apply. Frozen live week: 48 of 53 P7 trades are rechase.

## 5. What v1.9 must code, and ambiguities
Code: (a) per-book P7 counters kept by the strategy itself: entries per session, last P7 exit time; (b) at the signal bar close, session open price, last 1-min close, ATR5(14 Wilder) of the 5-min bars (existing REG ATR), and the tag; (c) store the tag on the trade (field EntryRegime or cut-line id) at entry and, at the first manage second, load the cut cell by tag: fresh 45/5x/w5/p10, rechase 30/10x/w5/p2 (the existing cut cell structure: arm, mult, win, pers); (d) the policy file third column can match the tag (fresh/rechase) if EntryRegime is added; DayRegime cannot.
Ambiguities / differences between the research hook and a live implementation: (1) the hook counts n_sess and gap from CLOSED engine trades (s.trades): a P7 trade still open when the next signal arrives is not counted in n_sess and does not set gap (the research replay of the tag in the page counts open trades; live should decide; differences are small but real); (2) session open price: first 1-min bar open at or after the session start in the NQ state bars (back-adjustment ignored inside a session); (3) ATR5 uses the last COMPLETED 5-min bar, up to 900 s stale; (4) which book's trades set gap/n_sess: P7 only (K7/M7 context cuts were different, untested as winners); (5) the live 30-minute same-side blackout after a P7 stop or cut interacts with gap_min (rows with gap < 30 exist in the research run: it is on in LPB_BASE too, but I did not verify which sides those rows are); (6) results: 43 months LPB base, L6M +30.6 percent stack drawdown cut / +2,129 $/wk, HOLD +1.8 / -866, PRE +28.4 / +335, X' +24.3 / +191; JN04RM base +9.0 / +1.0 / +2.3 / +45.9 with negative means; acceptance_p7 FAIL (6 of 20 sessions vs 3 for LPB_BASE), scorecard full stops 24.9/wk vs 24.5; trend-week clause: the 5 best weeks 458k -> 369k (80.5 percent), 9 winning weeks turned losing.
