## Things worth attacking
* The layer builds recompute the loss-row z-scores on the union; v3 restores the published z for the old rows (verify_v3 'published_part_columns_differing' = {}), the extras keep the union z. The v1.3 rules do not use z.
* PB7 and O7 rows sit in trades_arm with sim_ok = False on purpose (d-table double count; ls_data.LOTS KeyError otherwise).
* ids of a book are not monotone in entry time (extras after the old block); any code that sorts by tid assumes the old order.
* The O7 rule is reconstructed from the user's DS7-code answer (DIRECTIVE_O7_PIN_20261104.md): the floor 'min 10 pts' and the last signal bar (09:43 vs 09:44) are interpretations; both variants are built (o7_rows_{A,B,A_w44,B_w44}.parquet in /mnt/l2/l2vast/ds3_base4/otherbooks/); the blind week rows are identical in all four and match the 3 live O7 entries to the minute and side (09:41, 09:43, 09:42).
* Not built: profit-mode decision-row tables for the extras, X books, DN/WT/CR rows, POI streams for extras.
