# WATCH_BTCM_KELTNER_SPEC: Shadow-only persistence watch of the MBT Asia 10-minute Keltner flip (S4b, 2026-11-06)

Status: WATCH, best-of-F, CAP-bound (DS3_INSTR2_BTC.md crypto follow-up; lead acceptance 2026-11-06). **Size, stated first:** under the 1.7% participation cap (MBT Asia 1.43 contracts per 5 min) one contract risks $34-$80 per trade and earns about $8-15 per trade before the in-sample optimism is removed, 39-59 trades per year: **about $6-15 per week, 0.1% of the NQ stack's $17.7k per week. This watch tests whether the regularity PERSISTS out of sample, not whether it pays.** Shadow only: signals are logged and outcomes are computed from owned 1-minute bars; no order is ever sent, no purchase is made, nothing goes live without the user. Sha256 of this file is in DECISIONS.md (logged before any forward bar is scored).

## 1. Exact definition (PRIMARY; the only line that decides)
- Instrument and data: CME Micro Bitcoin (MBT), outright continuous 1-minute bars, volume-rolled and additively back-adjusted (the same construction as /mnt/l2/l2vast/ds3_instr/MBT_1m.npz, builder l2vast/ds3/code/instr/s4b_data.py); a bar exists only for minutes with at least one print. Forward data = the owned top-up files of the same vendor feed (data/ext_*), appended to the series as they arrive; **no purchase, no new source**; if a top-up does not contain MBT the watch simply has no new bars.
- Clock clock: ET (America/New_York, tz database); label day = ET + 6 h; all times below are the START of the 1-minute bar.
- 10-minute bars: epoch-minute buckets // 10 (clock aligned: :00, :10, ...); the bar is valid for a signal only if its nominal last minute (:x9) has a printed bar. Indicators run on every 10-minute bar of the continuous series (including bars whose last minute did not print), seeded at the first bar of the series (use at least 500 warm-up bars).
- Signal (Keltner flip): EMA(8) of the 10-minute closes (alpha 2/9) and Wilder ATR(20) of the 10-minute bars (TR from the previous 10-minute close; seed = simple mean of the first 20 true ranges); state long if close > EMA + 2.5 x ATR, state short if close < EMA - 2.5 x ATR, otherwise the state is unchanged; a signal fires at the close of a valid 10-minute bar when the state changes to long or to short (side = the new state). Fires only if the ATR exists.
- Session filter: the ENTRY bar (the next 1-minute bar) must start between 18:00 and 01:59 ET (Asia), must not start in 16:30-17:59 ET, and must start within 180 s of the signal bar's last minute (no halt / weekend hole); Sunday-open signals (entry from 18:00 Sunday) are included.
- Entry: market at the open of that 1-minute bar, plus 2 ticks adverse (10 points). One position at a time: a signal that arrives while a position is open (before its exit bar) is ignored.
- Stop: 3.0 x ATR14 of the last COMPLETE 5-minute bar at the signal bar's close (Wilder, 5-minute clock-aligned bars, seed as above), resting, filled at the stop level or the bar open if gapped through, plus 3 ticks (15 points) adverse; tested first when a bar touches both stop and target.
- Target: 1.5 x the stop distance from the entry price, resting; a bar fills it only if its extreme trades at least 1 tick (5 points) beyond the level; filled at the level.
- Time stop: 120 minutes after the entry bar start, exit at the open of the first bar at or after that time, 2 ticks (10 points) adverse. Flat at 16:55 ET (open of the first bar at or after 16:55), same cost. A gap of more than 10 minutes inside a trade closes it at the last bar's close with the flat cost.
- Size and fee: 1 MBT contract per trade (the cap 1.43 allows 1; the equal-$1,200-risk lots would be 15-40 contracts and are not executable); fee proxy $1.78 round trip per contract (PROXY-MICRO from FEES.md's MES value; CME Globex MBT exchange + clearing + NFA about $0.50 plus the broker commission, unverified) = 17.8 points; reported R is net: R = side x (exit - entry) / stop_pts - 17.8 / stop_pts (the fee is 0.03-0.13 R per trade depending on the stop distance).
- Spread: the real Asia MBT spread is not measurable on this box (no MBT L2 / ticks); the 2/3/2-tick costs are the assumption (the median MBT Asia 1-minute bar range is 25 points); a cost multiplier 2.0 is tracked as a secondary column.

## 4. Forward evaluation and the PASS / KILL rules (fixed now)
Unit: net R per trade (after costs and the $1.78 fee) of the PRIMARY line on forward signals only (entry bars after 2026-10-31, the end of the owned sample); trades per year about 39 (so 20 trades is about 6 months, 60 about 18 months, 90 about 28 months). With a trade standard deviation of about 1.0 R the standard error is 0.22 R at n = 20, 0.13 at n = 60, 0.105 at n = 90; the watch is therefore SLOW and the first verdicts are late.
- **KILL (regularity does not persist):** forward t-statistic (mean / SE with the realised SD) <= -1.0 at any n >= 20; or mean net R <= 0 at n = 60; or mean net R < +0.03 at n = 90; or mean net R < +0.05 at the hard stop n = 120.
- **PERSISTS (WATCH continues, label 'regularity persists, CAP-bound'):** mean net R >= +0.08 and t >= 1.0 at n = 60; or mean net R >= +0.06 and t >= 1.3 at n = 90; and PRIMARY minus the random-side control >= +0.10 R at the same n.
- Otherwise CONTINUE to the next checkpoint (n = 20, 40, 60, 90, 120).
- Reported at each checkpoint: n, mean net R, SE, t, hit rate, net $ at 1 contract and per week, share of Asia sub-windows (18:00-22:00 vs 22:00-02:00), the cost-multiplier-2 column, the three secondary lines and the control. A PERSISTS verdict does not change the size statement: it would show that a documented Asia trend regularity of BTC futures exists, not that it is worth running at the cap. Nothing here is a PASS claim.
