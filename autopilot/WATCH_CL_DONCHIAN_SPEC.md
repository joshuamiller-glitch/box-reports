# WATCH_CL_DONCHIAN_SPEC - Shadow-only forward watch of the CL daily Donchian-20 stop-and-reverse cell (S4a, 2026-11-06)
Status: BEST-OF-F cell of the FIND_DAILY family (DS3_FIND_DAILY.md; parent prereg sha256 bf1de6c6902656b32ab9c86981f7b0d3e138ebde062ae719b2ded22839f72129, stress prereg sha256 2c0008b7988dc46cda4511746e581c4fe6311ea36bbbd95fe78ea4b5562c7dd9). Not a lead, no PASS, nothing live, no purchase. Pass/kill criteria below are fixed NOW, before any forward bar is read.
## 1. The rule (exactly what was tested)
CL volume-front 1-minute series, multiplicative roll-adjusted, label days (17:00 ET roll, weekend to Monday). Once per label day at 15:55 ET: S = close of the 15:54 bar; H/L = day high/low through 15:54 (all adjusted). Upper = max of the previous 20 days' H, lower = min of the previous 20 days' L (days t-20..t-1). If S > upper: target = +1 (long); if S < lower: target = -1 (short); otherwise keep the previous target (stop-and-reverse, always in the market once the first break has happened). Lots: first label day of each month, lots = clip(round($1,200 / (EWMA(0.94) daily percent vol known on the previous day x raw price x $1,000)), 1, 6); the Shadow watch runs at a FIXED 1 lot (the 1-lot version: 13-year $132,164, maxDD $42,947, Calmar 0.22; the vol-lot version: $145,271, maxDD $47,782, Calmar 0.22 at 1.1 lots on average). Costs in the model: 1 tick ($10) slip per side + $2.42 per side per lot (fee $4.84 round trip, proxy), roll = 2 slips + one RT fee per lot held.
## 2. Execution (NT8 / Rithmic)
* One strategy on a 1-minute CL chart, time-driven: at 15:55:00 ET (first tick of the 15:55 bar) compute the target from completed bars and send ONE market order for the difference (flat to +/-1: 1 lot; reversal: 2 lots). No stop orders, no brackets: the open risk is the stop-and-reverse channel itself (expected worst 13-year drawdown $47.8k at 1 lot).
* Liquidity: CL settles 14:30 ET; the Z2 profile (instr2_sessions.json) puts the median 5-minute volume at ~1,400 contracts at 09:00-09:30 ET, 323 at 15:30 and 183 at 16:00; 15:55 is the thin tail (flat_1655 verdict THIN-CLOSE, volume share 16:55-17:00 0.13%) but 1-2 lots are small against ~320 per 5 minutes; model slippage 1 tick. An alternative anchor near the 14:30 settlement was NOT tested; the 15:55 anchor is the only tested one.
* Margin: not in the repository. PROXY: notional per lot is $12.6k-$124k (median $66.7k); at 8-10% of notional that is about $5-7k per lot, held overnight (overnight margin applies). Use the broker's current schedule before any use; a 1-lot Shadow needs no margin.
* Roll rule for execution: the backtest switches at the volume-front date, a median of 1 business day (153 of 165 rolls within 2 business days) before the approximate monthly expiry, which is later than a trader would roll. The spec rolls at 15:55 ET on the 6th business day before expiry (CL expiry: 3 business days before the 25th calendar day of the month preceding delivery), as a market spread or two legs; the adjusted-series signal does not change. Roll cost model unchanged. April 2020: the series never held the May contract at its negative settlement (volume-front switched to June on 2020-04-20); a position following the calendar rule would have been rolled around 2020-04-13.
## 3. Forward data and review
Data = the owned CL 1-minute feed top-ups ONLY (find_c/cache_c/CL.npz extended by the standing top-up job from 2026-10-17 on); no purchase; if the top-up stops, the watch pauses (not kills). Review every Monday with the daily_lib/cl_stress code (daily_lib.build('CL') then the cell), 1 lot, percent frame, costs as above. Forward P&L is counted from 2026-10-17 (the data end of this research). Forward trades are episodes (a run of the same sign).
## 4. Kill criteria (any one ends the watch)
* K1 drawdown: forward drawdown from the forward equity peak >= $47,782 at 1 lot (the 13-year maximum).
* K2 early loss: forward cumulative net <= -$26,761 (one annual sigma of the cell) at any review.
* K3 no edge by 24 months: forward cumulative net < 0 at the 24-month review (2028-10-16).
* K4 structure: two or more consecutive forward losing years with each loss > $10,000 (the cell lost -$8k, -$18k in its worst two single years).
## 5. Continue / eligibility criteria (cannot produce a PASS)
At the 36-month review (2029-10-16), only if no kill has fired: forward net > 0, forward Calmar >= 0.10, at least 12 forward episodes, and no single forward trade more than 80% of the forward net. If all hold the cell is ELIGIBLE for a new, separately preregistered confirmation (new family count, with the 408-cell parent family and this watch counted); if any fails the watch is closed as NULL. At 1 lot the edge is about $200 a week, 1% of the live stack's HOLD weekly mean, and 13 years of history cannot separate it from zero (about 50 years would be needed at its volatility: (2.8 x $26.8k / $10.3k)^2), so the watch is a kill-test, not a confirmation.
## 6. What would not be accepted
Changing N, the exit channel, the filters, the anchor time or the lots after seeing forward data; counting forward data before 2026-10-17; any non-owned data.
