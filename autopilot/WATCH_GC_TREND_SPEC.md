# WATCH_GC_TREND_SPEC - Shadow watch of the GC Keltner-flip NY-AM book (ZL-1, 2026-11-06; pass/kill fixed BEFORE any forward week)
Label REGIME / WATCH. Shadow only. No purchase: the forward GC feed is the weekly top-up only (ohlcv-1m of the front month, as the other forward registers). No live order, no promotion without a separate user decision.
Origin: GC-DISSECT (autopilot/DS3_INSTR2_GC.md block, prereg l2vast/ds3/prereg/GC_DISSECT_prereg.md sha256 ac803b1278e4f81423dcb85c754c789babcdd10fa15a9e6820feb21b377a5900). What the watch is: a test whether the 2026 gold regime (high volatility, net-down year, trend flips paying on the short side) persists, NOT a claim of a trend book (13-year history negative; pre-2023 2 of 10 years positive).
## Exact live definition (code: t2s4a/sig.py KELT, t2s4a/evalk.py kernel; reproduced bar by bar by l2vast/ds3/code/gcdis/step3.py::book)
* Instrument GC front month (continuous, volume roll; roll day and next day not traded). Bars: 1-min, aggregated to CLOCK-ALIGNED 5-minute bars that complete at their nominal last minute; ET wall clock.
* Signal (Keltner flip, b = 5, e = 34, n = 14, m = 0.5): mid = EMA(34) of the 5-min closes; ATR = Wilder ATR(14) of the 5-min bars (true range with a >30-min-gap reset); state = +1 when close > mid + 0.5 ATR, -1 when close < mid - 0.5 ATR, unchanged in between; a signal is the change of state into +1 (long) or -1 (short); no signal until 68 bars after any gap > 30 minutes (warm-up).
* Session: the signal bar's last minute lies in NY-AM 09:30-12:00 ET (minute of day 570..719).
* Entry: market at the open of the first 1-minute bar after the signal bar's close. One position at a time (a signal while in a trade is ignored). Stop: 3.0 x ATR5(14) (5-minute Wilder ATR at the signal bar), resting stop order, minimum 2 ticks. Target: 1.5 R (4.5 ATR) limit. Time stop: 60 minutes after entry at market; flat at 16:55 ET at the latest.
* Size: equal risk $1,200 per trade: contracts = min(1200 / (stop distance x $100), 6, 18 per 5-min cap for NY-AM). GC integer lots: round to the nearest integer, min 1 (risk then differs from $1,200 by > 25% in about 45% of trades; median stop risk about $1,040 per lot). Micro MGC (10 oz, tick $1): contracts = round(10 x the GC lot figure), same risk; MGC fee unmeasured (the GC fee of $4.84 round trip per contract is NOT assumed for MGC; log the broker fee). Costs in the model: entry slip 1 tick, stop exit slip 2 ticks, market exit slip 1 tick, fee $4.84 per GC round trip.
## Forward log (Shadow register row per trade, appended weekly)
signal bar end (epoch s), side, entry px, stop px, target px, exit px and type (stop / target / time / flat), lots (GC and MGC), net $ at the model costs, ATR5, and a column 'regime_on' (trailing 20-day VR(12) and 60-day vol percentile of GC, logged but NOT used to filter).
## Fixed thresholds (from the 2023-04..2026-10 evaluation of this exact book, integer equal-risk GC lots, carry weeks dropped: HOLD (2025-11..2026-10, 41 weeks) mean +$557/wk, MDE $1,821, maxDD $25,176; L6M mean +$1,004/wk, maxDD $4,340; PRE -$270/wk; X' -$204/wk)
Scoring starts with the first full trading week on or after 2026-11-09. 'Active week' = a week with at least one trade.
* KILL (any one, checked every Friday): K1 cumulative net since start <= -$12,588 (-0.5 x HOLD maxDD); K2 drawdown from the forward cumulative peak >= $25,176 (the HOLD maxDD); K3 after 13 active weeks the mean weekly net < -$1,616 (-0.5 x MDE_13, MDE_13 = 2.8 x sd_week / sqrt(13) with sd_week = $4,162 from the HOLD window); K4 after 26 active weeks the mean weekly net < $0.
* PASS-WATCH (Shadow label upgrade only: 'regime confirmed forward', still no live): after >= 26 active weeks AND >= 100 trades, ALL of: mean weekly net >= $278 (0.5 x the HOLD mean); cumulative net > 0; forward maxDD < $25,176; at least 55% of the 4-week blocks positive; the short side not the whole story: net of the forced-same-direction drift book (same entry times, side forced to the sign of the 20-day return) is > 0.
* Between the two: continue; at 52 active weeks without PASS-WATCH or KILL the watch is closed as 'did not confirm'.
* Reporting: every Monday one line in MORNING.md (weeks, trades, cumulative, mean/week, maxDD, blocks positive, status), no other action.
## What would change the label
A forward PASS-WATCH plus a pre-2023 explanation (the 13-year history stays negative) would still leave the label REGIME; a trend BOOK would need the full bar (placebo p <= .10, family p <= .10, walk-forward positive, |corr| <= .3, stack Calmar up) which the 2023-26 evaluation did not meet (placebo p_book .82, family 1.00, walk-forward 6m +169 / 12m -17 $/wk, stack k* = 0).
