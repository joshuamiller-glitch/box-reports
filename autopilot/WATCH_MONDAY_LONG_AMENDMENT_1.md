## Why (the problem with clause (ii))
A cumulative-P&L <= 0 run of 13 weekly reads is a random-walk event, not evidence. With the historical Monday sd 120.6 bps (NQ FULL leg, 2013-2026) and 104 Mondays, simulated 200,000 paths: the ORIGINAL kill set (hard-loss + 13-consecutive clause) fires on 39.5% / 56.9% / 69.5% of paths within 26 / 52 / 104 Mondays when the true mean is 0 (a correct kill), but ALSO on 21% / 29% / 33% when the true mean is +16 bps and on 8.9% / 10.8% / 11.2% when it is the historical 2023+ mean +32.5 bps (wrongful kills of a real effect). The hard-loss kill alone: 7.8% / 10.8% / 13.7% at mean 0 and 2.6% / 3.0% / 3.2% at +16 (0.7% at +32.5).
## The SPRT replacing clause (ii)
Sequential probability ratio test on the mean net per Monday x_t (bps of price, FULL leg after cost, variant A), x_t ~ N(mu, sigma^2) with sigma = 120.6 bps FIXED NOW (historical NQ Monday sd; not re-estimated forward), H0: mu = 0 versus H1: mu = +16 bps (the historical 2023+ Monday mean +32.5 halved), alpha = .05, beta = .20. Statistic after n forward Mondays with cumulative S_n = sum x_t: LLR_n = (16 / 120.6^2) x (S_n - 8 n). Boundaries: A = ln((1-beta)/alpha) = +2.7726, B = ln(beta/(1-alpha)) = -1.5581. In cumulative bps: ACCEPT H1 (evidence for the effect; NOT a pass by itself) when S_n >= 2,520 + 8 n; ACCEPT H0 (closes the watch as NULL, a kill) when S_n <= -1,416 + 8 n. Examples (cumulative bps, mean equivalent): n = 8: H1 >= 2,584 (323/Mon), H0 <= -1,352 (-169/Mon), hard-loss -682; n = 13: 2,624 (202), -1,312 (-101), -870; n = 26: 2,728 (105), -1,208 (-46), -1,230; n = 52: 2,936 (56), -1,000 (-19), -1,739; n = 104: 3,352 (32), -584 (-6), -2,460. Expected sample size: 152 Mondays under H0, 217 Mondays under H1 (about 3 and 4 years): the SPRT is SLOW; it is evaluated at every weekly read; truncation: at n = 104 with no boundary hit the watch is INCONCLUSIVE and stops.

Reading: wrongful kills of a real effect fall from 21-33% (+16) and 9-11% (+32.5) to 5-10% and 1% respectively; the cost is that, with no edge, the amended rule closes the watch on 21% by week 52 (46% by week 104) instead of 57% / 70%. The 50%-power caveat: at the historical 2023+ mean (+32.5) the 26-Monday pass read passes on 54% of paths; at +16 on 27%; the SPRT H1 boundary needs a median of more than 52 Mondays even at +32.5 (58% hit by 104). Failing the pass read at 26 Mondays is therefore NOT evidence of no effect; only the H0 boundary and the hard-loss kill end the watch.
