# WATCH_MONDAY_LONG_SPEC - Shadow-only forward watch of the equity-index MONDAY long (CHAR-A, 2026-11-06). Nothing live, no purchase, no PASS claim.
Label: WATCH / REGIME, best-of-F (DAILY2 block A + MONDAY follow-up in DS3_FIND_DAILY.md; prereg shas in DECISIONS.md: DAILY2 cf9c0f3a..., MONDAY 78e43952...). Historical facts used below: weekday-permutation joint equity-family p .15-.63 (RTY .15 best, 4-index average .18), level shift 2023 (sup-t p .003-.023, pre-2023 Monday excess +3..+6 bps, 2023+ +16..+28 bps), tolerant gate FAILS (L3M negative), walk-forward positive, uncorrelated with the NQ stack (|corr| <= .12).
## 1. Definition (frozen now)
* Instrument for the watch: NQ front contract (the forward NQ 1-s / 1-min tables already arrive weekly: tables/NQ/1min/*ext_*, tables/NQ/1s/*), so NO new data purchase. ES / RTY / YM would need new tables: a metadata cost quote only (metadata.get_cost), default = no buy; they are NOT part of the pass test.
* Leg FULL: long 1 unit at the Sunday 18:00:00 ET Globex open (market order at the open; the first 1-min bar's mid_first is the reference), exit by market order at the 16:55:00 ET flat (the NQ-stack house flat time) of the Monday label day. Monday = label day beginning Sunday 18:00 ET (so holiday Mondays with no Sunday session are skipped; shortened sessions exit at the stack's flat rule). One position; no add; no stop (variant A). Variant B = same with a catastrophic resting stop-market 250 bps (~2.1 historical Monday sd) below the entry; stop exits charge 2 ticks slippage. Variant A is the PASS variant; B is reported beside.
* Granularity: 1 NQ-class lot (about $570k notional at 28,600) and 1 MNQ (1/10: about $57k notional, $2/pt, fee proxy $1.28 RT = MES published, MNQ proxy). The watch is booked at 1 MNQ ("micro"); the NQ-lot $ is derived.
* Costs charged: instr2_sessions NQ cost_pts round trip (fee + slippage proxies, about 0.8 bps at the current price); 18:00 open is thin: the entry reference is mid_first of the first minute, which understates the real open spread (documented, not corrected).
* Start of the forward sample: Monday 2026-11-09 = week 1. (Monday 2026-11-02 is already in the data: NQ -132 bps, excluded because it was seen before this spec was frozen.) Log: `python3 l2vast/ds3/code/find/monday_watch_log.py` (reads the forward NQ 1-min tables, bin-END timestamps, appends to /mnt/l2/l2vast/ds3/out/find/monday_watch_log.csv; prints the checklist). It must be run every Tuesday after the 17:00 ET roll; week 1 cannot exist before Tuesday 2026-11-10 (today the feed ends 2026-11-05).
## 2. Historical numbers that fix the thresholds (NQ FULL leg, bps of price, label days Sunday-open to 16:55 flat)
* Monday: mean +17.5, sd 120.6 (n 690, 2013-2026); pre-2023 mean +9.9 / sd 133.4; 2023+ mean +32.5 / sd 100.3. Excess over Tue-Fri of the same week: +12.2 (z 2.2; 2023+ +28.4). Worst Monday (ES) -644 bps (2020-03-09) = -$8.8k per ES, -$880 per MES; ten worst ES Mondays -$3.2k to -$8.8k per contract. sigma_M used below = 120.6 bps = $690 per MNQ at 28,600.
* Power: with sigma_M 120.6 and a true excess of 28 bps the standard error of the mean after 26 Mondays is about 23 bps, so the PASS test has roughly 50% power; failing to pass at 26 is NOT evidence of no effect (it is why the kill rules, not the pass rule, are the practical test).
## 3. Criteria (fixed now)
PASS-candidate (Shadow only; still needs the user; NOT live) requires ALL of: (a) >= 26 completed forward Mondays; (b) mean net P&L per Monday > 0 at 1 MNQ (variant A); (c) one-sided weekday-permutation p <= .10 on the forward sample (Monday excess over the other weekdays of the same ISO weeks, labels permuted within weeks, 2,000 draws; requires the forward Tue-Fri days, available from the same feed); (d) |corr| of the Monday P&L with the NQ stack (V0 and V2, daily and weekly, forward sample) <= .30; (e) equal-risk stack Calmar with carrying weeks dropped not lower than without the book (or the forward window too short: reported only); (f) variant B mean net > 0 as well.
KILL (watch ends, label DEAD/REGIME) if ANY: (i) after n >= 8 Mondays, cumulative net <= -2.0 x 120.6 x sqrt(n) bps (n = 8: -682 bps = -$3.9k per MNQ... i.e. -2 sigma of the historical Monday sd for a zero-edge book; n = 26: -1,230 bps); (ii) 13 consecutive weekly reads (Tuesday after each Monday, from week 1) with cumulative net <= 0; (iii) a Monday move beyond -600 bps happens AND variant B's stop would have saved more than 50% of it AND cumulative <= 0 (tail policy review, no automatic kill); (iv) feed break or contract-spec/session-hours change.
INCONCLUSIVE at week 52 if neither pass nor kill: report n, mean, sd, p and stop spending attention. All reads are posted to DS3_FIND_DAILY.md ('MONDAY WATCH read') with the checklist; nothing is promoted without the user.
