## 1. Definition (frozen; computable from NQ ohlcv-1s bars with volume only, no mbp-10)
* Data: NQ front contract (the instrument id with the largest RTH volume on the previous label day; the SAME id is used for today's bars and the prior-day levels, so rolls cause no gap), 1-second OHLCV bars (prints only), ET clock, label day = ET date of (wall + 6 h).
* Levels: PDH / PDL = highest bar high / lowest bar low of the previous label day's RTH 09:30:00-15:59:59 ET.
* Event: in 09:30:00-11:59:59 ET, the FIRST 1-s bar of the day whose HIGH >= PDH while the previous print bar's CLOSE < PDH (side = LONG), and independently the first bar whose LOW <= PDL while the previous close > PDL (side = SHORT); at most one event per level per day. No volume/burst filter (this row is the 'all touches' class).
* Entry (1 s latency): market order in the approach direction, filled at the OPEN of the first 1-s bar with ts >= ts_touch + 1 s. One position at a time (an event while in a trade is skipped).
* Exit: resting stop at entry -/+ max(2 ticks, m x S ticks) with m = 1.0, S = median of |d30| (ticks) over the previous 60 events (d30 = approach-direction move from the entry bar's open to the open of the first bar >= entry + 30 s); stop exit slips 1 tick; resting profit target at +1R (= the stop distance); otherwise MARKET exit at the OPEN of the first bar with ts >= entry_ts + 30 s (deadline clock starts at entry_ts); flat 16:55 ET. The S history is seeded with the last 60 bar-based historical events (pdl_bar_events.parquet) so the first forward event can trade.
* Granularity and costs: booked at 1 MNQ; NQ-lot equivalent is x10 (NQ-sized equal-risk lots would be 14 contracts, above comfortable size for a 30 s market-in/market-out book: not used). Cost per round trip at 1 NQ: 2 ticks (0.5 pt) + $4.84 fee = $14.84 (PROXY-STD; slippage ticks = the measured NQ median spread 2 ticks at the touch); at 1 MNQ $2.28 (0.5 pt x $2 + $1.28 fee) conservative.
