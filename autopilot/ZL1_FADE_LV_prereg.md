# ZL1_FADE_LV prereg (ZL-1, 2026-11-03/04): T-FADE slice = LEVEL ANCHORS faded by session (ledger FADE_LV)
Written and hashed BEFORE any outcome statistic of the cells. Research only; Shadow only. Instrument NQ, base = Q1-A ts2 stack, evaluation = ZL-2 MultiHarness (NQ, live fill model: signal at the 1-min bar close B, first fill B+1 s, one lot per 2 s, resting stop/target, stop 2.45 pt worse, market exits 0.80 pt, fee 0.242 pt), BookHarness windows L1M L2M L3M L6M HOLD MID PRE X', gate v1 (NOW-ONLY / TOLERANT / STRICT), gate_v2a (minimum sample), gate_v2 (placebo p Bonferroni on n_tested) and the family max-statistic null; stack marginal at 0.5x and 1x with the carrying weeks dropped. Data = 1-s path (ds3_g2_redo) aggregated to 1-min bars (+ per-minute volume from bars2 for VWAP); label day 18:00 ET; research cap 2026-10-31.
## Sessions (NQ ET tags): ASIA 18:00-02:00, LONDON 02:00-08:30, NYAM 09:30-12:00, NYPM 12:00-15:00. No entries 15:00-18:00 (flat 16:55 ET).
## Anchors (a level L; the upper side is faded SHORT, the lower side LONG; both sides of a pair are one cell unless noted)
PD = prior-day RTH H/L (09:30-16:00 ET of the prior label day); GD = prior label day Globex H/L; PDC = prior RTH close and PDM = prior-day RTH (H+L)/2 (two-sided: price above fades short, below fades long); ON = overnight H/L (18:00 -> now, NYAM and NYPM use 18:00-09:30); AS = Asia range H/L (18:00-02:00, faded in LONDON, NYAM, NYPM); LO = London range H/L (02:00-09:30, faded in NYAM and NYPM); PW = prior-week H/L (previous Mon-Fri label days); RN = round numbers (nearest 00 and 50 in the RAW contract frame); VB1 / VB15 / VB2 = session VWAP +- 1 / 1.5 / 2 sigma (sigma = volume-weighted standard deviation of 1-min closes about the session VWAP, session VWAP reset at the session start; NYAM and NYPM share the RTH VWAP from 09:30); IB30 / IB60 = H/L of the first 30 / 60 minutes of the session (active after the IB completes); OR5 / OR15 / OR30 = H/L of the first 5 / 15 / 30 minutes (NYAM = from 09:30, others from the session start; active after completion); GAP = 09:30 gap fill (prior RTH close vs the 09:30 open, gap >= 20 pt: at 09:31 enter against the gap direction, target the prior close, one trade a day, NYAM only, no trigger).
Applicability: PD GD PDC PDM PW RN VB* IB* OR* in all four sessions; ON in LONDON NYAM NYPM; AS in LONDON NYAM NYPM; LO in NYAM NYPM; GAP in NYAM. Cells = anchor x session x trigger.
## Triggers (1-min bars, signal at the close of bar t; ATR = Wilder ATR14 of 5-min bars as of the last closed bar)
T1 touch-and-reject: bar t high >= U (low <= L) and close back on the inner side (close < U) while the previous close was inside; T2 failed break: bar t-1 closed beyond the level by >= 0.1 ATR and bar t closes back inside; T3 exhaustion: the last 5 1-min closes monotonic into the level, bar t high within 0.25 ATR of the level (or through it) and bar t closes against the run (first reversal bar). One trade per (anchor, side, session, label day). A level breached by > 2 ATR before the trigger is retired for the session.
## Shape
Stage 1 (screen, default shape): stop = (extreme of the last 5 bars incl. t) + 0.5 ATR; target = 1.0 R (R = stop distance from the signal close); time stop 60 minutes; flat 16:55; no re-entry; 1 lot.
Stage 2 (for the top 12 stage-1 cells by key): buffer {0.25, 0.5, 1.0} ATR x target {VWAP at the signal (static distance), session mid so far (static), 0.5R, 1R, 1.5R, 2R} x time stop {15, 30, 60, 120} min = 72 shapes per cell; targets nearer than 4 pt or on the wrong side are skipped. Stage 3 (top 5 cells of stage 2): scale-out half at VWAP (half lot target VWAP, half lot the cell's target), re-entry once after a stop (same anchor, new trigger within the session), no re-entry.
Lots 1-2 equal-dollar risk to a NQ lot: reported at 1 lot; stack marginal at 0.5x and 1x (the stack is at live lots).
## Key and selection (fixed now)
key = mean of the weekly means over L3M and L6M (recent first), requiring >= 20 trades in L6M and >= 8 in L3M; rank stage 1 by key; stage 2 / 3 as above. No selection on PRE / X' (REGIME books are not killed by 2023-25).
## Gates and regime reporting
Every cell reports L1M L3M L6M HOLD MID PRE X' (mean $/wk, maxDD), NOW-ONLY, TOLERANT, STRICT, gate_v2a, and REGIME-conditioned numbers beside the pooled: (a) causal switch: trailing-20 / 40-day own-book P&L > 0 (strictly earlier days), first-hour range share > median (trailing), OR book hit rate < 0.5 (trailing 20 days); (b) vol tercile (vol_tag_entry_day); (c) B02 quiet / normal (exact rule); (d) weekday; (e) event day (CAL calendar) vs not. A book is labelled REGIME if its pooled TOLERANT fails but the switch-on days pass NOW-ONLY with the switch-on mean in 2023-25 >= 0 (then 'conditional'), or 2026-only (then 'NOW-ONLY/ARTEFACT?').
Placebos (BookHarness): shift5 (random delay 5-120 min inside session and label day), side (random side), days (same clock time on another label day), 100 draws each for every stage-1 cell that reaches NOW-ONLY and for every stage 2 / 3 cell that reaches TOLERANT. Family max-statistic null: 100 random-side worlds (every event side flipped with probability 1/2) of the whole stage-1 family: the statistic = the best key and the best HOLD Calmar over the family; p_maxstat = share of worlds >= the real best. n_tested = stage 1 cells + 12 x 72 + 5 x 4 (registered).
## Flag rule: a cell with TOLERANT, placebo p_book <= 0.10 (and Bonferroni stated), p_maxstat <= 0.10 reported, and positive stack Calmar gain with the carrying week dropped (HOLD and L6M) is flagged at the top of MORNING.md the same hour with the exact live definition.
