# ZL1_FLIP_FAMILIES_prereg - flip streams from other level families (2026-11-02, written BEFORE any family was scored; the BT swing-flip result is the only prior knowledge)
Single definition, no tuning, identical for every family: a LEVEL is known at its knowable second; it is BROKEN at the first second at which a trade prints >= 10.0 pts beyond it on the side opposite to where price was when the level became known (high-type levels: broken upward; low-type: downward; all other levels: the side is where price was 300 s after the level became known); at the break second a FLIP LEVEL is created at the level's price (support after an upward break, resistance after a downward break), valid 4 h, fresh until price returns to it after having left it by 0.5 pt (touch_away 0.5). A level that is not broken within its pre-break life expires: 5 days for session/day/week levels, 20 days for month levels, to the end of the CME day for round numbers, 1 h for VWAP snapshots, the stream's own life for shipped lines and equal highs/lows. Row rule: a base signal is flagged if a fresh flip level lies between entry and target within min(40, target distance) pts (poi feature kernel, ahead distance). Test: V13Y (d7_v13y) lock=None taken trades, saved dollars = minus the flagged taken trades' base net, windows L3M L6M HOLD MID PRE X' and per calendar year and half (H1 2023-08..2024-12, H2 2025-01..2026-10), 200-draw row-level placebos (A same count per book x session, B same count per day, C other-day same book x session; rank = share of placebo sums at least as good), overlap with the BT swing flip (5-min swing, Jaccard of the flagged rows and the union's saved dollars). Best-of-N: n = 818 earlier ZL-1 cells + the families below (16 new cells) = 834; a family is a lead only if min(L6M, HOLD, PRE) saved dollars > 0 with the three placebo ranks <= 0.05 in L6M and HOLD, and it beats the 1,980-family same-statistic null (bnull2_V13Y) at its own flagged-row density.
Families (16): 1 prior-day high/low (CME day), 2 prior-day close, 3 Asia session high/low (18:00-02:00 ET, known 02:00), 4 London session high/low (02:00-09:30, known 09:30), 5 overnight high/low (18:00-09:30, known 09:30), 6 RTH initial-balance high/low (09:30-10:30, known 10:30), 7 weekly open and monthly open prints (known 1 s after the open), 8 prior-week high/low (known at the Friday close), 9 prior-month high/low (known at the month's last close), 10 round-number handles at every 50 pts within +-250 pts of the day's first price (known at the CME day open), 11 the 8 shipped zone-line values (poi_store_redo streams MAS|ich_kijun|1h, PDX|day|M, CRT|M|4h, MAS|kc_up|4h, PIV|cam_H3|rth, FIB|ote62_up|15m, FIB|ote705_up|15m, FIB|ote79_up|15m: each row's level at its own known second), 12-14 session VWAP and +-1, +-2 sigma bands (Asia, London, RTH anchors, snapshots at every 15th minute), 15 equal highs/lows 15-min (LQP eqH2 / eqL2), 16 equal highs/lows 1-h. Reference: BT swing flip (5-min fractal swings), already scored.
No other family, parameter or variant is tried; if a family is empty or unbuildable it is reported as such.
