# ZL1_Q4_OPTIONS prereg (ZL-1, 2026-11-04 ~19:20Z): options / 0DTE structure around 2025-10-23 and its relation to the NY-AM reversion (Q4 slice, section ZL-1 of Q4_NQ_STRUCTURE.md)
Written and hashed BEFORE any outcome statistic. Descriptive + causal day-level association only; no trading claim.
Data (all on disk): OPRA NDX and NDXP daily bars (opts/NDX_ohlcv-1d_*, NDXP_ohlcv-1d_*; volume per option symbol, expiry parsed from the OSI symbol), NDX OPRA statistics (opts/NDX_statistics_*, stat_type 9 = open interest), CME NQ options and ES options daily bars (NQOPT_ohlcv-1d, ES_ohlcv-1d, parent quarterly symbols only: a coverage caveat), the 0DTE GEX tables (opt0dte/gex_NDXF, gex_SPXF: F0 and gamma, computed from NQ/ES options) as reference. Underlying level for 'near spot' = the previous-day NQ raw close (path minus frame offset) for NDX and the ES close is not used. Days = label days 2023-04-03..2026-10-13 (options data end 10-13/10-16).
Day features (each computed for day d from day d options data, used for day d+1 = CAUSAL previous-day value): F1 total NDX+NDXP option volume; F2 0DTE share of volume (expiry = trade date); F3 NDXP share of volume; F4 put/call volume ratio; F5 volume within +-1% of spot / total; F6 0DTE volume within +-1% of spot / 0DTE total; F7 NDX OI put/call ratio (all expiries, from statistics type 9; coverage reported per month); F8 OI share within +-2% of spot (OI concentration); F9 OI skew proxy = put OI in [0.90, 0.97] S / call OI in [1.03, 1.10] S; F10 CME NQ-option volume / ES-option volume (parent-symbol subset); F11 weekday 0DTE indicator (Mon/Wed/Fri) x F2; F12 first-hour NQ opening-range share is NOT an option feature and is not used.
Targets: T1 daily P&L of the 3-book NY-AM fade bundle (out/nyam_corrected_daily.pkl, ONRTH+VWAPF+ORBF, study lots); T2 signed OR-break outcome of the day (CAL fade_cond_tables hit flag: +1 / -1 / 0; reversion = hit -1 or 0 per CAL definition; use the stored 'hit').
Tests: (a) level table: each feature's mean by quarter 2023Q2..2026Q4 and 2026 vs 2023-25 (Welch t, Mann-Whitney); (b) day-level Spearman of F(d-1) with T1(d) and T2(d) in three samples (all, 2023-25, 2026) = 12 x 2 x 3 = 72 tests; (c) quarter-level: Spearman across the 15 quarters between the quarterly feature mean and the quarterly mean bundle P&L and OR-break hit rate = 24 tests; (d) NDX availability: coverage of NDX OI and NDXP volume by month and whether the reversion quarters line up with the availability changes. n_tested = 96. Family control: 2,000 shuffled-day-label draws (days permuted within calendar quarter) for the day-level tests, statistic = max |rho| over the 12 features; p_family reported with BH q across the 96.
Verdict rule: a feature is 'associated' only if its day-level rho has raw p <= 0.01, the same sign in 2023-25 and 2026, and family p <= 0.10; otherwise descriptive.
