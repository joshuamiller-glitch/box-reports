# ZL1_SIZEUP_FAMILIES_prereg - size-up on rows with a flipped level of the families whose flagged trades were winners (2026-11-02, written BEFORE any size-up cell was scored; the only prior knowledge is the skip-test result of ZL1_FLIP_FAMILIES_prereg: skipping these rows lost money)
Families (the same flip masks as ZL1_FLIP_FAMILIES, same single flip definition: level broken by a print >= 10 pts, flip valid 4 h, flagged if a fresh flip level lies ahead within min(40, target distance)): S1 the 8 shipped zone-line values, S2 VWAP Asia bands, S3 VWAP London bands, S4 Asia session H/L, S5 prior-day close.
Single action, no tuning: planned lots x1.5 on flagged rows (engine extra_mult 1.5, integer half-up with minimum 1 lot as the engine does). Second action, one variant of the same idea: on rows that the shipped zone layer halves (7 lines at D20 and OTE-62 at D30), when a flip of the family is present restore the full lots (extra_mult 2.0 on layer-and-family rows). 10 cells in all (5 families x 2 actions); no other multiplier, family, window or threshold is tried.
Scoring: D7 V13Y lock=None engine (headline), windows L3M L6M HOLD MID PRE X', per calendar year; first-order row-level dollars (+0.5 x the flagged taken trades' base net for x1.5) with 200-draw row placebos (A same count per book x session, B same count per day, C other-day same book x session; rank = share of placebo gains at least as good); engine mean delta, maxDD, worst-20 and the equal-risk mean (cell mean scaled to the base worst-20) per window; overlap with the BT swing flip (Jaccard). A cell is a lead only if the engine mean delta is positive in L6M, HOLD and PRE with placebo ranks <= 0.05 in L6M and HOLD AND the equal-risk mean (at base worst-20) is not below the base mean in HOLD. Size-up adds risk; a mean gain that disappears at equal worst-20 is labelled VOLUME.
