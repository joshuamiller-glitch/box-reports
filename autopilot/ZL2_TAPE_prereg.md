# ZL-2 PREREG (T-ZONES-3 ADDENDUM part 2: TAPE zone streams), written 2026-11-01 ~17:00Z, before any outcome statistic

Scope: descriptive statistics only (no strategy scoring; ZL-3 does the drawdown scoring). Instrument NQ, path frame (ds3_g2 SC), tape = de-duplicated monthly files (out/tape_dedup NQ_dropin), CAP is a parameter, built to the OLD CAP (label day 20260206, labelled OLD CAP) and then extended by appending days (T-RECENT-REDO), never rebuilt.
Disclosure before the statistics: the stream builders were run first (counts only). gen_pools.py also computed the outcome-like columns of pool_events.parquet (mfe15, reclaim5, hold15) but no statistic of them was looked at before this file. The first draft of TDA used thresholds that produced 97 events in 3 years; thresholds were moved on event FREQUENCY only (outcomes not computed) to (5m: 80 contracts, 2x median bin, share 0.55; 15m: 150, 2x, 0.55); the 30-m window was replaced by 15 m. Offsets of the path frame per (4-h block, contract) are estimated from the path array; 13 roll days where the library switched contract before the tape's front rule (bad_days.csv) are excluded from every stream.

## Streams (parameters fixed in code/zl2/*.py docstrings, which are part of this prereg)
TPF closed-anchor tape profile lines (sess day week month sw15 sw1h x poc vah val hvn0-2 lvn0-2); TCP composite 5/20/60 completed label days; TVP developing POC / HVN (>= P90 1-pt bin) / LVN (<= P10 interior) / single-print VOID (<= 0.1 x median) for the label day and the D6 session; TDA delta-at-price absorption 5m/15m; TCD cum-delta high/low (confirm 300 s), divergence bear/bull (gap >= 100), burst (record |delta60| >= P99 of previous 20 days, confirm 60 s); TLP prints >= 25/50/100 kept 1 h / 4 h and the 3-print cluster; TLQ measured liquidity pools (pool = aggressor volume within 2 ticks beyond the level in the 60 s from the break; strength = median of the last 200 completed same-type pools).
Formation rule: a row is knowable at tk = last second used + 1; ek = first path index >= tk. Freshness = never touched since ek (end); touch episodes end/end2/end3 (gap 60 s). Decay = cap (profile lines: next row of the anchor, <= 30 d; TVP: leaves the node set, else anchor end + 4 h (session) / 24 h (day); TDA/TCD div/burst: 4 h; TCD extremes: label-day end; TLP: 1 h / 4 h).

## Descriptive statistics (fixed now)
1. Stream table: rows, rows/good label day, share touched before cap, median seconds to first touch, share with >= 2 / >= 3 touch episodes, strength terciles, share of the 44,370 capped signals with a fresh row AHEAD within 20 pts (between entry and target) and BEHIND within 20 pts.
2. Level event study per stream (support / resistance hit-rate): <= 1,500 rows sampled (seed 7) with the approach side consistent with dr; first touch within min(cap, 4 h) of formation; touch rate; HOLD5 = after the first touch price retraces 5 pts back toward the side it came from before trading 5 pts through the near edge, within 60 min (ambiguous = through); HOLD10 same with 10. Placebos: 200 random-level draws (the stream's own |distance from price at formation| values permuted across rows, same count, own side and time) and 200 shuffled-day draws (distances cyclically shifted across the time-ordered rows by >= 20 % of n). Report real, placebo mean and 2.5/97.5 pct, rank of the real value, by strength tercile, and by window (X' / PRE / HOLD / all; new windows L1M..MID to be added at the cap extension).
3. Pools: per level type: n levels, share broken before cap, pool quantiles (P10 / 50 / 90), share with pool < 10 / >= 100 / >= 300 contracts, by D6 session; whether the pool predicts the move beyond: tercile (within type) of the realised pool (same window) and of the causal expected pool -> mean mfe15, reclaim5 rate, hold15, with the difference top - bottom tercile and a 200-draw within-type shuffle of the pool labels as the null.
4. Density features at the 44,370 signals (definition now, ZL-3 scores): for the sets S8 (coded lines), S11, TAPE (all tape streams except the 1-h large-print lines), TAPE+S11: counts of fresh lines within 20 / 40 / 60 pts AHEAD (between entry and entry + side x D) and BEHIND (entry - side x D .. entry), distinct 2-pt buckets, distance to the nearest ahead / behind / any, ratio (n_ahead40 + 1) / (n_behind40 + 1). L2 zones of ZL-1 added when its streams land.
5. Causality audit: 100 random rows (across all stream families) recomputed with the tape truncated at their as-of second; max difference in level and strength; plus the path-array checks (ek monotone, tk <= t_ek).
No P&L, no dP(target), no trade outcome is computed in ZL-2. Ranking of anything is by the descriptive hold-rate rank vs placebo only and is a LEAD label at most (family of ~128 streams: with 200 draws a rank >= 0.975 occurs by chance in ~3 streams).
