## 1. THE SET (N = 11 streams) - POST-HOC-SELECTED
Chosen AFTER seeing the sign of the adjusted dP(target) in all windows (DS3_POI_MAP poi_map.parquet, group ALL, fresh, D=20 and D=40, strata-adjusted). The user named 8 streams (MAS:ich_kijun:1h, PRF:lvn1:sw1h, PDX:day:M, PRF:vah:sw15, CRT:M:4h, MAS:kc_up:4h, PIV:cam_H3:rth, "PRF lvn/hvn on sw1h" = lvn1 + hvn0). Objective rule applied to the other 586 streams with rows: dP < 0 in PRE, X', L6M, HOLD AND L3M at D=20 and at D=40 (fresh, ALL), every one of the four non-L3M windows <= -0.025 at D=20 and <= 0 at D=40, n(ahead) >= 50 in each of the four windows and >= 30 in L3M. The rule returns 8 streams: CRT:M:4h, MAS:kc_up:4h, MAS:ich_kijun:1h, PRF:lvn1:sw1h, PRF:hvn0:sw1h, FIB:ote62_up:15m, FIB:ote705_up:15m, FIB:ote79_up:15m. Of the user's 8 names, PDX:day:M fails the D=40 rule, PRF:vah:sw15 and PIV:cam_H3:rth have a positive L3M; they are kept because the user named them (labelled named-not-objective). Set = those 8 + PDX|day|M + PRF|vah|sw15 + PIV|cam_H3|rth = 11. Sign agreement in the 4 non-L3M windows alone is cheap (82 of 586 streams at D=20), in all five windows at D=20 50 streams, at both D 24 streams: the table is out/zones2/set_selection_table.csv. PRE and X' are NOT independent evidence of the set (they were part of the sign screen); no window is an out-of-sample test of the SET. The tests that can still say something are the P&L actions (a different statistic from dP(target)), the random-level placebo and the shuffled-day placebo.
Exact definitions (causal, knowable only after the source bar/segment closes; price frame = the study path):
* PDX|day|M  prior completed Globex day (18:00-17:00 ET) (high+low)/2, active the whole next day.
* MAS|ich_kijun|1h  Ichimoku kijun-sen of 1-h bars: (highest high of the last 26 closed 1-h bars + lowest low of the same 26)/2; updated at each 1-h close.
* MAS|kc_up|4h  upper Keltner of 4-h bars (anchored 18:00 ET): EMA20(close) + 2 x ATR10 (simple mean of the last 10 true ranges); updated at each 4-h close.
* CRT|M|4h  midpoint (high+low)/2 of the last CLOSED 4-h bar (CRT range 50%).
* PIV|cam_H3|rth  Camarilla H3 of the prior completed RTH session (09:30-16:00 ET): Close + 1.1 x (High-Low)/4.
* PRF|vah|sw15  value-area high (70% of volume, standard POC-outward expansion, 1-s volume spread uniformly over the ticks of each second, 1-s bar approximation) of the volume profile of the leg between the two last consecutive confirmed fractal-2/2 pivots on 15-m bars; active from the confirming bar close until the next pivot.
* PRF|lvn1|sw1h  second-lowest-volume trough (Gaussian-smoothed 2-pt sigma profile, trough prominence >= 8% of peak) of the profile of the leg between the last two confirmed fractal-2/2 pivots on 1-h bars. PRF|hvn0|sw1h = the highest peak (prominence >= 10%) of the same profile.
* FIB|ote705_up|15m  with H3 = max of the last 3 confirmed fractal-2/2 swing highs and L3 = min of the last 3 confirmed swing lows on 15-m bars (range >= 4 pts): L3 + 0.705 x (H3-L3); ote62_up uses 0.62, ote79_up 0.79.
All eleven are LINES (acting as support or resistance depending on side). A POI is "ahead" for a signal if it lies between the entry and the target (distance from the entry to the line, <= target distance); "fresh" = not touched since it formed (set at its first touch); "touched" = the nearest already-touched line ahead (touched at least once; the exact count "touched once" is not in the store - touched = >=1, stated); "any" = nearest fresh or touched.

## 2. Variants (the multiplicity)
* Units: SET (any-of-11) + each of the 11 alone = 12 units. Stacked-POI variant (two set streams within 5 pts of each other, both within D) on the SET.
* D: 10 / 15 / 20 / 30 / 40 pts and 25 / 50 / 75 % of the target distance (8 settings). Freshness fresh / touched / any (3).
* Actions (ahead POI): RT = re-target to POI minus 2 ticks (0.5 pt) keeping lots and stop (if POI-0.5 < 2 pts the trade is skipped, counted); SKIP; Z50 = size 50%; WT = wait until price trades 1 tick through the POI then enter at market (stop and target as the signal, target re-based on the fill; whole planned lot); B73 = 70% now, the 30% added at market after price trades through the POI (original stop and target). Bases: MKT (signal at market, 1 lot) and SPL (the 70/30 dip-3 split, live base). 5 actions x 2 bases.
* Opposite uses (SET only): TC = target = nearest fresh set POI at distance >= 30 pts (and <= 60 or <= 90 pts) minus 2 ticks, else the signal's own target (MKT and SPL, 2 caps); R30 = the 30% rests as a limit AT the nearest fresh set POI behind the entry (1..30 pts, above the stop), 1-tick trade-through needed, via the CF engine mechanic b (SPL base).
* Per book (10) / session (6) / side (2) as group columns on every cell. Lock-engine stack on the primary cells and on every cell that is >= 0 per signal in L6M and HOLD and PRE.
* Placebos (set cells): (i) random-level: same fire count per (book x session x target-distance bin x side) stratum, distances sampled from the real fired distance pool of the book; (ii) permuted-distance among the real fired trades within book; (iii) shuffled-day: the whole POI-ahead vector (fire and distance) taken from another trade of the same book x side x session on a different label day. 300 draws for the PRIMARY cells, 60 for the rest. N-basis gains are the placebo scale.
* PRIMARY cells (stated before reading): SET, D=20 pts, fresh, RT / SKIP / Z50 / WT / B73 on SPL, and RT on MKT; TC (cap 60) on SPL; R30 on SPL. 9 primary cells.
* Honest cell count = every (unit x D x fresh x action x base) scored, counted in out/zones2/CELL_COUNT.json and written to UF_TRIALS side=plan36_zones2 (n_cells, n_eff).
Tiers by V2 gates (PASS needs L6M and HOLD and PRE and X' >= 0, above MDE in L6M and HOLD, placebo rank >= 0.95, stack >= 0 in L6M and HOLD, cumulative RC p<=0.05); never softened. Bases M, N, LIVE-P9 side by side; profit results must be >= 0 on N.
