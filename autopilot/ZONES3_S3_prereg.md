## 1 Base and units
Reference R0 = shipped one-convention zone layer: skip every signal with any of the 8 coded lines (MAS ich_kijun 1h, PDX day M, CRT M 4h, MAS kc_up 4h, PIV cam_H3 rth, FIB ote62/705/79 up 15m; fresh; ahead; D = 20). Reference R1 = D7 v1.0d zone layer = 7 lines skip D20 + OTE-62 B73 at D30 (unit O62_30 x b73).
UNITS (exclusive ownership, rows in R0's skip set): one per line = signals where THAT line is the only one of the 8 within 20 pts (8 units); STK = >= 2 lines within 20; O62_30 = OTE-62 within 30 and none of the other 7 within 20 (superset of the OTE62 exclusive unit; the extra 20-30 pt rows are NOT skipped in R0); ALL = whole R0 skip set. Distance a = nearest fresh ahead distance of the unit's line(s) from the signal price E.
ACTIONS on the unit's rows (everything else stays as R0): skip (= R0, delta 0 by construction), take (un-skip: the reference for what the skip is worth), half (lots x0.5 through the engine multiplier, integer min-1 rounding as the engine), rt (target = line - 2 ticks = a - 0.5 pt, 70/30 split re-simulated on the 1-s path with the L2SE cell; skipped if new target < 2 pts), b73 (F now, 1-F added at market the second after price trades 1 tick through the line), wt (whole lot at market the second after the trade-through; stop original level, target fill + td), rb (F now; 1-F rests as a limit AT the line, armed after the trade-through, fills only on a 1-tick trade-through back to the line (the reclaim retest); fill at the line), d1/d3/d5 (the whole 70/30 trade re-fired N minutes later at market, new bracket from the new fill; dropped if past the arm end). F per book x session as the engine (KB .5, SM .75, HM .75/.5, others .7); add slip 0.25/added lot for market adds and the delayed/WT market fill, none for the rb limit. PE 45-s confirm rows and PB (market-entry book) rows keep the shipped skip for entry-changing actions (rt b73 wt rb dN); half and take apply to them. All units x actions = 11 x 10 = 110 cells (ALL included); cells swept for selection = 9 line-type units (8 lines + STK + O62_30; ALL is context) x 8 non-reference actions (half rt b73 wt rb d1 d3 d5; take and skip are references) = 80 + ALL 8 = 88 scored alternatives; with 3 fill bases = 264 runs.
FILLS: N = shipped convention (no target-fill correction; ties to the quoted 22.9k/97.0k/241.2k maxDD); M = measured (target correction tc per year on every target exit, base rows rebuilt with the same kernel); LC = LIVE-CAL = M + stop-class exits 2.45 everywhere + passive-fill haircut on the action's LIMIT leg (rb: strict 1-tick trade-through already in all bases, then keep 60% of fills, deterministic per-row uniform). The base dip leg of the 70/30 itself is not haircut in LC (applies equally to R0 and every cell). RANKING BASIS = M (measured); N and LC beside.

## 2 Measures (drawdown first), every cell vs R0, per window L3M / L6M / HOLD / PRE / X' (+ALL, SEL)
Weekly cumulative series of the composite: maxDD, worst-20 weeks (sum of the 20 worst; windows shorter than 20 weeks report the sum of all losing weeks as the engine's stats do), weekly SD, Calmar = mean / maxDD; delta vs R0 (negative maxDD change = cut), mean $/wk and its paired-weekly MDE (2.8 x SE of the weekly delta). EQUAL-RISK mean: lots re-scaled through the engine lot table (lscale, secant on ALL worst-20 to R0's) and the mean change reported. Per book x session cube (6x6xND) stored for every cell; winners shown per book and session. Volume = sum of final per-book sizes; volume ratio vs R0 reported.
RISK-KEEP label: maxDD cut >= 20% in L6M (or in PRE) with the weekly mean change inside its MDE (for an action vs R0 the cut is vs R0's maxDD), AND beats the same-volume placebo (rank >= 0.90), AND PRE / X' / HOLD agree in sign (cut >= 0 in each). Labels are LABELS (no PASS claims): RISK-KEEP / LEAD / DEAD-ISH.
Three gates on the delta vs R0 (STRICT / TOLERANT(literal, T-SIXTY addendum) / NOW-ONLY): STRICT = delta mean >= 0 and maxDD cut >= 0 in all five windows; TOLERANT = L3M and L6M delta mean > 0 and maxDD cut >= 0, PRE and X' delta mean >= -0.5 MDE, PRE worst-20 no worse than R0's, drop L6M->PRE less than 100% of the L6M gain; NOW-ONLY = L3M and L6M delta mean > 0 and maxDD cut >= 0 (anything earlier). TOL-B / TOL-nc (tolerant without the worst-20 / without the fade clause) post-hoc beside.
Forfeited-target count: rows of the unit that exit at target under R0-take but whose action exits other than target, vs stops avoided (the RISK-OFF lesson), per cell, L6M and all.

## 3 Placebos and nulls (M basis)
Placebo per (unit, action) cell, 50 draws, seeds fixed: RANDOM-LEVEL same-count: draw the same number of rows from R0's skip set within book x session x target-bin x side strata (zl.random_fire strata), apply the SAME action with a random distance drawn from the unit's real distance pool; for half the lot cut is the same volume. SAME-VOLUME random skip/size-cut for the lot-reducing actions (take, half, rt-drops, wt no-fills): random rows of the skip set un-skipped / halved so that the total volume change matches (calibrated within 5%). Rank = share of draws the real cell's maxDD cut (and Calmar change) beats. BEST-OF-N chance null: the same selection rule (best action per unit on PRE+X' ... and best cell overall) applied to the placebo matrix draw by draw (draw j of every cell of the unit, action labels carried by the placebo), giving the null distribution of the best-of-N maximum; the real best is reported against its 95th percentile. Also leave-one-episode-out for each winner: baseline drawdown episodes (runs of consecutive weeks below the running max of R0 in ALL) are dropped one at a time and the maxDD cut, Calmar and delta mean recomputed; report the minimum and the share of the total gain carried by the largest episode.
Rankings: HONEST = selected on SEL (PRE u X') by Calmar change then maxDD cut and judged on L6M / HOLD (and L3M); POST-HOC = ranked on L6M Calmar change / maxDD cut with PRE / X' TOLERANT. Both shown; ranks not kills; the user picks.
Composite at the end: best action per unit under each ranking (chosen on its own selection window, all units at once, then re-run as ONE composite on the engine) vs R0 and vs R1; every window; plain sentence.
Cells scored N_cells = 88 alternatives x 3 bases + 3 references + equal-risk runs (~6 per M cell) + placebo 50 x 88 = ~5.2k engine runs; cumulative-program deflation not applied.
