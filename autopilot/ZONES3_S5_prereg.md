# ZONES-3 section 5 (S5): zone density as a regime. PREREG, written before any outcome statistic on D7 v1.2 (2026-11-01 ~22:40Z)
Base: D7 v1.2 (code/d7base/d7_v12.py run_v12, python3 -I): TH-L2 loss ON / profit OFF, U7 30 min (agent-built assumption), zones HALF (7 non-OTE lines of the 8 at D20), OTE-62 B73 D30, X1-X5 + signal-state shadow, NY-AM 1 lot, 12R lock. Lock-off and v1.1 (u7=0) frames for finalists only.
Windows (code/cap/windows.py, label days to 20261030): L1M, L2M (view), L3M, L6M, HOLD, MID, PRE, X'; Feb-Oct = MID+... shown as F9 = 2026-02-07..10-31. Old-cap numbers are not quoted.
Measure: all rules are ADDED to the base through d7lib extra_skip / extra_mult hooks (OR-ed with the shipped zone half); rule = mask over engine d rows; no look at any outcome to build masks.

## Features (D7-computable from its own state; all causal at the signal second kin)
Set = the 8 coded SET8 lines (kijun1h, PDmid, CRT4h, KCup4h, camH3, OTE62, OTE705, OTE79; OTE-62 is one of the 8, B73 handling in the base unchanged). Per line i the nearest FRESH (never touched since formed) POI of its stream: a_i = distance AHEAD (line above a long entry / below a short) and b_i = distance BEHIND (line already on the other side of E), both UNRESTRICTED by the target/stop distance (poi_core.feat_kernel with td=sd=1e9; ahead tie-out with the engine zl.mat 45,220 finite values, max abs diff 0.0).
nA_x = #lines with a_i <= x ; nB_x = #lines with b_i <= x ; nT_x = nA_x + nB_x ; dmin = min_i min(a_i,b_i) (inf = no line) ; dA = min_i a_i ; rho_x = (nA_x+1)/(nB_x+1). Variant 'any' = fresh or touched (min(af,am)).
No-man's land (NTE-1 px.loc, nt_g_px definition reproduced from the 1-s arrays): loc = (E - low)/(high - low) of (PDR) the prior-day bar (cf_engine 1d), (ON) the overnight range 18:00 to min(now,09:30) (undefined 09:30-10:00), (SESS) the session range so far (>= 15 min into the session); defined if width > 8 pts; mid third = 1/3 <= loc <= 2/3.

## Cells (all-scope, per-book and per-session read from the cube of the same run)
48 masks: congestion ahead nA_x>=k (x 20/40/60, k 2,3; plus k=1 at 40/60), behind nB_x>=k (same), around nT_x>=k (k 2,3,4 at 20/40/60; any-freshness at 40 k 2,3), open space dmin>y (y 20/30/40/60), open ahead dA>y (40, 60), ratio rho_x>=2 (ahead-heavy) and <=0.5 (behind-heavy) at x=40/60, no-man's land mid_PDR, mid_ON, mid_SESS, any of the three, PDR&ON, and mid_R & open40 / mid_R & nT40<=1 (R in PDR, ON, SESS).
Actions: skip (row removed before the lock engine; run only if the mask flags <= 35% of rows), half (lots x0.5 via the ER-row multiplier, integer half-up min 1), up (x1.5), re-target rt (congestion-ahead and ahead-heavy masks only: target moved to the nearest fresh line ahead minus 2 ticks (0.5 pt, floor 0.25 pt); approximation: the flagged live rows become market entries at the signal (no 70/30 dip, PE-delayed and B73 rows untouched) -> the control rtc is the SAME market entry with the ORIGINAL target and rt is judged against rtc as well as against the base). 146+10 cells.
Finalists (fixed here): union of (i) top 12 cells by the honest score = mean over PRE and X' of the maxDD cut (fraction of base), (ii) top 12 by the post-hoc score = mean maxDD cut over L6M, HOLD, MID, (iii) every cell with L6M maxDD cut >= 20%, (iv) the best cell of each family (congA, congB, congT, open, ratio, nml, nml_open) by the mean of both scores. Finalists get: per-scope reruns (per book, per session), placebos, equal-risk, lock-off and v1.1 frames.

## Metrics (drawdown first, every cell, every window L1M L2M L3M L6M HOLD MID PRE X' and F9)
weekly-cumulative maxDD, worst-20-weeks sum, weekly SD, Calmar, mean $/wk with the paired MDE (2.8 x SE of the weekly delta), n weeks (short windows have < 20 losing weeks, so worst-20 is not a tail measure there; shown, not relied on); equal-risk mean (the lot table scaled via lscale until the rule's worst-20 on the ALL window equals the base's; mean change at that scale); per book (6 core + X) and per session from the cube.
Placebos on finalists: A = random rows, same number flagged, uniform over the scope (density-shuffle); B = same count stratified by book x session (same volume composition). Same action. 40 draws per finalist-action in the first pass (extended to 100 where the rank is < 0.10). Rank = share of draws with maxDD cut >= observed (smaller = better); also rank on Calmar gain.
Labels: RISK-KEEP = L6M maxDD cut >= 20% with the L6M mean delta inside the MDE, plus honesty columns (beats placebo A and B at rank <= 0.10; HOLD/PRE/X' each cut > 0); (volume effect) = cut not distinguishable from placebo B; (L6M only) = HOLD, PRE, X' do not agree. No PASS claim, everything post-hoc, Shadow only.
Three columns (gain = maxDD cut, mean delta used for the noise clauses): STRICT = cut > 0 in L3M, L6M, HOLD, MID, PRE, X'. TOLERANT (literal, headline) = cut > 0 in L3M and L6M AND the mean delta in PRE and X' each >= -0.5 x that window's MDE AND PRE worst-20 not worse than the base's AND the drop from L6M to PRE cut less than 100% of the L6M cut (i.e. PRE cut > 0). TOL-B (clause 5 loosened: PRE cut > -1.0 x L6M cut) and TOL-nc (clause 5 dropped) post-hoc beside. NOW-ONLY = cut > 0 in L3M and L6M. L1M/L2M never select.
Sessions/books: every finalist per book x session; no per-session D or line-set tuning beyond reading the same cell per session.
dead_lookup is run on every cell spec before scoring (out/zones3_s5/dead_lookup.txt); append_cells after. Outputs: autopilot/DS3_ZONES_3_S5.md; code l2vast/ds3/code/zones3_s5/; results /mnt/l2/l2vast/ds3_zones3_new/S5/ and out/zones3_s5/.
